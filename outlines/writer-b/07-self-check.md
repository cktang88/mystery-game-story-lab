## 7. What passes, what fails, what was fixed

**Passes:** every seed lands visibly; the banned list is clear; the founding crime, rules, factions, two-column timeline, receipts ledger and dates are sealed and computed; each pillar has its own verb and they collide; the anchor persists; each act opens on a small hook; the climax hinges on a small earlier choice; refusal is respected at every committed choice; the clue matrix meets three-clue, two-area; no device undoes a death.

**Partial:** the midpoint handoff nearly tells (mitigated by gating); chapter 15's choice is close to false (fix proposed); jargon load (fix proposed); two late elements graded only here; the 1980s Seoul strand is more structural than visual in places.

**Fails (stage-dependent):** no outside playtests; cold-reader clarity unverified.

**Fixed during writing:**
1. `01-options.md`: three receivers of the hall corrected to two; reach limit (first jar), failed sends and the open-receipts register, the scope of the memory cost, the election date, the opening-image clarification and the rehearsal all added and logged there.
2. Almaz's vision window (22:29-22:45) begins after the demand (22:22); her vision was rewritten in `02` and `05` to show Sitota *standing as the party during the reading*, not making the demand.
3. The Ethiopian date was computed: Pagume 5, 2155 EC is 11 September 2163 (not the 10th), so the whole present-day chronology was set from it; Tsehay's loss (39 days), the rehearsal's loss (9 days) and day 100 (20 December) were recomputed.
4. The founder's vision was given a detail ("my bare hand", a grown woman's hand) that excludes Almaz, Tsehay and Hiwot as its sender, so the climax choice is genuinely between two people.
5. The Guest's choice of Hiwot in the hall was made consistent with genome-blindness: it stops before the only body holding something out.
6. Almaz's knowledge was made consistent: she learns on day one (from Tigist's public certificate) that she killed Aster, so she does not kill again, and the raid is Tsehay's.
