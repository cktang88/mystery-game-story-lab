## Known weaknesses for the grader

1. **The founding forgery is guessable from Act 2.** The player suspects it by Day 19. The drama is proof and price, not surprise; the unpredictable beats are the guard, the sister, Varkhuman's death, Shayn's sale, the alien handler and the YT 78 plate.
2. **Vocabulary load.** About a dozen invented terms. Each is introduced through an object first, but a cold playtest is needed.
3. **The Draxt law arrives in Act 4 in detail.** It is planted (pattern-selves, naming by figure, the refusal to unmake), but its consent rule could feel convenient.
4. **Farnxund presides on Day 30 while guilty.** Justified (the guard cannot testify unnamed; her clone status is Shayn's secret), but a grader may want a scene showing why no one can remove her.
5. **The player never sends a message.** The time pillar's verbs are forensic, not active; this is deliberate (sending unmakes days and only Draxt can), but it could be read as a passive time pillar.
