# Writer B: key scenes

Each sketch gives what the player **does**, what they **see and hear**, what was **planted** for it, and what it **changes**. No scene needs reading to follow; on-screen text is limited to numbers the world itself displays (the Mircha boards, gate tallies, the vote wall) and a few spoken lines.

---

## 1. Opening: "Normal" (prologue, 22 August, dusk)

**Does.** Walks Hiwot home along the Narrow with a splint bag on one shoulder. Hiwot runs ahead; the player can catch up or let her go. At a shola fig Hiwot stops and splits a fruit; the player can kneel and look.
**Sees and hears.** A trench three hundred metres deep, the sky a blue strip overhead, monoliths with cut windows, neon cut into basalt, a camel train swaying salt bars past. Inside the fig, a tiny dead wasp. Hiwot, in a sing-song: "The mother stays inside." A Mircha board on the trench wall flips from 21 to **20**. At the mouth of the Guest's Spur, behind a brass-barred gate, the Guest stands at its door: three metres of folded rust-coloured pleats, perfectly still. A veiled woman pours it coffee from a clay jebena. Hiwot waves her half-painted campaign fan. The Guest does not move. A Fig Guard patrol watches it.
**Planted.** Fig wasp; fan; jebena; the veiled woman's posture (she pours with a carrier's lean); the Guard's watch; the board.
**Changes.** Nothing: this is what normal looks like, so the player will know when it breaks.

## 2. Opening image with a question: "The Giveaway" (ch.1, 23 August, dawn)

**Does.** Kneels on a camel boy's back and resets his shoulder with a heel-and-pull (haptic: feel the head slip home). Turns when the trench goes quiet.
**Sees.** The Guest coming out of the Spur for the first time anyone remembers, carrying objects, and putting one into the hands of each person it passes: a brass bell, a broken comb, a child's shoe, a salt bar wrapped in palm. Each person, startled, gives something back. It reaches Tigist and holds out a camel's knucklebone. The player picks something from Tigist's bag to give (chalk, a bandage, a coin). The two objects sit side by side for a beat in close-up: the game's only "subtitle" for exchange.
**Hears.** No voice from the Guest, ever. Camel bells; a murmur that rises as people realise it is emptying its house. Then a shout from the Spur.
**Sees, then.** The Guard dragging a body out of the doorway into the trench. Dahir on his knees. A Guardsman lifts the veil. **The face is the face on every Lemlem poster in the city**, the face of the woman beside Tigist in the crowd, and of Tigist. The crowd's eyes go from the body to every Lemlem in the trench, and stop on her.
**Question it plants.** Why was one of them living veiled with the Guest, and why is it giving everything away? (Its meaning will flip twice: grief, then a demand, then a foundress shedding before she enters.)

## 3. "Nobody Died" (ch.2)

**Does.** Bone-reads the body in a cold room: palpates the neck and feels the top two vertebrae turned on each other; flexes the knees and feels stair wear; runs a thumb down the spine; opens the hands; takes radiographs (a heavy plate camera, a flash, the developing tray coming up grey). Fills the certificate by placing tokens (age range, wear, cause, "is she the Shadow?").
**Sees.** On the plates: one clean clay line; punched-out holes in three vertebrae; an old healed left wrist.
**Hears.** Almaz outside the door, calm, talking to the Chilot clerk. Then the clerk reading the ruling in a flat voice: "A body of Lemlem Lemlem has died. Lemlem Lemlem lives. No death is recorded."
**Changes.** The player learns the law by watching it applied: a woman has been killed and the court has just said she was not. Tigist's certificate says she is not the Shadow, so the Shadow is somewhere.

## 4. "311" (Act I climax, ch.7)

**Does.** Watches television in a trench cafe with Jae-won. Can take photographs of the screen.
**Sees.** The lottery drum turning on a studio carved in rock. The presenter draws: **Lemlem Lemlem**. By custom the winning person's bodies stand. The broadcast cuts between cafes, school yards, a quarry, a jar-house: identical women standing up, one or two at a time, everywhere. A counter in the corner of the screen, the Tsehafi's live tally, climbs: 287... 302... 309... 310... **311**. In the cafe a Lemlem waitress stands with a tray, sits, goes back to work. Tigist does not stand. The counter already includes her: legally, her standing is optional.
**Hears.** The cafe laughing at the spectacle; Jae-won very quiet.
**Changes.** A Lemlem elder is dead and none is missing. Someone extra is standing. The Act I question closes and the Act II question opens in one number.

## 5. "Eight" (staging, ch.11, 3 September)

**Does.** Waits in the Lemlem glass room with Hiwot and Etagegn as the clock reaches 15:44 and the board outside the door flips to **8**. The player has walked this room before, in Mahlet's fever, as a blurred space. Now it is solid and the fever-space is overlaid faintly, like a memory of the future: where the chalked hands will be, where the veil scrap will be held up, where the finger will point. The player re-enacts it for sixteen minutes (hold up the veil, point to the kiln hatch, let Hiwot draw on the floor in chalk, because she did in the vision). At 16:00 the iron plate glows.
**Chooses.** Lay Tigist's hand on the plate and turn the glass; let Hiwot do it ("Can I?"); or do nothing, and Etagegn does it.
**Sees and hears.** The sand runs. Nothing visible happens to the world; something happens to a face. If Tigist: that evening Hiwot asks "Did you bring it?" and Tigist doesn't know what. If Hiwot: Hiwot asks why she has chalk on her hands and who gave the Guest her fan. If Etagegn: the old carver looks up from her stone at Hiwot and asks her name.
**Changes.** The player has paid, at small scale, the exact price the ending will ask for, and learned that a fixed event still leaves the payer open.

## 6. Midpoint: "The Wrist" (ch.12, 4 September)

**Does.** Twelve elders, one at a time, on a stool in the Lemlem house's examination cell: palpate, radiograph, match to a medical leaf, place the name. The game confirms in threes. Almaz watches from the doorway; the player can feel her there by the sound of her ring on the doorframe. The thirteenth is "Aster", left wrist in a sling. Setting a fresh break is a longer haptic sequence: traction, align, feel the ends meet. Midway the player's hands find what is not there: no old callus where Aster's 2140 break should be. The bones around it are thick, worn like a porter's. The guiding radiograph comes up in the tray: **two** clay lines.
**Sees.** "Aster" looking at Tigist, not at the wrist. With her good hand she puts something into Tigist's palm: a small clay figurine, the same one that hangs round Hiwot's neck.
**Does, then.** Signs the certificate: *Aster.* The pen is the player's input.
**Hears.** Almaz's ring stop tapping.
**Changes.** Every Act I fact is still true and means something else: the body was Aster; the woman who walked out of the Spur at 23:35 is in front of her; the 311 included her; Almaz's grief at the jar was for her sister. Tigist has lied to a court for the first time.

## 7. Memorable small moment: "Which One Is Older?" (ch.13)

**Does.** Plays Tigist checking the splint at night in her own setting room. Hiwot wakes.
**Sees.** Hiwot in the doorway, looking from Tigist to the old woman with her face, to Tigist again. "Which one of you is older?" Sitota holds out her hand for Hiwot's figurine. Hiwot gives it. Sitota gives back her own, older and worn smooth. Hiwot laughs: she understood. Tigist did not.
**Changes.** The anchor and the hostage are bonded in the alien's grammar, without a word Tigist can follow. It plants the hall.

## 8. "The Founder's Fan" (ch.13, playable as Sitota)

**Does.** Passing: walks the Lemlem house as Aster to fetch Aster's roll papers. Takes a cup without returning one (her hands want to give something back; the player has to hold the button down to stop them). Walks stairs upright, not leaning. In the founder's room, a glass case holds the lineage relic: a long pleated rust-coloured fan.
**Sees.** Sitota stops. The camera holds on the fan, then on her face, then cuts to a memory-shot of the Guest's back, the same pleats. She does not touch the case.
**Changes.** The founder exchanged something with the Guest and kept its wing. Question: what did she give it, and what was promised?

## 9. M3: "The Palm" (ch.16, 7 September)

**Does.** Cleans Almaz's infected left hand at the setting-room bench under one lamp: unwrap, irrigate, probe. Lays the bagged shard from the Guard beside the wound: the curve matches. Then takes Almaz's right hand to bind the wrist and, as she does, the photograph of the ochre print on the veil is pinned to the lamp; the player turns Almaz's palm into the light and traces the lines against it.
**Sees.** Almaz letting her. They have done this a thousand times, the other way round.
**Hears.** Almaz, quietly, in plain sentences: the fever, the child in the hall, "our mother saw her hand open the jar and never did", the woman at the door, the face under the veil, the morning the court said "not the Shadow". Then: "Nobody died, Tigist. You certified it."
**Changes.** The killer is known and the law cannot touch her. Minutes later, the raid.

## 10. M2: "Every Hand That Is Mine" (ch.18, 9 September)

**Does.** Descends the iron-leaf spiral in Bete Tsehafi, lamp in hand, to the Lemlem shelf of 2131. Wakes the founder's last session. **Leaf-waking at scale:** the scrub bar is a long ribbon of sound; the player moves through it, pausing on voices, and asks the 2131 Tsehafi questions by laying tokens (the fan, the glass, the fig, the word "vision").
**Hears.** An old woman's voice, dry and amused, among elders. She describes the 2090 fever: the hall, the bodies in election cloth, numbers changing, the Guest folding itself into the jar, "a small one of me, holding out a glass of black sand, and my hand, clay to the wrist, taking it." An elder: "Then you must open it." The founder: "I saw it paid. By my own hand. Every hand that is mine is my hand." Silence. "Outstanding: one."
**Sees.** The leaf rusting on its shelf as it plays. Dawit at the top of the spiral, listening, not moving.
**Changes.** Nothing was ever refused. Someone in the hall must send this tonight, and lose everything lived since 2 November 2090. The founder knew it would be a copy, and waited for one.

## 11. Climactic image: "311 and the Guest" (ch.20, 11 September, 22:40)

**Does.** As Tigist, stands in the roll among 310 women in identical white election cloth after the doctrine has fallen. Can only watch, and look for Hiwot.
**Sees.** The vote wall behind the dais still rolling, one body at a time, Mekdes's column climbing past Dawit's. The great door to the Narrow opens. The Arho come in first with lamps and camel bells, Dahir at their head. Then the Guest, walking slowly, shedding a pleat every few steps; each falls like a dropped fan onto the white cloth of a woman it passes. It walks through all of them, and to it they are all one person. It stops before the smallest.
**Hears.** Bells, then nothing.
**Planted.** The first pleat (ch.6); the fig wasp (prologue); the fans everywhere; the founder's dictation (ch.18).

## 12. "By Reflex" (ch.20, 22:44-22:45)

**Does.** Nothing can be done; the player can move the camera.
**Sees.** Hiwot, who has stepped out of the roll holding up the fan she painted. The Guest reaching toward her. On the dais, Tsehay's ringed, spotted, shaking hand closing on the glass at her throat, finding the plate, turning. The sand running. Tsehay's face changes the way the glass-widows' faces did. She looks round the hall as if she has just walked in, sees Almaz, and asks her, very politely, why everyone is staring.
**Changes.** The vision that set the steward on the murder was sent by the woman who swore to refuse it, in the attempt to prevent what it showed. The loop closes in front of the player.

## 13. The choice: "The Glass" (ch.20, 22:46-22:52)

**Does.** Watches the Guest take Hiwot's fan and place a glass of black sand in her hands; watches it fold itself into the fig's mouth and the mouth close from within. Then Hiwot turns with the glass held out in both hands, to Tigist and to the old woman beside her, both with clay to the wrist from lifting the book. On the wall: the founder's receipt, **open**.
**Chooses.** Take it and lay Tigist's hand on the plate; step back so Sitota takes it; or stand still. (Standing still is respected: after a long beat Sitota takes it, with the Guest's gesture for *it was given to me*.)
**Sees.** Whoever turns it, the last sixteen minutes going back seventy-three years into a fever in a refugee camp. The face of the one who sent, looking at Hiwot as at a stranger's child. Then, through every shaft in Bete Tsehafi, the night's leaves sealing early: **"Outstanding: none."**

## 14. Quiet beat: "The Naming"

**Does.** Hears Jae-won's broken hand fumble a cassette into his recorder; Aster's voice fills the hall: *"Tonight she walks out in my robe and I stay in her veil."* The Afe Chilot asks the identifier whose hand was at the dead woman's neck.
**Chooses.** Name Almaz (point; the photograph of the palm print goes up on the wall), or say nothing. If Tigist has paid, the player is choosing for a woman reading her own notes about a stranger with her face.
**Sees.** Almaz standing very straight either way.

## 15. Final images

**(c) Branch T, "Someone Set This Well."** Months later, the setting room. Tigist, not remembering any of it, works with hands that remember everything. Hiwot sits on the stool and puts Tigist's hand on her right forearm. Tigist's fingers stop at the old break. "Someone set this well." "You did."

**(b) Branch S, "Sitota Engida."** The Tsehafi's window in Bete Tsehafi. A housing-lottery ticket prints, slowly, with a new name. Sitota, who does not remember ever having lacked one, looks at Dahir to see whether this is good. Dahir, who remembers, holds it for her.

**(a) Every branch, "Again."** Day 100. The hall at dawn, nearly empty. Guards with arms lowered; Dawit among them. Almaz at the back, or not, depending. The fig's mouth opens from inside. Hiwot stands alone in front, holding out a new paper fan. Something small and wingless comes out into the light, looks at her, takes the fan, and gives her back the clay-stained fan its mother carried in. Hiwot looks at the old fan, then at the new one in its grip, and smiles: new for old means *again*. Cut to black on the sound of the hall's leaf sealing.
