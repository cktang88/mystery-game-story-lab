## 1. The AI held the founding answer for anyone who asked, and the weight was redundant

**What changed.** The Charter of Silence is now three layered rules (`01` Round 12):

- **(a) Gate by naming.** The Sartapao answers a named person only about ledger lines that name that person. The dead's lines pass to their heirs. Lines under the Ikhshid's seal are read only under that seal.
- **(b) Misleading ownership.** The YT 41 grounding was booked under the founder's Council seal ("Council seal of Tishdhat"), with no executor and no wave sensors recorded. Only an Ikhshid can read it, and read cold it looks like a safety order.
- **(c) Asking is dangerous.** Every question is itself a ledger line, visible to the Master of Dust.

On night −13 the 85-year-old spymaster covered her tracks: she staged a column fault, so the line was automatic and named only the officer of the watch, Pukar. His own question on Day −12 is what alerted her. After his death only his heir can ask: his mother does so on Day 16, and is detained for it.

The other routes to the founding answer were closed too:

- The Old Column's collar was scrubbed in a YT 60 restoration ordered by the Dust, so rings exist only for later arrivals.
- Alien readings are dismissed as interested.

The weight is now necessary arithmetically. Overturning needs two-thirds of the seats voting, and only the weight moves Weigher A.

**Files.** `02` (rules 6, 8 and 9; YT 41, YT 60, Day −13, Days 16 to 18, 31 and 38), `03` (AGI pillar), `05`, `06` (scenes 7 and 14), `01` (Rounds 6 and 12).

**Why.** Each gate explains a different silence: 63 years in which no one could ask, a founder's seal that misleads, and a spymaster who learns who is asking. And the climax again turns on its object.

## 2. Economic arithmetic and the freed children

**What changed.**

- **The bubble.** The shadow quota is now about 1.5 million clones over 16 years (YT 88 to 104, with Shayn's tenure re-dated to YT 88). Named persons went from 3.5 million to 5.0 million on a flat pool, so farn per head is 70% of its old level: the stated 30% fall.
- **The pool.** Tolls 52%, tribute 35%, labour tithes 10%, Draxt relay retainer 3%. The implausible 7% plate-reading fee is gone.
- **Clone bonds.** I took the core of the grader's P3: a licensed clone's farn pays its licensor until 16, and from 16 to 36 the clone pays a 30% labour tithe. Licences trade as bonds, so the first shadow cohort coming of age with no work becomes the war party's motive (crew the Ordu fleets).
- **The reversal.** The tribute ends (−35%), and restitution is negotiated at 10% of the pool for 40 years (−10%), about half the sum taken, with the rest settled in Kish rights. Farn falls 45%, and the arithmetic table in `03` shows it.
- **The children.** Clones are decanted as infants, so the crash cannot "free" them into abandonment. Instead, farn houses can no longer cover crèche fees and try to surrender licences. Abandonment is illegal, so the Vat Bench takes 1.5 million wards. Shayn's Wards Decree voids the shadow licences: no labour tithe, and their own farn goes to their crèches. The children are freed from a bond on their futures, not cast out. The crash is played on Day 39.
- **Smaller fixes.** "About one arrival a decade" replaces "a handful", matching 14 arrivals in 104 years. Chash's share is stated (20% of the tribute; 30% of its farn).

**Files.** `02`, `03` (economy section), `04` (Shayn), `05` (epilogue), `06` (scene 24), `01` (Rounds 6 and 7).

## 4. The disguised founding-debt-to-alien shape

**What changed.** The Draxt cup that rewrote Miwnay's face and palate is gone, together with the taste-loss pathos the grader called engineered. The price is now paid to the human victims, as politics:

- The Ordu, ruled by the former hostage, demand restitution and, by their custom, a hostage "of the blood of the wrong" for four years.

## 5. Time travel needed active verbs

**What changed.**

- **Standing sends** are now a world rule: a conditional commission, "if this happens, send this back to then". A Hush at the named moment means the feared future happened and has been unmade.
- **Composing a send.** On Day 31 Aytash commissions one with a Pench loan. The player writes the sixty glyphs knowing that her enemy, the Acting Master of Dust, will read them first, choosing between a plain warning, a deck-song code and an appeal to the Draxt.
- **Act or wait.** On Day 32 the Hush comes, and the player chooses whether to act on an unconfirmable warning or wait. Miwnay has an earlier act-or-wait decision over the Day 4 plate (Day 13: publish or hold).
- **The Draxt contradiction.** "Unmaking is what liars do" is removed. The Draxt hold that unmade days were never lived, so unmaking wrongs no one; humans mourn them. This is now stated as a standing disagreement between the peoples, and the consent law applies to writing into bodies.
- **Rejected:** a direct personal send to undo a death (Round 13, V2), because it is a reset that would also unmake every other thread.

**Files.** `02` (rules 1 and 3; Days 13, 31 to 32), `03` (time pillar), `05`, `06` (scenes 5, 15, 16), `01` (Rounds 6 and 13).

## 6. Forged-plate rules

**What changed.**

- **Seals.** A seal is a fingerprint of the whole ledger on its sending day, checkable only on that day and only if nothing has changed. A forger needs only the format, which every Duster knows. Any well-formed seal on an acted-on plate is indistinguishable from a genuine one (rule 6).
- **The weight.** It now weighs 96 drachms, not 71. The deficit is perceptible only to a master taster's trained hand. No one has ever weighed it against anything, because it *is* the standard: it sits on its cushion and is set in an empty pan at verdicts.
- **The breadcrumb.** The contrived empty slot is replaced by an intended clue. Nanai-farn had two weights cast, kept the solid twin, and left it in her ossuary on purpose for her heir. Her heir can also read the YT 41 payment line: "two weights of one hundred drachms; sealing fee".

**Files.** `02` (rules; YT 41; Day 31), `05`, `06`, `03`.
