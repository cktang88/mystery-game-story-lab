# Writer B: world, rules and factions

## The place

**The Sielacht** is a small sovereign confederation of tidal islands and diked marsh in an alternate North Sea, about 140,000 people. Its capital, **Sielhaven**, sits behind the sea dike on the mainland marsh. Off it lie the **halligen**: low, undiked islands where houses stand on dwelling mounds (**warfs**) and the sea washes over everything else in a storm. The nearest to the reef is **Grauwarf** (church, pilot station, the Witch's Gate sluice); the most remote is **Lütjewarf** (a single eel-fisher's hut). Between them, twice a day, the sea leaves 20 km² of mud, sand and gullies: **the flats**. Under the flats lies the reef, and under the reef's south-east end the drowned town of **Ammerwarf**, whose church tower stump shows at the lowest springs.

Look and sound of the world: slate water, wide skies, a horizon with nothing on it; the suck of mud; curlews; the dike bell that rings the tide; the low **hum** that only some people feel, which the instruments render as a sound like a bowed wire under the floor.

Visual identity by era, so threads never blur: **2026** cold blue dawns and sodium-free LED white; **2001 to 2019** orange sodium lamps, minidisc and VHS grain; **1371** torch amber and storm black; **hum reconstructions** white line-drawings built out of echoes.

## The four rules (each with mechanism, cap, cost, relationship, verb)

### 1. Crossing (time travel)

- **Mechanism.** Lie inside an empty hollow of the reef at the exact tide its waterline names. A copy of you, body and mind as you are at that moment, climbs out of the same hollow on the date it was made, in the past. You get up, cold and wet, and go home. Islanders say it plainly: **"Nobody crosses. Somebody stays."**
- **Caps.** *No hollow, no crossing*: you can only fill a hollow that already exists, and it is shaped exactly like you. *Nothing crosses but you*: no objects, no clothes. *The window*: never before January 1371, never after the Last Tide of 20 March 2026. *The past is fixed*: every hollow has always been filled by its person; what the record shows will be done.
- **Cost.** A person is created who must live their whole remaining life in the past, out of place, and may die there. One of Mem's children is spent to make them. The sender lives with knowing.
- **Damages** the bond between parent and child (Okke hides for 22 years that his copy lives a mile away). **Only it can save** Jildou's grief for her mother: the copy was there, and she was not alone.
- **Verbs.** *Fulfil the record* (play in the past against constraints you must make true: a theft report, a rescue photograph, a medieval chronicle). *Read the hollow* (lift a plaster face, date the silt over it, read the waterline to compute its tide).
- **Teaching order.** Anomaly (Ch1: an owed boy lies down and nothing visible happens; his 1952 record is stamped PAID); experiment (Ch2: a hollow fits a body exactly); named rule (Ch3: Okke says it); personal use (Ch4: Okke's crossing); bill (Ch7: Gerlof cannot save Sjoukje).

### 2. The Slikborn (cloning)

- **Mechanism.** A copy is perfect: same genome, same scars and fillings, same memories up to the crossing. From the moment it climbs out, it diverges.
- **Caps.** **No navel.** No other way exists to make a copy; human vat cloning has always failed (the "Leiden vats", texture). A copy's foreknowledge runs out when the present reaches its sending date ("being overtaken").
- **Cost.** The **Younger Sibling Law** (1612): a copy is the legal younger sibling of its original, bound to that family and its will. Street contempt: "Smooths". Cosmetic navels are a quiet industry.
- **Damages** the bond between Marrit and Femke (the copy lived the life the original was schooled for; the original is the spare). **Only it can save** Jildou's relation to her father after his death: she keeps playing him, alive, in the past.
- **Verbs.** *Attribution across ages* (same face, different decades, different scars: assign names to bodies, photographs and reconstructions). *Ask the original* (an original can predict their copy: "What would I have done?").

### 3. KERF (AGI)

- **Mechanism.** An auditing intelligence built in 1987 in the Rekkenkeamer. It holds the ledger of every hollow, matches lifted faces to living people, computes each hollow's tide, and builds **reconstructions**: walkable dioramas of an event from seismometers, cameras, phone pings and tide gauges.
- **Caps.** It knows only what its sensors and ledgers hold. By statute it may not analyse Kerf Nul; since 2011 it may not hear the Rib's audio. It never states what it cannot verify; in its reconstructions unknown people appear as blank grey figures. It can be rolled back by its keepers, but by Rekkenkeamer law every version is archived as a ledger book and may be consulted.
- **Want (one sentence).** To close the books: every hollow matched to a name, every name paid.
- **Cost.** Every rollback deleted a true conclusion. The trust between KERF and its keeper is the casualty.
- **Damages** Marrit's control of the truth (its archived versions testify against her). **Only it can save** the world from the Unravelling doctrine (its 1998 proof, and its legal duty to publish a closed ledger).
- **Verbs.** *Reconstruction* (scrub, place, annotate). *Version archaeology* (put the same question to archived versions, v4 in green terminal text, v6 in a flat synthetic voice, v7 in a holographic face; diff their answers to recover a deleted conclusion).

### 4. Mem (aliens)

- **Mechanism.** The reef is a living brood-mother who perceives her whole life at once and hums every sound ever made on or over her body. The pitch of each moment's sound follows the tide height of that moment.
- **Caps.** Only her own body is in range (the reef, the drowned town, the flats over them). Future sound is faint; only her call carries back clearly, as the Promise. Her throat was cut in 1371: she can hum, not sing. Humans hear the hum only through hydrophones tuned by tide phase; Slikborn feel it in the body but cannot select a date.
- **Want (one sentence).** To call her hidden children home before she dies, and let each one choose.
- **Cost.** To hear the past is to hear its crimes. The truth will make the Slikborn look alien to the people who raised them.
- **Damages** the trust between the Slikborn and everyone else. **Only it can save** Marrit from her misreading: hearing her own copy's words in 1371.
- **Verbs.** *Tide-tuned listening* (match a target night's tide curve to pull its sounds out of six centuries; place each sound on the reef map). *Echo-walk* (with KERF: rebuild a vanished space from how sound reflected in it, and walk it).

### The one admitted cheat

Mem's perception across her lifespan. It is never explained, it creates the premise, and it is never used to solve a problem. Everything that solves a problem runs on the rules above, all taught in play before they matter.

### The key resource and its contradiction

The **hollows** are the Sielacht's wealth and its curse. Every hollow is a door into the past and a debt someone must pay; the richer the past was in copies, the heavier the present's bill. The 20th century spent freely, so the 2020s pay: the **Reckoning Decade**, ending at the Last Tide.
