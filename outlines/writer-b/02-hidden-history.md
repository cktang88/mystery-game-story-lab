# The Light Weight: sealed hidden history (revision 2)

This is the solution document. Everything the player can find is a view onto something written here. Canon tier: **locked** unless marked *working*. One keeper (the writer). Changes since the first draft are logged in `08-revision-log.md`.

## Calendar, units and travel

- **YT** (Years of the Thread, from the cable's completion) is the writers' notation. Players see "years since the thread" in the few places a year matters. The present is **YT 104**.
- The game's **Day 1** is the first day of the Marqandi new year, when the Scale opens its session. **Unburned Day** is Day 28, the anniversary of the founding message's "arrival" in YT 41. The First Weighing's verdict fell on the 33rd day of YT 41, and the conspirators set the Second Weighing's verdict for Day 33 to echo it.
- Weight is measured in **drachms**. The Scale's ceremonial weight is nominally 100 drachms; it weighs 96.
- **Travel.** By fast climber: Root to Spark, 2 hours; Spark to Height, about 36 hours; Height to Counterweight, about 64 hours. By ship: Height to Counterweight, about 12 hours; Counterweight to the Spark's shuttle ring, about 20 hours; Counterweight to the Grove (the Draxt court, on a low-gravity moon of Sogh's outer gas giant), about 6 hours. A fold jump to a neighbouring system takes about a day.
- **No faster-than-light signals.** News between star systems travels by ship, so a decision made at Usrushan reaches Marqand a day later at best.

## The rules the history obeys

1. **Who sends, who receives.** Only a Draxt can write a message into the thread, and only at the Counterweight. Only the plates at the Root's Well can receive one. The Draxt sell sends to any client, including **standing sends**: a commission to send a fixed text on a fixed day *if* a stated condition holds.
2. **Reach and its price.** A message arrives earlier than it was sent, by up to 311 days. Sends of more than about 200 days kill the Draxt sender, and its dying leaves a **death mark** at the end of the plate's figure.
3. **Unmaking.** History is single. When a message arrives, every day between its arrival and its sending is unmade for everyone, and nothing survives of those days except the plate. Humans mourn unmade days. The Draxt hold that unmade days were never lived, so unmaking them wrongs no one. This is a standing disagreement between the two peoples, not a contradiction in Draxt law.
4. **The Hush.** A real arrival drains the static of the whole elevator from top to bottom in about forty seconds, as a visible wave of sparks running down every rail and conduit (seen from orbit as a ripple of light down the cable), and is followed by nine minutes without a single spark. It leaves a faint downward scorch streak on every grounding conduit and a soot ring on the collar of the **Old Column** at the Root. A grounding of the city, whether ordered or caused by a fault, gives the nine minutes of silence but no wave, no streaks and no ring.
5. **The figure.** A plate's discharge figure carries an internal charge gradient running from the edge where the charge entered. A Draxt reads that gradient at a glance. Humans see only the glyphs, and once a plate is removed from the Well, nothing on it tells a human which edge was up.
6. **Seals.** Every genuine message carries the Sartapao's ledger seal of its sending day, which is a fingerprint of the entire ledger on that day. It can be checked only when that day arrives, and only if nothing has changed. A message that was acted on changes the ledger, so its seal can never be confirmed. A forger therefore needs only the seal's *format*, which every Duster knows; any well-formed seal on an acted-on plate looks exactly like a genuine one.
7. **Writes** (synthetic biology in tea) change bodies, never memories. Adult bone cannot be rewritten, so a rewritten face keeps its skull and voice. Every write has a taste a master palate can read before it is drunk.
8. **The Sartapao** sees only named persons. Under the **Charter of Silence** it answers a named person's question only about ledger lines in which that person is named (as payer, payee, giver or subject of an order). Lines naming no living person belong to the floor's keeper; the dead's lines pass to their heirs; lines under the Ikhshid's seal are read only under that seal. **Every question is itself a ledger line, and the Master of Dust may audit all questions.** It volunteers nothing, and its ledger can never be edited.
9. **The Old Column's collar** was scrubbed during a restoration in YT 60, on the Master of Dust's order. Rings exist only for arrivals since then.

## The true chronology

### Before the order (YT −6 to YT 41)

- **YT −6 to YT 0.** The Draxt spin the thread for the merchant city of Marqand on Sogh. Under the old agreement the Draxt may write into the thread; humans hold the Root and relay every Draxt plate faithfully and in full; and the Counterweight is Draxt territory. What the Draxt want from it is to hear their own future: they can send but cannot receive, because no Draxt can live at the bottom of a gravity well.
- **YT 0 to YT 40.** Marqand is ruled by the Council of Caravans. The Sartapao is the elevator's freight-master. Seven genuine plates arrive in these years.
- **YT 19.** Nanai-farn is born unnamed at the Root. **YT 28.** A charity licence of the tea tasters' guild (the Cup) names her, at nine, and she is trained as a Duster. **YT 25.** Makhdhat is born at the Root.
- **YT 37 to YT 41. The Toll War.** The Ordu fleets, who carry most of the region's cargo, demand a thread of their own. Marqand blockades the Counterweight's docks and the Ordu blockade Sogh's freight. The second elevator city of the region, **Chash**, finances Marqand's war. The Root starves, and Nanai-farn's younger brother dies of hunger.
- **YT 41, Unburned (night of Day 28).** Nanai-farn, 22 and on night watch at the Well, presses a charged stamp onto a blank plate from below. The plate reads: *FROM THE SCALE, DAY 256: YABGHU SARBARS'S WRITE BURNED USRUSHAN'S CHILDREN. 300,000 DEAD. WEIGH HIM.* It claims to have been sent 228 days later, which is long enough to have killed its sender, yet it carries no death mark: at 22 she did not know long sends leave one. Its seal is well formed and unconfirmable. To fake the Hush she has her patron, the Council member **Tishdhat**, issue a city-wide grounding under his Council seal at 02:09. (Before the Sartapao was rechartered, its logs did not record who carried an order out, and it had no wave sensors.) Makhdhat, 16, working the Root's grounding switches, sees the young Duster's face at the switch-house window and notices that no sparks ran down the column first.
- **YT 41, Days 28 to 33. The First Weighing.** Tishdhat convenes a new tribunal, **the Scale**. Its seats are Marqand (three Weighers), the Draxt (one, a plate wired to the Counterweight), Usrushan (one, as the "saved" world) and Chash (one, as Marqand's war ally). At Nanai-farn's suggestion the Draxt are barred from approaching the founding plate, "lest Draxt fields erase it". The Ordu, threatened with Draxt closure of the Counterweight docks, submit. **Verdict (Day 33):** Yabghu Sarbars is convicted of a prevented war crime and exiled. The Ordu are to pay restitution in perpetuity: a tithe on cargo, of which 80% goes to Marqand and 20% to Chash for its war costs. Each Yabghu's heir must send their eldest child to Marqand at nine as a hostage, to stay until the next such child arrives. The Ordu may never own a thread.
- **YT 41, after the verdict.** Tishdhat is proclaimed **Ikhshid** (a revived title). The Sartapao is rechartered to pay **farn**, an equal daily share for every named person, from tolls and the tribute, and it books the tribute as "restitution pending confirmation". The Charter of Silence is written in at Nanai-farn's suggestion. The Root stops starving. Chash copies the farn system for its own elevator, paid from its tribute share. Nanai-farn commissions from the Cup's bronze-caster two weights cast to the Cup's 100-drachm master: one solid, which she keeps, and one with a small sealed cavity in which she places her stamp. The thick shell leaves it 96 drachms. That one goes to the Scale as its ceremonial weight. No one ever weighs it against anything, because it *is* the standard; it sits on its cushion and is set in an empty pan at each verdict. The payment line reads: *two weights of one hundred drachms; sealing fee.*

### The order (YT 41 to YT 103)

- **YT 49.** Sarbars dies in exile; his ossuary, sent home, is empty (texture). **YT 50.** Nanai-farn becomes **Master of Dust**. **YT 52.** She licenses her first clone (her "second"), Farnxund, and raises her as heir. **YT 60.** She orders the Old Column's collar scrubbed during a restoration, which erases the record of the YT 41 night. **YT 66.** A boy is born unlicensed at the Root; his mother calls him Mahvandak. At 17 (YT 83) he swears the chakar oath and gives the name up.
- **YT 69.** Tishdhat I dies. His grandson **Tishdhat II** becomes Ikhshid and is told the founding secret at accession ("the Ikhshid's Secret"); every Ikhshid is told it.
- **YT 70.** Farnxund, 18, has her face rewritten (soft tissue, colouring and hair) and takes a new licence-name, so that the Master of Dust's kin can one day sit on the Scale, which the law forbids. She is *not* told the founding secret. In the same year Nanai-farn licenses a second clone, **Miwnay**, and places her with a Cup family in the Spark.
- **YT 75.** Takhsich is born at the Root. **YT 77.** Farnxund, 25, a clerk of the Scale who is writing a study of the First Weighing, goes to the Root to ask about Unburned night and finds Makhdhat. She is the only person who has ever asked.
- **YT 78, Day 140. The suppressed future.** A genuine plate arrives, with a real Hush and a ring on the column. It was sent 290 days later, in a future thereby unmade, by the pattern of the Draxt envoy humans call **Wakhsh**, and its sender died in the sending, so it carries a death mark. It reads: *PATTERN WAKHSH TO ITSELF. DUST FALSE SINCE UNBURNED. FARNXUND WEIGHED IT. SPIN THE ORDU THREAD.* In those 290 unmade days, Farnxund had found the hollow weight and exposed the forgery. Nanai-farn reads it. That week she tells Farnxund the founding secret and makes her forge, by her own hand, the electret copy that is relayed to the Draxt: *PATTERN WAKHSH TO ITSELF. THE ORDU THREAD BURNS THE COUNTERWEIGHT. SPIN NOTHING FOR THEM.* Farnxund is now complicit, and her study is never finished. Dust law forbids destroying a genuine plate, so Nanai-farn seals the real one in the lid of the ossuary she has had made early, as Marqandi elders do. Aytash is born this year to **Tolun**, heir of the Yabghu who succeeded Sarbars.
- **YT 79.** The Ordu petition the Draxt for a thread, and the Draxt refuse, citing their own future. Some Draxt notice that the relayed copy carries no death mark, though a 290-day send must have killed its sender. The Dust replies that copies do not carry marks. From this the Draxt court at the Grove divides into two factions. The **Keepers** voted for the refusal and keep the Marqand relay. The **Spinners** suspect the Dust and want the relay moved and a thread spun for the Ordu.
- **YT 86.** Miwnay, 16, refuses Nanai-farn's offer of a place in the Dust and joins the Cup. **YT 87.** Aytash, nine, arrives in Marqand as hostage (Tolun is then the heir); Dewasht, a retired Marqandi soldier, becomes her keeper. **YT 88.** Farnxund becomes a Weigher, and the nameless chakar joins her household as her seventh chakar. Shayn, 26, takes the Vat Bench. **YT 92.** Tolun becomes Yabghu. **YT 99.** Farnxund becomes Chief Weigher.
- **YT 88 to YT 104. The vat bubble.** Each licensed clone's farn is paid to its licensor until it turns 16; from 16 to 36 the clone pays a labour tithe of 30% of its wages into the farn pool. Licences are sold as bonds on that future tithe. Shayn issues licences far over quota to allied Height houses: about 1.5 million clones in 16 years, raised in vat-crèches at the Root. Named persons grow from 3.5 million to 5.0 million while the pool stays flat (Ordu freight is slumping), so farn per head falls by 30%. The first shadow cohort turns 16 in YT 104, and there is no work for them; the war party's answer is to take the Ordu fleets and crew them with clones. Farnxund, auditing the Bench, keeps the shadow quota as leverage.
- **YT 101.** Miwnay becomes taster to the Scale. The Spinners have a Height-born pilot, **Kanak**, recruit Takhsich while posing as an Ordu agent; Takhsich passes dust-lifts of the plates he carries up the elevator on the courier rota.
- **YT 103.** The Ordu petition again for a thread. Farn riots begin in the Spark.

### The present crisis (Day −40 to Day 0)

- **Day −40.** Nanai-farn (85, her longevity writes failing), **Ashtvan** (Lord of the Height, the Ikhshid's nephew) and Farnxund agree on a second pre-emptive case. Nanai-farn wants to double the tribute and save the order. Ashtvan wants war, the Ordu fleets for the coming-of-age clones, and the throne. Farnxund wants both the Scale and the Dust. Shayn knows nothing of the forgery but sees the war as the bubble's rescue. The dying Ikhshid is not told.
- **Day −13, 02:09.** Covering her tracks this time, Nanai-farn shorts the Well column's breaker so that the Sartapao books an *automatic* fault grounding, and presses a second stamped plate at 02:10: *FROM THE SCALE, DAY 95: AYTASH, HOSTAGE, POURED THE WASTING INTO THE ROOT'S TEA. 40,000 DEAD. WEIGH HER.* The automatic line names only the officer of the watch: **Pukar**. He saw no sparks run down the column.
- **Day −12.** Pukar, named in the line, asks the Sartapao about it. Answer: *fault grounding 02:09:40, nine minutes; plate logged 02:10:05; preceding wave: none recorded.* He tells his friend Takhsich. **Day −11.** Nanai-farn's audit shows her Pukar's question. **Day −10.** Takhsich tells Kanak. **Day −8.** Kanak, as an "Ordu agent", passes it to the Ordu legate. **Day −5.** Pukar is listed as a defence witness.
- **Day −4.** Farnxund tells Nanai-farn. Nanai-farn says: "Make him stammer." Farnxund decides privately that he will die.
- **Day −3.** The Long Chakar (Farnxund's first) orders an insulated witness sash from the Root embroiderer **Rokhshan**, paying in Dust ration tea bricks from Nanai-farn's store. Rokhshan's stitched ledger records: *witness sash, insulated, Weigher's house, collected by the seventh.* Farnxund sews in a Dust plate blank charged as a lethal capacitor; it bears the Dust's inventory glyph.
- **Day −1.** Farnxund tells her seventh chakar the sash will "make him stammer" and orders the swap for dawn.

## The Review (Day 38): procedure

- **Presiding:** the Ikhshid in Tribunal (Shayn), who does not vote. Farnxund is recused because she is named in Usrushan's petition for "perverting the Scale".
- **Voting seats:** Weigher A, Weigher B, the Draxt, Usrushan and Chash. Overturning a verdict of the Scale needs **two-thirds of the seats voting**; abstentions do not count.
- **Parties:** the Ordu (Aytash, as the wronged party), with the right to question witnesses.
- **Two matters**, under the charter's clause on "any perversion of this Scale": (1) the founding verdict; (2) Farnxund, for the murders of Pukar, Ashtvan and Nanai-farn and for perverting the Scale. The second needs a simple majority.

## Canonical ending: "Weigh, and go"

1. **The murders.** The named guard testifies that Farnxund ordered the sash, and Miwnay presents her retained sample. Aytash invokes the rule that every witness wears the Scale's grounding sash. Farnxund, out of her own heavy grounding for the first time in the game, sparks visibly at her denials, and Aytash reads them. Takhsich testifies to the canary trap and produces the forged YT 78 "original" Nanai-farn made in front of him.
2. **The founding.** At the Draxt's demand Shayn reads the YT 41 grounding line under her seal in open court: *Council seal of Tishdhat.* The gallery takes it for the founder's safety order. Wakhsh, carried in its frame to the founding plate, reads it: written from below, and without the death mark a 228-day send must carry. Farnxund, from the dock, calls it alien word against human record, with alien interests (the Kish thread) behind it. The count stands at Draxt and Usrushan for overturning, Weigher B against, Weigher A undecided and Chash abstaining: two of three voting, short of two-thirds.
3. **The weight.** Miwnay walks to the balance with the solid twin from the ossuary (the player may stop and walk back). She sets the Scale's weight against its twin. The Scale's pan rises. On the Draxt seat's instruction the bailiff strikes the sacred weight; it splits along its seam and holds a stamp. Dusted onto a blank plate, the stamp prints the founding message in mirror image. Weigher A votes to overturn: three of four, and the First Weighing falls. If Miwnay promised Shayn on Day 33, this is the moment she breaks her word.
4. **The reclassification.** Shown the YT 78 text, Farnxund hears that in a future that was unmade, at 26, she was the one who weighed it. Her only answer is to ask the bailiff for the broken halves. He refuses.
5. **Verdicts.** Farnxund is convicted and **unnamed for life**. The doors no longer open for her, and her former seventh chakar opens one.
6. **Terms.** Aytash, as Yabghu-elect, states the Ordu terms, negotiated against the Kish thread: restitution of 10% of Marqand's farn pool a year for 40 years (about half of what was taken), the tribute's end, and, by Ordu custom, a hostage "of the blood of the wrong" for four years as surety. Shayn names her own son, aged twelve. **Choice:** Miwnay, the forger's pattern and heir, offers herself in his place (canonical), or lets the boy go.
7. **The Sartapao, untouched by anyone.** At midnight it reverses the restitution entry according to the Review's terms. At dawn on Day 39 every named sash in Marqand gets a weaker spark: farn has fallen 45%.
8. **The crash (Day 39, played).** The Bench board collapses. With farn down 45%, the farn houses draw for their child clones no longer covers the crèches' costs, and they try to surrender their licences. Abandoning a child is illegal, so the Vat Bench must take 1.5 million crèche children as wards. Shayn's **Wards Decree** voids the shadow licences: those children will owe no labour tithe; their own farn will go to their crèches; and the houses lose their bonds. At Chash, farn falls by 30% and its streets riot (seen on a Height feed).
9. **Departure (Day 40).** Miwnay leaves for the Ordu fleet as hostage, with the guard, who is now free to go where he likes. The conduit boy has his licence in either case: if the guard was named by Shayn's licence, the Cup's charity licence went to the boy.
10. **Coda (months later, silent).** At an Ordu hangar, Aytash holds a hostage's naming in the Ordu manner: guests of the camp are named by their hosts. The guard tastes the cup. The player chooses her Ordu name (**Tuz**, "salt"; **Ülüsh**, "a share"; or she keeps **Miwnay**). Then a first strand of thread at Kish; Takhsich, acting Master of Dust, at the Well beside a Draxt audit plate; Makhdhat at a real Hush, holding up her hand for the sparks coming down first; Farnxund, nameless, at a Root tea stall, tasting a seventh cup.

**Final emotional image:** two cups on a felt tray in a steel hangar, one tasted by each of them, and a bronze weight that is the right weight.

## Branches (all respected, each with its own epilogue)

- **The Apprentice (Day 3, accept Nanai-farn's offer).** Miwnay's name returns at once and she works at the Well under watch. Without her, the Second Weighing convicts Aytash on Day 33, she is exiled rather than executed (Nanai-farn's correction), and the Ordu go to war anyway. The game ends on Day 34 with Miwnay dusting the war's first plates. A short ending.
- **Day 33: refuse Shayn.** The guard can be named only through the Cup's charity licence, the one promised to the conduit boy. *Name the guard:* he testifies as above, and the boy waits another year (seen in the epilogue). *Keep the promise to the boy:* the guard stays unnamed and cannot testify; Farnxund is convicted of Nanai-farn's murder only, on the retained sample and Aytash's questioning. Farnxund's sentence is the same, but she is acquitted of the Pukar and Ashtvan counts, and the guard is never named in law. In his epilogue he is called by whatever name she used for him.
- **Day 38: weigh, but let the boy go.** As canonical, but Shayn's son goes to the Ordu, and Miwnay stays in Marqand as the most recognised face in a poorer city: her original's. Her last scene is at a Spark stall, strangers staring.
- **Day 38: do not weigh (the Keeper).** The vote stalls at two of three and the founding verdict stands. Farnxund is still convicted of the murders. The Spinners prevail at the Grove: the Draxt move all relaying to Kish and withdraw from the Scale. The Ordu, under Aytash, stop paying a tribute no Draxt backs, and the fleets settle into armed standoff. Farn holds for now; the Bench board drifts down. Shayn gives Miwnay the House of Dust. Her last scene is alone at the Well, wearing her original's sash, with the hollow weight back under the glass.
