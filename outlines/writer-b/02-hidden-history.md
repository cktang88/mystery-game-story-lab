# The Light Weight: sealed hidden history

This is the solution document. Everything the player can find is a view onto something written here. Canon tier: **locked** unless marked otherwise. One keeper (the writer) holds it; any change needs a re-audit of the clue matrix in `05-outline.md`.

## Calendar and units

- **YT** = Years of the Thread, counted from the thread's completion (YT 0). The present is **YT 104**.
- The Marakandi year begins with the month **Nawsard**. The game's **Day 1** is 1 Nawsard YT 104, the day the Scale opens its new-year session. Days before it are written Day −n.
- **Unburned Day** is 28 Nawsard (Day 28 in the present year), the anniversary of the founding message's "arrival" in YT 41. The First Weighing's verdict fell on 33 Nawsard YT 41; the Second Weighing's verdict was deliberately scheduled for Day 33 to echo it.
- Weight: the **drachm**. The Scale's ceremonial weight is nominally 100 drachms; it actually weighs 71.
- Travel on the elevator by fast climber: Root to Spark, 2 hours; Spark to Height, about 36 hours; Height to Counterweight, about 64 hours (about 12 hours by ship). A Draxt ship from the Counterweight to the Spark's shuttle ring takes about 20 hours.

## The rules the history obeys (summary; full rules in `03-world-and-factions.md`)

1. Only a Draxt can write a message into the thread, and only at the Counterweight. Only the plates at the Root's Well can receive it.
2. A message arrives earlier than it was sent, by up to 311 days. Sends beyond about 200 days kill the Draxt sender, and its death leaves a terminal flourish in the plate's figure.
3. History is single. When a message arrives, every day between its arrival and its sending is unmade for everyone. Nothing of those days survives except the plate.
4. A real arrival drains static down the whole elevator in a visible wave from top to bottom (the Descent), then nine minutes without sparks (the Hush). It scorches a faint downward streak along every grounding conduit and a soot ring on the collar of the Old Column at the Root. A city-wide grounding ordered through the Sartapao produces the nine minutes of silence but no wave, no streaks and no ring.
5. A plate's discharge figure branches downward from its top edge when the charge came down the thread. A charge pressed onto a plate from below branches upward.
6. Every genuine message carries the Sartapao's ledger seal of its sending day, which can only be confirmed if that day arrives unchanged. A message that was acted on can never be confirmed.
7. Writes change bodies, never memories. Adult bone cannot be rewritten. The Sartapao sees only named persons and answers only what a named person asks.

## The true chronology

### Before the order (YT 0 to YT 41)

- **YT −6 to YT 0.** The Draxt spin the thread for the merchant city of Marakand on Sughd. The **Compact of the Thread** (YT 0): the Draxt may write into the thread; humans hold the Root and relay every Draxt plate to the Counterweight faithfully and in full; the Counterweight is Draxt territory. The Draxt want one thing from this: to hear their own future, which they can send but cannot receive, because no Draxt can live at the bottom of a gravity well.
- **YT 0 to YT 37.** Marakand is ruled by the Council of Caravans, the great merchant houses of the Height. The Sartapao is the elevator's freight-master. Messages from the future arrive rarely: seven genuine plates in these 41 years. (Six more arrive between YT 41 and YT 103, the YT 78 plate among them, and one on Day 4 of the game: fourteen genuine arrivals in all. The Register of Plates lists sixteen, because it includes the two forgeries.)
- **YT 19.** Nanai-farn is born unnamed at the Root (her mother could not get a licence); she is named at nine by a charity licence and trained as a Duster.
- **YT 25.** Makhdhat is born at the Root.
- **YT 37 to YT 41. The Toll War.** The Ordu fleets, who carry most of the region's cargo, demand a thread of their own; Marakand blockades the Counterweight's docks; the Ordu blockade Sughd's freight. The Root starves. Several thousand die of hunger, among them Nanai-farn's younger brother.
- **YT 41, 28 Nawsard (Unburned).** Nanai-farn, 22, on night watch at the Well, presses a charged stamp onto a blank receiving plate from below. The plate reads: *FROM THE SCALE, DAY 228: YABGHU ISHBARA'S WRITE BURNED USTRUSHANA'S CHILDREN. 300,000 DEAD. WEIGH HIM.* (The "Scale" named in it did not yet exist; she named the court that she meant to have founded.) At 02:10 she requests, through the Sartapao, a nine-minute grounding of all floors "for plate safety", which imitates a Hush. Makhdhat, 16, working the Root's grounding switches that night, sees the young Duster's face at the switch-house window and notices that no sparks run down the column first. Nanai-farn takes the plate to her patron, the Council member **Tarkhun**.
- **YT 41, 28 to 33 Nawsard. The First Weighing.** Tarkhun convenes a new tribunal, **the Scale**, with seats for Marakand (three), the Draxt (one) and Ustrushana (one). The Draxt seat is a plate wired to the Counterweight, and at Nanai-farn's suggestion the Draxt are barred from approaching the founding plate in person "because Draxt fields would erase it". The Ordu, threatened with Draxt closure of the Counterweight docks, submit. **Verdict (33 Nawsard):** Yabghu Ishbara is convicted of a prevented war crime and exiled; the Ordu will pay restitution in perpetuity (a tithe on cargo, "the Unburned Tribute"), send each Yabghu's heir to live in Marakand as a hostage, and never own a thread.
- **YT 41, after the verdict.** Tarkhun is proclaimed **Ikhshid**, a revived title. The Sartapao is rechartered: it now pays **farn**, an equal daily share, to every named person, from tolls and the tribute, and it books the tribute as "restitution pending confirmation". The **Charter of Silence** is written into it at Nanai-farn's suggestion: it answers named persons' questions about the ledger and volunteers nothing. The Root stops starving. The Scale's **ceremonial weight** is cast in bronze to match the tea tasters' guild master weight of 100 drachms; Nanai-farn has the smith cast it hollow and seals her stamp inside it.

### The order (YT 41 to YT 103)

- **YT 49.** Ishbara dies in exile. His ossuary, carried home by the Ordu, is empty; his bones were never released by the world that held him (texture).
- **YT 50.** Nanai-farn becomes **Master of Dust**.
- **YT 52.** Nanai-farn licenses her first clone ("second"), decanted as an infant and raised in her household: **Farnxund**.
- **YT 66.** A boy is born unlicensed at the Root; his mother calls him Tishvandak. At 17 (YT 83) he swears the chakar oath and gives up the name.
- **YT 69.** Tarkhun I dies; his grandson **Tarkhun II** becomes Ikhshid (age 39). Each Ikhshid is told the founding secret at accession by the Master of Dust ("the Ikhshid's Secret").
- **YT 70.** Farnxund, 18, has her face rewritten (soft tissue, colour, hair; bone cannot be changed) and takes a new licence-name, so that the Master of Dust's kin can later sit on the Scale, which the law forbids. The same year Nanai-farn licenses her second clone, **Miwnay**, and places her with a family of the tea tasters' guild (the Cup) in the Spark. Miwnay grows up knowing she is "dhat of Nanai-farn"; she never knows there was a first.
- **YT 75.** Takhsich is born at the Root; named by licence; a Dust scholarship takes him in at fourteen.
- **YT 78.** Aytash is born to Tardu, son of the Yabghu who succeeded the exiled Ishbara. (Tardu becomes Yabghu in YT 92.)
- **YT 78, day 140. The suppressed future.** A genuine plate arrives (real Descent, a ring on the Old Column). It was sent 290 days later, in a future that is thereby unmade, by the Draxt envoy's own pattern (the envoy humans call **Wakhsh**), and the sender died sending it. Its figure carries the death flourish. It reads: *PATTERN WAKHSH TO ITSELF. DUST FALSE SINCE UNBURNED. SPIN THE ORDU THREAD.* Someone in those 290 unmade days exposed the founding forgery; who it was is unknowable (texture, forever). Nanai-farn, now 59 and Master of Dust, relays to the Counterweight an electret copy she has rewritten: *PATTERN WAKHSH TO ITSELF. THE ORDU THREAD BURNS THE COUNTERWEIGHT. SPIN NOTHING FOR THEM.* Dust law forbids destroying a genuine plate, and she believes in the law if not in the truth, so she seals the real plate inside the lid of her own ossuary, which she has had made early, as Marakandi elders do.
- **YT 79.** The Ordu petition the Draxt for a thread; the Draxt refuse, citing their own future. The Draxt notice that the relayed copy carries no death flourish, though a 290-day send must have killed its sender. The Dust explains that copies do not carry flourishes. The Draxt are not satisfied, but they cannot reach the Root.
- **YT 86.** Miwnay, 16, refuses Nanai-farn's offer of a place in the Dust and is apprenticed to the Cup. Nanai-farn lets her go.
- **YT 87.** Aytash, nine, arrives in Marakand as the hostage heir; Dewasht, a retired Marakandi soldier, becomes her keeper.
- **YT 88.** Farnxund becomes a Weigher; the nameless chakar is assigned to her household as her seventh chakar. **YT 99.** Farnxund becomes Chief Weigher.
- **YT 94 to YT 104. The vat-farn bubble.** Shayn, the Ikhshid's daughter, takes the Vat Bench in YT 96 and issues about 40,000 licences over quota to allied Height houses, who collect their clones' farn. Farn per head falls 30% in ten years. Farnxund, auditing the Bench for the Scale, learns of the "shadow quota" and keeps it as leverage.
- **YT 101.** Miwnay becomes taster to the Scale. The Draxt, still suspicious, have a human pilot of the Height docks, **Varzakk**, recruit Takhsich while posing as an Ordu agent. Takhsich carries Dust relay copies up the elevator on the courier rota and passes the Draxt dust-lifts of the originals; he believes he is helping the Ordu avoid a war.
- **YT 103.** The Ordu petition again for a thread. Farn riots begin in the Spark.

### The present crisis (Day −40 to Day 0)

- **Day −40.** Nanai-farn (85, her longevity writes failing), Varkhuman (Lord of the Height, the Ikhshid's nephew) and Farnxund agree to a second pre-emptive case. Their aims differ. Nanai-farn wants to double the tribute and save farn, and so the order, before she dies. Varkhuman wants a war that hands Marakand the Ordu fleets and him the throne. Farnxund wants to end up holding both the Scale and the Dust. The dying Ikhshid is not told.
- **Day −13, 02:10.** Nanai-farn presses a second stamped plate at the Well and requests a nine-minute grounding "for plate safety". The plate reads: *FROM THE SCALE, DAY 95: AYTASH, HOSTAGE, RELEASED THE WASTING IN THE ROOT'S TEA. 40,000 DEAD. WEIGH HER.* The junior Duster **Pukar**, on watch at the grounding column, sees no sparks run down it before the silence.
- **Day −12.** Pukar tells his friend Takhsich. **Day −10.** Takhsich tells Varzakk. **Day −8.** Varzakk passes it to the Ordu legate **Qutlugh**, Aytash's advocate. **Day −5.** Pukar is listed as a defence witness.
- **Day −4.** Farnxund reads the witness list and tells Nanai-farn, who says: "Make him stammer." Farnxund decides privately that he will die.
- **Day −3.** Farnxund's first chakar (called the Long Chakar) orders an insulated witness sash from the Root embroiderer **Rokhshan**, paying in Dust ration tea bricks drawn from Nanai-farn's store, which Farnxund uses freely as her protégé. Rokhshan's stitch ledger records: *witness sash, insulated, Weigher's house, collected by the seventh.* Farnxund sews into it a Dust plate blank charged as a lethal capacitor; the blank carries the Dust's inventory glyph.
- **Day −1.** Farnxund tells her seventh chakar that the sash will give the witness a shock "to make him stammer", and orders him to swap it for Pukar's in the robing room at dawn.

## The canonical ending and its alternatives

### Canonical: "Weigh, and drink" (Day 38 to the coda)

1. The named guard testifies that Farnxund ordered the sash. Miwnay presents the thinning-write evidence for Varkhuman and Nanai-farn. Farnxund attacks the guard as a confessed killer and argues that the YT 78 plate is a Draxt forgery meant to break Marakand's monopoly; only Draxt can read branch-script, so only Draxt can vouch for it. Much of the gallery agrees.
2. Wakhsh is carried, in visible pain at nine-tenths gravity, to the founding plate under its glass for the first time since YT 41 and reads its figure: written from below. The gallery dismisses that too.
3. Miwnay sets the ceremonial weight on the Scale's balance against the Cup's 100-drachm master weight. It rises. Broken open, it holds the stamp. Dusted, the stamp prints the founding message in mirror image. Breaking her promise to Shayn is the cost the player chooses here.
4. Wakhsh offers the re-spinning cup. Farnxund refuses it. Miwnay tastes it, the last fine tasting of her life, and drinks.
5. **Verdicts.** The First Weighing is overturned. Farnxund is convicted of three murders (Pukar, Varkhuman, Nanai-farn) and of perverting the Scale, and sentenced to unnaming for life, the order's harshest sentence for its own people. Rokhshan's killer, the Long Chakar, is dead. The doors of the hall no longer open for Farnxund; her former seventh chakar opens one for her.
6. **What the Sartapao does, untouched by anyone.** At midnight it reverses the restitution entry: the tribute stops and the 63 years of tribute are repaid from tolls over 31 years. Farn falls by nearly half. At dawn on Day 39 every named person in Marakand feels a weaker spark.
7. **The market.** Licence prices on the Height's Bench board collapse. With clones' farn now worth less than their keep, Height houses release the vat debts of about 40,000 clones, whose farn becomes their own.
8. **The Draxt.** The pattern is closed. The Draxt keep relaying through Marakand under audit, and agree to spin a second thread for the Ordu at Kesh, which will end Marakand's monopoly in about twenty years. The Ordu fleets stand down. Aytash goes home as heir.
9. **The cast.** Shayn keeps the throne of a smaller city and signs the restitution charter. Takhsich becomes acting Master of Dust under joint audit. The guard's name is the one the player chose on Day 37.
10. **Epilogue (Day 39 to Day 46).** The rewrite takes a week of fever. It changes Miwnay's skin, its charge signature and her taste receptors; it cannot change her bones or her voice. She can still taste warmth, sweetness and salt, but not the fine bitter notes. The rewrite has erased her name-mark, so the Sartapao cannot see her. On Day 46 she is named again; the guard, who has watched her taste for forty days, tastes the cup and nods. The player chooses her name. The next dawn the farn spark reaches her sash: weaker than it used to be, but hers.

**Final emotional image:** two cups on a tray, one tasted by each of them, and a weight that is the right weight.

### Alternative 1: "Weigh, and refuse the cup"

The founding lie is exposed and Farnxund is convicted as above. No instance of the pattern consents, so the Draxt declare Marakand a false party: they stop sending through the thread, spin the Ordu thread at once, and move their relay to Kesh. Marakand keeps its elevator and its tolls but loses the Scale's foreign seats and its place at the centre. Farn still falls by nearly half. Miwnay keeps her face and her palate and is the most recognised face in a poorer city: her original's. The last image is her in the Spark, tasting tea for a stall, with strangers stopping to stare.

### Alternative 2: "Do not weigh" (the keeper)

Miwnay keeps her promise to Shayn. Farnxund is still convicted of the murders on the guard's testimony. The founding verdict stands in Marakandi law; the Draxt, disbelieved, withdraw as in Alternative 1; the Ordu refuse to pay a tribute the Draxt no longer back, and the fleets settle into an armed standoff. Farn holds. Shayn rewards Miwnay with the House of Dust. The last image is Miwnay alone at the Well, dusting a plate, wearing her original's sash, with the hollow weight back under the hall's glass.

All three outcomes are respected: none is labelled a failure, and each has its legible consequences in the epilogue.
