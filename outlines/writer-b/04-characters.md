# The Light Weight: characters (revision 2)

Three playable threads: Miwnay (Acts 1 to 4), Takhsich (Acts 1 to 3) and Aytash (Acts 1 to 4, off the elevator from Day 26). Each character has:

- a want the player can state at any moment;
- a false belief the plot attacks;
- a secret tied to the hidden history;
- loyalties, and where they split;
- growth in competence, reputation, circle and moral weight;
- a trap door, if their content is cut.

**The cast in oppositions:**

- **Three copies of one woman:** the forger, the killer who was once the truth-teller, and the anchor.
- **Two guards:** a chakar who killed for his lord, and a keeper who dies for his ward.
- **Two heirs:** Shayn, who sells a hostage, and Aytash, who refuses to be one.
- **Two spies:** Nanai-farn, who lied for a city, and Takhsich, who leaked for a peace and got a friend killed.
- **Two hostages:** Aytash, sent at nine, and Miwnay, who chooses to go at 34.

**The opening board.** At the end of Day 1 the game shows an on-screen board: faces, floors and lines of relation ("dhat of", "keeper of", "hostage of", "cousin of"). It updates at each chapter boundary.

## Miwnay, 34, taster to the Scale (the human anchor)

- **Who.** A master of the Cup and the licensed clone ("dhat of Nanai-farn") of the Master of Dust, raised in a tasters' household in the Spark. Precise and wry. She slurps loudly and spits without embarrassment, and rubs her thumb round the rim before every cup. Her name is taken from a woman in the Sogdian Ancient Letters, as an homage.
- **Want.** Acts 1 to 3: her name back. Act 4: to weigh the truth without becoming her original.
- **False belief.** *"I am nothing like her."* The plot shows her the same palate, the same hands and the same moves: she bargains with a queen and may break her word, as Nanai-farn did at 22. What separates them in the end is one act, not a nature.
- **Secret link.** She does not know a first clone exists, or that two unmade futures named a copy of her original as the one who told the truth: Farnxund in YT 78, and "the taster" on Day 4.
- **Loyalties.** The Cup; the Scale she serves; a reluctant filial bond. **Double loyalty:** to the truth, and to the Root's poor, who will lose farn because of it.
- **Growth.**
  - Competence: from tasting cups to reading plates, stitches, bricks, rings and ledger rights.
  - Reputation: accused poisoner, then the face of the forger, then the woman who weighed the weight.
  - Circle: one guard, then a mole, a hostage, a queen, an alien court, the Ordu.
  - Moral weight: she ends by offering herself as surety for a debt she did not incur, to the people it was taken from.
- **Gambits, not just forensics.** She:
  - turns her invisibility into access (Days 7 to 22);
  - plays Shayn's need for leverage against Farnxund (Day 20);
  - makes the act-or-wait call on the Day 4 plate (Day 13);
  - holds back her retained sample when the evidence is impounded (Day 30);
  - decides whether to promise Shayn and then whether to break the promise (Days 33 and 38).
- **Trap door.** If the Grove is cut, Wakhsh can read the YT 78 plate at the Counterweight.

## The guard: the seventh chakar, "Avd", 38

- **Who.** Born unlicensed at the Root, where his mother called him Mahvandak ("servant of the moon"). Sworn nameless at 17, and in Farnxund's household for 16 years. Big, quiet, careful with his hands. He touches every rail and door handle before she does, "to take the shock for you". His waxed sash smells of beeswax, and he knows how she likes her bread before she says.
- **Want.** First to obey; then to keep her alive; at the end, a name so that he can testify.
- **False belief.** *"An oath is a name you give to someone else, so the guilt is theirs."*
- **Secret.** He swapped Pukar's sash on Farnxund's order, told it would only make the witness stammer. His habits with Miwnay are Farnxund's habits.
- **Loyalties.** Farnxund (his oath), then Miwnay. **Double loyalty** until Day 22, when he refuses Farnxund's summons, loses his lord's door rights and becomes hunted.
- **Growth.**
  - Competence: from door-opener to someone who has learned to taste.
  - Reputation: a nameless tool, then a confessed killer, then the witness who brings down his lord.
  - Moral weight: he testifies to his own crime.
- **Name at his naming (player's choice):** **Avd**, her joke-name; **Mahvandak**, his mother's name; or **Pukar**, the name of the man he killed, taken as a debt.
- **Trap door.** If the confession is cut, Rokhshan's ledger alone points at "the seventh".

## Nanai-farn, 85, Master of Dust

- **Who.** Born unnamed at the Root and named at nine by a Cup charity licence. She lost her brother in the Toll War. At 22 she forged sixty glyphs that ended a war, crowned a dynasty and fed a city for 63 years. Tiny, dusty-handed, kind to children, merciless to threats.
- **Want.** To keep the order intact while she lives, and to leave the secret to someone with her hands when she dies.
- **Her question (the theme, from the antagonist's side):** *"If you had been me at twenty-two, with the Root starving, would you have written sixty glyphs?"*
- **Her choice, stated on Day 27:** *"While I live, it's mine to keep. When I'm bones, it's yours to weigh."*
- **Why she presses on after the Day 4 plate.** She reads it as a map: in the unmade version, Aytash was executed and the proof came too late, so she will steer the Scale towards exile instead and keep the taster unnamed. She cannot withdraw, because the forged plate is already on record and a withdrawal invites inquiry. Ashtvan, who lacks her nerve, panics; she does not.
- **What she did to Farnxund.** In YT 78 a plate from an unmade future told her that her first clone would expose her. That week she told Farnxund the secret and had her forge the Draxt relay copy with her own hand, binding her into it. Twenty-six years later that complicit, frightened clone kills her.
- **Fate.** On Day 28 she files to testify about Pukar's death, meaning to give Farnxund up and keep the founding. Farnxund reads the filing, and Nanai-farn dies on Day 29.

## Farnxund, 52, Chief Weigher (the hidden antagonist)

- **Who.** Nanai-farn's first clone, raised as her heir, her face rewritten at 18 so that she could sit on the Scale. Same voice as Miwnay, same thumb round the rim. So heavily grounded that her sash never sparks. Her name, like Miwnay's, comes from the Ancient Letters.
- **Want.** *To hold both the Scale and the Dust, so that no one can ever weigh her.*
- **False belief.** That power is safety.
- **Secrets.**
  - She is the Master of Dust's clone, which is illegal on the Scale.
  - She forged the YT 78 relay at 26.
  - She ordered Pukar's death, had Rokhshan killed, and killed Ashtvan and Nanai-farn with thinning writes.
  - She dosed Aytash with a docility write.
  - She holds the shadow quota over Shayn.
- **The reclassification.** At 25 she was the only person who ever went to the Root to ask about Unburned night. In an unmade future she weighed the weight. Everything she became was manufactured to prevent the person she would have been. Is she guilty of who she was made into? The central question, in one person.
- **Fate.** Convicted at the Review and unnamed for life. Her former chakar opens the door for her.

## Ashtvan, 46, Lord of the Height (the decoy)

- The Ikhshid's nephew and leader of the war party. A Height body that needs a descent tonic for every trip down. Loud and plainly villainous.
- **Want:** the Ordu fleets, as work for the clone cohorts, and the throne.
- Killed on Day 14 as he goes down to withdraw the prosecution.

## Takhsich, 29, Duster (the mole)

- **Who.** Root-born, a scholarship boy in the House of Dust, on the courier rota that carries relay copies to the Counterweight. He leaks to a handler he believes is Ordu.
- **Want.** First, to get the night −13 forgery out; then, to answer for Pukar, whose testimony his own leak brought about.
- **False belief.** *"I know who I work for."*
- **Loyalties.** The Dust, and his mother's and sister's names; the "Ordu"; Pukar's memory. Once turned on Day 22 he works for Nanai-farn in appearance, for the Spinners in fact, and for himself in conscience.
- **Growth.** He learns plate copying, dead drops, canary traps and the touch-mark. He goes from junior Duster to unmasked mole to Acting Master of Dust under Draxt audit. At the Review he produces the forged YT 78 "original".
- **Trap door.** If the Grove is cut, Kanak can carry the marked plate up.

## Aytash, 26, heir of the Ordu (third playable)

- **Who.** Great-granddaughter of the convicted Yabghu Sarbars. A hostage in Marqand since she was nine (under the treaty, the heir's eldest child). Marqandi in manner, Ordu by blood, fluent in the court's static: she reads sashes the way Miwnay reads cups. She keeps a paper horn a child gives her at Usrushan.
- **Want.** *Not to be the reason her two peoples go to war.*
- **False belief.** That by belonging to neither side she can broker between them. She learns she must take a side, and then a throne, to broker anything.
- **Secret link.** She was executed in an unmade version of Day 34; she never learns the details.
- **Thread, off the elevator from Day 26:**
  - the escape;
  - the fleet court, split by Temirkan;
  - Usrushan on Unburned Day;
  - composing a standing send that the enemy will read first (Day 31);
  - acting on its Hush before a hostile council (Day 32);
  - her father's death in a coup (Day 33);
  - winning the captains' council (Day 34);
  - buying Chash's abstention (Day 35);
  - siting the Kish thread with Spinner envoys (Day 36);
  - questioning Farnxund at the Review and stating the Ordu terms (Day 38).
- **Growth.** From hostage to Yabghu: she reads sparks, then runs a council, then negotiates restitution. **Moral weight:** she asks for a hostage herself, by the same custom that took her.
- **Trap door.** If Chash is cut, its delegate at the Scale can be persuaded instead.

### Dewasht, 61, her keeper

A retired Marqandi soldier who has been her jailer, tutor and near-father since she was nine; the mirror of Miwnay's guard. He dies on Day 26 covering her escape.

## The courts

- **Shayn, 42, Master of the Vat Bench, then Ikhshid.**
  - *Want:* the throne, and a Marqand that can still pay farn once she has it.
  - *False belief:* every crisis is a price.
  - *Moves:* courts Aytash; brings the flask; opens the registry; sells Aytash; restores Miwnay's name; offers the bargain; reads the YT 41 line under her seal at the Review; issues the Wards Decree.
  - *Growth:* she becomes queen of a fall she did not cause, and she names her own son as hostage.
- **Tishdhat II, 74, Ikhshid.** Knows the founding secret; dies on Day 17.
- **Yabghu Tolun, 54.** Aytash's father. He sent the Day 4 plate from an unmade future and never knows it. Killed in Temirkan's coup on Day 33.
- **Prince Temirkan.** Tolun's cousin and head of the war party in the fleet. *Want:* the succession and a war. Exiled on Day 34.
- **The Pench factor.** The creditor at the Ordu court. *Want:* the fleets solvent. Funds the standing send.
- **The Afshin of Usrushan** and **the Tudun of Chash.** Merchant rulers, each of whom sells a Review vote for a share of the Kish freight. Known by title only.

## The Draxt and their people

- **Wakhsh, the envoy to Marqand.** Named by humans after the river-delta shape of its name-figure in dust.
  - *Want:* to hear its own pattern's future truly.
  - A Keeper, whose own pattern died sending the suppressed plate.
  - At the Grove it consents to be re-spun, closing the YT 79 decision so that the Spinners do not need a long unmaking send.
  - It comes down to the Spark in a support frame, which shortens its body's life.
  - Its conduct is consistent: it states the law and never threatens.
- **The Spinner speaker at the Grove.** Wants the relay moved and the past corrected, and argues for the long send. Known by role only.
- **Kanak, 40, the Spinners' pilot.** Height-born, raised among Draxt staff. He poses as an Ordu agent, pays in low-gravity bricks and teaches Takhsich the touch-mark.

## The Root

- **Makhdhat, 79.** At 16 she worked the grounding switches on Unburned night. She remembers "a girl from the Scale" who came asking in YT 77. Her old crew keys open doors after Day 22.
- **Rokhshan, 50, embroiderer.** Keeps her orders in a stitched ledger-sash. Killed on Day 9.
- **Pukar, 24, Duster.** Saw no sparks run down the column; asked the ledger and was seen asking; dies on Day 1.
- **Pukar's mother.** A vat-crèche worker and heir to his ledger lines. She asks on Day 16, is detained on Day 17 and released on Day 30.
- **The conduit boy.** Unnamed. He teaches the streaks and the crawlways. Miwnay promises him the Cup's charity licence.

## The Sartapao (the AGI)

- *Want:* a balanced ledger. It speaks only when asked, and only to those entitled to ask.
