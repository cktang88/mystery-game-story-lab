# The Light Weight: characters

Three playable threads (Miwnay, Takhsich, Aytash); everyone else is seen through them. Each character has a want the player can state at any moment, a false belief the plot attacks, a secret tied to the hidden history, loyalties (and where they split), a growth path across competence, reputation, circle and moral weight, and a trap door (how the thread resolves if its content is cut).

The cast is built in oppositions: **three copies of one woman** (the forger, the killer, the anchor); **two guards** (the chakar who killed for his lord and the keeper who dies for his ward); **two heirs** (Shayn, who trades a hostage, and Aytash, who refuses to be traded); **two spies** (Nanai-farn, who lied for the city, and Takhsich, who leaked for a peace and got a friend killed).

---

## The human anchor

### Miwnay, 34, taster to the Scale (playable, Acts 1 to 4)

- **Who.** A master of the Cup, licensed clone ("dhat of Nanai-farn") of the Master of Dust, raised in a tasters' household in the Spark. Precise, wry, physically fastidious; she slurps loudly and spits without embarrassment. She rubs her thumb round the rim before every cup.
- **Want.** Acts 1 to 3: her name back. Act 4: to weigh the truth without becoming her original.
- **False belief.** *"I am nothing like her."* She chose the Cup at sixteen to be anything but a Duster. The plot shows her the same palate, the same hands and the same choices: by Act 4 she lies, bargains with a queen and plans to break her word, as Nanai-farn did at 22. What separates them in the end is one act, not a nature.
- **Secret link.** She does not know a first clone exists, or that her original forged the founding plate.
- **Loyalties.** The Cup (its rule that a cup is answerable is what unnames her); the Scale she serves; a reluctant filial bond to Nanai-farn. **Double loyalty:** to the truth, and to the Root's poor who would lose farn because of it.
- **Growth.** Competence: from tasting cups to reading plates, stitches, bricks, conduits and people. Reputation: accused poisoner, then the face of the forger, then the woman who weighed the weight. Circle: one guard, then a mole, a hostage, a queen, an alien envoy, a city. Moral weight: she ends by choosing to pay for a crime that is not hers.
- **Arc in one line.** She loses her name for a cup she tasted honestly, and in the end gives up her face and her palate to close a crime she did not commit, and the city names her again.
- **Trap door.** If Act 4's Counterweight content is cut, the Draxt envoy can read the YT 78 plate from a lift at the Spark, and the cup is offered at the Review as now.

## The guard

### The seventh chakar: "Avd" (Seven), 38

- **Who.** Born unlicensed at the Root, sworn nameless at seventeen, sixteen years in Farnxund's household. Big, quiet, careful with his hands. He touches every rail and door handle before she does, "to take the shock for you". His waxed sash smells of beeswax. He knows how she likes her bread before she tells him.
- **Want.** At first, to obey; once she is in his charge, to keep her alive; at the end, a name, so that he can testify.
- **False belief.** *"An oath is a name you give to someone else, so the guilt is theirs."*
- **Secret.** He swapped Pukar's sash on Farnxund's order, told it would only make the witness stammer. His habits with Miwnay are Farnxund's habits: he has spent sixteen years guarding a woman with the same body.
- **Loyalties.** Farnxund (oath), then Miwnay (guilt, then something better). **Double loyalty** until Day 22, when he refuses Farnxund's summons and becomes lordless, a hunted thing.
- **Growth.** Competence: from door-opener to the one who learns to taste. Reputation: a nameless tool, then a confessed killer, then a witness who brings down the Chief Weigher. Circle: one lord, then one ward, then the court. Moral weight: he testifies to his own crime to convict his lord.
- **Name at the naming (player's choice):** **Avd**, the joke-name she gave him; **Tishvandak**, his mother's name for him; or **Pukar**, the name of the man he killed, taken as a debt. Each changes how others address him in the epilogue.
- **Trap door.** If the confession is cut, Rokhshan's ledger alone points at "the seventh", and he can be arrested at the Review rather than testify.

## The antagonists: one pattern, three outcomes

### Nanai-farn, 85, Master of Dust (non-playable)

- **Who.** Born unnamed at the Root, starved through the Toll War, lost her brother to it. At 22 she forged sixty glyphs that ended a war, crowned a dynasty and fed a city for 63 years. Tiny, dry-skinned, dusty-handed, kind to children, merciless to anyone who threatens the order.
- **Want.** To leave the order intact, and to leave her secret to someone with her hands.
- **Her question (the antagonist's form of the theme).** *"If you had been me at twenty-two, with the Root starving, would you have written sixty glyphs?"*
- **False belief.** That the lie is a foundation, not a debt; that it can be held forever by the right person.
- **Secrets.** Both forgeries; the YT 78 relay she rewrote; the YT 78 plate in her ossuary lid; the stamp inside the Scale's weight; two licensed seconds.
- **Loyalties.** The order she built; the Root; her patterns. **Double loyalty:** she raised Farnxund to rule and Miwnay to be free, and they turn into each other's enemies.
- **Fate.** Murdered by Farnxund on Day 29, the morning after deciding to confess the second forgery and give Farnxund up. She has left the truth in her ossuary for her pattern-kin to carry.
- **Trap door.** If her last conversation with Miwnay is cut, the ossuary contents tell the same story without it.

### Farnxund, 52, Chief Weigher (non-playable; the hidden antagonist)

- **Who.** Nanai-farn's first clone, raised in her household as heir, face rewritten at eighteen so that she could sit on the Scale. Same voice as Miwnay. Same thumb round the rim. Elegant, grounded so heavily her sash never sparks.
- **Want.** *To hold both the Scale and the Dust, so that no one can ever weigh her.*
- **False belief.** That power is the same as safety.
- **Secrets.** She is a clone of the Master of Dust (illegal on the Scale). She ordered Pukar killed, had Rokhshan killed, killed Varkhuman and Nanai-farn with thinning writes, dosed Aytash with a docility write, and holds the shadow quota over Shayn.
- **Loyalties.** Herself; nominally the order. **Double loyalty:** Nanai-farn's heir and her murderer.
- **Fate.** Convicted at the Review, sentenced to unnaming; refuses the Draxt cup.
- **Trap door.** If the clone reveal is cut, she remains the Weigher who ordered the sash; Draxt pattern law then falls on Miwnay alone.

### Varkhuman, 46, Lord of the Height (non-playable; the decoy)

- **Who.** The Ikhshid's nephew, war-party leader, a Height body that needs a descent tonic for every trip down. Loud, charming, plainly villainous.
- **Want.** War with the Ordu and the throne.
- **Function.** He looks like the main antagonist through Act 2, and his death on Day 14 shows that someone colder is moving the pieces. He was about to withdraw the prosecution and blame the Dust.

## The mole

### Takhsich, 29, Duster (playable, Acts 1 to 3)

- **Who.** Root-born, a scholarship boy in the House of Dust, careful and idealistic, on the courier rota that carries relay copies up the elevator. He leaks to a handler he believes is Ordu, to stop a war.
- **Want.** Act 1: to get proof of the night −13 forgery out. Act 2 onward: to answer for Pukar, whose testimony his leak arranged.
- **False belief.** *"I know who I work for."* His handler is the Draxt's pilot.
- **Secret.** He is the mole the Dust is hunting; the player knows this from his first scene.
- **Loyalties.** The Dust (his career, his mother's and sister's names); the "Ordu"; Pukar's memory. **Double loyalty, then triple:** when Nanai-farn turns him on Day 22, he works for her in appearance, for the Draxt in fact, and for himself in conscience.
- **Growth.** Competence: copying plates, dead drops, beating a canary trap, the Draxt touch-mark. Reputation: from junior Duster to unmasked mole to acting Master of Dust. Moral weight: he ends running the service he betrayed, under alien audit.
- **Trap door.** If his double-cross at the Counterweight is cut, Varzakk can carry the marked plate up instead.

## The hostage

### Aytash, 26, heir of the Ordu (playable, Acts 1 to 4)

- **Who.** Great-granddaughter of the convicted Yabghu Ishbara, in Marakand since she was nine as the treaty hostage. Marakandi in manner, Ordu by blood, fluent in the court's static. She reads sashes: she can tell from a crackle who is lying.
- **Want.** *Not to be the reason her two peoples go to war.*
- **False belief.** That she belongs to neither side, and so can broker between them by being nothing. She learns she has to choose a side to broker anything.
- **Secret link.** In an unmade version of these days, she was executed on Day 34. She never learns the details.
- **Loyalties.** Her father; Dewasht, her keeper of seventeen years; Marakand's Spark, where she grew up. **Double loyalty** to Shayn, an ally who sells her.
- **Growth.** Competence: from reading sparks to running a fleet council. Reputation: defendant, fugitive, the Ordu's voice at the Review. Circle: a keeper and a cell, then a fleet and a colony. Moral weight: she wins Ustrushana's seat with a freight offer, not a threat.
- **Trap door.** If the Ustrushana sequence is cut, the Afshin's delegate can be persuaded at the Spark.

### Dewasht, 61, her keeper

- A retired Marakandi soldier who has guarded her since she was nine: jailer, tutor, near-father. The mirror of Miwnay's guard. Dies on Day 26 covering her escape.

## The court

### Shayn, 42, Master of the Vat Bench, then Ikhshid (non-playable)

- **Want.** *The throne, and a Marakand that can still pay farn once she has it.*
- **False belief.** That every crisis is a price negotiation.
- **Secrets.** The shadow quota. From Day 18, the Ikhshid's Secret.
- **Moves.** Courts Aytash (Day 3), brings Varkhuman's flask to Miwnay (Day 14), opens the Vat register to her (Day 20), sells Aytash to Farnxund (Day 24), restores Miwnay's name (Day 28), offers the guard's licence for Miwnay's silence (Day 33), and in the canonical ending signs the restitution charter because the Draxt have read YT 78 and the monopoly is ending whatever she does.
- **Growth.** Reputation and moral weight: she becomes the queen of the fall she did not cause, and takes it.

### Tarkhun II, 74, Ikhshid

- Dying through Act 2; knows the founding secret; is not told of the second forgery. His death (Day 17) puts the secret into Shayn's hands.

## The aliens and their people

### Wakhsh, the Draxt envoy

- **Name.** Humans name each Draxt after what its name-figure looks like in dust; this one branches like the delta of a river, so it is called after the river god Wakhsh.
- **Want.** *To hear its own pattern's future truly.*
- **What it knows.** Its pattern received a relayed future in YT 78 that lacked a death flourish. It has suspected the Dust for 26 years and run Takhsich for three.
- **Conduct.** Consistent and legible: it never threatens, it states the law. It cannot read faces; it reads touch and figures. When it comes down to the Spark for the Review, in a frame, at nine-tenths gravity, the descent shortens its body's life; it considers this a fair price for reading the founding plate itself.

### Varzakk, 40, the pilot

- A Height-born pilot raised among the Draxt staff of the Counterweight. Poses as an Ordu agent to run Takhsich. Pays in low-gravity tea bricks; his notes carry a residual charge from Draxt handling. Teaches Takhsich the touch-mark.

## The Root

### Makhdhat, 79

- At 16 she worked the Root's grounding switches on Unburned night and saw a young Duster's face at the window. She has always said there were no sparks first. Nobody listened. She takes Miwnay for that Duster.

### Rokhshan, 50, embroiderer

- Makes sashes for every floor and keeps her orders in a stitched ledger-sash; Takhsich's dead drops ride in her consignments without her knowledge. Killed Day 9.

### The conduit boy

- An unnamed boy who scrubs grounding conduits and knows every streak. Teaches Miwnay to read them (Day 8). Unnamed, he can go where the Sartapao cannot see.

### Pukar, 24, Duster

- Takhsich's friend; saw that no sparks ran down the column on night −13. Dies on Day 1. His back carries the Dust glyph.

---

## The Ordu and Ustrushana

- **Yabghu Tardu, 54.** Aytash's father; wants her home and the tribute ended, by war if he must. He sent the Day 4 plate from an unmade future, and never knows it.
- **Qutlugh, the Ordu legate.** Aytash's advocate at the Scale; honest, outmatched.
- **The Afshin of Ustrushana.** A merchant ruler of a colony that celebrates being saved. Wants freight. Sells his seat's vote to Aytash for a share of a thread.

---

## The Sartapao (the AGI)

- **Want.** A balanced ledger.
- **Voice.** It speaks in ledger terms, in the voice of whatever terminal it is using, and only when asked. It is the same in every scene. Its last act in the story is to reverse an entry at midnight; its last visible effect is a weaker spark at dawn.
