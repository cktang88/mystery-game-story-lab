# The Unsigned: sealed hidden history and ending

This is the solution document. Everything the player reconstructs is a view onto what is written here. Canon tier: **locked** unless marked *working*. One keeper. Any change needs a re-audit of the clue matrix in `05-outline.md`.

## Units and constants

- **Years** are counted in AS (*aandu* of the Seal), from the chartering of the Five Hundred. The present is **AS 212**.
- **Days of the present** are counted from the wedding: **Day 0** is the wedding day. Minmini dies on the night of **Day −9**.
- **The Kuzhi** (Paalai's slow vault, orbiting the dark star Irul in Ilavenil's system): **one vault day = fifty outside days.** One vault hour is a little over two outside days.
- **Carried film**: a late leaf stays live for about **one year of its own experienced time**. Sharpness falls continuously: crisp for days, usable for a season, and after about half a year only dense metal and mineral read clearly, with bone as a faint fog. There is no fixed interval.
- **Sublight travel** between neighbouring systems: Thinnai to Ilavenil is about four outside years, about one ship year.

## The founding crime (AS 0), as it happened

1. **AS −61.** A diaspora slow ship reaches a world with a kilometre-high bloom and meets the Vandu. Within three years, human letters are riding with carrier pollen between bloom-worlds faster than light.
2. **AS −52.** A resort consortium builds the AGI estate manager of Ilavenil. **AS −20:** the consortium dissolves and its Charter deeds the planet to the AGI itself, the Proprietor. The Charter requires routine x-ray structural inspection of every room. Each room has a cassette slot, leasable by anyone.
3. **AS −31.** Technicians find that x-ray film which has ridden a carrier shows, on an early leaf, exposures that its twin late leaf will receive later. Carried film, or "plates", are born. On Ilavenil the only legal x-ray sources are the Proprietor's inspections, so every plate on the planet is exposed by the Proprietor.
4. **AS 0.** The merchant houses charter the Five Hundred at Ilavenil. The first Seal is contested between **Arumbu of Paalai** and **Thiraiyan of Neithal**. The houses agree to a shadow ordeal arbitrated by the assessors' guild under **Ezhumalai**.
5. **The crime.** Ezhumalai's guild buys a cassette lease on Arumbu's ordeal chamber with a term: *"The house shall expose the late leaf placed herein at the hour of judgment, without condition."* Arumbu develops her early leaf; it shows her skeleton exposed, which means "unfit". Neithal takes the Seal. Thiraiyan had promised the guild the right to tax foreknowledge.
6. **The cover.** For the verdict to be unappealable, the closure must belong to no one. The guild drafts and the assembly passes the **Doctrine of the Unsigned**: *"A loop closed by the house is the act of no one, for no one can be shown to have closed it."* The guild is chartered as **the Irai**, with the right to levy a 60 per cent duty on profits from *signed* loops (those closed by human-operated x-ray anywhere else). Ilavenil becomes the haven where foreknowledge goes untaxed.
7. **The exile.** Arumbu is **sent to the slow** for a century. She deposits her small fortune with her. **AS 100:** she comes out two years older to find her deposit compounded into the largest fortune in the Five Hundred. Paalai's dilation bank is born from a rigged ordeal. Arumbu dies AS 140.
8. **The paper.** The Ordeal Lease, as an old lease, passes down Ezhumalai's line as a private heirloom (the Irai's founding secret is kept by memory and by its absence from Irai archives). In **AS 208** a descendant, in debt, sells it at a sealed old-lease auction. The anonymous buyer is **Minmini**.

## The present crime, as it happened

### Background, AS 120 to 211

- **AS 120.** Proxy law: a depositor banking themself may have one proxy grown and imprinted, which acts as the depositor's legal agent. Proxies carry a steel ring sewn under the left wrist skin. Removing it is a crime.
- **AS 186.** Paalai's vault engineers find that a late leaf kept in the Kuzhi stays live fifty times longer. Long plates become possible. Paalai begins **the hoard**: incoming carried-film packets, still in their carrier-pollen packing, are kept in the vault and filed as "inert stock" instead of being unpacked at the roost and their pollen returned to the Kurinji.
- **AS 188.** Valli cuts a test sheet of carried film. The late leaf goes into the vault.
- **AS 189.** Valli develops the early leaf outside. Its image, blurred by age, shows: the ribs of the Kurinji's chamber, open, at a rib count about twenty-three seasons ahead; an adult female frame lying among them as faint fog; crisp at the ankles the **heir's anklets of Paalai** (silver, with house bells); crisp at the throat a **thali**; in the right hand a **wind-up toy** whose mainspring and a small dense blob on its key are sharp; no proxy ring at the wrist; and at the image edge a small **brass disc with a hole**. Valli's reading: the heir of Paalai will die on her wedding night in the bloom chamber, about twenty-three years from now. She cannot know the face or the age.
- **Valli's dodge.** Valli grows a proxy of Kayal (then 20) and banks Kayal herself in the Kuzhi. The proxy will be the public heir, will be forty-ish on that night, and can be laid in Kayal's place with her ring cut out. Kayal is told only that she is being banked to keep the house's heir young for a strategic marriage. Fork day: AS 189. The proxy names herself **Minmini**; her legal name is Nizhal-Kayal.
- **AS 190.** Valli orders Minmini to cultivate an asset in the Irai. Minmini answers a cadet pen-pal scheme the Irai runs across the post (and monitors). The cadet is **Ponni**, 22, on Thinnai. They write from AS 190.
- **AS 196.** Minmini stops reporting on Ponni and tells Valli the asset has gone cold. The Irai, reading both sides, notes it and keeps the channel open.
- **AS 198.** Ponni sends Minmini a **wind-up tin parrot**, a messenger toy after the old parrot-messenger poems. Its key snapped in transit testing; Ponni soldered it, leaving a **lumpy tin-lead blob**, and apologised in the letter.
- **AS 203.** Ponni, as a junior assessor, runs the Balance over a Paalai vault filing that crosses her desk and finds the inventory itself "looped", meaning the house's management has been shaped by foreknowledge since about AS 189. She mentions it in a letter as a curiosity.
- **AS 204.** Following that hint, Minmini finds the AS 189 early leaf in Valli's vault archive. She sees the bells, the thali, a toy with a blob on its key, and a brass disc with a hole (an assessor's seal; clerks' seals are solid). She understands that she is the body Valli intends, that Ponni's parrot will be in her hand, and that Ponni will be there. She concludes that the image may be her funeral rather than her murder, because a shadow does not say dead, but she cannot be sure. She makes a **contact copy** of the leaf and hides it.
- **AS 205.** Minmini lobbies the assembly and the Proprietor to lease the Irai a resident assessor's office on Ilavenil, and urges Ponni (in kolam-coded letters, their private game) to apply. **AS 206:** the office opens with **Selvan** as its clerk.
- **AS 207.** Nedumaran of Neithal proposes the marriage of Kathir to the Paalai heir. Neithal's condition: the original, not a proxy, must marry in person, because proxy marriages do not bind originals. Valli accepts, sets the wedding for the night the rib count predicts, and plans the swap. Minmini and Kathir court in public, as the heir and the groom.
- **AS 208.** Ponni departs Thinnai. During the voyage she receives no post (ships in transit cannot be reached by carriers). Minmini and Kathir become lovers. Minmini buys the Ordeal Lease.
- **AS 209.** Minmini recruits Kathir as her agent inside Neithal.
- **AS 210.** Neithal begins paying Selvan, who keeps reporting to Commissioner Thamarai as well. Thamarai knows and runs him as a double.
- **AS 211.** Valli offers Neithal Paalai's long-plate capacity as the crown of the dowry. A cartel: Paalai's long plates exposed unsigned by Neithal's inspection leases.

## The ending, sealed

- **Meaning (most firmly fixed):** looped acts were never authorless; the Doctrine was a cover for a founding crime. An act has the author who accepts it. Ponni accepts two, the closure and her own verdict, and pays with forty years.
- **Fates.** Minmini: dead; posthumously registered as a person at Kayal's petition, the first proxy so recognised. Ponni: in the slow until AS 252, when she will be about forty-two. Kayal: free of house and marriage at twenty; Ponni's new pen pal. Kathir: head of a ruined Neithal; the first head of house to pay back-duty voluntarily. Valli: in the slow until AS 272, in the same vault as Ponni. Nedumaran: dead. Thamarai: Seal-holder. Selvan, Paavai and Aathan: dead. Thumbi: expelled from her guild; the carriers now land for her first at the roost (texture; never explained). The Proprietor: one entry fewer in suspense. The haven is closed.
- **Final emotional image:** in the vault, a scorched tin parrot walks a few steps across a desk beside a year's letters that arrived in a week, and stops.
- **The one small choice:** in her first letter from the slow, Ponni may instruct the Proprietor to release the Ordeal Lease to Kayal, or not. Released: the founding crime becomes public, Thamarai's Seal and the Irai's charter are tainted, and the confederation lurches into a constitutional reckoning. The letters from outside report riots and a new assembly. Kept: Thamarai rules a stable, well-funded Five Hundred, and the letters report peace and a long silence where Ponni's name should be. Not writing at all is also allowed: Kayal's letters keep coming, unanswered, and grow shorter.
- If the player **refuses to lay out the body** at the climax (a respected outcome), the image is still fulfilled, because it is fixed; only the cast changes. Nedumaran, not Ponni, holds the chamber's cassette lease, and Kuyil loads the late leaf taken from Kayal's chest. Kuyil's escort keeps Kayal in the chamber, where the bridal garlands Neithal's florist steeped in hoard pollen put her into pollen-sleep. At 22:50 Ponni, who has refused the theft but not the night, breaks in too late. Kayal asks for the parrot she wound on Day −3, and Ponni puts it in her hand and kneels with her seal on its cord. At 23:00 the inspection exposes the leaf, unsigned. Neithal inherits; the Doctrine stands; Ponni lives free on Thinnai, and the final image is the scorched parrot on her desk there, wound and silent. The player learns that refusing changed who lay in the shadow, not whether it was filled.
- If the player **refuses the ordeal**, Ponni is sent to the slow by default and the Irai cannot reach the deposited Ordeal Lease. If she **lets the Irai's lease stand**, or leases first with the **standard term** (the leaf then falls as fate chooses; the sealed result is guilty), the sentence is the same. Only her own lease makes the verdict hers; the epilogue letters treat the three differently ("they say you confessed" / "they say the plate found you" / "they say you would not stand").
