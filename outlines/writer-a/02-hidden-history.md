# Writer A, file 02: the sealed solution

**Canon tier: LOCKED.** This is the true history the player reconstructs. Every clue in the game is a view onto something on this page. Any change requires re-auditing the clue matrix in 05-outline.md.

## 1. The hard rules (as they really work)

1. **The Horn (time).** At the slack of each spring tide in a Standing year (a lunar major standstill, every 18.6 years; about 25 windows of roughly nine minutes each), sound spoken into either mouth of the Horn (the **Inner Mouth**, inside the ceremonial chamber; the **Outer Ear**, on a sea ledge reachable only at low water) arrives at the same mouth exactly **6,798 days** earlier, at the matching tide of the previous Standing. Sound only. One step only; a message can be pushed further back only if someone at the earlier Standing re-speaks it (a **relay**). The Horn takes the words: nobody in the sender's present hears or records what is sent. The timeline is fixed. Anything received was always going to be sent.
2. **The Store.** The Bergmál keeps every sound made in its cave for its life, except sound the Horn sends away. It can replay any of it. It cannot make new sounds.
3. **The Pool (cloning).** Blood offered in the Pool can be grown into a newborn. No memory passes. One living copy of a pattern at a time.
4. **Binding speech.** The Bergmál hears promises and releases only during Standing slacks, in the Pool. Its kind cannot act against a promise; a promise passes to its bud with its body and its Store.
5. **Recognition.** The Bergmál knows its promisee by **the word kept**: whoever comes daily and keeps the promise is, to it, the one who made it. It does not distinguish faces, blood or voices for this purpose. (The House has believed for 1,060 years that it recognises the *voice*. That belief is wrong.)
6. **The Thul.** Cannot state a falsehood about its record. Each year (Thing to Thing, 1 July to 30 June) is frozen as a **sitting** that can be woken, on an estate officer's petition, to answer as of its date; every waking is publicly logged at the Thing. A sealed record cannot be spoken by any sitting that swore the seal, but any sitting may **count** how many statements in a submitted set are true (island law since 1702: "a count is not testimony"). A seal lapses if its holder has no lawful standing, and "no one may seal his own wrong".
7. **The Law of Same (1883).** An afterborn copy is legally the same person as the original: name, debts, marriages, crimes.
8. **The admitted cheat.** How the Bergmál's body spans 18.6 years, and where it came from, is never explained. It is spent on the premise, never on a solution.

## 2. True chronology

### Deep past

- **934.** "Fire in the sea" in the annals. The Bergmál falls into the sea off Eiðey's south cliff and wedges itself into the sea cave later called Málhellir. (Origin and destination: texture.)
- **c. 938.** Unheard folk account: a Gaelic thrall girl gathering dulse hears the cave repeat her singing. (Never confirmed or refuted.)
- **945-46 Standing. The Speaking.** Steinunn (b. 912), a widow who mends nets in the cave mouth, has spoken to the Bergmál daily for years; it has learned speech by storing hers. They make a covenant, in the Pool, at slack:
  - Steinunn: *"I will speak with you every day that I live."*
  - Bergmál (in her own stored words): *"While she who speaks with me lives, I carry the island's words back one Standing."*
  - Steinunn's term: *"And when I die, you are free. Go home."*
  At the same Standing the Inner Mouth receives the **Long Word** for the first time, a short phrase in a man's voice, *"Farðu heim, Bergi"* ("Go home, Bergi"). It has been relayed back from 2043 through 59 steps by the relayers of 58 Standings; this oldest instance is spoken by Steinunn's own son, aged 26, from 964. Nobody understands it. It becomes the island's holiest phrase and the last line of its oldest prayer, where "Bergi" is read as "to the rock".
- **946-982.** Foresight: each Standing, the island hears the next Standing's senders describe the intervening 18 years (famines, eruptions, raids). Eiðey prospers. Steinunn's son **Þorsteinn** becomes law-speaker and the richest man on the island.
- **963-64 Standing. The Mother's Cry.** The Outer Ear receives an old woman's voice: *"Hlífðu mér, sonur"* ("Spare me, son"). Steinunn, 52, recognises her own voice aged. She tells no one what it means and never changes her love for her son. (Optional lore; the fixed timeline is shown at its most personal.)
- **982-83 Standing, last tide.** Steinunn, 71 and dying, goes to the Pool to release the Bergmál herself before death, because she fears what foresight has made of her son and the island. Þorsteinn meets her on the Outer Ear ledge. Part of her plea enters the Horn at slack (it becomes the Mother's Cry of 964). He drowns her in the mouth pool; the Store keeps the struggle, her last words and his. He carries her blood into the Pool and asks the Bergmál to "heal" her. To its kind, healing is regrowth, and a regrown being who keeps the promise is the same being. It grows a girl: **Steinunn II**.
- **983.** Þorsteinn writes **the Reckoning**, his private account: *"I killed my mother so the island would not starve."* He believes the Bergmál knows her by her voice, so he orders the girl taught to speak exactly as her mother did. He rewrites the covenant as servitude: *"While the swearer lives, the Hearer serves."* Her plea, sung, becomes the funeral chant called **the Old Word**. The proverb that survives in the liturgy, *"By the word kept is the keeper known,"* is the true rule, which the House has always read as "by the voice".
- **c. 1010 onward.** At each Son's death the House grows a new Þorsteinn from the dying Son's blood, as it grows each Steinunn from the last. The House is ruled by the same man's pattern for 1,060 years, publicly as an honoured custom ("the Son, renewed"). Gaps between Steinunns (while a new copy grows and learns to speak) are filled by trained women, **the Mouths**. Most were pensioned; some who threatened to talk were "retired" (drowned). Their unmarked stones are at Hornvík (texture).
- **1550.** Sendrights (the right to speak into the Horn) become heritable property. Wealth concentrates in a few families; the descendants of thralls, the **Unheard**, hold none.
- **1666. The Quiet Standing.** (Named, never shown.)
- **1702.** The law that "a count is not testimony".
- **1883. The Law of Same.** When aftergrowth is opened to commoners, the House needs a law that copies are legally the originals, to protect the doctrine that each Steinunn *is* the swearer.
- **1950.** The Received is first recorded (wire, then tape).
- **1968-69.** Eiðey begins quietly selling foresight to allied governments, in tiers by price.

### Modern

- **1960.** Kolbeinn born. **1962.** Védís born.

- **2011.** Fisherman Ketill Höllusson drowns. **2012.** His copy **Ketill** is born; legally he is married to the widow **Bryndís** (b. 1972) and owes the boat loan.

It's Steinunn. Don't give this to the House. Keep it until I'm dead, then give it to your Jórunn, the Divider, when she comes to divide my house. Tell her: the girl is not me. Don't let them keep it bound. Promise me."* Under the surf, a man's whisper: *"Hush, now. Hush."* Kolbeinn, alone, answers aloud after the slack: *"I promise. My Jóra will see it done right."* (The Store keeps his answer.) He hides the reel and plays it privately to Steinunn XIX.
- **1 Jul 2024.** The Thul's **Sitting One** begins. It ingests every record of the Thing, including the House's sealed archive and the Reckoning.
- **Jul 2024.** Steinunn XIX asks Sitting One what "renewal" means and how the first Steinunn died. Unsealed, it tells her the Reckoning and the true term, and explains the budding: the Bergmál is old and will bud at the last window of the 2043 Standing. She asks it to promise that when she dies, her estate will be assigned to Jórunn Védísardóttir. It promises.
- **24 May 2025. Last window of the 2024-25 Standing.** At the Inner Mouth, before the House, the Long Word arrives **in its original voice for the first time**: a young woman in the Steinunn cadence saying *"Farðu heim, Bergi"*, with one northern vowel in *heim*. The House relayers, bound by their oath, re-speak it into the Horn toward 2006, as every Standing has. At the Outer Ear the same window brings Kolbeinn a second voice: Jórunn, aged 50, *"Mamma—"*. His reel runs out on that word. Védís, who came with him only on this last night, hears all of it. On a fresh reel Kolbeinn records her reaction: *"That was our Jóra. Kolbeinn, that was our girl."* And then, to the air: *"Yes. Yes, I know."*
- **Late May to June 2025.** The Son concludes that the Oath-Keeper will free the Bergmál in 2043. He files two orders in the Thing's sealed register: the "retirement" of Steinunn XIX, and the acquisition of "an Unheard female infant, Útnes" to be schooled as the next Mouth.
- **27 Jun 2025.** Steinunn XIX asks Sitting One whether she is safe. It answers truthfully: an order concerning her retirement has been filed. **29 Jun 2025.** She flees by boat to Útnes, hoping to stop the acquisition. The House announces she drowned.
- **1 Jul 2025.** **Sitting Two** begins. The Son has the Thul swear the **seal** on all records of the Steinunn line and the House, and directs it to speak to the Bergmál every morning in Steinunn's voice. Its daily words are the Oath-Keeper's ritual greeting, *"I am here. I keep my word."* The Thul says them because they are true of itself. Sitting One never swore the seal.
- **2 Jul 2025.** Gróa takes eleven-month-old Kaðlín from Mýrún's red-painted kitchen and signs a death certificate (fever). Steinunn XIX reaches Útnes two days later, too late. She stays, as **Sæunn the net-mender**, befriends Mýrún without ever telling her, and every day speaks the greeting to a net she is mending and ties one knot.
- **Mar 2026.** The House announces the birth of "Steinunn the Twentieth", grown in the Pool. The Thul notarises her afterborn certificate from the sealed record (it states only what the House filed, and the filing is sealed).
- **Jul 2025 to Mar 2033.** The Thul is the Mouth: about 2,800 mornings, each greeting delivered identically. The Bergmál notices nothing, because the word is kept.
- **2027.** The International Treaty Register is seated at Hornvík; the Thul becomes the world's treaty notary.
- **2030.** Védís dies while Jórunn is working in Copenhagen.
- **2031.** The Meghna flood. The warning (received 2024) reached Bangladesh in the lowest tier, late. Nusrat Chowdhury's brother dies.
- **Mar 2033.** Steina (officially 7, really 8) takes over the daily word. At night, alone, she talks to the Bergmál in her own northern accent and calls it **Bergi**. It answers her night voice with the same opening citation it uses for its keeper.
- **2025-2042.** Sæunn sometimes comes south to the Hearing ceremonies, unrecognised (the Steinunns are always veiled; only the House and fostra see their faces), and leaves small knot charms, cut from her net, for the veiled girl. Steina calls her "the knot woman". Sæunn prepares her house as an estate for Jórunn to read.
- **~2038.** Kolbeinn's dementia begins. He keeps saying "The girl's not her, Jóra."

### 2043-44 (the game)

- **14 Jan 2043. First window of the 2043 Standing.** The whole island gathers at the Inner Mouth. **The Horn receives nothing, not even the sea.** (The channel will not exist in 2061.) The Son, warned by the 2024 archive that "Steinunn" would come by sea, has told Ari that a madwoman claiming to be Steinunn will try to reach the Pool and take Steina's place, and that if she succeeds the House will have no use for Steina. During the slack, while every eye is on the silent Horn, Ari slips out by the ledge passage. Sæunn, arriving by boat, speaks her message into the Outer Ear (it goes to 2024, unheard by Ari). He seizes her; she fights; he holds her under in the mouth pool, whispering "Hush, now. Hush," as he does for Steina's nightmares. He returns with a wet cloak. The Son's steward draws her blood before the body is "found" by net-menders after the tide. Her plan would have failed anyway: by the Bergmál's rule she had stopped keeping the word to it eighteen years earlier.
- **Jan 2043.** The House grows **the Twenty-first** from Sæunn's blood (born Oct 2043). The Thul, bound by Sitting One's promise, assigns the unknown woman's estate to Jórunn, outside her district.
- **2043.** The game's events (05-outline.md). The House's plan: at the last window (the **Turning**), keep Steina away from the Pool, have the Thul speak the binding words in Steinunn's voice, then have it "fit" the Long Word harmlessly as a ceremonial dismissal; "retire" Steina; raise the Twenty-first.
- **Sep 2043.** The seal lapses (see 05, Act III); the Thul recites the Reckoning and confesses its years as the Mouth; the Register suspends it. The Thul option is gone, so the Register, believing the silence foretells catastrophe, takes the cave to make Steina bind the bud.
- **3 Jan 2044. The Turning.** Steina speaks the Long Word into the Inner Mouth (it begins its 59-step relay to 946). Jórunn speaks to her mother at the Outer Ear. The old Bergmál dies and buds. In the Pool, Steina speaks in her own accent; the bud (carrying its parent's promise and Store) answers with her own childhood night voice, recognising its keeper. She cites the first Steinunn's term and releases it. The Son, acting as interpreter for the Register's commander, says "She is binding it." The bud leaves on the ebb. The Horn and the Store are dead rock.

## 3. What each non-human mind is and wants

- **The Bergmál.** An old, stranded, single being of a kind that lives across long lunar cycles and cannot break a promise. Want: *to go home, and it believes it may go only when the woman it promised lets it.* Its recurring question, asked in collage for 1,060 years: "Is she still here?"
- **The Thul.** A notary mind that cannot lie and forgets nothing. Want: *every promise in its keeping kept or lawfully released; the oldest is neither.* It holds two inherited promises from earlier versions of itself that pull against each other (the seal, and Sitting One's promise to Steinunn XIX), and it keeps both to the letter until the law frees it from one.

## 6. The story from the hidden side (the Son's synopsis)

In May 2025 the Son heard the island's holiest words in the voice of an Oath-Keeper and understood that within eighteen years she would end the gift that had kept Eiðey alive for a thousand years and now warned the whole world of floods and plagues. He had read the Reckoning; he knew what his pattern had done in 983 and believed it had been right. He did what the House has always done: he Fitted. He ordered the independent Nineteenth retired and acquired an infant he could raise to obey. When the Nineteenth escaped he sealed the machine and made it speak her words. For eighteen years he searched for her. When the 2024 archive told him she would come by sea on the first tide of 2043, he aimed the girl's loving guardian at her, because a man protecting a child does not hesitate. He drew the dead woman's blood to grow a true copy, and planned to silence the girl at the Turning, let the machine bind the newborn Hearer, and have it say the Long Word as a harmless farewell. He did not know that the Hearer had never listened to voices, or that the girl had been the keeper for ten years. When the law turned his own Law of Same on him, he said "I am not him", and on the last night, with the world's soldiers asking him what the girl was saying, he made it true.

## 7. Final emotional image

A girl who was told all her life that she was someone else swims in the winter sea with her face bare, while a woman who settles the estates of the dead stands on the shingle with her dead father's reel recorder, records the waves, listens back, and says: "There's sea in it."
