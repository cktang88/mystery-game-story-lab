## 4. Fixed while drafting

1. **Ch1 showed Ari's absence on an audio recording.** Fixed: the wet cloak is seen live; the six minutes are reconstructed later from phone clips (Ch11).
2. **The midpoint put Jórunn's "Mamma—" in the House's archive,** which only recorded the Inner Mouth. Fixed: it is on Kolbeinn's private Outer Ear reel.
3. **Relay count** miscounted (57); corrected to 59 steps across 58 Standings of relayers.
4. **Sitting One would have been sealed** if the seal applied to the whole year. Fixed by the Thing-to-Thing calendar: the seal was sworn by Sitting Two, so Sitting One never swore it.
5. **Why didn't the Nineteenth's release work?** She had stopped keeping the word to the Hearer itself for eighteen years. This makes Ari's crime doubly pointless and the substitute the true keeper.
6. **Anchor choice.** Kolbeinn was the first anchor candidate, but he dies at the end of Act II; moved the anchor to Steina.
7. **Ari's redemption death** removed from the canonical path (now a choice consequence).
8. **The Register** was first called "the Concordat"; renamed as generic.

## 5. Open risks

- **Act III reveal density.** Reveals 8 to 11 land in four chapters (the over-stacking failure critics found in Virtue's Last Reward). Mitigation: Ch13 is a comic down beat; the Goðar's copying is public from Ch1 so only its meaning lands; Ch16 is a replay, not a new mystery. Must be tested.
- **Rule load.** Horn, Store, Pool, sittings, seal, the Count, the Law of Same and Fitting. Only the first six are on the critical path; Fitting and relays are optional depth. Watch for jargon overload, the criticism aimed at 13 Sentinels.
- **The seal's lapse** is a double-bind of law; on screen it must be one sentence from the bench.
- **The midpoint gives away the ending** (the release will happen). Intentional, for suspense over surprise; the second half's questions are who pays and how. A cold test should confirm players feel dread rather than deflation.
- **Scale of low-text mechanics over a long game** is unproven (the reference games are short). The Greeting Wall and collage systems need a prototype first.
