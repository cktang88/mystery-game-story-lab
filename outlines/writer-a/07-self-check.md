## Known risks for the grader

1. **Rule load is still real.** There are eight verbs and about twelve rules, but at most one is new per chapter, and all are introduced by Ch 18.
2. **Branch cost.** Choice 1, choice 2, two votes, the urn and the ordeal term multiply endings. The outline fixes canon and lists the variants, and production will need a branch budget.
3. **Cultural care.** The thali tied in death and the sumangali honour should be reviewed by Tamil readers. The invented "laying-off" rite of the first draft has been removed.
4. **Self-conviction as martyrdom.** It is now motivated (it discharges Kayal and Kathir under the sole-author rule) and optional (there are three other terms). It must still play as choice, not doom.
