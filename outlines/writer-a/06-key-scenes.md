# The Unsigned: key scenes (fourth pass)

Each scene says what the player **does**, **sees** and **hears**, and what it plants or pays off. Chapter numbers refer to `05`.

**Tone:** cool rather than spooky; sleek rather than gross. The set pieces are a dock under a kilometre-high bloom, a chase over kolam-lit rooftops, a deck court on a starship, an alien envoy waiting for an answer, a vault heist against two clocks, and a swap under guard.

---

## 1. The scan rises (prologue)

A white vault room in AS 188. A leaf comes up from a developing tray in blue-white light:
- a hall of mineral ribs;
- a table;
- bright anklet bells;
- a seal pressed to something the scan cannot show;
- a tin bird with a bright blob on its key;
- at the edge, a faint figure wearing a disc with a hole.

Valli, younger: "Whoever stands there signs for us." The title appears.

## 2. Under the bloom (Ch 1)

A slow ship docks beneath the Kaanthal, a kilometre-high lily of mineral silk, shut. Ponni walks a city drawn in kolam lines of light. Each line blocks her until she **leases** passage, and the blank *purpose* field glows on every form she signs.

## 3. The door (Ch 2)

Paalai's staff are carrying crates out of Minmini's rooms, and the whole cast is on the landing. "Loss of property, licensed." Ponni holds the stamp down: *UNDER ASSESSMENT*. The crates go back in.

## 4. The double coin and the heavy bird (Ch 3)

Ponni weighs a brass coin. The needle settles at one day. She enters it in the ledger, and the needle frees and swings to ten years. The player has learned the rule by doing it. She weighs:
- the bed: two days;
- the toy on the pillow: looped;
- the parrot: **twenty-four years**.

She lifts the parrot and frowns: it is **heavier** than she remembers. On the board outside, her readings post within a minute.

## 5. The building replays the night (Ch 9)

Ponni stands in the bedroom and the building performs the night again: the gate chime, the doors locking, the air stopping, a hum from a cassette slot marked **not hers**, and then the vents roaring. She walks through it as it happens.

## 6. "It only says *there*" (Ch 11)

**Fit:** the broker's leaf laid over the live alley, coin aligned to coin. At the hour, a snore.

## 7. Rooftop chase (Ch 14)

A figure with a clockwork fan slips out behind the toymaker's empty shop. Ponni chases him across terraces striped with kolam light, through gardens and over a gambling hall's lightbox roof. He drops into the clockwork streets, where no lease line glows, and the house cannot follow him.

## 8. The council of nakhodas (Ch 8, 23, 30)

A teak deck that never stops moving: monsoon charts in brass frames and captains with astrolabe tokens. Each undecided captain sets an object on the chart table: a berth key for an aged mother, a child's shoe from a ship that never arrived, a chart with a reef marked. Thumbi **trades tokens**.
- Session 1: Jauhar wins.
- Session 2: Saleha turns.
- Session 3, at dawn, with the coupon paid: Saleha takes the monsoon seat. Jauhar stands alone among the elders' bunks, the face of the cost.

## 9. Ottai (Ch 16): a second world and an individual

On Thinnai, the small bloom closes over Aruvi. An old carrier with a **holed vane** folds in, a luminous seed-head the size of a room, lands in front of her and waits.

**Parley.** She reads three coats: pale *closed*, pale *sympathy*, rust *drop the hub*. On the roost floor, five lanes glow faintly toward five partner blooms. She lays out the few bundles she has. The lens shows one partner settled. Ottai takes the reply and leaves.

On the board, Thinnai is marked *in breach: no vote*.

## 10. The Treasury Bench (Ch 22)

Three treasurers sit at brass counters. Each sets out an object:
- a sealed scandal file;
- a promotion warrant;
- the rulebook.

Aruvi **trades beads** with the retention orders, which carry Thamarai's signature, and wins two of the three. She carries the seized packets to the roost. Ottai lands; its coats are split. She lays the bundles in their lanes. Then she slips a letter into the pollen, and Ottai does not shake it off.

## 11. Midpoint: the long leaf (Ch 21)

A soft copy of the scan, fitted to the Sealing Hall rib by rib. Kayal comes in with a lead sheath. It reads twenty-four years. Kayal lifts a cup **left hand under**, and Ponni has to step out.

## 12. The stair and the gantries (Ch 26)

A shot from the dark. Ponni's lease line stops at the door. She chases across the carrier gantries, over gold-lit drops, as carriers fold in overhead, and loses the runner in the pollen-light. On a stretcher, Selvan says: "She said let it run."

## 13. Frame-break: the bird (Ch 26)

At the lightbox the parrot goes onto the original leaf: **blob over blob**. The small bright **bar** inside the scanned bird lines up with the clasp under the real bird's wing. The seal goes onto the disc: **hole over hole**. Ponni opens the clasp, and a folded deed slides out in Minmini's hand. The player enters *Document: the deed. Witness: Ponni.* Ponni says: "It was never the merger."

## 14. Round 3 at the hub (Ch 28)

On Ilavenil's roost, Ottai lands with its coats split. Beside it a **rust-coated envoy from Thondi** settles and sets down its *price*. Thumbi **parleys** with six bundles, five lanes, and the order to choose. The lens shows partners settling, and rust coats answering each concession. Canon: one concession, a price of 32%. At dusk the Kaanthal opens rib by rib, and pollen-light rolls down the terraces. On Thinnai, the dome opens too.

If the layout fails, the ribs stay shut for two more days, and the board turns red over the bonds.

## 15. The wedding (Ch 31)

Kolam light on every terrace. The nadaswaram and thavil. Jasmine. Kathir ties the thali on Kayal, a living bride sealing an alliance her house chose. Children chase toy carriers along the balustrades. Across the city, in an empty lab, a clockwork box clicks open.

## 16. The extraction (Ch 32)

A shuttle crosses to the Mella, a ring of white garden-rooms around a dark star. Inside, every wall carries two clocks, and each step costs time outside. It is played as stealth through the vault's own leases: Kathir's nominee codes, Kayal's controlling seal, Ponni's registry map, and the outside clock racing. A berth door opens.

Minmini sits up. For her it is the same night. She looks at the woman beside the bed and knows her from twenty-two years of handwriting. "You came." "You said bring the bird."

They free the toymaker. They lift the clay jar from Arumbu's room. They are out at 19:00.

## 17. The sealing (Ch 33)

The Sealing Hall: mineral ribs rising in green-gold light, and the scan rig idling in the wall. Neithal's runner waits at the threshold with his back turned, as custom requires.
- Kayal hands Minmini the anklets and the seal and leaves through the far ribs with Kathir.
- Ponni's key clicks the bracelet open.
- Minmini winds the parrot. It walks across the table and its clasp springs.
- She seals her deed. The merger charter lies beside it, unsealed.

At 22:55 Ponni slides the live leaf into the cassette from the safe corner. The *purpose* field glows on the floor, and the player may walk away from it. In canon she signs.

In the fitting lens everything aligns: bells, bird, bar, hole. At 23:00 the rig hums.

## 18. Between the pistol and the chair (Ch 34)

At the Slow House, Nedumaran arrives with the breach clause in hand. Valli winds the clockwork pistol, and the ratchet is the only sound. The player, as Ponni, steps between them or steps aside. In canon: "Let me assess him." Valli lowers the pistol.

## 19. Across the table (Ch 35)

A guard room, one lamp, the parrot on the table. Nedumaran: "I licensed a loss." The player lays out the toymaker's testimony, the lease page and the nominee codes, in any order. His face moves from anger to law to silence.

## 20. The roost at night (Ch 35)

Ponni and Minmini sit on the gantry edge as carriers fold in over the reopened bloom. It is their first conversation aloud. No one dies, and no one is mourned. It is two women who have only ever written to each other, laughing at how wrong they each got the other's voice.

## 21. The altered term (Ch 37)

The Irai's count lease for Ponni's Proof: *"as the Commission instructs."* The player knows the standard term from Ch 18 and can spot the change. Ponni re-leases the AS 0 hour, and the AS 0 lease carries the same words. On the lightbox, the AS 0 hall film shows a woman walking out with a clay jar of bright silver beads, the majority, and beside her the arbiter's **holed seal**. Kayal sets the same jar on the table.

## 22. The duel (Ch 38)

**In private.** One table. Ponni lays out the courier log, the retention orders, the film and the jar. Thamarai answers each with her own: the debt retired, the fleet paid, the charge sheet. "Someone had to let it run. You signed. I only counted."

**In the hall.** Beads move. On Ponni's side: Sevvanthi and the bench, the farm bloc, and the jar. On Thamarai's: Saleha's token, the shipyards, and a warning of constitutional void. Kathir stands against Thamarai with the plate bloc. In canon, silence is traded for the decree. Sevvanthi stands up on the bench and does not sit down.

## 23. The Proof (Ch 39)

Three rounds of exhibits on the great lightbox. Ponni files her count lease on the standard term, then rises: "I stand as sole author." The beads are counted as they lie: she is convicted, and Kayal and Kathir are discharged.

## 24. Final image (epilogue)

A bright garden berth on the Mella ring. Bales of letters arrive, a year in each, with scans from three worlds. A box comes holding the tin parrot. Ponni winds it. It walks past the letters and stops. On the top letter, in Minmini's hand: *assessed and found sufficient.*
