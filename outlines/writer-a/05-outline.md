# The Unsigned: outline

## One sentence

In a galaxy where the future arrives as an x-ray shadow, a grieving tax assessor must decide who will fill a fixed death, and then sign for it.

## One paragraph

Ponni, an assessor of the Five Hundred's revenue office, crosses four light-years to meet the pen pal she has written to for twenty-two years and arrives on Ilavenil, the AGI-owned pleasure world, a day after her friend Minmini was found dead. The death is "looped": someone saw it on a future plate before it happened, so by law it is the act of no one. Ponni learns that Minmini was a clone, the proxy of a bride who has just returned young from a time-dilation vault to marry the heir of the enemy house. **First disaster:** the killing released pollen that the vault had hoarded for decades, so the great bloom closes and the alien post that binds forty-one worlds stops. Markets seize, creditors give the confederation a deadline, and the old Seal-holder dies for want of post-borne medicine. **Second disaster, caused by Ponni's own investigation:** every reading she takes is published, so the people she gets close to (the toymaker, then her own clerk, who was the hand) are killed before they can talk, and what she uncovers is worse than murder. Twenty-three years ago the bride's grandmother saw a blurred x-ray of "the heir" lying dead in the bloom's chamber on her wedding night and grew Minmini to fill it. Minmini was killed to make the real bride fill it instead, so that the groom's house would inherit her vault. The shadow cannot be changed, only cast. Ponni finds her own soldered toy and her own seal in the old image and understands that it was never a death: it is Minmini's funeral, and Ponni will lay it out. She steals her friend's body from its pyre, cuts out the clone's ring, lays her in the bride's anklets and thali with the toy in her hand, and leases the inspection in her own name. The bride lives, the alliance dies, and the murderer is shot by the grandmother he deceived. **Third disaster, caused by Ponni's fix:** her signature is the first ever on a looped act, so it destroys the law that made Ilavenil a haven, enriches the state, and lifts to power the commissioner who let Minmini die and who now needs Ponni silenced. Ponni discovers that the very first ordeal of the Five Hundred was rigged by her own office's founders, writes the term of her own ordeal, convicts herself, and goes into the vault for forty years. There, she begins writing to the bride.

## The threads

Three plot lines, four playable threads, one interstitial voice.

| Plot line | Thread | Viewpoint | Place | Look | Plainly stated want |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Who killed Minmini, and who will fill the shadow? | **A** | Ponni | Irai office, Minmini's house, the cold room, lease-quarters | ochre and ledger-amber; kolam light-lines | name the author; then keep Kayal out of the shadow; then sign |
| 2. The wedding and the Seal | **B** | Kayal | Slow House, assembly, the Kuzhi | black stone, white lamps, silver bells | pass as the woman everyone knew; survive; give her shadow its place |
| 2. (spy side) | **C** | Kathir | Neithal plate-house, the roost at night, the chamber | salt-white walls of lightboxes | find out who killed Minmini without his father knowing |
| 3. The closed bloom and the galaxy's markets | **D** | Thumbi | roost, unpacking floor, bloom base, Mawsim flagship | dust-gold, pollen masks, carriers folding in | reopen the bloom before the post's collapse ruins everyone |
| Interstitial | — | the Proprietor | everywhere | kolam lines and inspection hum | an author for every entry (shown as its suspense count on the ledger board between chapters) |

Start together: all four viewpoints, both patriarchs, the commissioner, the clerk and the dying Seal-holder are at the pyre in chapter 2. Only Ponni arrives from elsewhere, which is the one allowed exception.

A persistent **ledger board** (the Irai's public board, in-world) is shown at every chapter break. It lists the current question, each thread's want, the days to the wedding, the Mawsim deadline, and the Proprietor's suspense count.

## Full outline

### Prologue: the night of Day −9

**Narrative question:** what are we looking at?

1. The game opens on an x-ray sweep of a city at night, the Proprietor's routine inspection. The player pans the beam across bone and metal: dancers with anklet bells, lovers' thalis touching, a gambler's coins, the mineral ribs of a vast closed flower. No faces.
2. The beam settles on a bedroom: a woman in bed, a steel ring at her wrist, winding a toy carrier and setting it to walk. The player can follow it across the coverlet until it stops.
3. Hard cut to the inspection hum at 04:00. In a cassette, a narrow strip of film darkens. Title: **The Unsigned.**

The player has just seen the victim, the weapon, the proxy ring and the medium of the future, and can read none of them.

### Act 1: "Nobody's" (Day −7 to the night of Day −7)

**Act hook (small, personal):** a woman goes to meet her pen pal.

**Chapter 1 (A). Dock, Day −7, dawn.** Ponni's slow ship docks after four outside years. The roost post office is eerily idle; the bloom is shut; unpackers sit on empty bins. A postmistress hands Ponni the bale held for her: four years of Minmini's letters, the last dated Day −10. Ponni walks the bale through the lease-quarters toward the address she has written to for twenty-two years; at each private threshold a kolam line of light blocks her until she leases passage (*lease* taught at zero stakes). At the gate: mourners.

**Chapter 2 (all). The pyre, Day −7, morning.** At the foot of the closed Kurinji, Paalai is about to burn a body. Everyone is here and every relationship is stated aloud by the officiant's roll: Valli, head of Paalai; Kayal, "the heir, returned from the slow"; Kathir of Neithal, her betrothed; Nedumaran, his father; Commissioner Thamarai of the Irai, on Ilavenil for the debt talks; Selvan, Ponni's clerk-to-be; Thumbi of the Thoothar, under the shut bloom; old Seal-holder Aathan, frail. The Proprietor's voice gives the determination: "Act of no one. Entered in suspense." Ponni sees Minmini's face for the first time: forty-three, laughing lines, a steel ring under the left wrist skin. Valli calls the body "estate of the house". Ponni uses exactly that: under Five Hundred law no estate may be disposed of before assessment, so she stamps the shroud **under assessment**, and the pyre stops. The body goes to the Irai's cold room. Copy status is disclosed: the pen pal was the bride's proxy. Kayal, twenty, has Minmini's face as it must have looked when the letters began. Two small things pass almost unnoticed: Thamarai squeezes Ponni's arm and says she approved Ponni's cadet pen-pal letters herself, back in 190; and Aathan's aide fusses that the Seal-holder's heart medicine is due on the next post from Uppalam.

**Chapter 3 (A). The estate, Day −7.** The Proprietor offers Ponni the executor's lease of Minmini's rooms, and the kolam lines open. The player handles the inventory: twenty-two years of Ponni's own letters, bundled; a shelf of wind-up toys; a dozen clockwork boxes full of **old leases** (Ponni grasps, with dread, that her friend owned part of the archive no one admits); on the bedside, a run-down toy carrier; and, on its own cushion, the **tin parrot** Ponni sent in AS 198, with the ugly **solder blob** on its key that she apologised for. The **Balance** is taught: weigh the body (looped, about a day deep, heavy); weigh the toy carrier (looped); weigh the parrot: **looped, twenty-three years deep.** Ponni has no explanation. Within minutes her readings appear on the public ledger board (the cost, taught). The gate room's safety film from the night of Day −9, which now belongs to her as executor, shows a 19:30 visitor whose only crisp metal is a **solid brass disc** at the chest. Selvan, beside her, wears one; so does every Irai courier. Ponni jokes, "Clerks get the solid ones; we get the hole," and taps her own **seal with a hole**. Selvan winds a clockwork box on her new desk every morning and steps out to the stair for a bloom-wine pipe; she notices both and asks about neither.

**Chapter 4 (B). The Slow House, Day −7.** Kayal is twenty and everyone at court treats her as a woman they have known for twenty years. The *pass* verb is taught: at a mourning reception she lifts a cup right-handed and a courtier's eyebrow rises (a suspicion marker). Valli corrects her quietly. On Valli's desk is an antique clockwork pistol. A playable flashback to **the folding, Day −11**: in a white room, Minmini hands Kayal the anklets and the heir's seal, shows her how to hold a cup with the left hand under ("You'll need it"), and smiles at the toy Kayal says she has ordered for her. Back in the present, Kayal learns that the carrier on Minmini's bedside was "your gift", and the guilt lands. She asks who her proxy was. Valli: "A placeholder. Placeholders don't need names."

**Chapter 5 (C). The plate-house, Day −7.** Kathir hides his grief among his father's lightboxes. Playable flashback, **Day −30**: Kathir tells Nedumaran that Paalai means to put Minmini in Kayal's place on the wedding night, that she says she won't survive it, and that his father must stop it or Kathir will stop the wedding. Nedumaran: "I'll handle it." The player now knows something no one else in the cast does. In the present, Kathir opens his locket: a carried-film strip, an x-ray of his hand in hers. He starts spying on his own house.

**Chapter 6 (D). The roost, Day −7.** Thumbi's brother has been laid off with every other unpacker. The guild master, Kaatravan, announces the closure as "the bloom's refusal of the alliance". Thumbi, unconvinced, takes a box of smuggled packets from the guild's evidence locker (packets kept by smugglers, never returned) and unpacks them onto the roost floor. One petal of the shut bloom shivers open for a few seconds and closes again. The anomaly and the harmless test: the bloom answers to kept things being returned. Kaatravan forbids further experiments. The *strata* lens is taught on the packets' pollen: each grain shows one coat per fold and a season marker.

**Chapter 7 (A). Letters, the night of Day −7.** Ponni opens the bale. Letters are objects: pressed pollen, a toy key, and a kolam drawn in one unbroken line on each. The *trace* verb (following a kolam's single line through its dots) decodes their old private game. Voiced lines, never walls: an old letter about a gambler who panicked over a leaf of a man lying in an alley ("The shadow doesn't say dead, Ponni. It only says *there*."); and the last letter: "If I'm not there to meet you, you'll find me where the ribs open. Bring the bird. Wind it for me." Ponni reads it as grief and mysticism. She opens her first **assessment entry**: *Act: death of Minmini. Author: —. Owed: —.* She will not write "no one".

**Act 1 answers:** she was a proxy; the death is looped and therefore legally no one's; the bloom is shut. **New questions:** who looped it? Why is Ponni's own gift looped twenty-three years deep? Why did the bloom close?

### Act 2: "The Shadow Market" (Day −6 to the night of Day −5)

**Act hook (small, personal):** an assessor wants to see one room on one night.

**Chapter 8 (A). The re-lease, Day −6.** As executor, Ponni re-leases Minmini's last night, and the building **re-performs** it around her in real time, compressed at her request: the 19:30 gate chime; 22:00, the quiet lease seals the bedroom (doors lock, air stops, sound deadens); 04:00, an inspection hum from a **cassette slot that was not Minmini's**; 06:00, the vents roar. The Proprietor will say whose slot it is only to its holder. Ponni uses her office's power instead: the slot's lessee, the **Uppalam Salt Concern**, does business on Ilavenil, so its books can be compelled.

**Chapter 9 (D). The vent filters, Day −6.** Thumbi's brother brings her vent dust from the lease-quarters. Strata: Kurinji-bound pollen from a crossing **twenty-four years ago**, **unaged**. "It's been kept somewhere slow." Only the Kuzhi is slow. Kaatravan buries her report; he is in the Mawsim envoy's pay. The Mawsim deliver their **ultimatum** on the public board: restore the post by the Seal vote or the bonds are called. Thumbi takes her strata to the assessor, and threads A and D collide.

**Chapter 10 (A). The Shadow Market, Day −6.** Ponni compels Uppalam Salt's books on the trading floor while the market panics around her. Two teaching scenes run in the background as she works:
- a broker shrieks over a leaf showing a man prone in an alley; Ponni weighs it, walks to the alley at the hour, and finds a drunk asleep. **"The shadow doesn't say dead."**
- a broker accused of cheating faces a **shadow ordeal**; her rival has bought her ordeal cassette; her advocate invokes **the accused's priority**; she leases her own cassette with the standard term; the leaf comes up blank. The player learns that ordeals can be rigged by a lease term, and that the accused leases first.
Nedumaran, passing, compliments Ponni on a reading she took an hour ago (the publicity cost, felt). Thamarai, watching the bond screens: "Let it run. Panics find their floor." Uppalam Salt's books show that the bedroom cassette lease was bought on **Day −10**, the day after it lapsed at the folding, and was registered from an **Irai terminal on Ilavenil**: Ponni's own office. They also show a payment to the toymaker Paavai.

**Chapter 11 (B). Walking the shadow, Day −6.** At the folding Kayal inherited Minmini's leases, so she can re-lease Minmini's *routines*. The building lights the routes Minmini walked every day, and Kayal follows them (*fork-find*): the roost post office (daily; letters), the Irai office lease she lobbied into existence, the old-lease salerooms. She learns three of Minmini's habits and uses them to pass at an assembly reception, where the suspicion markers fall. Asked by Kayal why she does not mourn, Valli says: **"You can't stop a leaf. You let the leaf run."** (The decoy is planted.)

**Chapter 12 (C). Tailing Kuyil, the night of Day −6.** Kathir tails his father's runner Kuyil through clockwork-only alleys the Proprietor cannot see (*tail*). Kuyil's fan opens into a dead drop at Paavai's shop. In the plate-house, Kathir copies a page of his father's cassette leases: *Uppalam Salt: bedroom, Slow-quarter 4*, Minmini's room. He now knows. He cannot speak without confessing the Day −30 leak, so he sends a wind-up toy walking to Ponni's door with a slip: "Ask Paavai who ordered the second carrier."

**Chapter 13 (A). The toymaker, Day −5.** Ponni sends Selvan ahead to secure Paavai's shop while she gets a warrant. When she arrives, **Paavai is dead** at her bench, and a tin carrier walks in circles beside her until it runs down. Selvan: "I found her like this." The Balance shows looped: another licensed death. Paavai's order book, in clockwork cipher, opens with the key in her apron (a gear-train puzzle) and shows **two carrier orders**, one from "the heir of Paalai" (Day −12) and one from Uppalam Salt (Day −14), and **one delivery**, collected by "the Irai courier, for duty inspection". On the bench is a half-built **dead-man's box**, with Paavai's tag: *opens if unwound three days; burns if forced.*

**Chapter 14 (D and B). A death in the assembly, Day −5.** Seal-holder Aathan dies in his sleep. Thumbi learns his heart medicine came from Uppalam by post every ten days and stopped when the bloom closed: the closure is now killing people. The Seal vote is moved to the wedding morning. In the assembly (court scene), Kayal watches the factions count voting beads: with Paalai's bloc, Nedumaran wins. A clerk reads the **marital estate law** aloud during the succession procedure: "a spouse dying without issue leaves her deposits to the surviving spouse's house."

**Chapter 15 (B). The chest, Day −5.** The routine inspection film of Kayal's room, which is hers as lessee, shows a dense rectangle inside the lining of her wedding chest. She cuts it open: a sealed vault envelope bearing Paalai's vault seal and the mark "188". She does not open it. She hides it in her bridal clothes.

**Chapter 16 (A). The vault notes, the night of Day −5.** Ponni weighs a Paalai vault note bought on the market floor: **looped since about AS 189.** She remembers her own AS 203 letter about a looped Paalai filing. The playable memory is a single line from her younger self: "Your house's inventory is looped, isn't that funny?" A leaf reaches back a season. What reaches back twenty-three years?

**Act 2 answers:** the death was licensed by a cassette lease bought by a Neithal shell; the weapon was a gift toy swapped at delivery by "the Irai courier"; the pollen was kept in the slow vault; the closure is killing people. **New questions:** who in Ponni's office? What has Paalai been seeing since AS 189? What is in Kayal's envelope?

### Act 3: "The Long Leaf" (Day −4 to the night of Day −3)

**Act hook (small, personal):** a kolam that does not close.

**Chapter 17 (A). The midpoint, Day −4.** One kolam in the last letters has a deliberate gap. Traced with the gap closed, its line spells a **post box number** at the roost. Inside is a contact copy of an x-ray leaf. In a leased lightbox room, Ponni *fits* it: the ribs of the Kurinji's chamber, open; a faint lying figure; crisp **anklets with house bells**; a crisp **thali**; a **toy** in the right hand; **no ring** at the wrist; a small **disc** at the edge. The copy is soft and the details smear. Kayal arrives unannounced: she saw Ponni's vault-note reading on the board. She brings the envelope. It holds a **live late leaf**. Together, with the Balance (the envelope is looped, twenty-three years deep), they understand. **This is a long plate, the heir's body on her wedding night, and Minmini was grown to lie in it. With Minmini dead, it is Kayal.** Kayal lifts a cup with her left hand under, and Ponni has to leave the room: that habit came from her own letters.

**Chapter 18 (A). The clerk, Day −4.** Ponni closes the circle on Selvan with three independent pieces: Thumbi reads **AS 188 coats** in the seam dust of his courier satchel; Ponni puts the gate film's **solid disc** at 19:30 against his duty roster (he signed out a "gift inspection" for that hour); and the Uppalam lease was registered from **his** terminal. In the interrogation (a short scene in which the player chooses which evidence to lay down, in what order), Selvan breaks: a licensed death, Neithal's money, the toy swapped at Paavai's shop. His short early leaf, from his locker, shows a crisp skeleton in bed with a proxy ring and a toy carrier. He agrees to **sign a confession** before the assembly on Day −2, which would be the first signature on a looped act. Ponni leases her office's doors shut around him. It plays as a triumph. Her reading of Selvan appears on the public board within the hour.

**Chapter 19 (C). Too late, the night of Day −4.** From the public board, Kathir pieces together Kuyil to Selvan to his father. He sees Kuyil leasing the cassette of the **Irai office stair**. He sends a clockwork warning, and it walks too slowly.

**Chapter 20 (A). The stair, the night of Day −3.** Selvan steps out for a bloom-wine pipe, a habit seen twice before. The kolam line of Ponni's lease ends at the office door; the stair is outside it. Ponni finds him at the foot of the stair, neck broken, the Balance showing looped. His last words: **"She said let it run."** Ponni hears Valli. His dead-man's box sits on the desk upstairs, wound that morning.

**Chapter 21 (B). Valli, Day −3.** Kayal lays the copy in front of Valli. Valli confesses: the AS 189 leaf; the dodge by dilation; a granddaughter grown "to stand where the leaf said the heir would lie"; the planned sleeping draught; the ring to be cut. She gives Kayal the **original early leaf** from the Slow House strongbox. Then the knife: "She was assigned to your assessor in 190. Your pen-pal friend was an Irai asset we cultivated. She said it went cold in 196. I never believed her." Kayal: "She had a name." Valli: "I know. I chose not to use it."

**Chapter 22 (A, B, C). Three confessions, Day −3.** Kathir comes to Ponni and Kayal and confesses the Day −30 leak. With the estate law and Uppalam Salt, the motive is plain: Nedumaran removed the substitute so that fate would kill the bride and her vault would pass to Neithal. Kayal relays Valli's knife. Ponni re-reads the earliest letters as objects: the AS 190 to 195 letters are full of questions about Irai procedure. Then the AS 196 kolam, which she never decoded because it looked ordinary, traces to: **"I have stopped writing to you for them. This one is mine."** Ponni weighs it and finds it not looped. It is the most unremarkable reading in the game, and it is the one the player remembers.

**Act 3 answers:** the long leaf; the substitute; who killed Minmini and why; the friendship's origin and its turning. **New question, the engine for Act 4:** a shadow cannot be changed, only cast, so who will lie in the ribs on the wedding night?

### Act 4: "Where the Ribs Open" (Day −2 to the night of Day 0)

**Act hook (small, personal):** a bride must sign something in a vault.

**Chapter 23 (B). The Kuzhi, Day −2, 06:00.** The hoard can be released only by the heir, in person, inside the vault. Kayal goes in, explaining her absence as bridal seclusion. Inside, a clock shows outside time racing: **each vault minute costs fifty outside minutes**. The player chooses how long to stay: walking to the hoard and signing takes about thirty vault minutes, while optional rooms (Arumbu's kept room from her century of exile, the slow counting house) cost more outside hours. She signs. She comes out at 07:00 on Day −1. While she is inside, the other threads advance.

**Chapter 24 (A). The court order and the funeral, Day −2.** Kuyil has told Nedumaran that the assessor holds the body. Nedumaran obtains a **Seal-court order**: the proxy's body goes to Paalai for burning on Day 0 at 16:00. Ponni cannot block it. Kayal (before entering the vault) has left her the **original early leaf**. Ponni fits it at full sharpness, laying the actual parrot on the glass: the **blob on the key** matches. She lays her own seal on the glass: the **disc has a hole.** She weighs herself: **looped, twenty-three years deep.** **Frame-break:** the shadow is not a death, because a shadow doesn't say dead. It is Minmini's body, laid in the bride's anklets and thali, holding the parrot, with Ponni kneeling beside her. *Bring the bird. Wind it for me.* Minmini read it right in AS 204 and bet her death on her friend. Ponni plans backwards from the image (the *stage* verb): the body, the ring, the anklets, the thali, the open bloom, the late leaf, the cassette. She **leases the chamber's cassette slot for Day 0 at 23:00 in her own name, purpose: "to close a leaf"**. This is the committed choice. The player may refuse to lease, and the refusal path (see the ending) is fully played.

**Chapter 25 (D). The reopening, Day −1.** Kayal emerges and the hoard comes out of the vault in sealed bales. Thumbi, her brother and the laid-off unpackers open packet after packet onto the roost floor while the guild watches. As dusk falls, the Kurinji's ribs creak and open one by one and pollen-light rolls over the city; that night the first carriers fold in. The post resumes and the Mawsim stand down. Kaatravan expels Thumbi publicly for contradicting the guild; Nakhoda Saleha watches and says nothing. In the plate-house (C), Nedumaran learns the dowry's crown is gone, recalculates, and smiles: the Seal and the vault by fate remain.

**Chapter 26 (A). The ring, the night of Day −1.** In the cold room, with Kathir holding the lamp, Ponni cuts the steel ring out of her friend's wrist. It is played slowly and quietly, with no music. She washes the wrist. She winds the parrot and lets it walk across the steel table to test the repaired key.

**Chapter 27 (B). The vote and the wedding, Day 0.** At 09:00 in the assembly, Kayal must pass as the heir everyone knew while beads drop. Ponni, unsure of Valli because of "let it run", has not told her what Nedumaran did. Valli keeps the alliance's bargain, and **Nedumaran is elected Seal-holder.** At 11:00, the wedding: Kathir ties the thali on Kayal. Both perform perfectly for a court that has no idea.

**Chapter 28 (all). The pyre, Day 0, 16:00.** At the foot of the reopened bloom, Paalai burns its proxy under court order. Ponni has leased the funeral ground's light to dim at the moment of the first great carrier flight. As the sky fills with folding carriers and pollen-light and every face turns up, Ponni and Kathir swap the body on its bier for a pollen-packed bundle of the same weight. Kayal, who knows, lights the pyre and does not look back. (This is a stealth set piece: the player times the swap to the flight, watching Kuyil's eyeline.)

**Chapter 29 (all). The chamber, Day 0, 21:00 to 23:00.** Custom takes the newlyweds into the Kurinji's chamber, with Kuyil as Neithal's escort to the threshold. Inside, among the ribs, Ponni waits with the body. Kayal kneels, takes off the anklets with their bells, and **lays her thali on Minmini**: the laying-off rite, a wife's right to give her marriage to another. (The player, as Kayal, may instead use a duplicate thali and stay married; both fit the shadow. Dissolving the marriage ends the alliance; keeping it holds the houses together under a hollow merger without long plates.) Kathir lays Minmini in the ribs. Ponni puts the parrot in her right hand, winds it, and lets it walk to a stop in the palm. Kayal and Kathir leave by the far ribs. Ponni kneels at the edge with her seal on its cord. At 23:00 the inspection hum fills the chamber, the late leaf in the cassette darkens under Ponni's lease, and **the loop closes, signed.**

**Chapter 30 (B). The Slow House, Day 0, 23:20 to 23:40.** Kayal comes home alive and tells Valli everything, including that Neithal killed Minmini to make Kayal die. At 23:40 Nedumaran arrives with Kuyil to "bring the bride to her husband's house". Valli rises from the desk with the clockwork pistol and shoots the new Seal-holder. Kuyil is held by the guard.

**Act 4 answers:** the funeral; the casting; the bloom reopened; the killer dead. **New question:** what does a signature cost?

### Act 5: "Signed" (Day +1 to Day +10, and the epilogue)

**Act hook (small, personal):** a single entry in a ledger.

**Chapter 31 (A). Mine, Day +1.** Ponni completes her entry: *Act: closure of the long leaf. Author:* she crosses out the printed "no one" and stamps **Mine**. The Proprietor: "Leased. Entered. Thank you." The board's suspense count falls by one. Ponni deposits the **Ordeal Lease**, found among Minmini's old leases and so far understood only as an antique, with the Proprietor, which by Charter will show it to no one but her. Kayal and Kathir burn Minmini properly. Kayal sifts the ashes for the **scorched parrot**. Ponni returns the exposed late leaf to the roost floor, and one more rib of the Kurinji opens.

**Chapter 32 (C, D). The markets, Day +2 to +3.** The Seal-court rules that the Doctrine's premise ("no one can be shown to have closed it") is false. The Doctrine is void going forward and for ten years back. The Shadow Market crashes, Neithal owes a decade of duty, and Kathir, now its head, pays first and in public. Thamarai resigns as Commissioner to stand for the Seal, and on Day +3, with the Mawsim's support, **Thamarai is elected Seal-holder.** Thumbi, stripped of her guild licence, stands on the roost at dawn, and the first carriers land at her feet before anyone else's (unexplained).

**Chapter 33 (A). Let it run, Day +4 to +5.** The new Commissioner charges Ponni with desecrating a proxy mark, taking a body from rites and defying a court order, and seeks a shadow ordeal. The Irai files a lease on the ordeal cassette. On Day +5 Ponni returns to her sealed office for her papers and finds **Selvan's dead-man's box open**, its spring run down since Day 0. His courier log: Day −14, *"Reported Neithal licence, target Paalai proxy. C. replied: let it run."* The red herring falls: "she" was never Valli. As the accused, Ponni re-leases the cassette's history and reads the Irai's term, *"expose the late leaf at the hour of judgment, without condition"*, word for word the term of the AS 0 Ordeal Lease that Minmini bought. **Second frame-break:** the first Seal was rigged by the assessors' guild, Ponni's own office, and the Doctrine was written so that no one could ever be shown to have done it. Ponni goes to Thamarai, and it is a quiet scene. Thamarai does not deny it: "Someone had to let it run. You signed. I only counted."

**Chapter 34 (A). The ordeal, Day +6.** In the assembly's ordeal chamber, under the accused's priority, the player chooses:
- **lease first with the stricter term** (expose without condition): Ponni convicts herself by her own hand. This is the main path;
- lease first with the standard term, leaving it to the leaf, which the Irai can no longer rig (the result falls as the game's sealed coin falls: guilty);
- let the Irai's lease stand: guilty, unsigned;
- refuse the ordeal: sent to the slow by default.
All four end in the same sentence, **forty years in the slow**. They differ in what the outside world believes and in the epilogue letters. Ponni develops her early leaf in front of the court: her skeleton, and on its cord, her seal with the hole.

**Chapter 35 (B, D). Partings, Day +7 to +10.** Valli refuses an ordeal and takes sixty years in the slow for Nedumaran's death. Kayal petitions the assembly to register Minmini posthumously as a person, the first proxy ever so recognised. At the vault shuttle, Kayal tells Ponni: "I'll write." Ponni: "You'll be old when I read them." Kayal: "Then I'll write a lot." Kayal signs her first letter with Ponni's own old sign-off: *assessed and found sufficient*.

**Epilogue. The Kuzhi.** Weeks of Ponni's time pass. Bales of letters arrive, each a year of Kayal's life. One week a small box comes: the scorched parrot, its solder blob intact. Ponni writes her first letter out. **The small choice:** whether to enclose the instruction that tells the Proprietor to release the Ordeal Lease to Kayal. The following weeks' bales show the consequences in Kayal's voice: riots and a new assembly, or a long prosperous peace and a silence where Ponni's name should be. The player may also write nothing, and then the letters keep coming, shorter. **Final image:** the parrot, wound, walks a few steps across the desk past a stack of a year's letters that arrived in a week, and stops.
