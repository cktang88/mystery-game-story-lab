# The Unsigned: outline (revised)

## One sentence

Player-facing: *A grieving tax assessor must cast a foreseen death, and decide whether to sign for it.*
Internal pitch line, which gives away the midpoint: *When the future arrives as an x-ray, you survive your death by growing someone to fit it.*

## One paragraph

Ponni, an assessor of the Five Hundred's revenue office, crosses four light-years to meet the pen pal she has written to for twenty-two years. She lands on Ilavenil, the AGI-owned pleasure world, a day after her friend Minmini was found dead. The death was foreseen on a future plate and the plate was registered as a *vidai*, a licence of foreseen loss, so by law it is nobody's crime. Minmini was a proxy: a clone, legally property, of a bride who has just come back young from a time-dilation vault to marry the heir of the rival firm. **First disaster:** the weapon was pollen the vault had hoarded for decades. When it was released, Ilavenil's ancient bloom closed, a second bloom-world closed in sympathy, the faster-than-light post that binds forty-one worlds stopped, and the confederation slid toward default to the slow-ship creditors in orbit. **Second disaster, caused by Ponni's own investigation:** every reading she takes is published, so the toymaker and then her own clerk, who was the hand, are murdered before they can testify. What she uncovers is worse than murder. Twenty-four years ago, the bride's grandmother saw a blurred x-ray of "the heir" lying in the bloom's chamber on her wedding night and grew Minmini to fill it. Minmini was killed to make the real bride fill it instead, so that the groom's firm would inherit. The shadow cannot be changed, only cast. Ponni finds her own soldered toy and her own seal in the old image and understands that it was never a death: it is Minmini's funeral, and Ponni will lay it out. With the bride and the groom, she takes her friend's body from its pyre, cuts out the clone's ring, and lays her in the bride's anklets and the groom's thali, the parrot in her hand. The bride lives, the merger dies, and the murderer is shot by the grandmother he deceived. **Third disaster, caused by Ponni's fix:** she signs the closure, the first person ever to do so. That gives the courts standing to strike down the law that made Ilavenil a haven and licensed the killing of proxies, and it lifts to power the former commissioner who let Minmini die. Charged with her friends, Ponni goes into the vault to prove that the confederation's very first ordeal was rigged by her own office's founders. Then she writes the term of her own ordeal, convicting herself alone so that the bride and groom go free. Forty years in the slow, she begins writing to the bride.

**Start together:** all four viewpoints, both chairs, the commissioner, the clerk and the dying Seal-holder are at the pyre in chapter 2. Only Ponni arrives from elsewhere, which is the one allowed exception.

**The ledger board,** the Irai's public board, is shown at every chapter break. It lists the current question, each thread's want, the days to the wedding, the coupon date, the Seal bead count (from Act 2), the Crossing's state (open or closed, by world) and the suspense count.

## Full outline

### Prologue: Day −8, 04:00

**Question:** what are we looking at?

1. An x-ray sweep of a city at night: the Proprietor's 04:00 inspection of private rooms, shown on its ordinary safety film. The player pans across bone and metal: sleepers with anklet bells; two thalis touching; a gambler asleep on his coins; the mineral ribs of a vast closed flower.
2. The beam settles on a bedroom: a woman lying still, a steel ring at her wrist, a toy carrier stopped beside her. **The player cannot tell whether she is asleep.**
3. In the wall, a cassette window glows briefly as a strip of film darkens. Title: **The Unsigned.**

### Act 1: "Nobody's" (Day −7)

**Hook:** a woman goes to meet her pen pal. **New in this act:** lease, weigh, the proxy fact.

**Ch 1 (A). Dock.** Ponni's slow ship docks after four outside years. The roost is idle; the bloom is shut; unpackers sit on empty bins. A postmistress hands her the bale held for her: four years of Minmini's letters, the last dated Day −10. At each private threshold on the way, a kolam line of light blocks her until she **leases** passage (taught at no stakes). At the gate: mourners.

**Ch 2 (all). The pyre.** At the foot of the shut Kaanthal, Paalai is about to burn a body. The officiant's roll states every relationship aloud: Valli, chair of Paalai; Kayal, "controlling holder, returned from the slow"; Kathir, chair-designate of Neithal, her betrothed; Nedumaran, his father; Commissioner Thamarai, on Ilavenil for the debt talks; Selvan, the Irai's clerk; Thumbi of the Roost Sangam; old Seal-holder Aathan. The Proprietor's voice: "Act of no one. Entered in suspense." Ponni sees Minmini's face for the first time, and in the lens, the **steel ring** under the wrist skin. Valli: "Property of the house." Ponni turns that against her: a proxy's death passes property to its original, so the Irai must assess folding duty, and nothing under assessment may be destroyed. She stamps the shroud **UNDER ASSESSMENT**. The pyre stops and the body goes to the Irai cold room in her custody. Two small things pass almost unnoticed: Thamarai tells Ponni warmly that she approved Ponni's cadet pen-pal letters herself, in 190; Aathan's aide frets that the Seal-holder's heart medicine is due on the next post from Uppalam; and as the crowd breaks up, Thamarai, answering a Mawsim purser's question about the bond rumours, says lightly: "Let it run."

**Ch 3 (A). The rooms.** The Proprietor grants Ponni an assessor's custody lease of Minmini's rooms. The player inventories: twenty-two years of Ponni's own letters; a shelf of toys; a dozen clockwork boxes of **old leases** (legally Kayal's, as everything a proxy acquires is), which Ponni lists without opening, including one dated AS 0 and noted as "antique"; on the bedside, a run-down toy carrier; on its own cushion, the **tin parrot** Ponni sent in AS 198, its key fat with ugly solder. The **Balance** is taught. On the body: looped, about two days deep. On the carrier: looped. On the parrot: **looped, twenty-four years deep.** Within a minute the readings are on the public board (the cost). The gate's quarter-hour safety film of 19:30 on the night of Day −9 shows a visitor whose only crisp metal is a **solid disc**. Selvan wears one, as do all Irai couriers. Ponni taps her own seal: "Clerks get the solid ones; we get the hole." Selvan winds a clockwork box on his desk each morning and steps out to the stair for a bloom-wine pipe.

**Ch 4 (B). The Slow House.** Kayal is twenty, and the court treats her as a woman it has known for twenty years. At a mourning reception she lifts a cup right-handed and a delegate's eyebrow rises. On Valli's desk sits an antique **clockwork pistol**. A short playable memory: **the folding** on Day −11, in a white room. Minmini hands over the anklets and the controlling seal, shows Kayal a cup held **left hand under** ("You'll need it"), and smiles at the toy Kayal says she has ordered for her. In the present, Kayal hears that the bedside carrier was "your gift". Valli: "A placeholder. Placeholders don't need names."

**Ch 5 (C). The plate-house.** Kathir hides his grief among his father's lightboxes. A wordless memory from **Day −30**: Kathir pleads with a back-lit silhouette in a lightbox room ("They'll put her in Kayal's place. Stop it, or I'll stop the wedding"), and the silhouette **nods**. Its shape could belong to Valli, Nedumaran or Thamarai, all of whom visit lightbox rooms. In his locket is a carried-film strip, the x-ray of his hand in hers. He begins to watch his own firm.

**Ch 6 (D). The idle roost.** Thumbi's brother has been laid off with the other unpackers. Kaatravan, the Sangam's chief reader, has not yet spoken. High over the shut bloom, a few carriers circle and fold away without landing. The unpackers call them "empty flights", as people have for two centuries. There is no verb here: it is a plant.

**Ch 7 (A). Letters.** Ponni opens the bale that night. The letters are objects: pressed pollen, a toy key, a kolam on every page. Two voiced lines: an old letter about a gambler who panicked over a leaf of a man lying in an alley ("The shadow doesn't say dead, Ponni. It only says *there*."), and the last letter: "If I'm not there to meet you, you'll find me where the ribs open. Bring the bird. Wind it for me." She opens her first **ledger entry**: *Act: death of Minmini. Author: —.* She will not write "no one".

**Answers:** she was a proxy; the death is legally no one's; the bloom is shut. **New questions:** who looped it? Why is Ponni's gift looped twenty-four years deep? Why did the bloom close?

### Act 2: "The Shadow Market" (Day −6 to Day −4)

**Hook:** an unpacker's brother brings his sister a bag of dust. **New in this act, one per chapter:** strata, re-lease and the vidai, fit, mimic, tail, trace, trade beads, the Crossing, the wedding custom, the ordeal, shielding.

**Ch 8 (D). Day −6, dawn: the petal test.** Thumbi takes a box of smuggled packets from the Sangam's evidence locker (packets kept, never returned) and unpacks them on the roost floor. One petal of the shut bloom shivers open and closes. The bloom's law shows itself: **kept things returned**. The **strata** lens is taught: each grain has a coat per fold and a season marker. Within the hour her brother brings vent dust from the lease-quarters, and it pays off: Kaanthal-bound pollen from a crossing **twenty-four years ago, unaged**. "It's been kept somewhere slow." Kaatravan, paid by Saleha's purser, declares on the board that the bloom has refused the alliance. The Mawsim post their **ultimatum**: the coupon is due on Day −3, paid by post from Thinnai; miss it and the bonds are called. Thumbi takes her dust to the assessor (A and D collide).

**Ch 9 (A). Day −6: the building re-performs the night.** With custody, Ponni **re-leases** Minmini's last night. Around her, the building re-performs it: the 19:30 gate chime; 22:00, the quiet lease seals the bedroom (doors lock, air stops); 04:00, a hum from a cassette slot **not leased by Minmini**; 06:00, the vents roar. Thumbi's 07:30 bloom closing sits on the same timeline. Under the Irai's power to compel the books of any firm trading on Ilavenil, Ponni opens the slot's lessee, **Uppalam Salt Concern**. It holds a cassette lease for "Day −8, 04:00 only", bought on Day −10 from an **Irai terminal on Ilavenil**, and a **vidai** registered on Day −10: *foreseen loss of property, one proxy, fee paid*. The statute is taught plainly. Proxies are property; a foreseen loss of property is nobody's; free persons are never covered. In law, Minmini's death is a licensed loss. Ponni stamps nothing that day.

**Ch 10 (A). Day −6, afternoon: the alley.** On the Shadow Market's floor, a broker waves a leaf at her: a man prone in an alley, coins at his hip. "He's dead tomorrow, and he owes me." The **fit** verb is taught: Ponni lays the leaf over a live view of the alley and aligns coin to coin and drainpipe to drainpipe. At the hour, she finds the debtor asleep in spilled bloom-wine. Minmini's voice: "It only says *there*." Nedumaran, passing, compliments her on a reading she took that morning.

**Ch 11 (B). Day −6: the board.** Kayal must chair her first Paalai board meeting as controlling holder. Before it, she **re-leases Minmini's routines** (Minmini's leases passed to her at the folding) and walks the lit paths her proxy walked daily: the roost post office, the Irai office she lobbied into being, the old-lease salerooms. She **mimics** three habits. At the board, the suspicion markers fall as she performs them. The balance sheet is pinned up: berth-notes, the slow rate, "inert stock", a line nobody explains. Afterwards Valli, asked why she does not mourn: **"You can't stop a leaf. You let the leaf run."** (The decoy is planted.)

**Ch 12 (C). Night of Day −6: tailing Kuyil.** Kathir **tails** Neithal's runner Kuyil through clockwork-only alleys the Proprietor cannot see. Kuyil's fan opens into a dead drop at Paavai's shop. Back in the plate-house, Kathir copies a page of his father's leases: *Uppalam Salt: bedroom, Slow-quarter 4*. Neithal owns the shell. He cannot speak without confessing the Day −30 plea, so he sends a wind-up toy walking to Ponni's door with a slip: "Ask Paavai who ordered the second carrier." From across the street he sees a figure at Ponni's lit desk reading it. Ponni is asleep upstairs.

**Ch 13 (A). Day −5, morning: the toymaker.** At breakfast Ponni idly **traces** a kolam from an old letter, their private game, and the verb is taught. At Paavai's shop she finds **Paavai dead** at her bench, a tin carrier walking circles by her hand until it stops. The Balance shows looped, but Paavai was a free woman, so no vidai covers her: **this is murder**, and the player enters it as such. The order book's gear cipher, traced, gives **two carriers ordered** (the heir of Paalai on Day −12; Uppalam Salt on Day −14) and **one delivery**, collected by "the Irai courier, for duty inspection". On the bench, a half-built dead-man's box is tagged: *opens if unwound three days; burns if forced.*

**Ch 14 (B). Day −5: the Seal.** Seal-holder Aathan dies; his post-borne medicine never came. The vote is set for the wedding morning. That evening **Thamarai resigns** as Commissioner to stand, with Mawsim money and Kurinji's shipyards behind her, and the bead count appears on the board. In the round hall, **trade beads** is taught. Mullai's delegate will move her house's beads for a berth for her dying mother; Marutham wants the vidai kept (its clinics sell proxies); the proxies' bench wants nothing anyone has offered. During the succession procedure, a clerk reads the **marital estate law** aloud: a spouse who dies without issue leaves her holdings to the surviving spouse's house.

**Ch 15 (D). Day −5, evening: the envoy.** An "empty flight" lands on the closed roost for the first time anyone remembers. Thumbi reads its pollen: Thinnai's bloom, coated closed. Thinnai has shut **in sympathy**. The flights were never empty. They are envoys, and the blooms are deliberating as a body: **the Crossing**. The board's Crossing panel shows two worlds dark. The Irai's coupon cannot be posted from Thinnai.

**Ch 16 (C). Night of Day −5: a procession.** Another couple's wedding night. Kathir watches from Neithal's florist's shop, where his firm supplies chamber garlands, as the groom's house always does. The groom's house escorts the couple to the threshold, the chamber hums at 23:00, and at dawn the couple emerge laughing. Every rite of Day 0 has now been shown once.

**Ch 17 (A). Day −4: an ordeal.** On the market floor a broker accused of fraud faces a **shadow ordeal**. The urn of beads, the cassette, the hour. Her rival has leased her cassette; her advocate invokes **the accused's priority**; she leases her own with the standard term; the leaf comes up blank, and the urn is burned unopened. Her clerk, co-accused, grumbles that if she had taken the stricter term and lost, he would have walked free as her instrument (the **sole-author rule**, heard once in passing). Thamarai, campaigning among the brokers, says of the bond panic: "Let it run. Panics find their floor."

**Ch 18 (B). Day −4: the chest.** Her room's 04:00 safety film, which is hers as lessee, shows a **dense rectangle** in the lining of her sealed bridal chest. She breaks custom and opens it: a lead sheath stamped *Paalai vault, 188-1, late, live*, and an engraved warning: *unsheathe only in a cassette*. **Shielding** is taught. She keeps it sheathed.

**Ch 19 (A). Day −4, evening: the note.** Ponni weighs a Paalai berth-note: **looped since about AS 188.** A one-line memory of her own AS 203 letter ("Your house's inventory is looped, isn't that funny?"). A leaf reaches back a season. What reaches back twenty-four years?

**Answers:** the death was a registered vidai bought by a Neithal shell from an Irai terminal; the weapon was a swapped gift; the pollen was kept in the vault; the Crossing is a polity. **New questions:** who in Ponni's office? What has Paalai seen since AS 188? What is in the sheath?

### Act 3: "The Long Leaf" (night of Day −4 to night of Day −3)

**Hook:** a kolam that does not close.

**Ch 20 (A, B). Midpoint.** One kolam in the last letters has a deliberate gap. Traced with the gap closed, it gives a roost **post box** number. Inside is a contact copy of an x-ray leaf. In a leased lightbox room Ponni **fits** it over a live view of the chamber: the open ribs; a faint lying figure; crisp anklet bells; a thali; a toy in the right hand; no ring; a small disc at the edge. The copy is soft and the small details smear. The door opens: Kayal, who saw the berth-note reading on the board. She holds out the sheath. Ponni weighs it unopened: looped, twenty-four years. **A long plate: Paalai's heir lying in the ribs on her wedding night. Minmini was grown to lie there. With Minmini dead, it is Kayal.** Kayal lifts a cup of water **left hand under**, and Ponni has to leave the room: that hold came from her own letters.

**Ch 21 (D). Day −3, morning: the flagship.** The coupon is missed. Thumbi is summoned aboard the *Kaaru*, to the council of nakhodas, a foreign court of captains on a moving deck under monsoon charts, voting by astrolabe tokens to call the bonds. She lays out her strata (unaged pollen; Thinnai's sympathetic closure) against Kaatravan's omen. Saleha, who bought that omen, turns in front of her own council: a false reading will cost the Mawsim the debt. She moves to **defer the call** to the Seal vote, provided the Crossing readmits Ilavenil by its **third envoy round**, which Thumbi reckons as dusk on Day −1. The motion carries by four tokens.

**Ch 22 (A). Day −3: the clerk.** Ponni closes on Selvan with three proofs: Thumbi reads **AS 188 coats** in the seam dust of his courier satchel; his roster shows a "gift inspection" at 19:30 on the night of Day −9, matching the gate film's solid disc; and the Uppalam lease came from **his** terminal. In the interrogation, the player chooses which proof to lay down and when. Selvan breaks: Neithal's money, the vidai, the swap. His licence leaf, from his locker, shows a crisp skeleton in bed with a proxy ring and a toy carrier. He agrees to file a **purpose of closure** for it at the assembly's session on Day −1. It would be the first signature in the Five Hundred's history and would give the courts **standing** to hear a challenge to the Doctrine. Ponni leases her office doors shut around him. It plays as a triumph.

**Ch 23 (B). Day −3, afternoon: Valli.** Kayal lays the copy in front of Valli. Valli confesses: the AS 188 leaf; the dodge by dilation; a granddaughter grown "to stand where the leaf said the heir would lie"; the planned draught; the ring to be cut; the chest chosen because no one may open a bridal chest. She gives Kayal the **original early leaf** from the strongbox. Then the knife: "She was assigned to your assessor in 190. The Irai's own scheme. She said the asset went cold in 196. I never believed her."

**Ch 24 (C, A, B). Day −3, evening: confessions.** Kathir, who has seen Kuyil lease the cassette of the **Irai office stair**, comes to warn Ponni and finds Kayal with her. He confesses the Day −30 plea, and **the silhouette that nodded was his father**. With the estate law, the shell and Selvan's statement, the motive is plain: Nedumaran removed the substitute so that fate would put Kayal in the ribs and her holdings would pass to Neithal. Kayal relays Valli's knife. Ponni re-reads the earliest letters as objects (AS 190 to 195 are full of questions about the Irai). She traces the AS 196 kolam, which she never decoded because it looked like decoration: **"I have stopped writing to you for them. This one is mine."** The Balance shows it not looped. It is the flattest reading in the game.

**Ch 25 (A). Night of Day −3: the stair.** Selvan steps out for his pipe. The kolam line of Ponni's lease ends at the office door; the stair lies outside it. She finds him at its foot, alive for a few seconds: **"She said let it run."** Ponni: "Valli?" He does not answer. This is murder (Selvan had standing), and Ponni enters it against Kuyil. Upstairs, Selvan's dead-man's box sits on his desk, wound that morning.

**Ch 26 (A). Late night of Day −3: the frame-break.** The original early leaf is sharper than the copy. Ponni lays the real parrot on the glass, key over key, and **the blob fits the blob**. She lifts her seal on its cord and lays it on the disc at the image's edge: **hole over hole.** She weighs her own hand. The shallow loops she has touched (the alley leaf, Selvan's licence) are already entered in her ledger, so the needle passes them and settles deep: looped, twenty-four years (sealed; not published). Minmini's voice: "Bring the bird. Wind it for me." The player enters it: *Body: Minmini. Laid out by: Ponni.* Only then does Ponni say aloud: "It was always her." It is a funeral. Minmini read it right in AS 204 and bet her death on her friend.

**Answers:** the long leaf; the substitute; who killed Minmini and why; the friendship's origin and its turn; the shadow's meaning. **New question:** a shadow cannot be changed, only cast, and it can be cast more than one way. How will they fill it, and who will sign?

### Act 4: "Where the Ribs Open" (Day −2 to the night of Day 0)

**Hook:** a twenty-year-old must sign a release inside a vault.

**Ch 27 (B). Day −2, 06:00: the Kuzhi.** Only the controlling holder, in person, can release the "inert stock". Kayal goes in under cover of bridal seclusion. Two clocks on every wall, and **each vault minute costs fifty outside minutes**. The direct route takes thirty vault minutes. Optional rooms (Arumbu's sealed door, the slow counting house) cost outside hours. She signs the release. She will come out at 07:00 on Day −1. Every other thread moves while she is inside.

**Ch 28 (A, C). Day −2: the order.** Nedumaran and Valli jointly obtain a **Seal-court order** releasing the body to Paalai for burning on Day 0 at 16:00. Nedumaran wants the weapon's pollen, which traces to the dowry, gone; Valli wants the hoard's evidence gone. Neither knows what Ponni intends. Kathir, copying his father's orders, finds the florist's ticket for Day 0's chamber garlands: **"steeped"**. Ponni prepares for both ways of filling the shadow and cannot ask the bride.

**Ch 29 (A, B). Day −1, 07:00: committed choice 1.** Kayal comes out a day older to the world and minutes older herself. First she does something that is hers to do: as legal owner of everything her proxy acquired, she **gives Ponni Minmini's archive**, Ponni's own letters and the old leases (including the AS 0 "antique"). "She'd want you to have the letters. The rest is worse in my hands." Then the three of them (Ponni, Kayal, Kathir) choose **how to fill the shadow**:
- **cast Minmini's body** (canon);
- **Kayal lies there alive**, which needs the garland swap and Kuyil held at the threshold;
- **try to dodge it**, hiding Kayal.
Each is played (see Paths). The board shows nothing; this choice is private.

**Ch 30 (D). Day −1: the third round.** The hoard comes out of the vault in sealed bales. Thumbi, her brother and the Sangam's unpackers open them on the roost floor while the readers' bench watches. At dusk the third envoy round folds in; their coats turn from closed to readmitting, and the Kaanthal's ribs creak open one by one. Pollen-light rolls down the terraces. The Crossing posts its sanction in envoy strata: **Ilavenil's route share is cut by a third for a generation**. The post resumes, at a higher price. The Mawsim stand down. Kaatravan has Thumbi **struck from the readers' bench** for contradicting him. Saleha says nothing and sends Thumbi a brass astrolabe token.

**Ch 31 (A). Night of Day −1: the ring** (cast path). In the cold room, with Kathir holding the lamp, Ponni cuts the steel ring from her friend's wrist. It is slow and quiet, with no music. She washes the wrist. She winds the parrot and lets it walk across the steel to test the key.

**Ch 32 (B). Day 0, 09:00 and 11:00: the vote and the wedding.** Kayal must pass as the heir everyone knew while beads drop. **Trade beads**: she can keep Paalai's bloc with the merger (canon, so as not to alert Neithal before the night) or move it, which wins votes but warns Nedumaran: Kuyil doubles the watch on the pyre and the chamber, and the later stealth beats get harder. Valli, untold (Ponni still suspects her because of "let it run"), votes the bargain. Canon: **Nedumaran is elected Seal-holder.** At 11:00, the wedding. Kathir ties the thali on Kayal; both perform flawlessly.

**Ch 33 (D). Day 0, 11:00: the box.** Since her expulsion, Thumbi has worked in the Irai office's strata lab. At the stroke of eleven, Selvan's dead-man's box, unwound for three days, springs open on the desk beside her. Inside is a courier log in his hand. *Day −14. Reported Neithal vidai, target Paalai proxy. C. replied: let it run.* She knows the phrase; she has heard Thamarai say it twice. She runs.

**Ch 34 (C). Day 0, 14:00: the garlands.** Kathir **tails** the florist's runner to the chamber's anteroom and swaps the steeped garlands for clean ones before Kuyil's inspection. On the cast path, failing only makes Kayal ill on the way out. On the live path, failing kills her.

**Ch 35 (all). Day 0, 16:00: the pyre.** It is the scene of chapter 2 again, now under an open bloom, with the whole court watching a proxy burned by court order. Ponni has leased the funeral ground's light to dim on the first great carrier flight. As every face turns up into pollen-light, Ponni and Kathir swap the body for a pollen bundle of equal weight, timing it to Kuyil's eyeline. Kayal lights the pyre and does not look back. In the crowd, Thumbi presses the courier log into Ponni's hand. **Ponni now knows Thamarai let it run**, and knows that a signature tonight will hand Thamarai what she wanted.

**Ch 36 (B). Day 0, 19:00: the lease.** Kayal, as Paalai's controlling holder, holds the chamber cassette Valli leased in AS 207. She **assigns it to Ponni**, with the purpose field empty. "Sign it or don't. It's yours."

**Ch 37 (all). Day 0, 21:00 to 23:00: the chamber.** Kuyil escorts the couple to the threshold, as the groom's house always does, and turns his back as custom requires. Inside, among the ribs, Ponni waits with the body. **As Kayal,** the player kneels and closes her own anklets, bells muffled in her palm, on Minmini's ankles: a gift to the woman who wore them for twenty-three years. **As Kathir,** the player ties a thali on Minmini, marrying the woman he loved, so that she is laid out as a married woman and honoured as a sumangali. He lays her in the ribs where the fitting lens shows her outline. Kayal and Kathir leave by the far ribs at 21:40. **As Ponni,** the player puts the parrot in her right hand and winds it; it walks two steps across the dead palm and stops against the thumb. At 22:55 comes the tense beat: she must **unsheathe** the live leaf and get it into the cassette in the chamber's dark, never in the beam's path, with the hum warming. **Committed choice 2:** the purpose field. Canon: she writes *to close a leaf* and signs **Ponni, Assessor**. She kneels at the edge with her seal on its cord. In the fitting lens the old shadow and the room align: bell to bell, blob to blob, hole to hole. At 23:00 the hum swells and fades, and **the loop closes, signed.**

**Ch 38 (B). Day 0, 23:20 to 23:40: the pistol.** Kayal comes home and tells Valli everything, including who killed Minmini and why. At 23:40 Nedumaran, Seal-holder for fourteen hours, arrives with Kuyil to "bring the bride to her husband's house", expecting to find a household in mourning. Valli lifts the clockwork pistol from the desk where it has sat since Act 1, winds it (long enough for everyone in the room to understand), and fires. Kuyil is taken by the guard.

**Answers:** the funeral; the casting; the reopening; the killer dead; the hidden hand named. **New question:** what does a signature cost, and who else will it buy?

### Act 5: "Signed" (Day +1 to Day +12, and the epilogue)

**Hook:** a single ledger entry. **This act has its own investigation:** the founding crime.

**Ch 39 (A). Day +1: Mine.** Ponni completes her entry. Against *Author*, she crosses out the printed **no one** and stamps **Mine**. Her sealed self-reading publishes with it. The Proprietor: "Leased. Entered. Thank you." The suspense count falls to 4,116. She deposits the old leases with the Proprietor, which will show them only to her. Kayal and Kathir burn Minmini properly, and Kayal sifts the ashes for the **scorched parrot**. Kathir declares before the assembly that he tied a thali on another woman last night, so his morning marriage is void ("one thali, one wife"), and the merger lapses. Kayal files a petition to register Minmini as a person. That evening Thamarai finds Ponni on the roost: "You signed." "You let it run." Neither raises their voice.

**Ch 40 (C, B). Day +2: the ruling.** With Ponni as the first party with standing, the Seal-court hears the challenge and rules the Doctrine's premise false. **The Doctrine and the vidai are void**, and duty is reopened for ten years. **Kuyil is charged** with the murders of Paavai and Selvan. The Shadow Market crashes. Neithal owes a decade of duty, and Kathir, now its chair, pays first. Berth-notes fall on the hoard scandal and Valli's arrest. At Paalai's board, Kayal keeps control by agreeing to publish berth prices. The Seal is vacant; the re-vote is set for Day +10.

**Ch 41 (A). Day +3: the charge.** Acting Commissioner Iniyan charges **Ponni, Kayal and Kathir** with taking a body from rites in defiance of a court order, and Ponni also with removing a proxy mark. The ordeal is set for Day +10, after the re-vote. The Irai leases the ordeal cassette with an **unconditional** term.

**Ch 42 (A). Day +4: the antique.** As holder of the AS 0 lease, Ponni re-leases the ordeal chamber's hour of judgment two hundred and twelve years ago, and the building re-performs it: the hum at the hour, the urn's plinth, a door opened and closed. The **hall safety film** of that hour, which belongs to the same lease, shows a woman leaving with a clay **urn** under her shawl, its beads crisp on x-ray: **silver, the majority.** Ponni overlays the Irai's new term on the AS 0 term (*expose without condition*): word for word. **Second frame-break: the first Seal was stolen by the assessors' guild, Ponni's own office, and the Doctrine was written so that no one could be shown to have done it.**

**Ch 43 (A). Day +5 to Day +9: Arumbu's room.** The film proves the count; the court will want the urn. With Kayal's leave, Ponni enters the Kuzhi to find Arumbu's sealed room. She stays two vault hours, about four outside days, and the outside clock races as she works. Inside: a desk, a century's dust laid down in two of Arumbu's years, a child's drawing of a flower, **the urn**, and Arumbu's diary, which knew everything and could prove none of it. Ponni comes out at dawn on Day +10.

**Ch 44 (B). Day +5 to Day +9: the bead war.** Without Ponni, Kayal works the re-vote: **Thamarai** (the Irai's houses, Kurinji, Mawsim money) against **Kathir** as the compromise. She can win Mullai (the berth for the delegate's mother), Marutham (whose clinics lost the vidai and want proxy contracts regulated, not ended), Kurinji (fleet contracts) and the proxies' bench (Minmini's name). Thamarai visits her with an offer: Paalai's beads in exchange for a **Seal decree registering Minmini as a person** on the first day of her reign. Kayal must choose between the woman who let Minmini die and the only guaranteed path to Minmini's personhood.

**Ch 45 (D). Day +6: the Crossing.** At dawn the season's first carriers fold in over the roost and land at Thumbi's feet before anyone else's (never explained). The envoy strata she reads now are an atlas: forty-one worlds, open, closed and readmitting, a polity talking to itself. Saleha asks her to come with the fleet as its reader. Thumbi answers later, in the epilogue letters.

**Ch 46 (all). Day +10: the re-vote and the ordeal.** Ponni walks from the shuttle into the round hall with the urn under her shawl, as Arumbu did. **Choice:** bring the urn and the film before the re-vote, or hold them. Canon: she holds them, with the Mawsim fleet in orbit and the bonds barely saved, rather than bring down every Seal since AS 0 in one morning. Kayal takes Thamarai's offer, and **Thamarai is elected Seal-holder.** In the afternoon comes the **ordeal**. Under the accused's priority the player chooses the term:
- **stricter** (expose without condition), canon: Ponni convicts herself as **sole author**, and Kayal and Kathir are discharged as her instruments;
- **standard**: the urn decides, as the player's bead play and revelations have made it;
- let the Irai's lease stand;
- or refuse.
In canon Ponni writes her term in front of the court and develops her early leaf by her own hand. The image rises in the tray: her skeleton, crisp, and on its cord, her seal with the hole. **Guilty.** Twenty years for her own charge, plus the two accomplice terms of ten she has absorbed: **forty years in the slow.** Thamarai, presiding, signs the first decree of her reign the same hour: *Minmini, once Nizhal-Kayal, a person.*

**Ch 47 (B, A). Day +11 and +12: partings.** Valli refuses an ordeal and takes sixty years (she shot a sitting Seal-holder). At the vault shuttle, Kayal: "I'll write." Ponni: "You'll be old when I read them." Kayal: "Then I'll write a lot." Ponni takes the urn into the slow with her.

**Epilogue: the Kuzhi.** Weeks of Ponni's time pass. Bales of letters arrive, each a year of Kayal's life, with ordinary x-ray portraits enclosed, as the custom is: Thumbi on the Mawsim's deck, Kathir at Neithal's emptied lightboxes on Uppalam, a new veranda on Thinnai. One week a small box comes: the scorched parrot, its solder blob intact. Ponni writes her first letter out. **The small choice:** whether to enclose the instruction that releases the old leases to Kayal and to send the urn with it. The following bales show the consequences in Kayal's voice: a new assembly and riots, or a long prosperous peace and a silence where Ponni's name should be. She may write nothing, and then the letters keep coming, shorter. **Final image:** the parrot, wound, walks a few steps across the desk past a year's letters that arrived in a week, and stops.
