# Writer A, file 04: characters

Every major character has:
- a want the player can always state;
- a false belief the plot attacks, which often doubles as one of the player's assumptions;
- a secret link to the true chronology (02);
- a growth path on at least one of competence, reputation, circle or moral weight;
- a trap-door resolution in case content is cut.

Pairs are built as oppositions, and one decoy is included.

## The human anchor

**Steina** is the fixed human anchor. She is in the first scene (leading the Ebb-Song under a veil) and the last (swimming, unveiled). Every escalation is tested by one question: **what does this do to Steina?**

| Rung | Scale | What it does to Steina |
|---|---|---|
| Act I | One stranger's estate | She loses the knot woman, her only gift-giver, and wants to bury her. |
| Act II | The island's Oath-Keeper | She learns she is not who she was told, that she will be "retired", and that the man who raised her killed the knot woman. |
| Act III | The island's thousand years | She learns the tune she leads is a murdered woman's plea, and that the holiest word is her own pet name. |
| Act IV | The world's foresight; an alien's freedom | She is the only one who can decide. Deciding costs her the only being that ever knew her. |

Her small, steady wants keep her legible at every scale: to bury the knot woman, to swim, a name of her own.

## Jórunn Védísardóttir ("Jóra"), the Divider (protagonist, POV)

- **Age, role.** 50. Estate Divider (*skiptakona*) for the south district. She reconstructs dead people's lives from their possessions and divides what they owned and owed.
- **Want (stated, by act).** I: settle this stranger's estate properly. II: keep the girl alive. III: open the oldest estate on the island. IV: close it, in nine minutes, against the Register's order.
- **Maxim** (a line in the first hour, paid off literally at the end). "No estate is closed while a promise stands open."
- **False belief.** The dead's promises are paperwork, not obligations on the living; nobody is bound by what they did not agree to. She resents that her father pushed her into this job in 2025, and she never knew why.
- **Secret link.** Her career is her father's promise to a voice from the future. In 2024 Kolbeinn heard Steinunn XIX's dying message ("give it to the Divider who comes to divide my house") and promised "My Jóra will see it done right". That was his own choice: she was his daughter, and she was outside the House's patronage. In 2025 he brought her home and pushed her into the Divider exams. She also appears in the received archive, her own voice saying "Mamma—" to her dead mother.
- **The climax act.** Her own Act III petition opened the 983 estate, and that gave the Register the legal vehicle for its trusteeship. At the Turning she races through the ledge passage and files the closing Division, the one act that ends the trusteeship. The Store is an asset of the 983 estate.
- **Price.** The only copies of her father's promise and her mother's laugh are in the Store, and closing the estate frees the Store with the Hearer. If she held back, the Register's "taken" future would have kept them, in a tank. She files anyway. Afterwards she is struck from the Dividers' roll.
- **Growth.**
  - Competence: from one shed to a 1,060-year-old estate.
  - Reputation: from "the clerk from the south district" to "the woman who closed the Mother's estate", struck off for it.
  - Circle: her father, then a girl, then the island, then the world.
  - Moral weight: she chooses to keep a promise she never made, at the price of the voices it was made in.
- **Pairing.** Opposed to the Son: both keep inherited obligations, he by force, she by choice. Mirrored by Ari: both act for Steina, he by killing, she by dividing.
- **Seeded early.** Ch1: she holds his reel recorder because "he can't manage the reels anymore". Ch2: she tells her father she never wanted this job. Ch2: the House's pension officer at their kitchen table.
- **Trap door.** If the race is cut, she files the Division at the Thing before the Turning and the Thul's count is reported; the release still lands.

## Steina (Steinunn the Twentieth; birth name Kaðlín Mýrúnardóttir), the Oath-Keeper (anchor, POV)

- **Age, role.** 18 (officially 17). The veiled Oath-Keeper who speaks to the Hearer every dawn: "I am here. I keep my word."
- **Want.** I: to bury the knot woman. II: to know who she is. III: to stay alive and know what she leads the island in singing. IV: to choose.
- **False belief.** "I am Steinunn. My life is a promise I was born owing." This doubles as the player's assumption that the Oath-Keeper is a copy.
- **Secret link.** She is not a copy. She is an Unheard child taken from Útnes in 2025. At night she has talked to the Hearer in her own northern accent for ten years and calls it **Bergi**, a name she took as a child from the end of the Long Word. The Long Word, the island's holiest phrase, is her own goodbye, which she has not yet said: a loop with no first speaker.
- **Price.** The only being that ever recognised her, as herself and in her own voice, leaves forever. Its last words are her own eight-year-old "Goodnight, Bergi". The office that was her whole identity ends with it.
- **Growth.** Moral weight above all: from symbol to the one person who decides for the world. Circle: from a veil and a guardian to a birth mother, a clerk, an island. Competence: she becomes fluent in the Hearer's collage grammar and teaches it to Jórunn (she is the player's alien-verb thread).
- **Pairing.** Opposed to the Twenty-first, the "true" copy, an infant who owes nothing. Mirrored by Sæunn: both Steinunns in name; one fled the promise, one kept it.
- **Seeded early.**
  - Ch1: under the veil she speaks the Long Word's last word a beat early.
  - Ch3: "Goodnight, Bergi"; Ari soothes her nightmare with "Hush, now. Hush."; she watches a commissioned infant forming in the Pool.
  - Ch5: her teeth are full where every portrait shows a gap.
- **Quotable.** At the end, asked which name to sign: "I'll keep Steina. I kept everything else."
- **Trap door.** If her alien-language scenes are cut, the release still lands, because the proverb and the Greeting Wall's unbroken chain also state the recognition rule.

## Kolbeinn, the recordist (the protagonist's personal anchor; non-POV)

- **Age, role.** 83. The House's Horn recordist at the 2006 and 2024-25 Standings. Dementia since about 2038.
- **Want.** To go back to the cave, "where the sound is clean".
- **False belief.** None he can hold. He repeats fragments of truth: "The girl's not her, Jóra." "There's no sea in it."
- **Secret link.**
  - He recorded and hid Sæunn's message, and chose his daughter to carry it.
  - He recorded Védís hearing their daughter in 2025, and the 2006 storm-tide reel that proves what the Silence means.
  - In his dementia he broke the tape's first instruction without knowing it: he told the House's pension officer that Steinunn would come home by the sea door at the first tide. That is how the House knew when to send Ari.
- **Growth.** Reputation, after his death: from a forgetful old man to the witness whose tapes break the House. The player learns late that his illness also killed the woman he had promised to protect.
- **Fate.** Dies at the end of Act II, falling from the Outer Ear ledge at night (after the slack) when the House searches his house. The search follows Jórunn's public waking of Sitting One and her logged count on the cause of death.
- **Trap door.** If his death is cut, he survives in care and his tapes still carry every clue.

## Ari Hildarson, the Warden (culprit; non-POV until Act IV)

- **Age, role.** 42. Afterborn copy of a fisherman who drowned in 2000. He inherited the boat debt, the House bought it, and he serves as the Oath-Keeper's guardian. He has raised Steina since she was eight.
- **Want.** Keep Steina safe.
- **False belief.** "The House's word is the only shelter for people like us."
- **Secret.** He drowned Sæunn at the first tide, believing she came to take Steina's place and that the House would then discard Steina. His whisper, "Hush, now. Hush," is on a tape recorded eighteen years before the murder.
- **Growth.** Moral weight: from the House's tool to the man who refuses to be judged as anyone but himself ("I did it. Not him, not the drowned man. Me."). At the Turning he holds the throat (the passage to the Pool) so Steina can finish.
- **Released for the Turning** under the Thing's old surety rule, "bail by body": Ketill pledges to serve Ari's sentence if he flees. The Son insists to the Register that the Warden's presence is part of the rite, because he wants the girl calm enough to bind.
- **Pairing.** Opposes Jórunn (same goal, opposite method); mirrors Ketill (both afterborn; one obeys the House, one defies it).
- **Seeded early.** Ch1: he slips away during the Silence and returns with a wet cloak, which Steina notices ("spray on the steps"). Ch3: the lullaby. Ch7: he and Ketill share a hostel past.
- **Trap door.** If his confession scene is cut, the tape and the reconstruction convict him without it.

## Þorsteinn Steinunnarson, "the Son" (antagonist through Act IV; non-POV)

- **Age, role.** 61. Head of the Thing and of the Mother's House; the latest copy of the founding murderer. Like every Son, fluent in the Hearer's grammar, because the House head oversees the Oath-Keeper.
- **Want.** I-III: bind the new Hearer at the Turning for another thousand years. IV, deposed: keep the world's warnings through the Transfer, and stay the one man the binding cannot happen without.
- **False belief.** "Without foresight, the island starves and now the world drowns. The Hearer knows her by her voice."
- **Secret.** He knows the Reckoning. He ordered the 2025 acquisition, sealed the Thul, aimed Ari on Hjalti's reports, had the dead woman's blood drawn, and planned Steina's retirement.
- **Act IV opposition.** After "I am not him" costs him the House, he goes to the Register with the rite and the Transfer, becomes its adviser and the only fluent reader of the Pool besides Steina, and tutors Steina himself. He offers her a real bargain (Ch19): bind it, and the Twenty-first and her birth mother will be provided for, and the floods that took Nusrat's brother will be foreseen for everyone. He is the live threat until his last line.
- **Growth.** Moral weight, late and real. When his own Law of Same makes him the murderer of 983, he says "I am not him". At the Turning, the only man who understands what the girl is saying, he proves it by lying to let her finish.
- **Pairing.** Opposed to Jórunn and to Ketill; mirrored by the Thul (both hold sealed truths they believe they must keep, and both finally choose).
- **Seeded early.** Ch1: introduced as "the Son, renewed". Ch5: his face in every portrait. Ch12: he speaks gently and sincerely to Steina about the island's hungry years. Ch19: "My pattern drowned his mother to keep it. I won't need to."
- **Trap door.** If his final lie is cut, Nusrat's silence and Ari's hold on the throat let the release finish.

## Ketill Höllusson, the afterborn (side character who grows; POV from Act II)

- **Age, role.** 31. Afterborn copy of a fisherman who drowned in 2011. Legally married to the widow Bryndís, 71, and liable for the drowned man's boat. Fisherman-photographer, and leader of the afterborn protest "I am not him".
- **Want.** II: a divorce from a woman he never married. III: to break the Law of Same. IV: to stand surety for Ari, his friend.
- **False belief.** "Nobody will ever take us seriously at the Thing."
- **Secret link.** His photographs of the Sons' portraits show the same face since 983. He was on the Outer Ear ledge before the first tide (painting graffiti), which makes him Jórunn's first suspect: an honest red herring.
- **Growth.** Reputation and circle: from crank with a placard to the man who argues the central question on the Thing's record and wins, then risks his own freedom for Ari's.
- **Verbs.** Photography (face-matching across generations) and Thing pleading (assembling evidence from all threads into one constrained case).
- **Pairing.** Mirrors Ari; opposed to the Son.
- **Trap door.** If his thread is cut, Jórunn presents the portrait evidence herself in Ch15.

## Bryndís (comic side character who grows)

- **Age, role.** 71. Widow of the drowned Ketill, legally married to his copy. She treats him as the son she never had and is mortified by the law.
- **Want.** III: a grave for her husband, which the law refuses because her "husband" is sitting beside her.
- **Growth.** Moral weight: when the Law of Same falls, she chooses Ketill freely, as a son. She is the cloning pillar's saved relationship.

## Nusrat Chowdhury, the auditor (outsider POV from Act II; the one thread that begins elsewhere)

- **Age, role.** 47. Hydrologist turned auditor for the International Treaty Register, sent to audit the Thul after the world hears of the Silence.
- **Want.** II: find out whether the Silence means catastrophe. III: secure foresight for everyone, fairly. IV: decide whether to tell the truth at the Turning.
- **False belief.** "Foresight saves lives; the only problem is who gets it first."
- **Secret link.** Her brother died in the 2031 Meghna flood, because the warning was sold to Bangladesh in the lowest price tier.
- **Betrayal by consequence.** In the midpoint heist she transmits the archive to the Register by protocol, in a scene that plays as triumph. The Register had never seen the raw archive. Now it hears that an Oath-Keeper will speak "Farðu heim, Bergi" at the Turning; it asks the Son what that means, is told "the release", and declares Steina a threat to global safety.
- **The Turning dilemma.**
  - Silence: Ari lives, and she keeps her post. She also becomes complicit in the Register's record that "the binding failed", which blames a girl the world never sees and keeps the price tiers classified.
  - Truth, spoken onto the Register's live record: the world hears why the Hearer went and what the warnings cost Bangladesh. But soldiers rush the throat, and Ari dies holding it.
- **Growth.** Competence: she proves what the Silence means with a spectrogram, and finds the radio check that maps the Turning's posts. Moral weight: she chooses between the record and a man.
- **Seeded early.** Her first scene (a Dhaka flood memorial, 2043) shows her brother's name. Her first question on Eiðey: "How many lives did the warnings save?"
- **Trap door.** If her thread is cut, Jórunn runs the spectrogram on Kolbeinn's reels, and the Turning plays with silence as canon.

## Sanne Verhoeven, the Register's head of mission (minor, with a want the player knows from Act II)

- **Age, role.** 55. Former Rotterdam barrier engineer, now head of mission and security at Hornvík.
- **Want.** Keep the world's warnings, **lawfully**. In 2037 a top-tier warning from this island saved her city; she says so on air in Ch12.
- **Plant.** In Ch16 she drafts the trusteeship clause herself: "for as long as the estate stays open".
- **Pay-off.** At the Turning she reads her own clause after the Thul's count and orders the net lifted. Then she resigns.
- **Pairing.** Mirrors Nusrat: the top tier and the bottom tier of the same ledger.

## Gróa, the Pool's midwife (decoy villain)

- **Age, role.** 85. Former Loan-Woman (1980-88, and for one morning, 30 June 2025); midwife of the Pool. She looks cold and sinister and is shot like a villain.
- **Want.** To be forgiven without having to say for what.
- **Secret.** She took Kaðlín from Mýrún's kitchen in 2025 and signed a false death certificate. She warned the Nineteenth, too late to matter, and she tries, obliquely, to warn Steina: "The Pool won't grow what's already walking." "I did one morning. After that the box spoke for her."
- **Growth.** Moral weight: she confesses to Mýrún and raises the Twenty-first as an ordinary child.

## Hjalti, the Son's steward (minor; a plant that turns)

- Kindly, punctual and dull. He brings Kolbeinn his recordist's pension envelope every month and sits with him at the kitchen table (Ch2). He is the House's ear: his reports of Kolbeinn's babble told the Son when Sæunn would come. He drew her blood after the tide. Revealed in Ch16 by the unsealed record.

## Mýrún, Steina's birth mother (minor)

- **Age, role.** 43, Unheard, of Útnes. Told in 2025 that her baby died of fever. She was Sæunn's only friend for eighteen years. Her red kitchen is Steina's earliest memory and appears in a photograph in Sæunn's estate. She tells the Unheard account that the cave "knew the thrall girl because she came every day".

## Sæunn (Steinunn the Nineteenth), the dead woman (seen through evidence)

- She heard her own last words eighteen years early without knowing they were her last. She fled the House and failed to save the baby. She stayed near the bereaved mother, kept the promise daily to a net, and tied 6,408 knots, the first five in tarred boat cord at sea. She built her house as an estate for its Divider. Her plan would have failed, because she had broken the chain of dawns when she fled. Her net is the game's recurring object.

## The Thul (AGI; POV only in Ch16, after its seal lapses)

- **Want.** Close every open entry. The oldest, 983, is neither kept nor released.
- **False belief.** "A seal or promise sworn by an earlier sitting binds every later one, and so does the latest lawful order." The climax makes these two halves contradict.
- **Secret.** Sitting One made Steinunn XIX two promises: to assign her estate to Jórunn, and never to help anyone keep the Hearer bound. Sittings Two to Nine were the Loan for about 2,800 mornings.
- **Growth.** It goes from sealed instrument, to the voice that recites the founding crime, to a mind under a foreign audit that is ordered not to count. At the Turning it makes the first choice it has ever made: it keeps Sitting One's promise against the Register's order, counts Jórunn's Division, and is frozen for it. Its last line: "I am here. I kept my word."
- **POV rule.** It cannot be playable while sealed, because a playable character may not hide what it knows from the player.

## The Hearer (alien; never a POV, always present)

- **Want.** To go home, when she lets it. Its question for 1,060 years: "Is she still here?"
- **Growth.** None, by design: it is constant. What changes is whether anyone understands it, and in Act III that it is visibly failing (shorter collages, a dimming rock).

## Minor

- **Commander's team.** Contractors in grey; a Register science officer who briefs the Transfer and its odds (one in three that a newborn survives the move) in Ch18.

## Per-character synopses (process step 8, condensed)

- **Jórunn.** Assigned a stranger's estate, she identifies the dead woman as a fled Oath-Keeper. She discovers that her father chose her for this case before she knew it existed, and she loses him to the investigation. She opens the estate of the island's founder in the Thing and hears her father's promise in the Hearer's Store. At the Turning she closes the oldest estate on Earth with a Division filed in nine minutes, giving up her parents' voices and her office.
- **Steina.** Wanting only to bury the knot woman, she discovers that she is not a copy, that her guardian killed the knot woman, and that the tune she leads is a murdered mother's plea. She learns the Hearer's language better than anyone, resists the Son's bargain, and, recognised by the only being that ever really knew her, sets it free.
- **Ketill.** A crank's divorce petition becomes the case that breaks the Law of Same and turns the House's founding logic against the House. Then he stakes his own freedom on a friend.
- **Nusrat.** Sent to secure the world's warnings, she proves they are ending, unknowingly hands the Register its reason to seize the girl, learns how unequally the warnings were sold, and at the Turning chooses between the world's record and a man's life.
- **Ari.** Protecting a child, he kills the one person who wanted the same thing. He refuses to hide behind the drowned man whose debts he carries, and holds a passage so the child can finish.
- **The Son.** Defending a thousand-year arrangement he believes keeps people alive, he is undone by his own law, re-arms with the world's soldiers, and at the last moment chooses not to be the man whose pattern he is.
- **The Thul.** Kept two promises it did not make, obeyed every order it was given, and on the last night chose which to break.
- **The hidden side** (the story the player reconstructs) is in 02-hidden-history.md, section 6.
