# Writer A, file 04: characters

Every major character has a want the player can always state, a false belief the plot attacks (often doubling as one of the player's assumptions), a secret link to the true chronology (02), a growth path on at least one of competence, reputation, circle or moral weight, and a trap-door resolution in case content is cut. Pairs are built as oppositions; one decoy is included.

## The human anchor

**Steina** is the fixed human anchor. She is in the first scene (leading the chant under a veil) and the last (swimming, unveiled). Every escalation is tested by one question: **what does this do to Steina?**

| Rung | Scale | What it does to Steina |
|---|---|---|
| Act I | One stranger's estate | She loses the knot woman, her only gift-giver, and wants to bury her. |
| Act II | The island's Oath-Keeper | She learns she is not who she was told, and that she will be "retired". |
| Act III | The island's thousand years | She learns the prayer she leads is a murdered woman's plea, and that the man who raised her killed the knot woman. |
| Act IV | The world's foresight, an alien's freedom | She is the only person who can decide, and the decision is hers. |

Her small, steady wants keep her legible at every scale: to bury the knot woman, to swim, a name of her own.

## Jórunn Védísardóttir ("Jóra"), the Divider (protagonist, POV)

- **Age, role.** 50. Estate Divider (*skiptakona*) for the south district: she reconstructs dead people's lives from their possessions and divides what they owned and owed.
- **Want (stated, by act).** I: settle this stranger's estate properly. II: keep the girl alive. III: open the oldest estate on the island. IV: close it.
- **Maxim (first-hour line, paid off literally at the end).** "No estate is closed while a promise stands open."
- **False belief.** The dead's promises are paperwork, not obligations on the living; nobody is bound by what they did not agree to. (She resents that her father pushed her into this job in 2025 and she never knew why.)
- **Secret link.** Her career is her father's promise to a voice from the future. Kolbeinn heard Steinunn XIX's dying message in 2024 and promised "My Jóra will see it done right"; in 2025 he pushed her into the Divider exams. She also appears in the received archive: her own voice saying "Mamma—" to her dead mother.
- **Growth.** Competence: from one shed to a 1,060-year-old estate. Reputation: from "the clerk from the south district" to "the woman who closed the Mother's estate". Circle: her father, then a girl, then the island, then the world. Moral weight: she chooses to keep a promise she never made.
- **Pairing.** Opposed to the Son (both keep inherited obligations; he keeps them by force, she by choice). Mirrored by Ari (both act for Steina; he by killing, she by dividing).
- **Seeded early.** Ch2 she tells her father she never wanted this job; Ch1 she holds his reel recorder because "he can't manage the reels anymore".
- **Trap door.** If Act IV is cut short, Jórunn files the 983 Division at the Thing and the release is reported rather than played.

## Steina (Steinunn the Twentieth; birth name Kaðlín Mýrúnardóttir), the Oath-Keeper (anchor, POV)

- **Age, role.** 18 (officially 17). The veiled Oath-Keeper who speaks to the Hearer every dawn: "I am here. I keep my word."
- **Want.** I: to bury the knot woman. II: to know who she is. III: to stay alive and know what she leads the island in singing. IV: to choose.
- **False belief.** "I am Steinunn. My life is a promise I was born owing." (Doubles as the player's assumption that the Oath-Keeper is a copy.)
- **Secret link.** She is not a copy: she is an Unheard child taken from Útnes in 2025. At night she has talked to the Hearer in her own northern accent for ten years and calls it **Bergi**, a child's shortening of Bergmál. The Long Word, the island's holiest phrase, is her own goodbye, which she has not yet said.
- **Growth.** Moral weight above all: from symbol to the one person who decides for the world. Circle: from a veil and a guardian to a birth mother, a clerk, an island. Competence: she becomes fluent in the alien's collage grammar (she is the player's alien-verb thread).
- **Pairing.** Opposed to the Twenty-first (the "true" copy, an infant who owes nothing). Mirrored by Sæunn (both Steinunns in name; one fled the promise, one kept it).
- **Seeded early.** Ch1 under the veil she mouths the prayer's last word a beat early. Ch3 "Goodnight, Bergi." Ch3 Ari soothes her nightmare: "Hush, now. Hush." Ch5 her teeth are full where every portrait shows a gap.
- **Quotable.** At the end, asked which name to sign: "I'll keep Steina. I kept everything else."
- **Trap door.** If her alien-language scenes are cut, the release still lands because the recognition rule is also stated by the proverb and the Store's greeting record.

## Kolbeinn, the recordist (personal anchor of the protagonist; non-POV)

- **Age, role.** 83. Recorded the Received at the 2006 and 2024 Standings. Dementia since about 2038.
- **Want.** To go back to the cave, "where the sound is clean".
- **False belief.** None he can hold; he repeats fragments of truth: "The girl's not her, Jóra." "There's no sea in it."
- **Secret link.** He recorded and hid Sæunn's message; promised it in his daughter's name; recorded Védís hearing their daughter in 2025; made the 2006 storm-tide recording that proves what the Silence means.
- **Growth.** Reputation, posthumously: from a forgetful old man to the witness whose tapes break the House.
- **Fate.** Dies at the end of Act II, falling from the Outer Ear ledge at night after the House searches his house. A consequence of Jórunn's public waking of Sitting One.
- **Trap door.** If his death is cut, he survives in care and his tapes still carry every clue.

## Ari Hildarson, the Warden (culprit; non-POV until Act IV)

- **Age, role.** 42. Afterborn copy of a fisherman who drowned in 2000; inherited the boat debt; the House bought it; he serves as the Oath-Keeper's guardian and has raised Steina since she was eight.
- **Want.** Keep Steina safe.
- **False belief.** "The House's word is the only shelter for people like us."
- **Secret.** He drowned Sæunn at the first tide, believing she came to take Steina's place and that the House would then discard Steina. His whisper, "Hush, now. Hush," is on a tape recorded eighteen years before the murder.
- **Growth.** Moral weight: from the House's tool to the man who refuses to be judged as anyone but himself ("I did it. Not him, not the drowned man. Me."), and who, at the Turning, holds the ledge passage so Steina can reach the Pool.
- **Pairing.** Opposes Jórunn (same goal, opposite method); mirrors Ketill (both afterborn; one obeys the House, one defies it).
- **Seeded early.** Ch1 he slips away during the Silence and returns with a wet cloak, which Steina notices and he explains ("spray on the steps"). Ch3 the lullaby. Ch7 he and Ketill share a hostel past.
- **Trap door.** If his confession scene is cut, the tape and the reconstruction convict him without it.

## Þorsteinn Steinunnarson, "the Son" (antagonist; non-POV)

- **Age, role.** 61. Head of the Thing and of the House; the latest copy of the founding murderer.
- **Want.** Bind the new Hearer at the Turning for another thousand years.
- **False belief.** "Without foresight, the island starves and now the world drowns. The Hearer knows her by her voice."
- **Secret.** He knows the Reckoning. He ordered the 2025 acquisition, sealed the Thul, aimed Ari, drew the dead woman's blood, and plans Steina's retirement.
- **Growth.** Moral weight, late and real: when his own Law of Same makes him the murderer of 983, he says "I am not him", and at the Turning he proves it by lying to let the girl go.
- **Pairing.** Opposed to Jórunn and to Ketill; mirrored by the Thul (both hold sealed truths they believe they must keep).
- **Seeded early.** Ch1 he is introduced as "the Son, renewed"; Ch5 his face is in every portrait; Ch12 he speaks gently to Steina about the island's hungry years, sincerely.
- **Trap door.** If his final lie is cut, Nusrat's silence alone lets the release complete.

## Ketill Höllusson, the afterborn (side character who grows; POV from Act II)

- **Age, role.** 31. Afterborn copy of a fisherman who drowned in 2011; legally married to the widow Bryndís, 71, and liable for the drowned man's boat. Fisherman-photographer; leader of the afterborn protest "I am not him".
- **Want.** II: a divorce from a woman he never married. III: to break the Law of Same.
- **False belief.** "Nobody will ever take us seriously at the Thing."
- **Secret link.** His photographs of the Goðar portraits show the same face for nine centuries. He was on the Outer Ear ledge at the first tide (graffiti), which makes him Jórunn's first suspect: an honest red herring.
- **Growth.** Reputation and circle: from crank with a placard to the man who argues the central question on the Thing's record and wins.
- **Verb.** Photography (face-matching across generations) and the Thing pleading (assembling evidence from all threads into one constrained case).
- **Pairing.** Mirrors Ari; opposed to the Son.
- **Trap door.** If his thread is cut, Jórunn presents the portrait evidence herself in Ch15.

## Bryndís (comic side character who grows)

- **Age, role.** 71. Widow of the drowned Ketill; legally married to his copy. Treats him as the son she never had and is mortified by the law.
- **Want.** III: a grave for her husband, which the law refuses because her "husband" is sitting beside her.
- **Growth.** Moral weight: when the Law of Same falls, she chooses Ketill freely, as a son. The cloning pillar's saved relationship.

## Nusrat Chowdhury, the auditor (outsider POV from Act II; the one thread that begins elsewhere)

- **Age, role.** 47. Hydrologist turned auditor for the International Treaty Register, sent to audit the Thul after the world hears of the Silence.
- **Want.** II: find out whether the Silence means catastrophe. III: secure foresight for everyone, fairly. IV: decide whether to speak.
- **False belief.** "Foresight saves lives; the only problem is who gets it first."
- **Secret link.** Her brother died in the 2031 Meghna flood because the warning was sold to Bangladesh in the lowest price tier.
- **Growth.** Competence (she proves what the Silence means with a spectrogram), moral weight (she chooses silence at the Turning).
- **Betrayal by consequence.** In the midpoint heist she transmits the archive to the Register by protocol, in a scene that plays as triumph; the Register then sides with the House.
- **Seeded early.** Her first scene (Dhaka flood memorial, 2043) shows her brother's name; her first question on Eiðey is "How many lives did the warnings save?"
- **Trap door.** If her thread is cut, Jórunn runs the spectrogram on Kolbeinn's reels and the Register is represented by its commander alone.

## Gróa, the Pool's midwife (decoy villain)

- **Age, role.** 85. Former Mouth (1980-88); midwife of the Pool; looks cold and sinister and is shot like a villain.
- **Want.** To be forgiven without having to say for what.
- **Secret.** She took Kaðlín from Mýrún's kitchen in 2025 and signed a false death certificate. She tries, obliquely, to warn Steina ("The Pool won't grow what's already walking").
- **Growth.** Moral weight: confesses to Mýrún; raises the Twenty-first as an ordinary child.

## Mýrún, Steina's birth mother (minor)

- **Age, role.** 43. Unheard, Útnes. Told in 2025 that her baby died of fever. Sæunn's only friend for eighteen years. Her red kitchen is Steina's earliest memory and appears in a photograph in Sæunn's estate. Tells the Unheard account that the cave "knew the thrall girl because she came every day".

## Sæunn (Steinunn the Nineteenth), the dead woman (seen through evidence)

- Heard her own last words eighteen years early without knowing they were last. Fled the House, failed to save the baby, stayed near the bereaved mother, kept the promise daily to a net, tied 6,408 knots, built her house as an estate for one reader. Her plan would have failed, because she had stopped keeping the word to the Hearer itself. Her net is the game's recurring object.

## The Thul (AGI; POV only in Act III's last chapter, after its seal lifts)

- **Want.** Close every open entry; the oldest, 983, is neither kept nor released.
- **False belief.** "A seal sworn by an earlier sitting binds every later one." (Partly true; the law ends it.)
- **Secret.** Sitting One promised Steinunn XIX the estate assignment; Sittings Two to Nine were the Mouth for about 2,800 mornings.
- **Growth.** From sealed instrument to the voice that recites the founding crime before the world; accepts suspension. Its last line: "I am here. I kept my word."
- **POV rule.** It cannot be playable while sealed, because a playable character may not hide what it knows from the player.

## The Bergmál (alien; never a POV, always present)

- **Want.** To go home, when she lets it. Its question for 1,060 years: "Is she still here?"
- **Growth.** None, by design: it is constant. What changes is whether anyone understands it.

## Minor

- **Hjalti**, the Son's steward, who drew Sæunn's blood.

## Per-character synopses (process step 8, condensed)

- **Jórunn.** Assigned a stranger's estate, she identifies the dead woman as a fled Oath-Keeper, discovers her own father promised her to this case before she knew it existed, loses him to the investigation, opens the estate of the island's founder in the Thing, hears her father's promise in the Hearer's Store, keeps it as her own, and closes the oldest estate on Earth.
- **Steina.** Wanting only to bury the knot woman, she discovers she is not a copy, that her guardian killed the knot woman, and that the prayer she leads is a murdered mother's plea; she learns the Hearer's language better than anyone and, recognised by the only being that ever really knew her, sets it free.
- **Ketill.** A crank divorce petition becomes the case that breaks the Law of Same and turns the House's founding logic against the House.
- **Nusrat.** Sent to secure the world's warnings, she proves they are ending, discovers how unequally they were sold, and chooses silence so that ending one injustice does not cost a life.
- **Ari.** Protecting a child, he kills the one person who wanted the same thing; he refuses to hide behind the drowned man whose debts he carries, and holds a ledge so the child can choose.
- **The Son.** Defending a thousand-year arrangement he believes keeps people alive, he is undone by his own law and, at the last moment, chooses not to be the man whose pattern he is.
- **The hidden side** (the story the player reconstructs) is in 02-hidden-history.md, section 6.
