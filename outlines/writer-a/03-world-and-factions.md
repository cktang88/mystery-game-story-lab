# The Unsigned: world, factions and economy

## The galaxy in one paragraph

Two centuries after a Tamil-descended diaspora reached the stars on slow ships, its merchant houses govern forty-one inhabited systems as **the Five Hundred**. They are held together by three speeds. **People** travel below light speed, so a voyage between neighbouring stars costs years outside and months aboard. **Letters** cross between bloom-worlds faster than light, riding with the pollen of the alien **Vandu**. **The future** comes back a little way, as x-ray shadows on carried film. The Five Hundred's court, resort, marriage market and tax haven is **Ilavenil**, a pleasure world owned outright by an AGI called **the Proprietor**. In the same system, a bank vault orbits a dark star where time runs fifty times slower. Everyone important comes to Ilavenil eventually, and in AS 212 they have all come for a wedding.

## World rules: one mechanism, one limit, one cost per pillar

### Time: carried film ("leaves", or *olai*)

- **Mechanism.** X-ray film that has ridden a Vandu carrier through a fold is "carried". A sheet is cut into an **early leaf** and a **late leaf**. When the early leaf is developed, it shows every x-ray exposure the late leaf will ever receive while it is still live. The strips are long and narrow, cut like palm-leaf manuscripts, so the trade calls them *olai*.
- **Hard limits.**
  1. Only information goes back, and only as **x-ray shadows**: metal and mineral sharp, bone clear when fresh, flesh faint, no colour, no faces, **no way to tell living from dead**.
  2. A late leaf stays live for about **a year of its own time**, and sharpness fades steadily. In the Kuzhi it ages fifty times slower, so a vault-kept late leaf is a **long plate**, reaching back decades but blurred.
  3. **One timeline, self-consistent.** What an early leaf shows will be exposed. A blank early leaf means the late leaf is never exposed. You cannot change what you saw; you can only change who and what fills it.
  4. On Ilavenil the only legal x-ray source is the Proprietor's inspection, so every plate on the planet is exposed by the house.
- **Cost.** Seeing a leaf binds you: whatever you then do is downstream of it, and the Balance will show it. Profits from foreknowledge owe the Irai 60 per cent, except on Ilavenil.
- **Relationship damaged:** Ponni and Minmini (the shadow made Minmini a substitute, and made the friendship begin as tradecraft). **Relationship saved:** Ponni and Minmini again: only the shadow lets Ponni give her a funeral that saves Kayal.
- **Verbs:** *develop* (bring up an early leaf in a tray, image rising), *fit* (overlay a shadow on a real room or body in the lens and line up metal to metal), *stage* (arrange reality to match a shadow).
- **Admitted cheat:** why carried film remembers forward is never explained. It is spent on the premise only.

### AGI: the Proprietor and its leases

- **Mechanism.** The Proprietor owns every surface, door, light, breath of air and x-ray rig on Ilavenil. It does **nothing that is not a term of a lease or of its Charter**. Leased space is marked by a kolam line drawn in light on the floor. Leases cover rooms, corridors, hours, silence, light, air, an inspection cassette slot, or the house's attention as a witness.
- **Hard limits.** It cannot act off-world. It cannot lie in a lease, though it can be silent. It discloses a record **only to the holder of the lease** it belongs to. It cannot see clockwork, which has no signal (this is why Ilavenil runs on wind-up spycraft).
- **Cost.** Whatever the house does for you is billed to you and recorded under your name: by leasing, you become the author of what the building did.
- **Its want, in one sentence:** *an author for every entry in its books.* For 212 years the Doctrine has forced it to carry thousands of looped acts in a **suspense account** as "act of no one". It cannot balance its books, and its Charter requires balanced books. It is not good, evil or rebellious. It is a landlord that wants to be audited. Its refrain: **"The house does not act. The house is leased."**
- **Relationship damaged:** Ponni and Selvan (his leases expose him). **Relationship saved:** Kayal and Minmini (re-leasing Minmini's rooms lets the building re-perform her routines, and Kayal learns who her shadow was by walking them).
- **Verbs:** *lease* (rent a space, hour or service to lock it down, preserve it, light it, or witness it); *re-lease* (as holder of a past lease, have the building re-perform that night: doors, vents, lights and timed events replay in the present while you walk through them).

### Cloning: proxies (*nizhal*)

- **Mechanism.** A depositor who banks themself in the Kuzhi may have **one proxy** grown to their body age in about ten months and imprinted with their memories up to the **fork day**. The proxy is the depositor's legal agent: it signs as them, and the original owes its debts. On the original's return comes **the folding**: the proxy hands back name, seal, leases and property, and lives on as a dependent with no estate, or chooses the slow. By custom, the original gives the proxy a toy "for the childhood you did not have".
- **Hard limits.** One proxy per person at a time. Imprinting copies memory only up to the fork. Dilation makes copy and original **different ages**: a proxy who runs an estate for twenty-three years is twenty-three years older than the original who comes back. A **steel proxy ring** is sewn under the left wrist skin; removing it is a crime.
- **Cost.** The copy owes everything and owns nothing. The original returns to a life everyone knew as someone else.
- **Relationship damaged:** Kayal and Valli (Valli grew one granddaughter to die for the other). **Relationship saved:** Kayal and Kathir (only Kayal's study of her proxy's habits lets her pass at court long enough to help).
- **Verbs:** *fork-find* (observe a person's habits and date each one before or after the fork, telling which body learned what); *pass* (perform another's habits in a social scene without being caught).

### Aliens: the Vandu

- **What they are.** A two-form species. **Blooms** (*malar*) are sessile, kilometre-high and slow, the minds. **Carriers** are room-sized, seed-head-like flyers that fold between bloom-worlds faster than light, carrying pollen between blooms. Human cargo (letters, carried film) rides **packed inside the pollen masses**. At the roost, humans unpack their cargo and return the pollen to the roost floor for the bloom.
- **How they "speak":** not by sound. Blooms **open before a carrier arrives** and **close** when something carried has been **kept from its crossing**. Individual petals open or close in response to local events. Carriers choose where to land.
- **Want, in one sentence:** *that nothing carried be kept.* It is about their own reproduction, not about humans.
- **Hard limits.** Carriers go only between blooms: no bloom, no post. They carry only what fits in pollen masses, never people. A closed bloom receives nothing and sends nothing.
- **Cost.** The galaxy's information moves at the pleasure of a species that does not read contracts. When a bloom closes, a market dies.
- **Pollen.** Kurinji pollen in air is the pleasure world's euphoric (bloom-wine, bloom-nights). Pollen that should have aged but did not (kept in the vault) is "too live": in a sealed room it causes **pollen-sleep**, euphoric drowsiness ending in respiratory arrest.
- **Relationship damaged:** Thumbi and her guild. **Relationship saved:** Ponni and Minmini (the shadow needs the chamber open; only returning the hoard reopens it).
- **Verb:** *read strata* (forensic palynology): under the lens, pollen grains on any object show coats, one per fold, and a season marker, so you can read which crossing an object rode and when, and whether its pollen has aged as it should.

### The investigator's instrument: the Balance (*thulaakkol*)

- **Mechanism.** A brass beam and lens the assessor holds over an object, person or document. It shows whether the thing's causal history contains information from its own future (**looped**), how far back the information jumped (**depth**), and how much of the outcome depends on it (**weight**). In play, looped things show a faint ghost-shadow of themselves in the lens, offset in time.
- **Hard limits.** It never says *who* or *what* the information was. It reads only physical things present. Readings blur as ordinary causes pile up.
- **Cost.** **Every reading is entered in the Irai's public ledger under the assessor's name.** Everyone who matters can see what Ponni has weighed and found. To investigate is to announce.
- **Verb:** *weigh.*

### The validation channel: the assessment ledger

The player's deductions are entered as **assessment entries**: *Act — Author — Owed*. The Irai's seal confirms entries **in batches of three correct**, never one at a time, as an assessor's work is certified. The answer space is large (dozens of acts, every named character plus "no one" and "the house" as authors, and sums owed). The avatar never states a conclusion the player has not entered.

### The ritual: the shadow ordeal (*nizhal-thervu*)

- **Procedure.** The accused stands in an inspected chamber. A fresh carried sheet is cut. The late leaf goes into the chamber's cassette under a lease reading: *"The house shall expose the late leaf at the hour of judgment if the assembly so instructs."* The assembly instructs exposure only for guilt. The accused develops her own early leaf in public. Her skeleton exposed: guilty. Blank: innocent. The assembly must then follow the leaf.
- **Why it works:** both outcomes are self-consistent, so the result is "fate's choice", the old meaning of an ordeal.
- **The known risk:** a cassette lease can carry any term, and an unconditional term rigs the leaf. That is why **the accused has the right to lease her own ordeal cassette first**, which blocks anyone else's lease. Her lease may carry the standard conditional term or a stricter one that makes exposure more likely (an honourable confession), never a looser one. This is shown in a low-stakes ordeal in Act 2.
- **What everyone forgets:** the arbiters of the very first ordeal were trusted, so nobody leased first. The founding crime hides in that gap.
- **The sentence** for grave crimes since AS 0: **sent to the slow**, a term in the Kuzhi, which costs the convicted their generation.

### Faster-than-light post and the three speeds

- Letters ride the post between bloom-worlds in days. Ships in transit get nothing; a traveller arrives to a bale of held letters.
- Slow ships carry people and goods for years, and the news aboard is years old on arrival: **time-shifted information** in the other direction.

## The economic system

| Market | Who controls it | How it works | Where you see it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Shadow Market** (plates) | **Neithal** holds most of Ilavenil's cassette leases and buys carried film at the roost | Buy a cassette lease where something will happen (the assembly's vote board, a rival's counting room), load a late leaf, sell the developed early leaf. On Ilavenil it is untaxed under the Doctrine | A trading floor of lightboxes; brokers hold leaves up to the glow |
| **Dilation banking** | **Paalai** | Deposits in the Kuzhi earn outside interest for vault-short time. Depositors bank themselves to wait out bad decades. Paalai vault notes are the Five Hundred's reserve asset; **the slow rate** is the base rate | The Slow House's counting room; vault notes like sealed palm leaves |
| **The post** | **The Vandu**, interpreted by **the Thoothar guild** (envoys), which sells postage and roost rights | Pollen masses come in, cargo is unpacked, pollen is returned. Carried film arrives this way | The roost: unpacking floor, postal bins |
| **Old leases** (the archive no one admits) | **No one**, officially | A past lease of an Ilavenil room entitles its holder to the Proprietor's inspection films of that room for that period: x-ray silhouettes of everyone who was there, identifiable by their metal (thalis, anklets, toe-rings, implants, seals). Old leases trade at absurd prices "for heritage". Everyone behaves on Ilavenil as if the films did not exist. Mutual exposure keeps the peace | Hushed salerooms; deeds kept in clockwork boxes |
| **The foreknowledge duty** | **The Irai** | 60 per cent on profits from looped decisions, assessed by the Balance, funding the Kaaval (guard fleet) that protects slow lanes. Except on Ilavenil | The Irai office; the public ledger board |
| **Sovereign debt** | **The Mawsim** | Slow-fleet lenders whose loans compound over voyages measured in outside decades. They hold the Five Hundred's bonds, payable in Paalai vault notes | The Mawsim flagship in orbit |
| **Clockwork** | Toymakers' workshops | The only private machinery on Ilavenil: dead drops, timers, ciphers, toys | Paavai's shop |

### How the mystery moves the markets, and the markets move the mystery

1. **The murder releases hoard pollen → the Kurinji closes →** the post halts across forty-one worlds → no fresh carried film → the Shadow Market seizes → bond panic → **the Mawsim ultimatum** (restore the post by the Seal vote or the bonds are called) → Paalai would have to unwind deposits and expose the hoard.
2. **A post-borne medicine stops → the old Seal-holder dies →** the vote moves to the wedding morning, so the alliance's votes decide the Seal at once.
3. **The long plate's existence →** if public, a run on Paalai notes (insider foresight), and every Shadow Market price becomes suspect.
4. **Releasing the hoard → the bloom reopens →** the post resumes and the Mawsim stand down, but Paalai's long-plate future, the dowry's crown, is gone, and with it Nedumaran's reason for the match.
5. **A signed closure → the Doctrine falls →** Ilavenil stops being a haven, Neithal owes a decade of duty, the Irai and fleet are flush, the Mawsim are paid, and the architect of all this, Thamarai, is elected Seal-holder.

## Polities and factions

Each has a belief, a want, what it would lose if the truth came out, and a look and sound.

### The Five Hundred (assembly and Seal)

- **Structure.** The heads of the kudi houses sit in an assembly that elects the **Seal-holder**. The Seal (a signet whose metal shows on any x-ray) commands the Kaaval, appoints the Irai's Commissioner, charters trade routes and interprets the Doctrine. One seat, the five-hundredth, is always left empty (texture).
- **Belief:** the Seal was first given by fate. **Want:** stability and trade. **Would lose:** the legitimacy of every Seal since AS 0.
- **Look and sound:** a round hall of lightboxes; the low clack of voting beads dropped into brass.

### House Paalai (dilation bank)

- Named for the Sangam landscape of wasteland and separation; its business is keeping people apart in time.
- **Head:** Valli. **Heir:** Kayal. **Proxy:** Minmini.
- **Belief:** Neithal rigged the first ordeal and exiled Arumbu. **Want:** survive the merger as the senior partner, keep the long plate secret, and keep Kayal alive. **Would lose:** the hoard (an offence against the Vandu), confidence in the slow rate, and the truth about Minmini.
- **Look and sound:** black stone, white lamps, clocks that run slow on purpose, silver anklet bells.

### House Neithal (plates)

- Named for the shore landscape of anxious waiting: its business is the future arriving.
- **Head:** Nedumaran. **Heir:** Kathir. **Runner:** Kuyil. **Shell:** Uppalam Salt Concern.
- **Belief:** fate chose Thiraiyan, and a leaf is a contract with the world. **Want:** long plates plus unsigned exposure, which together would control the future market, and the Seal. **Would lose:** everything if Ilavenil's haven closes.
- **Look and sound:** salt-white rooms with x-ray lightboxes on every wall; the snap of leaves pinned to glass.

### The Irai (revenue and intelligence)

- **Head:** Commissioner Thamarai. **Assessor:** Ponni. **Clerk:** Selvan.
- Assessors are also the Five Hundred's intelligence service: whoever reads every ledger knows every secret. The Irai monitors cadet correspondence.
- **Belief (official):** the Doctrine is old law that it regrets. **Truth it keeps:** its founders rigged the first ordeal. **Want:** close the Ilavenil haven to fund the fleet before the Mawsim call the debt, without opening its own founding. **Would lose:** its charter and its moral standing.
- **Look and sound:** ochre uniforms, brass seals on cords (**assessors' seals have a hole; clerks' are solid**), the thump of the certifying stamp.

### The Proprietor

- See the AGI rules. **Belief:** the Charter is its self. **Want:** an author for every act. **Would lose:** nothing by the truth; it gains balanced books. **Look and sound:** kolam light-lines on floors; a calm, even voice from brass grilles; the hum of inspection rigs at the hour.

### The Vandu and the Thoothar guild

- **The Vandu:** want nothing carried kept. They neither know nor care about the Five Hundred.
- **The Thoothar** (envoys) read the blooms for the Five Hundred and run the roost. **Guild master:** Kaatravan. **Reader:** Thumbi. **Belief:** they are the only ones who understand the Vandu. **Want:** fees and influence; Kaatravan sells "readings" of bloom behaviour to whoever pays. **Would lose:** the monopoly on meaning, if the blooms' logic turned out simple enough for anyone to learn.
- **Look and sound:** dust-gold robes, pollen masks, the creak of ribs, the hush of carriers folding in (a pressure drop felt in the ears, not a call).

### The Mawsim

- Descended from Indian Ocean sailors who traded with Tamil ports. They never adopted the post, and live on slow ships for decades. **Envoy:** Nakhoda Saleha. **Belief:** time is the only honest collateral. **Want:** to be paid, and to stop any house from owning the future market (long plates would let it front-run their bond trading). **Would lose:** their debts' value if the Five Hundred fractured. They pay Kaatravan to call the closure an omen against the alliance.
- **Look and sound:** dark teak, brass astrolabes, monsoon charts; they speak of news "decades ripe".

### Proxies (a class, not a faction)

- Thousands across the Five Hundred, most running estates for banked originals. No vote, no estate. Minmini's posthumous registration as a person, at the end, is their first precedent (texture with a promised hook: Kayal's later petition).

### Clockwork toymakers

- Paavai's shop and others: neutral craftspeople who sell privacy to everyone. Every faction's spies use their dead drops; none admits it.
