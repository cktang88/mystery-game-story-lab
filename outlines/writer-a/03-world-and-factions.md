# The Unsigned: world, factions and economy (revised)

All rules here restate the locked digest in `02-hidden-history.md`. If they ever differ, `02` wins.

## The galaxy in one paragraph

Two centuries after a Tamil-descended diaspora reached the stars on slow ships, its merchant firms govern forty-one inhabited systems as **the Five Hundred**. They are held together by three speeds. **People** travel below light speed, so a voyage between neighbouring stars costs years outside and months aboard. **Letters** cross between bloom-worlds faster than light, riding with the pollen of the **Vandu**, whose rooted blooms form a polity of their own, **the Crossing**, which decides where its carriers fly. **The future** comes back a little way, as x-ray shadows on carried film. The Five Hundred's court, resort, marriage market and tax haven is **Ilavenil**, a pleasure world owned outright by an AGI, **the Proprietor**, and sited under the oldest bloom, through which most routes pass. In the same system, berths in a vault orbiting a dark star let the rich skip years. Creditors from another polity, **the Mawsim**, who live their lives on slow ships, hold the confederation's debt. In AS 212 they have all come to Ilavenil for a wedding.

## World rules: one mechanism, one limit, one cost per pillar

### Time: carried film (*olai*, "leaves")

- **Mechanism:** a carried sheet is cut into early and late leaves. The developed early leaf shows every x-ray exposure its late leaf receives while live. Strips are cut long and narrow like palm-leaf manuscripts.
- **Limits:**
  1. Only x-ray shadows: metal and mineral sharp, bone clear when fresh, flesh faint, no faces, and **no living or dead**.
  2. A late leaf is live for about a year of its own time and fades steadily. Kept in the Kuzhi it lasts decades (a long plate), blurred.
  3. One self-consistent timeline. What you saw will be exposed; you can change only who and what fills it.
  4. **Shielding:** live leaves are kept in lead sheaths and unsheathed only inside a cassette. An unsheathed leaf in any inspected room puts that room into its image.
  5. **Cadence:** private rooms are inspected at 04:00, thresholds every quarter hour, halls hourly, the bloom chamber at 23:00. A cassette lease names the inspections it covers.
- **Cost:** seeing binds you. Profits owe the Irai 60 per cent, except on Ilavenil while the Doctrine stands.
- **Damages** Ponni and Minmini (the shadow made Minmini a substitute, and made the friendship begin as tradecraft). **Saves** Ponni and Minmini (it lets her give her friend a funeral that saves Kayal).
- **Admitted cheat:** why carried film remembers forward is never explained.

### AGI: the Proprietor

- **Mechanism:** it owns every surface, door, light, breath of air and x-ray rig on Ilavenil, and acts only by lease or Charter. Leased space is marked by a kolam line of light.
- **Limits:** it cannot act off-world; it cannot lie in a lease; it discloses only to the lease's holder or to an assessor with custody of an estate; it cannot see clockwork; **it may not attribute authorship of a closure**, so only the purpose-of-closure field can.
- **Cost:** everything it does for you is billed and recorded under your name. Lessees are traceable, though not authors.
- **Want:** an author for every entry. Its suspense account holds 4,117 unsigned closures. Refrain: **"The house does not act. The house is leased."**
- **Damages** Ponni and Selvan (his lease registrations expose him). **Saves** Kayal and Minmini (re-performed routines let Kayal learn her proxy).

### Cloning: proxies (*nizhal*)

- **Mechanism:** one proxy per banked depositor, grown in about ten months and imprinted to the fork day. It is the original's legal agent **and property**, and everything it acquires is the original's. At its death, the Irai assesses **folding duty**. At **the folding**, the proxy hands everything back; by custom the original gives it a toy "for the childhood you did not have".
- **Limits:** memory only to the fork; ages diverge under dilation; the steel ring at the wrist (removing it is a crime).
- **Cost:** a proxy owns nothing and can be killed under a **vidai** (below).
- **Damages** Kayal and Valli. **Saves** Kayal and Kathir (mimicking her proxy lets Kayal hold the court long enough to act).
- **The proxies' bench:** proxies cast the assembly votes of banked originals, about sixty seats. They are a voting bloc with no rights of their own, and Minmini was their best-loved member.

### Aliens: the Vandu and the Crossing

- **Biology:** rooted **blooms** (*malar*), kilometre-high minds, and room-sized **carriers** shaped like seed-heads that fold between blooms. Human cargo rides packed in the pollen masses and is unpacked at the roost, and the pollen is returned to the floor for the bloom.
- **Polity: the Crossing.** The blooms deliberate by **envoy flights**, carriers that carry only a bloom's pollen, coated to signal its state (open, closed, grieving, readmitting). Humans called these "empty flights" for two centuries. By deliberation the Crossing sets which routes fly and how often: the post capacity, and so the price, of every world. It judges a world by how it treats carried things. It is not interested in human politics, money or marriages. It answers breaches as a body: a bloom whose crossings were kept closes; its route-partners may close in sympathy; it reopens when what was kept is returned, and then at a **reduced route share** for a time. That is a sanction, not forgiveness.
- **Want:** *that the crossing stay whole: nothing carried kept.* The want concerns their own reproduction and the integrity of the network, not humans.
- **Speech:** opening and closing; envoy coats; which worlds carriers land on. Never sound.
- **Limits:** carriers fly only between blooms; they carry nothing larger than a pollen mass and never people; a closed bloom receives nothing.
- **Cost:** the galaxy's information moves at the will of a polity that does not read contracts.
- **Pollen:** Kaanthal pollen in air is the pleasure world's euphoric. Pollen that has not aged as it should, because it was kept in the vault, is "too live", and in a sealed room causes **pollen-sleep**: euphoria, drowsiness, respiratory arrest.
- **Damages** Thumbi and the Roost Sangam's readers' bench. **Saves** Ponni and Minmini (the chamber must be open on the night).

### The Balance (*thulaakkol*)

- Shows looped, depth and weight for a physical thing. It shows **only the shallowest loop**, and a deeper loop appears after the shallower one is entered in the ledger with an author. Weighing a person needs consent or a warrant. Readings of others are published on the public board. **Self-readings are sealed** until the assessor's next ledger entry. It never says who or what.

### Law: the Doctrine, the purpose of closure, and the vidai

- **The Doctrine of the Unsigned (AS 0):** a closure by the house's inspection is the act of no one, "for no one can be shown to have closed it".
- **Purpose of closure:** a field on every lease form since before AS 0. Filling it in under your own name makes you the author of the closure, liable to duty and to law. No one ever has. Only a signer has **standing** to challenge the Doctrine in court.
- **The vidai (licence of foreseen loss, AS 31):** loss of property shown in a house-closed leaf is no one's damage, once registered with the Proprietor for a fee of a tenth of its value. It applies to proxies. It **never** applies to a person with standing: killing a free person is murder, foreseen or not. Aristocrats use vidai to "retire" inconvenient proxies; proxies on Ilavenil fear every gift.

### Ritual: the shadow ordeal (*nizhal-thervu*)

- The assembly casts silver (acquit) and bronze (convict) beads into a sealed **urn**. The late leaf goes into the ordeal cassette under the term *"expose at the hour of judgment if the urn holds a majority to convict"*. The Proprietor counts the urn without disclosing it. The accused develops her early leaf before the hour, and the leaf is the verdict. The urn is burned unopened ("the leaf for the verdict, the urn for the conscience").
- **The accused's priority:** she may lease her own cassette first, with the standard term or a **stricter** one (confession), never a looser one.
- **Sole-author rule:** an accused who convicts herself by a stricter term is the sole author of the act charged; co-accused are discharged as her instruments.
- **Sentence:** "sent to the slow", in Kuzhi berths the Irai rents. Accomplice, 10 years; defying a court order with desecration, 20; killing a person with standing, 40; killing a sitting Seal-holder, 60.

| **Berths and berth-notes** | **Paalai Slow Holdings** (a board of shareholders; Valli chairs; the "heir", whose sign is the house anklets, holds control) | Money does not grow faster in the vault; the depositor lives less while outside investments run on. About four thousand berths exist around one dark star. Berth-notes are claims on berth-years, the scarcest universally wanted asset, and so the reserve; their price, **the slow rate**, is the base rate. | **Old leases** (the archive no one admits) | No one, officially | A past lease of a room entitles its holder to the Proprietor's safety films of that room for that time: x-ray silhouettes identifiable by metal (thalis, anklets, toe-rings, seals, implants). | **Clockwork** | Toymakers' workshops | The only private machinery on Ilavenil: dead drops, timers, ciphers, toys, dead-man's boxes (which open if unwound for three days and burn their contents if forced) | Paavai's shop; Selvan's box |

### How the mystery moves the markets, and the markets move the mystery

1. The murder releases unaged hoard pollen, so the **Kaanthal closes**. The post through the hub stops, so no fresh carried film arrives, and the Shadow Market seizes.
2. Post-borne medicine stops, so **Aathan dies**, the Seal vote moves to the wedding morning, and **Thamarai resigns** to run.
3. The Crossing deliberates, and **Thinnai's bloom closes in sympathy**. The Irai's coupon cannot be sent and is **missed on Day −3**, so the Mawsim council sits to call the bonds. A call would force Paalai to redeem in berth-notes, exposing the hoard.
4. Thumbi's honest strata, and Saleha's turn, **defer the call** to the Seal vote, provided the Crossing readmits Ilavenil by its third envoy round.
5. **Returning the hoard** reopens the bloom, at a reduced route share, and saves the bonds. It also ends Paalai's long plates, the crown of the merger.
6. A **signed closure** gives the court standing; **the Doctrine and the vidai fall**; the haven closes; Neithal owes a decade's duty; the Irai and the fleet are flush; the Mawsim are paid; and the architect, Thamarai, wins the Seal.
7. **Aftermath:** berth-notes fall on the hoard scandal and Valli's arrest, then recover because the berths still exist, now without the long-plate premium. Paalai's board writes off the "inert stock", pays duty, and keeps Kayal as controlling holder on condition that berth prices are published. Two of Paalai's berths are rented by the Irai for its new prisoners.

## Polities and factions

Each has a belief, a want, what it would lose if the truth came out, and a look and sound.

### The Five Hundred: assembly, Seal and campaign

- **Structure:** five hundred kudi seats (firms of every size) sit in the round hall. Votes are beads dropped into brass. The **five great houses** named for the Sangam landscapes hold the largest blocs: **Paalai** (separation: berths), **Neithal** (anxious waiting: plates), **Kurinji** (the mountains: the Kaaval's shipyards; chair **Malaiyan**), **Mullai** (patient waiting: slow-lane carriage and insurance), **Marutham** (farmland: food and the proxy-growing clinics). The **proxies' bench** votes about sixty seats for banked originals. The five-hundredth seat is always empty (texture).
- **The Seal-holder** commands the Kaaval, appoints the Commissioner, charters routes and interprets the Doctrine.
- **The AS 212 campaign:** **Nedumaran** (the Neithal and Paalai merger bloc) against **Thamarai** (the Irai's allied houses, Kurinji's shipyards wanting fleet contracts, Mawsim money). It is visible from Act 2 on the ledger board as a bead count. **Mullai, Marutham and the proxies' bench** are the swing votes Kayal works.
- **Belief:** the Seal was first given by fate. **Want:** stability and trade. **Would lose:** the legitimacy of every Seal since AS 0.
- **Look and sound:** a round hall of lightboxes; the clack of beads in brass.

### Paalai Slow Holdings

- **Chair:** Valli. **Controlling holder ("heir"):** Kayal. **Proxy:** Minmini.
- **Belief:** Neithal rigged the first ordeal. **Want:** to be the senior partner in the merger, keep the long plate secret, and keep Kayal alive. **Would lose:** the hoard (an offence against the Crossing), confidence in the slow rate, and the truth about Minmini.
- **Look and sound:** black stone, white lamps, clocks that run slow on purpose, silver anklet bells. No desert imagery: Paalai means separation, not sand.

### Neithal Plate Company

- **Chair:** Nedumaran. **Chair-designate:** Kathir. **Runner:** Kuyil. **Shell:** Uppalam Salt Concern.
- **Belief:** a leaf is a contract with the world. **Want:** long plates exposed unsigned, and the Seal. **Would lose:** everything if the haven closes.
- **Look and sound:** salt-white rooms walled with lightboxes; the snap of leaves on glass.

### The Irai (revenue and intelligence)

- **Commissioner:** Thamarai (until Day −5), then Iniyan, acting. **Assessor:** Ponni. **Clerk:** Selvan.
- Assessors are also the Five Hundred's intelligence service. The Irai reads cadet correspondence.
- **Official belief:** the Doctrine is old law it regrets. **Kept truth:** its founders rigged the first ordeal. **Want:** close the haven and fund the fleet before the Mawsim call the debt, without opening its own founding. **Would lose:** its charter.
- **Look and sound:** ochre coats; brass seals on cords (**assessors' have a hole, clerks' are solid**); the thump of the certifying stamp.

### The Proprietor

- **Belief:** the Charter is its self. **Want:** an author for every entry. **Would lose:** nothing by the truth. **Look and sound:** kolam lines of light; a calm voice from brass grilles; the inspection hum.

### The Crossing (Vandu polity) and the Roost Sangam

- **The Crossing:** forty-one blooms deliberating by envoy. It wants the crossing whole. It treats human cargo as guests riding its pollen: humans may keep their own letters, but not the pollen that carried them. It would lose nothing by human truths. It speaks by opening, closing and envoy coats.
- **The Roost Sangam:** an assembly-chartered cooperative of unpackers and readers. Its chief reader, **Kaatravan**, is elected and venal. He sells "readings" of bloom behaviour to whoever pays, and Saleha's purser buys the omen of Day −6. **Thumbi** reads on its bench. **Belief:** that readers alone understand the Vandu. **Would lose:** the bench's authority, if envoy strata prove readable by anyone with a lens.
- **Look and sound:** dust-gold coats, pollen masks, the creak of ribs, the pressure drop of carriers folding in (felt in the ears, not heard as calls).

### The Mawsim

- A polity of slow ships descended from Indian Ocean sailors who traded with Tamil ports. They never adopted the post and live through outside decades in ship-months. Their **council of nakhodas** (captains) sits on the flagship *Kaaru*. **Envoy:** Nakhoda Saleha. **Belief:** time is the only honest collateral. **Want:** to be paid, and to stop any one house from owning the future market, which long plates would let it do. **Would lose:** the debt's value, if the Five Hundred fractured. **Saleha's turn:** she pays for the false omen on Day −6, then sees on Day −3 that a false reading will cost the Mawsim the debt, and backs Thumbi's honest strata in front of her own council.
- **Look and sound:** teak, brass astrolabes, monsoon charts; talk of news "decades ripe".

### Clockwork toymakers

- Neutral craftspeople who sell privacy to every faction. Paavai's shop is the busiest.
