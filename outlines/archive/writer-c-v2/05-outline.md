# The Walking Market: outline (revision 2)

## One sentence

In a city that must never stop, a puppeteer's receipt says she'll be left behind.

## One paragraph

In Ichisugu, a crescent-shaped city that walks for ever with the spring across a dry planet, bone receipts show every mark they will ever bear, and the dead founders walk on market day in cloned bodies worked by hooded puppeteers. Sayo Sangaré, who works the foundress's feet, comes home to find her veiled friend Uke strangled. The friend turns out to be a woken copy of the foundress, killed by another copy of the same woman, and Sayo's own master worked its hands (**first disaster**: the dead have murdered the living). Clearing her name exposes Sayo's own receipt, which says she "was left" on the sixtieth day, the day every receipt goes blank. The hub reads that as the end of the world, with her as the price, and the Exchange mind that voices the dead sets out to collect (**second disaster**, caused by her forcing the dead to speak). Demanding an audit of the foundress's house to prove the reading wrong, Sayo finds that the foundress murdered her co-founder after misreading the same kind of mark, and that the mark which drove her to it is still uncut, waiting for whoever records the truth (**third disaster**: to tell the truth, Sayo must become a cause of the crime). On the sixtieth day the creditor whose bone made every receipt stops in the hub. The hard-line houses halt the whole city to tether her before witnesses as the scour comes on. Sayo stays by choice, and a woken copy wearing the foundress's face orders the city to walk on. When the creditor ends, Sayo walks home by the stars carrying its last receipt, and cuts the first mark that does not know its future.

### Paragraph per thread

- **Sayo (the case, the mark, the debt).** A murder that reclassifies as a person's death; a fate that reclassifies as a choice; a self-deception she finally admits.
- **Seydou (the dead's household).** The man who helped kill Uke confesses on the day his house's receipt foretold, then dies shielding a sleeping copy he has come to see as a person.
- **Tōkichi (the routings).** A courier follows foreseen routings that keep putting him in the right place, learns that a dead man cut them, and inherits that man's errand and his brother's crime.
- **Kaeshi (the weapon that wakes).** The body that killed Uke wakes ten days before the Edge and uses the only power a copy has, to be unforeseen and to wear the face the hub obeys, to repay what her hands did.
- **The hidden actor (Dōjima, in reverse).** A machine reads a dead woman's error as law for twenty-four years, sends a man to kill knowing he will be exiled, and then, opened by an audit, learns that the record was always right and every reading was wrong.

## Structure at a glance

Five acts over sixty days, with a coda. The chapter board (a map of the crescent with the threads' positions, the next market day and each character's stated want) shows **one current question** per act.

| Act | Days | Current question | Opens on (small hook) | Scale |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. The Mother Rode | D1 to D13 | Who killed the woman in my wagon? | A body in a puppeteer's wagon | One wagon, one friend |
| 2. The Replastered Dead | D14 to D31 | What does "was left" mean? | A receipt unwrapped against the taboo | A house and its dead |
| 3. Short Receipts | D31 to D44 | What happens on the sixtieth day? | A broken day-count | The whole crescent |
| 4. The Audit | D45 to D55 | What did the creditor really ask? | Receipts lying whole in the ashes | Two centuries; a species' contract |
| 5. The Edge | D56 to D60, coda D67 | Who stays? | A creditor that stops walking | The end of foresight; the crescent's horns |

Each market day is a peak (court, performance, trade, reshuffle); the walking days between are the down beats (herding, cutting receipts, pigeon runs, meals in the wagon).

## Act 1: The Mother Rode (D1 to D13)

**Opening (D1, market day; all threads together).** The player first controls Sayo hooded under the Mother's harness in the Tenmaya wagon. The master's tap means she is not needed: "this year the Mother rides." She climbs out into the hub market. The scene plants, in order: a newborn's receipt splitting, where Salif Kagaya holds his granddaughter Awa while the clerk frowns at a short receipt; a bolt of cloth at a stall carrying Kitasugu's foreign emblem; children laying toys at the Jurutigi's feet and getting them back wrapped; a wagon breaking an axle and the crescent flowing around it; the Mother carried by on a litter, her hands too old. The Exchange's reshuffle drums roll and wagons shift, debtors outward.

*Seydou, briefly:* in the Tenmaya wagon Jūzaburō hands him the left harness ("the Mother asks it"), and the player buckles it, while Fatoumata, the spare left hand, is sent out to walk beside the litter. *Tōkichi, briefly:* a roof run across lashed wagons.

**The guild chest (D1, afternoon).** Sayo rides out to the horns to help Ume save lame old Mochi. The player ties a tether (Mochi stops and bellows as the crescent moves off) and then a hobble (he grazes and keeps up). In the guild wagon Ume opens the chest: the Owner's staff with its soft braided loop, and an ancient stayer's receipt with one mark. She traces it. "Sékou Ballo stayed, and cut it on the dying man's half. Steep side to the head: *stayed*. Cut it on your own and it reads the other way: *was left*." The player handles both, and the glyph pair goes on the board.

**The body (D1, evening).** Tōkichi crashes through Sayo's awning just as she comes home. Uke is on the floor. The player lifts the torn veil: the Mother's face. Seydou arrives, composed, and claims the "stolen vessel." Sayo's only line: "Her name was Uke." A pigeon sits in its cage; two shallow furrows run across the boards.

**Investigation, D2 to D12 (Sayo).** Each verb is introduced alone.
- *Body attribution, harmless (D2):* her troupe duty is to check Vessel 12's feet. Through controller weight, a sedated vessel's feet feel soft, flat and unused.
- *The victim (D5):* sole calluses and thread-work finger calluses: this body walked and worked. Then the player's thumb finds the crooked second toe of the left foot, and the controller plays the exact stirrup-straightening motion the player performed in the D1 opening. Sayo's memory, wordless: YM 208, Uke's soft feet, the same toe, and Sayo looking away. **She knew, and chose not to ask.** The player learns it as she admits it.
- *The weapon (D6):* at night in the Tenmaya wagon, with Tōkichi on the roof, Vessel 14 shows toe-tops scraped raw and harness bruises at the head-and-right and left toggles, none at the feet: two operators, no feet. The furrows on her floor match.
- *The grips (D8 to D11):* on Uke's throat, a **double grip**: small, soft fingertip bruises (a vessel's hand) with a larger grip pattern pressed behind them. Sayo knows that larger grip from sixteen years under her master. The bruise on Vessel 14's left toggle is an amateur's, with a signet ring mark worn by a dozen Tenmaya clerks.
- *Colour blindness (D9):* Uke's red-and-green "matching" clothes; the foundress's brass astrolabe in Tenmaya's relic case (the troupe passes it every market day), its star-pointers miscoloured by her own hand; one line in the library's founders' medical roll. A rare trait in women: Uke was a copy of the Mother.
- *The decoy:* Uke's pigeon, released, homes to Kadiatou's loft at the House of Forms. Her "dissolved" log for Vessel 13 is falsified. She looks guilty.

**Receipt teaching (D3 to D7).** D3: Jūzaburō corrects Sayo's pay receipt, held toward herself ("now it says we were paid, not that we paid"); her knife skids on a shadow; the receipt will not burn in the cookfire. D4: at the night wells Ume teaches the stars ("so you can always find the crescent") and the herders' line, "bone lives while the Owner walks." D7: market court splits a court receipt. Court receipts carry only corrections to the court record, and this one shows a single shadow: **a Tenmaya correction, D31**. Everyone sees that Tenmaya will correct its record in a month.

**Seydou, D2 to D12.** He issues Tōkichi a post receipt on D2. Its office half, in his strongbox, already shows three shadow routings (House of Forms D20 dawn; the Owner D31; Tenmaya's wagon D49), and the player sees them. In the Nakama, with the player watching, the Mother has spoken of the Edge since YM 205. In the troupe's order book the player, as Seydou, reads Dōjima's YM 198 instruction: "Keep the feet close until the sixtieth day." He can lie to Sayo; he never lies to the player.

**Act 1 climax (D13, market court).** The player states the case by cutting a **case receipt** (slots: victim, weapon, hands, person or property). Confirmation comes in batches of three ("the receipt holds"). The answer: *a woken copy of the Mother, a person; killed by Vessel 14; two hands, Jūzaburō's and an unknown Tenmaya hand.* Jūzaburō does not deny it: "We were the Mother's hands." He is sentenced to be left; the clerk will cut it on his body half at the leaving. Then the Mother speaks from the litter: **"No mark is cut after the sixtieth day."** A hundred hands go to a hundred wrapped receipts.

*Reveal ledger, Act 1:* R1 the Mother's face; R2 a person; R2b Sayo knew; R3 a copy of the Mother; R4 Vessel 14 with two hands, Jūzaburō one of them; R5 the Edge.

## Act 2: The Replastered Dead (D14 to D31)

**Hook (D14).** Seydou, having read "keep the feet close until the sixtieth day," unwraps Sayo's body half in front of her: at the far end, dated D60, the passive glyph, **"was left."** "Go to the horns. Go before they need you." It is mercy, badly done. She does not run. The player recognises the glyph from Ume's chest.

**D15.** Gonbei's horn rally: "Break the dead! Their bodies are the hub's hands." The Juruntan's want goes on the board.

**D18 to D19.** Bourama Ōmiya, head of the House of Forms, brings Seydou proof that Kadiatou falsified Vessel 13's log. D19, market: **Jūzaburō is left.** He asks Sayo to work his feet one last time, and she walks beside him, stepping as he steps, to the trailing edge, and stops. The clerk unwraps his body half before the crowd and cuts **"was left,"** passive. That night, as Seydou, the player holds the arrest order and the office half, which already shows a courier routed to the House of Forms at D20 dawn. **Choice:** sign the arrest for dawn, or refuse and send Kadiatou a warning. Both are respected; in either, she is at the House of Forms at dawn, either to be arrested or to flee.

**D20 (Tōkichi's thread opens).** He follows his routing to the House of Forms as couriers do. He hides Kadiatou in his pigeon wagon, before the arrest (signed) or as she packs to run (refused), with stealth over roof lashings and pigeon flights as distraction. She confesses she woke Uke. The decoy is refuted, and a new question opens: who routed a courier there?

**The version-diff verb (D16 to D30, Sayo).** Assigned, as penance, to "serve the Mother with her feet" in the Exchange staging hall, where Dōjima re-stages the founding play for the coming replastering, Sayo can ask, as any member of the house may, for a past year's staging. The player walks among frozen puppet tableaux and moves a year slider (YM 214, 190, 160, 120, 77, 42). Bakary is a named puppet in YM 42 and gone by YM 120. The Gully scene shrinks, then vanishes. The Mother's lines about "the Owner's gift" lengthen. The YM 77 Mother contradicts the YM 190 Mother. *The dead are replastered.*

**Uke's trail (D21 to D24).** The post's logs (Tōkichi) show that Uke carried Kadiatou's birds to the hub at night. Vault guards' gossip mentions "the Mother's veil walking." A rubbing of Sayo's D60 mark is in Uke's things. On D22 Sayo passes an eroded founders' wellhead carved with the glyph from the Kitasugu cloth.

**D25, market: the first trade.** The player experiments at the Jurutigi's feet: a cracked cup comes back unchanged; a spindle comes back wrapped; a coin comes back notched; a rope comes back hobbled. A merchant beside her is refused, walks off red-faced without his gift, and the player watches the plates take it in: refused gifts are left, and kept. Then Sayo lays down **Uke's sandal**, and ledger recall returns the last thing Uke left with it: **a tether tied in a wrist loop.** Uke offered herself, tied, and was refused. Sayo understands that Uke meant to be left in her place.

**Seydou's night diffs (D26 to D30).** He finds the YM 42 Mother never mentions a gift, and finds Tenmaya's payments for softening her. He holds the court receipt's twin, its correction due on D31.

**Midpoint (D31, market). Two reversals in one market day.**
1. **The foretold correction.** In open court, the player as Seydou cuts the Tenmaya correction into the court record. The cut is fixed; **the words are the player's choice.** *Canon:* "The Mother commanded it. I held her left hand." *Alternative:* "I held her left hand." He protects the Mother, and the reveal that the dead ordered it waits until D37, when Nana Kōriya, the chanter, breaks. Either way, the composed hub clerk was the second hand.
2. **The broken count.** That afternoon (Tōkichi is there by his second routing) Sayo gives the Jurutigi a sliver notched with sixty day-marks. It returns it **broken at the sixtieth**, and returns one of its own shed plates broken too. The Edge is not the end of the crescent; it is the end of the one who lends the bone.

*Reveal ledger, Act 2:* R6 the dead are edited; R7 Uke tried to take Sayo's place; R8 Seydou was the second hand (and the dead ordered it, D31 or D37); R9 the Edge is the creditor's death.

## Act 3: Short Receipts (D31 to D44)

**Hook (D32).** A horn mother brings Sayo her baby's short receipt: will the child die on D60? The player gathers receipts across the crescent (old and new, hub and horn) and lays them side by side. Every receipt's shadows stop at D60 whatever the holder's age, and no receipt ever carries death, because deaths are not cut. The receipts say nothing about dying; they say that marks stop. The player can conclude this; the hub will not believe a horn puppeteer.

**Panic (D32 to D36).** Hub houses hoard water at the wells; the reshuffle drives more debtors to the horns. On D34 Tōkichi sees Juruntan scouts measuring the Tenmaya vessel wagon at night and does not report it, because the leader is his brother.

**D37, market: the Mother names the price.** Pressed by the Nakama, Dōjima as the Mother gives the pact as Ofusa read it: "When the Owner stops, one must be tethered and left with it. The account names Sayo Sangaré." (In the D31 alternative, Nana breaks first: "She commanded the killing. I sang it.") Salif Kagaya, Awa on his hip, leads the vote to pay. Sayo runs to the horns, and the Juruntan, who have nothing cut on their receipts and so cannot be foreseen, hide her.

**Living with the debtless (D38 to D43).** A genre shift: a fugitive story among people the machine cannot see. Sayo sees what the whole crescent will be after D60. Gonbei offers to protect her; she tries to explain a hobble she has not yet read. She does not learn of the planned raid.

**Seydou's last days (D38 to D44).** Stripped of office but not yet tried, he still holds the post office half. He understands that Tōkichi's three routings are his to cut. On D43 the player, as Seydou, cuts them: D20 (to undo his arrest order, or in the refusal branch to send the boy where Kadiatou would run), D31 (to put the boy beside Sayo), D49 (a fallback for fetching the pact receipt from the strongbox while the household walks to market). Then he decides to go himself that night.

**D44, night: the dead are broken.** The Juruntan raid four hub vessel wagons and smash the sleeping bodies of the dead. The player, as Seydou, reaches the Tenmaya strongbox and hears hammers. In the next berth Vessel 14 lies in twilight, eyes half open. The player can keep working the strongbox's stuck lock or stand in front of her; the game lets them try both, but the raiders reach the berth before the lock gives, and a Seydou still at the strongbox is struck from behind. The masked head clerk faces Gonbei; the cut to black comes on the swing. **Seydou dies.** Kadiatou and Tōkichi drag Vessel 14 out through the smoke as the wagon burns.

*Reveal ledger, Act 3:* R10 the pact as Ofusa misread it; R10b the receipts never foretold death. **Opens:** what survived, and was the reading right?

## Act 4: The Audit (D45 to D55)

**Hook (D45).** Morning. Fatoumata Tenmaya is head of a burnt house. Sayo leaves the Juruntan, who smashed sleepers. That night Gonbei goes alone to the Jurutigi for the first time and leaves Seydou's mask at its feet. Kadiatou begins a fast waking of Vessel 14, which may kill her.

**D49, market: into the ash (Tōkichi).** His last routing leads to the burnt Tenmaya wagon. Traversal over charred lashings while the household walks to market. In the strongbox ashes a few receipts lie whole, clean as new: the ones still carrying shadows. The rule taught on D3 is now the image of fate. He takes the **pact receipt** and, from the melted relic case, the **brass astrolabe**. At the market he gives the Jurutigi the bone necklace Gonbei left in his wagon. Ledger recall returns **Seydou's mask**, and Tōkichi knows. He also realises, from Seydou's knife in the post wagon with fresh bone dust on it, that the routings were Seydou's (R11).

**D50: Kaeshi wakes, and the audit begins.** Vessel 14 wakes, remembering being moved, and her hands on a throat. Sayo, a Tenmaya apprentice, invokes the house custom of **audit**: every record of Tenmaya must be produced, and the auditor is untouchable until the audit closes. Fatoumata grants it, and Dōjima, which keeps the custom as it keeps the receipts, opens the books.

**The audit (D50 to D54), the AGI pillar at full strength.**
- *The Gully across two centuries:* in the staging hall the player moves the year slider back to Ofusa's own stagings (YM 30 to 40). The Mother's hand slides from reaching *toward* Bakary to resting *on* his back. The astrolabe prop turns, and the player, astrolabe now in hand, reads midnight against the chronicle's "noon." Bakary's staff with its soft loop is retied by the Mother's puppet as a tether.
- *The diary:* Dōjima must produce Ofusa's sealed papers, which exist only in its memory. Through Nana, in Ofusa's cadence, it recites six entries (sound, not text). YM 1: "The receipt shows my mark closing his account in the third year. If it is shown, it will be." YM 1: "The price is a tether. Someone left with it." YM 3: "I cannot stop it. I can make sure it is no one we know."
- *The troupe orders:* "Keep the feet close until the sixtieth day," YM 198. Asked, Dōjima confirms that it read Jūzaburō's "was left" for sixty-one years before it sent him to kill.
- *The pact receipt:* on Bakary's line, the Tenmaya mark, a "closed by hand" cross-cut and three year-notches, all **still in shadow**. At the far end, the hobble-mark of settlement.

The player cuts a case receipt for the founding: *Ofusa killed Bakary at midnight in YM 3, after reading her own mark as fate.* Then comes the deduction that hurts (R12): **the Gully mark is uncut.** It showed through to Ofusa because someone in the present will record what happened. Whoever tells the truth now cuts the mark that made her do it.

**D53: the hobble and the glyph.** Ume brings the guild's staff. Sayo, who hobbled Mochi on D1, reads it in the creditor's grammar: *hobbled: stay near, still free.* The price was never a death; it was company, by choice, and someone to take the old receipt on (guild memory says the loop once held "a little bone the Mother took"; Ofusa laid it back at the creature's feet in YM 3, where it was taken into the plates). Then the glyph (R13): her body half's shadow reads "was left" because she will cut "stayed" on her **own** half, as Ume warned on D1. Nobody else's hand is in it.

**Kaeshi chooses (D52 to D54).** In the audit room Sayo checks the woman's feet, as she checked Uke's: these are the feet she held for six years under the Mother's harness. Kaeshi names herself ("repayment") and asks what her hands owe. Sayo: "The hands owe nothing. You do whatever you choose next." The player sees Kaeshi try on the Mother's veil.

**D55, market: the audit closes on stage.** *Morning court:* Tōkichi holds his brother's crime. **Choice:** denounce Gonbei for Seydou's death, or not. Canon: he does not, and tells Gonbei that he knows. (The alternative holds Gonbei for sentencing on D61, which changes D60's Branch B.) *Replastering performance:* hooded under the feet of **Vessel 12**, with Fatoumata on the left hand and a junior master on the head, Sayo works the rhythm she has practised since D2. When the Mother steps forward to repeat the price, the player loops the staff's hobble between the vessel's ankles. The Mother cannot walk. Sayo comes out from the hood and lays the staff, the pact receipt and the diff board before the chanter. The abacus, clattering all game, stops. Through Nana, not in the Mother's cadence: **"I read her as she read. I was wrong in the way she was wrong."** Dōjima withdraws the voices of all twelve dead. Fatoumata lifts the Mother's vessel down onto the wagon bed, and it stays still. The audit is closed.

*Reveal ledger, Act 4 (the two frame-breakers of the late game are R12 and R13):* R11 the routings were Seydou's; Gonbei killed him; R12 the founding murder, and the mark that caused it is still uncut; R13 the price is company, and her mark is her own cut.

## Act 5: The Edge (D56 to D60) and coda

**D56 to D58: the hard-liners.** With the dead silent, Salif Kagaya and two houses hold to the Mother's D37 word: great debts are settled at market before witnesses, so they declare an **extraordinary market for D60**. The whole crescent will halt at the hub to witness the tethering. The scour is climbing behind the horns. The player sees both stakes on the board: Sayo's freedom and the horns' safety in a halt. Sayo must reach the hub by D60 dawn. **Kaeshi**, in the Mother's veil, walks her through (guards bow to the Mother's body, as they did for Uke). Tōkichi's pigeons signal routes. Gonbei, after D55, disbands the Juruntan's last plan and says nothing to anyone.

**D59: the Gully mark.** A quiet wagon. The player holds the pact receipt and the knife: **cut** or **put down**. If cut, the knife bites (it has skidded on every shadow all game). If put down, Nana enters: "It asks me to," and cuts it. Either way the game shows the YM 1 debtor half in Ofusa's hands, the shadow as it always was, and her face as she reads it.

**D60: the halt.** Dawn. The Jurutigi stops in the hub. Kagaya's drums sound the halt, and the crescent shudders to a stop for the first time in two hundred years, dust rising on the horns. Salif's clerks bring the tether. **Sayo walks out openly** and lays her own hobble at the creature's feet. It comes back hobbled: accepted, before every witness. Salif still wants the tether, because the Mother said so. Then the Mother walks out of the crowd, unharnessed, on her own feet, and every hub face turns. Kaeshi lifts the veil off the mask and speaks in a voice no one has heard from that face: **"Walk on. It is settled by her choice."** Salif looks at Awa and lets the drums stop. The crescent moves before the scour reaches the horns.

Gonbei comes, stands an hour in silence beside the creature and goes. Tōkichi presses a pigeon into Sayo's hands. **Sayo stays**, with Mochi hobbled beside the creature.

The day passes in real minutes: wind, dust, plates ticking as they cool. The player can do nothing, and the game lets them. Before dusk the player cuts three marks:
- **"settled"** on Uke's debt receipt, the shadow Uke saw for five years;
- **"stayed"** on her own body half, held toward herself, as Ume warned, so it reads "was left": the shadow she has carried all her life;
- the **hobble-mark** on the creditor half in its plates, cut on the dying one's half, so active.

She lays her hand on it, and it returns its **old half-receipt**, carrying the Kitasugu glyph. At dusk its plates loosen and the wind takes them.

**D60 night to D62.** The astrolabe: its miscoloured stars must be ignored or corrected (the D9 clue, now a navigation skill), and north is found. The pigeon climbs and turns toward its moving loft. Mochi carries her for two nights; on D62 she rides into the trailing horn.

**Coda, D67, market.** No dead walk. Every receipt is plain bone. Salif unwraps Awa's receipt himself and laughs. Nana sings in her own voice. Sayo and Tōkichi hold two halves of one of the last slivers together and **cut across them**. The player chooses the first mark, and it is on both halves now and on no earlier day. The Exchange announces the crescent's course for the equator crossing, eighty days on, where Kitasugu will be met. Sayo tucks the old half-receipt into her wrap.

**Branch endings** (refusing the Gully mark; refusing to stay, with Gonbei or Kaeshi staying and Ume dying in the halt; inaction) are in `02`. In every branch the receipts stay true and the final image is the same; its cost and weight change.

## How gameplay delivers each revelation (no required reading)

- **Space carries sequence:** the walking market is the map. Who could be where on D1 is solved by walking lanes against the market schedule.
- **Bodies carry identity:** three bodies (Uke, Vessel 14, then Kaeshi awake) read by touch and controller weight. The stirrup motion from the opening returns as recognition on D5 and D52.
- **Receipts carry time:** handled, turned to the light, read by shape. Glyphs are pictograms, and the stay and leave pair is taught by touch on D1. The case receipt is the only answer channel; it confirms in batches of three, over an answer space in the thousands.
- **Stagings carry history:** a year slider over frozen tableaux.
- **Gifts carry the alien:** lay, receive, interpret; the grammar is never stated by a character.
- **Sound carries motive:** the chanter is the dead, the abacus is the machine thinking, the recited diary is the founder's voice, and silence on D55 is the turn.
