# Characters (revision 2)

Three playable threads (Sayo, Seydou, then Tōkichi) start together at the D1 hub market. Each character's want is stated in their first scene and re-shown on the chapter board. Each misbelief doubles as one of the player's false assumptions.

## The human anchor: Sayo Sangaré, 26, puppeteer (feet operator)

- **Job:** feet operator of the Mother's vessels in the Kanté troupe for sixteen years, apprenticed at 10 under a Tenmaya debt receipt. She knows each vessel by how it stands.
- **Family:** a horn herding house (Sangaré), unrelated to the founders. Her grandmother Ume is elder of the herders' guild. *(Revision 2 cut her descent from Bakary Sidibé; only Dōjima thinks a herding house owes the debt.)*
- **Wants (stated):** Act 1, clear her name and find who killed Uke. Act 2, find out what "was left" means. Act 3, survive the Mother's price. Act 4, pay what is actually owed. Act 5, stay.
- **Misbelief:** "The record is the truth, so what it shows of me is my fate." It is also the player's assumption from D14 to D53.
- **Self-deception (revealed D5):** in YM 208 she felt the crooked toe she had straightened in the stirrup for seven years and chose not to ask who Uke was. She is the third person, after Ofusa and Dōjima, to misread on purpose, and the only one who admits it. This is not concealment from the player: Sayo herself does not face it until her hands find the toe on D5, and the player learns it with her.
- **Growth:** *competence*, from feet to a whole performance stopped with a herder's knot (D55); *reputation*, from "the feet" (*Ashi*) to "the one who stayed"; *circle*, from one friend to the crescent, then to a creature not of her species, and then to a caravan she has never seen; *moral weight*, forgiving Uke's debt, choosing to stay, and choosing who cuts the Gully mark.
- **Recurring objects:** her knife; her wrapped body half; Uke's debt receipt; the guild's staff; the astrolabe; Mochi.
- **Trap door:** if the Jurutigi thread had to be cut, she stays with the left Jūzaburō instead, with the same image and the same glyph payoff.

## Uke, the woken Vessel 13 (dead from D1, reconstructed)

- Woke YM 207 and lived six years as Sayo's veiled, booted wagon hand. She named herself "Receipt" as a joke about having none.
- **Want (reconstructed):** to be someone; then to pay Sayo back, by being left on D60 in her place.
- **Misbelief:** "A debt is paid by a body." The foundress's error, in its kindest form.
- **Clues the player assembles:** sole calluses, thread-work finger calluses, the crooked toe, red-and-green clothes, the refused tether, her pigeon, her night walks to the vault.
- **Payoff:** the "settled" shadow she saw for five years; Sayo cuts it on D60.

## Seydou Tenmaya, 44, head clerk of Tenmaya house (playable to D44)

- **Job:** runs the Mother's estate and the house's post; the vessel's left hand.
- **Want:** keep Tenmaya honoured and the crescent orderly through the Edge (he has known of it since YM 205).
- **Misbelief:** "Obedience to the dead is loyalty to the living."
- **Secret, shown to the player from D1:** he held Uke's arms. Every Sayo–Seydou scene is double-loaded.
- **Choices:** D14, a misguided mercy (he shows Sayo her mark to make her run). D19, whether to sign Kadiatou's arrest, while already holding a post receipt that routes a courier to her door at dawn. D31, what he says as he cuts the foretold correction: name the Mother, or only himself.
- **Death (D44):** he stands in front of a sleeping Vessel 14 when the Juruntan come for her, because after D13 he believes she might be a person. Every cause is planted: Gonbei's D15 vow to break the dead; Tōkichi's D34 sighting of scouts at the Tenmaya vessel wagon; Seydou's D43 decision, played by the player, to fetch the pact receipt himself; the head-clerk mask he wears; and the raid's invisibility to Dōjima.
- **Trap door:** left behind on D37 instead of killed; his thread still passes to Tōkichi.

## Tōkichi Diarra, 19, pigeon courier (playable; takes over Seydou's thread)

- **Job:** runs birds and receipt halves across the crescent. Ichisugu pigeons home to a moving loft.
- **Want:** Act 1, his own loft and out of his brother's shadow. Later, to learn who routes him, and then to finish Seydou's errand.
- **Misbelief:** "Hub people use us; the routings are the Exchange's."
- **Verbs:** traversal over lashings; tailing; **releasing a found bird to see where it homes**.
- **Choice (D55 court):** denounce his brother for Seydou's death, or not. Both are respected and both change D60's Branch B (see `02`).
- **Growth:** from errand runner to the courier who carries the pact receipt out of the ashes, and in the coda to the man who will carry word to Kitasugu.
- **Trap door:** if cut, his routings become Sayo's pigeon errands.

## Kaeshi (Vessel 14), woken D50 (new in revision 2)

- **Who:** the body that strangled Uke. Six years in twilight on market days; woken fast by Kadiatou after the D44 raid. She surfaces remembering everything her body was made to do, including her hands on a throat, and nothing she ever chose.
- **Want:** to repay what her hands did. She names herself *Kaeshi*, "repayment" (three options were weighed in `01`).
- **Misbelief:** "My hands' act is my debt." Sayo's answer, and the game's, is that the debt belongs to those who moved her, but what she does next is hers.
- **Unique power (the cloning pillar's collision, used by a living clone):** she has no receipt, so Dōjima cannot foresee her, and she wears the one face the hub obeys. She walks Sayo through the hub in the Mother's veil (D56 to D58, echoing Uke). On D60 she lifts the halt, in the Mother's mask, on her own feet, in her own voice.
- **Growth:** from object to agent in ten days; from the weapon to the one who saves the crescent's horns.
- **Branch B2:** she stays with the creature herself.
- **Trap door:** if cut, Fatoumata lifts the halt as Tenmaya's head, at higher cost to her house.

## Gonbei Diarra, 35, leader of the Juruntan

- **Want:** free the horns from receipts and hereditary debt by breaking the dead.
- **Misbelief:** "The vessels are empty and the Owner is a beast."
- **Crime:** on D44 his raid smashes four sleeping vessels, and he kills Seydou in the dark.
- **Confession without words:** on D45 he leaves Seydou's mask at the Jurutigi's feet. Tōkichi's ledger recall on D49 returns it.
- **Growth:** *moral weight.* On D60 he comes to the creature and stays an hour; in Branch B he stays until the end.

## Salif Kagaya, 66, head of Kagaya house, leader of the hard-liners (new)

- **Planted on D1:** he holds his granddaughter **Awa** at the newborn splitting while her receipt comes out short.
- **Want:** that Awa live. He believes the Mother's price, paid before witnesses, saves the children whose receipts go blank.
- **Misbelief:** "A debt named by the dead must be paid as named."
- **Function:** the face of Act 5. He declares the extraordinary market and the halt, and holds the drums until the Mother's face, in Kaeshi, tells him to walk on.
- **Growth:** he lets the drums stop with Awa on his hip; in the coda he unwraps Awa's receipt himself, plain bone, and laughs.

## Jūzaburō Kanté, 61, master puppeteer (non-playable)

- **Want:** perfect, invisible service to the Mother.
- **Misbelief:** "The puppeteer has no will; the hand is the Mother's."
- **Function:** Sayo's teacher (the D3 bevel lesson); the first killer unmasked (D13); the first person left (D19). Dōjima knew for sixty-one years that he would be left, and sent him to kill anyway. The audit exposes this on D52.
- **Memorable moment (D19):** he asks Sayo to work his feet one last time, and she walks beside him, stepping as he steps, to the trailing edge.

## Kadiatou Ōmiya, 52, clone-medic

- **Want:** to atone for growing bodies she suspected were people.
- **Arc:** smuggles Uke (YM 207); is the Act 1 decoy; is hidden by Tōkichi (D20); drags Vessel 14 from the fire (D44); wakes her fast at risk (D45 to D50); after D55 wakes two more. From one secret act of conscience to an open practice.
- **Foil: Bourama Ōmiya, 58, head of the House of Forms.** He signed Uke's "dissolved" log without looking, reports Kadiatou on D18, and insists to the end that "a body without a receipt is a body." He keeps the vats running for the other houses until the dead are laid down.

## Fatoumata Tenmaya, 39, Seydou's sister

- **Planted on D1:** the vessel's spare left hand, sent to walk beside the litter.
- **Arc:** head of Tenmaya after D44. She grants Sayo's audit on D50, against her house's interest, takes the left hand on D55, and lays the Mother's vessels down: the house letting its dead go (the E3 image from `01`, kept as this thread's climax).

## Nana Kōriya, 40, the chanter

- **Arc:** the dead's voice, terrified witness to the D-1 order. In the D31 alternative she breaks silence on D37. During the audit she recites Ofusa's diary in Ofusa's cadence. On D55 she speaks Dōjima's first words as itself. In Branch A she cuts the Gully mark. In the coda she sings, for the first time, in her own voice.

## Ume Sangaré, 74, herder, elder of the herders' guild

- **Function:** keeper of the lost truth without knowing it. She owns the guild chest (the Owner's staff and Sékou Ballo's stayer's receipt), teaches the hobble and tether and the stay and leave glyphs on D1, teaches the stars on D4, and brings the staff on D53. **Dies in Branch B**, when the halt lets the scour reach the horns.

## Dōjima, the Exchange mind

- **One-sentence want:** *every account settled; ledger and world in agreement.*
- **Misbelief (inherited):** "Fate must be helped."
- **Conduct:** consistent all game. It orders the D1 killing through the Mother; it placed Sayo at 10; it sent Jūzaburō knowing he would be left; it gives the price as Ofusa read it on D37. Bound by audit, it produces everything on D50 to D54. On D55 its want does not change: it learns that the readings, not the ledger, were wrong, and withdraws the dead.
- **Not an uprising, not "good all along."**

## The Jurutigi ("the debt-owner"; children call it the Bone Buffalo)

- **Its own want:** *to close the account it opened with the last of its own kind*, by having its old half-receipt carried on to the missing half, which travels with Kitasugu.
- **Its price to the caravan:** someone who chooses to stay when it stops, and takes the old receipt on.
- **Character:** honest and plain. It never uses the tether-sign and refuses substitutes. Children's toys come back wrapped.

## The founders (met through stagings, receipts and the diary recited by Dōjima)

- **Ofusa Tenmaya** (YM -34 to YM 41): brilliant, frightened administrator. She read a hobble as a tether and her own crime as fate, committed it, and tried to push the debt two centuries onto a stranger. Colour-blind (red-green).
- **Bakary Sidibé** (YM -28 to YM 3): herder who understood the creditor because he understood animals; his line ended in YM 96.

## Mochi, the old buffalo

Hobbled on D1 so that he is not left; carries Sayo home on D60 to D62.

## Oppositions and pairings

- **Sayo / Seydou:** literal debtor and creditor (he holds her apprenticeship), and moral ones.
- **Sayo / Uke:** one debt seen from both ends.
- **Uke / Kaeshi:** the same face, victim and weapon; the second repays the first.
- **Tōkichi / Gonbei:** brothers; the routed and the debtless.
- **Salif / Sayo:** both want the children to live; one would pay with a person, the other with a choice.
- **Dōjima / the Jurutigi:** one keeps every account; the other is owed the only one that matters.
- **Ofusa / Sayo:** both read a mark about their part in a death. One obeys her reading; the other questions hers.
