## 5. Open risks

- **Climax density.** Ch20 now cross-cuts five beats in nine minutes. Mitigation: each POV has one verb (speak, run, assemble, choose, file), the tide clock is shared, and every beat was rehearsed earlier (route in Ch19, grammar in Ch14, Division slots since Ch2). Must be tested.
- **Legal mechanics on screen.** The recusal, the seal lapse and the clause are each one sentence and each planted, but three legal beats across the game could still feel procedural. Test that players feel them as character choices (the Son steps down; Verhoeven reads her own words).
- **Rule load.** Six critical-path terms remain; the Tidings Law is taught by a bilge-pump joke and need not be named.
- **Scale of low-text mechanics over a long game** is unproven. Prototype the Greeting Wall, the 983 hole and the Turning first.
