# Writer C: key scene sketches (revision 2)

Each sketch shows what the player **does** and **sees**, with almost no reading. A line marked "Says:" is voiced.

---

## 1. Opening: Tea Through the Wall (Ch 1, D0)

**Sees.** A narrow cell, lamplight, a black lacquered back wall. Tamoun's left forearm is green-veined. A porcelain cup with a crack, and steam. A pale vine noses out of a hairline crack in the wall.

**Does.** The vine leaves a slip on her wrist. The player turns the forearm to it, and touch-view opens: growth lines, a bud, a tilt. The player answers by choosing a slip from the sill pots (by species), a cut angle and a placement. The vine curls away satisfied, and on its way it leans toward the cup. Zooming in shows a green hair-line in the porcelain crack. Nothing happens. Tamoun knocks twice on the wall, and the vine twitches once.

**The hall.** The lacquered union, a waist-high swelling of black trunk with one bare palm-sized **reading port**. The player reads Paopi's slip through the port: *ours / ready / yours?* Through the port, on the Bnne face, a bud is swelling: **their shoot**. Paopi prunes an old greeting slip to make room, then turns to Pjol and visibly gropes for his name until its palm touches the cut slip again (a small laugh in the hall). The king, unmantled, lays his palm on the lacquer for the rehearsal: tall, with a pause on the third step. A reader recites from a sherd: **Says:** "…renewed at the Wall by the Ouro's hand."

**The court.** Kyra pays for the tea with counting beads, her cane tapping. Her younger self, gloved, signs the bill for her. **Maro says:** "What do the Bnne call a second?" Tamoun has no answer. Horsi of Pemdje, loud, raises a cup "to the end of debt".

**Phib's lesson.** A practice slip at the port. A clean cut: it lives. A pull: it blackens. **Says:** "Never pull. Cut. Clean. Here." Then: "And when a Bnne meets a stranger, the first thing it grafts is *near*."

## 2. The Murder in the Dust (Ch 3, D1 at 02:00, as Pamin)

**Sees.** A hundred dust-screens. The treaty hall at 23:00, frozen light.

**Does.** The player scrubs. Pjol, alone, kneels at the port; his hand disappears into a small dark bubble where the bare wood eats the dust. At 23:16 a **person-shaped absence** walks in. Pjol turns, relieved, and speaks to it. At 23:18 he is jerked backward by nothing. The player sees the ragged strip of green tissue come away and the old man fall. At 23:41 seven sparks flare.

**The choice.** A caption tray: *at home* or *killed*. A mood gauge for the realm. Pamin's lenses reflect the absence. **Says (to himself):** "Majesty. Why?"

## 3. The Queen's Walk (Ch 7, D10, as Pamin; the Act 1 reveal by play)

**Does.** Pamin buys dust from fifty years ago: the signing, Ouro Tsenoute at 52 in white, walking to the union. He opens a second pane: Tamoun at 34, walking into the hall on D2. He lays them side by side and slides an **age dial** on the queen until the faces converge, then sets a **cadence line** under each walk. Both pause, very slightly, on the third step. (The player has seen the king do the same; it is a family habit, and a misleading echo of Ch 10.) He drags two pins onto the board: *W.Y. 15, vat failure*, and *W.Y. 15, foundling registered*. The board snaps the set: **same root**.

**Sees.** Pamin taking his lenses off. He locks the pane. No line of dialogue.

## 4. The Gap That Walks Wrong (Ch 10)

**Does.** Two audits side by side: the king's public rehearsal walk and the gap's walk on the same steps. The player sets a height line against the banner rail (the gap is lower) and taps the footfalls: the king pauses on the third step; the gap does not. The player then drags court silhouettes over the gap. Only Kyra's height fits, but her cane rhythm doesn't. The gloved figure from Ch 1 fits both.

**Confirmed:** "Not the king." A second slot stays grey ("a body of Kyra") until the Mantle log.

## 5. MIDPOINT: Eleven Faces / The Cup (Ch 11, D19)

**Phib, in the crypt.** Twelve tall vats, one dry ("W.Y. 15, failure"). Eleven women float asleep, all with Tamoun's face. The warden hands him a lit taper and an order under the Treasurer's seal.
- **Choice:** hold the taper to the vent; set it down and walk out; or **set it down, unplug vat seven into the cold-cargo pod by the door, label it "bond archives", and wheel it out** (a short stealth beat past the warden's back).
- In every case the corridor wall changes colour behind him as the vents roar. No cutaway.

**Tamoun, the same hour.** The vine strains for the cup so hard its bark splits. In the crack, the green line has grown a knot shaped like the scar she cut into on D12. Fingertip, touch-view: *her / shoot / choose*. Talou's old recorded voice: "Choose it." She sets the cup down very carefully and knocks twice. The vine answers once.

## 6. The Warning (Ch 13, D24, watched as Pamin)

**Does.** Through Tsia's leaked feed, Pamin follows Senetom. At 17:50 she is at the freight dock with two Djeme guards, and a gloved young woman behind her turns away. It is the exact frame of her mote, and she lives through it. At 18:00 a pawned core's power draw blips on Pamin's secret meter. At 19:30 she cuts through a scrape toward cell 31. **The dust goes dark for thirty seconds.** She staggers out and falls. At 19:34 Tamoun runs in and kneels. A Treasury notice pops up in Pamin's tray: "Audit purchased and published: 9 s." On every screen, red hands.

## 7. Through the Wall (Ch 14, D26)

**Does.** Fists on the door. The player peels the buckled lacquer around the widened crack in sheets. Wood opens like lips. The HUD's dust overlay crackles and **switches off** as Tamoun pushes into bare heartwood. The guards' dust-sight shows an empty room and a black hole in the wall. She crawls through wet dark (breath, creak, sap-tick) into warm green. A tall, slow shape touches her face. A slip on her wrist: *near*.

---

## 8. The Cage and the Treasurer (Ch 18, D34)

**Sees.** A Koiak grove: pale wood grown shut in living bars around a small clearing. The cage's graft reads *guest / until / lapse*. Lamplight on the human side of the bars: Kyra with her cane, Maro one step behind.

**Does.** The conversation is a choice of three short lines with no text wall: *ask about Pjol*, *ask about the debt*, *ask her second's name*.
- **Kyra says:** "Stay here until midnight and tomorrow nobody hunts you. Go to that tree and you go as the woman who forged it. By your own law."
- If the player asks Maro, **Maro says**, "I have none." **Tamoun says:** "In my trade, you name a thing by the first thing it does."
- Kyra's knuckles whiten on the cane. Maro looks at the cage's graft, which she cannot read.

**After they leave.** Ethen's slips come through the roots one at a time. The player has the cage's grammar now.

## 9. Her Own Forgery (Ch 19, D35)

**Does.** Phib has crossed with Horsi's offer: a crown and the repudiation of the bonds. Choice: *accept* or *refuse*. Accepting branches to the "I am her" Hearing with Horsi's backing. Refusing (canon) makes Phib go quiet: his sister stays on the hulk. **Tamoun says:** "Then we owe her."

Alone, the player lays the forearm on the cage graft, cuts the *lapse* slip clean, and grafts a *fruit* slip at the same angle (the combinatorial grammar, under no timer, at night). The wood shivers and accepts. The interpreter has just done what Talou did.

## 10. The Vault (Ch 20, D36, as Pamin)

**Sees.** Racks of stripped weapon cores in cold blue, humming. A plain plate. A price appears beside each question as the player composes it.

**Does.** Questions are built from tiles: *Treasury*, *Koiak*, *after lapse*, *prepaid*, *Mantle*, *Kyra*. A question about rearming plans flashes a ruinous price. A question about the Mantle is high. The player rephrases: *Treasury / prepaid / settles after lapse*. **Price: one holok.** The answer: 2.1 million to Koiak, for wood-routes. A soft balance-click, the closest thing the machine has to relief.

## 11. THE CLIMAX: The Union (Ch 23, D40, 22:52 to 23:30)

**Sees.** The dark Bnne face of the trunk. Light: Tamoun steps out of the union into the human hall, and the dust renders her into existence mid-step before 42 banners. The crowd knows her face from Kyra's broadcast. Horsi's surgeons stand by a frosted pod.

**Does, beat by beat.** Each beat is a clip the player has watched.
- Phib pushes the kibot into her hands (**C3**).
- She cuts lacquer by the port, and the HUD's dust blooms into a void around the knife (**C1**).
- The trunk buds. The king kneels (**C2**). In a corner inset, Pamin's hand cuts the live feed.
- **Choice:** *graft* or *refuse*.
  - **Graft:** the final graft-writing puzzle, one-handed, then her forearm sinks into wood. In touch-view, from the far side, the vine meets her: Ethen.
  - **Refuse:** the pod opens, and a woman with her face is woken, shaking, and her arm is joined. Tamoun stands beside her.
- A gloved hand on the shoulder: Maro, from the Bnne face. The touch-view edges go red (the "tear" cue from Ch 1).
- The camera holds on Tamoun's face, 70 cm from the trunk. She lays her free hand on the elbow, looks at Maro (not at Phib), and **says:** "Cut there." (**C6**.)
- The player watches, with no control. A long breath. Maro's knife goes in clean. Guards take her arms; she doesn't resist.
- The arm hangs from the trunk, fading into nothing below the elbow (**C7**), exactly at the clip's angle.
- The buds open. Through the port, a tall shape on the far side goes still, then turns away politely, like a stranger.

## 12. The Cut and the Airing (Ch 24, D40, 23:38 to 23:51, as Pamin)

**Does.**
- **The send.** In the annex, three voices on one plate.
  - **Tsia:** "A third of every saver's coin."
  - **Horsi:** "Spend it on the debt."
  - **Tamoun:** "Someone already paid for those forty days. It was us." Her blood on the plate. Paopi touches Ethen's palm; Ethen, puzzled, sets its seal.
- **The cutting puzzle.** The player cuts seven clips from the night's dust. A wrong frame gives the balance-click: "That send does not arrive." The player tries D0 and Pjol, and gets the same click.
- **The broadcast tray, its delay bar draining:** *air the true cut* / *splice the rehearsal* / *nothing*.
- In canon the player builds the true cut, and the realm sees a kneeling king, a bared scar and a one-armed woman alive.

## 13. The Hearing of the Body (Ch 25, D60)

**Sees.** The nome court in the treaty hall, the union green and fruited behind the bench. Five governors in their colours. Kyra and Maro in custody, side by side, the same face thirty-four years apart. Nesi at the back, holding a cup in both hands.

**Does.** The advocates' positions appear as gestures, not speeches: Horsi points at the crown; the king's advocate at the sherds; Kyra's advocate, unexpectedly, at Maro. Phib interprets for Paopi. Then the player chooses one line as Tamoun: ***I am not her*** / ***I am her*** / *(silence)*.

In canon, after the first, the bench's clerk strikes a seal on a bond ledger, and across the hall Maro looks up as a bailiff writes a name beside hers for the first time.

## 14. CODA: The Set Places (1 Thout W.Y. 51)

**Sees.** Lamps along the black Wall, bowls and cups set before it. Cell 31. Tamoun, one-armed, and Nesi.

**Does.** Together they set the places one object at a time: a rice ball in Pjol's kibot; a sherd for Talou; wax and string for Senetom; ten pebbles; and a white cloth for **Ouro Tsenoute**. Nesi drinks from the cracked cup; it leaks a little at the crack, as always. Tamoun lays a fingertip on the green line for her: *choose*. The crack in the Wall is a smooth, callused scar.

**Knock, or don't.** If she knocks: silence long enough to give up, then a tick, the callus splits, and a pale shoot finds Tamoun's wrist: **near**. Nesi reaches out too.
