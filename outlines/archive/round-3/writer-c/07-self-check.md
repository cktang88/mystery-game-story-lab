3. **Graft grammar depth** needs a prototype.
4. **"Law of the Body", "root" and "sleeper"** need clarity testing.
5. **The Hearing could read as a coda-court.** It is a full chapter with on-screen factions and the last committed choice, but it must be paced as climax-adjacent, not an epilogue.
