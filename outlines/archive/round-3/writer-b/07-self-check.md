## Known weaknesses for the grader

1. **Density.** The revision adds courts, a coup and a second elevator city. Act 4 now runs two full threads in parallel. The board and per-thread questions are the mitigation, but a cold playtest is the only real test.
2. **Wakhsh's re-spinning** settles the Draxt schism neatly. It is planted on Day 6 and costs a character we know, but a grader may find it convenient.
3. **The standing send's Hush** gives Aytash a "yes" from the future. It is information the player authored and paid for, and it is unconfirmable, which is the political point. A grader may still see it as an oracle.
4. **The founding forgery is still guessable by Day 19.** Act 4's surprise is now Farnxund's reclassification, not the forgery.
5. **The Review's two-thirds arithmetic** must be shown visually (a seat board) or it will read as procedure.
