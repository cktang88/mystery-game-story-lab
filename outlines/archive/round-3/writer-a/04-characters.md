## The human anchor

### Ponni, Assessor of the Irai (41)

- **Look and sound:** ochre coat, the Balance on a strap, a brass seal with a hole on a cord. She counts under her breath.
- **False belief:** every act has an author who is waiting to be found.
- **Secrets she doesn't know:**
  - her AS 203 letter started Minmini's discovery;
  - her parrot and her seal are in the scan;
  - the Irai has read every letter she wrote;
  - her posting made her the sealing's legal witness.
- **Growth:**
  - *competence:* weighing, then leasing, then the heist, then the duel, then the Proof;
  - *reputation:* the first person ever to sign;
  - *circle:* her sister, the couple, a reader, and every proxy;
  - *moral weight:* she stops a revenge shooting and trades her leverage for others.
- **Price (canon):** forty years sent ahead, a day after she finally meets Minmini.
- **Lines:** "Nobody's" refused (Ch 2) and "Mine" (Ch 35). "Let me assess him." "Assessed and found sufficient."

## Thread characters

### Aruvi (36), treasury clerk on Thinnai: thread D

- **Look and sound:** white dust on ochre; a ring of treasury keys; she whistles while she counts.
- **Want:** get the quarter's remittance to Ilavenil so the coupon is paid.
- **Arc:**
  1. Ottai lands for her, and she learns that Thinnai has no vote because Thinnai itself is keeping something.
  2. She opens the evidence door and finds forty years of seized packets, kept on orders signed by Thamarai.
  3. She wins two of the three treasurers on the **Treasury Bench** (a small Thinnai court).
  4. She returns the packets, which restores Thinnai's vote.
  5. Ottai carries her letter.
  6. She is reprimanded, then promoted.
  7. She posts the retention orders that Ponni lays before Thamarai.
- **Never travels.**

### Kayal of Paalai (20) and Kathir of Neithal (31): thread B

- **Kayal.**
  - *Want:* to pass at court, and to escape the breach clause without selling the berths.
  - *False belief:* that Minmini was a placeholder.
  - *Growth:* she passes at court; trades beads; holds the vault access; gives up the anklets of her own will; wins the proxies' bench; signs a consenting dissolution of her marriage.
  - *End:* controlling holder of an open-trust Paalai, and Ponni's pen pal.
- **Kathir.**
  - *Want:* to find Minmini without his father knowing.
  - *Secret:* the plea to his father (seen in Act 1 as a silhouette; named in Ch 25).
  - *Growth:* follows his father's runner; copies the nominee codes; on the extraction team; chair of Neithal after his father's arrest; stands against Thamarai; pays the back-duty first.
  - *End:* with Minmini.

### Thumbi (26), roost reader: thread C

- **Want:** reopen the bloom, then keep the debt from being called.
- **Arc:** reads round 1 → expert witness, then token-trader in the council → parleys round 3 in front of Ottai and the rust envoy → struck off the readers' bench → reads Selvan's box → flies the extraction shuttle → becomes reader to the monsoon seat.

## Minmini (43), proxy of Kayal

- **Look and sound:** her face is Kayal's, twenty-three years on. She winds a toy each night, holds a cup left hand under, and draws a kolam in one line.
- **Seen first** in letters and in the building's re-performances. **Met** on Day 0 in the Mella.
- **Secrets:**
  - the friendship began as tradecraft and turned in AS 196;
  - she read the scan in AS 204;
  - she wrote the deed and hid it in the parrot;
  - she bought the AS 0 lease;
  - she ran Kathir.
- **Arc:** after she wakes, she has one night to do what she planned for eight years. She seals her own deed, and becomes a person by decree.

## Others with faces

- **Sevvanthi (46), proxy on the bench.**
  - She fears gifts, and sleeps on the open roost after one arrives.
  - She coaches Kayal's passing.
  - She is the bench's swing vote, and stands for the decree.
  - In the epilogue she meets her original "as a person".
- **Valli of Paalai (74).**
  - Grew Minmini to stand at the table.
  - Decoy: "You let the leaf run"; the silhouette; the pistol.
  - Confesses on Day −3, raises the pistol on Day 0, and asks to be sent ahead.
- **Nedumaran of Neithal (66).**
  - His plan: remove the substitute so the bride must seal or breach.
  - Infers a scan on Day −30; learns from the board on Day −4 that the long leaf is real; never learns about the deed.
  - Arrested on Day 0 and faces Ponni on Day +1. Forty years.
- **Thamarai (62), Commissioner, then candidate, then Seal-holder.**
  - Her "let it run" is a fiscal act: she lets a market and a disappearance run so that a test case lands.
  - It is a two-payoff bet. Either Selvan's exposure sinks her rival, or the sealing's witness, who can only be Ponni, signs. The second payoff is a long shot.
  - Learns of the leaf from the board (Day −4). Does not know about Selvan's box.
  - Confronted in private and in the hall on Day +3.
- **Selvan (32), clerk: the hand.**
  - Shot on Day −3; says "She said let it run"; sedated until Day +2, when he wakes and names "C.".
- **Neithal's runner (unnamed; carries a clockwork fan).**
  - Time-napped Minmini and the toymaker; shot Selvan; got away from Ponni twice; held on Day 0.
- **The toymaker (unnamed).**
  - Time-napped on Day −5, freed on Day 0, and testifies.
- **Saleha (54) and Jauhar (61), nakhodas.**
  - Saleha wants contract and patience; Jauhar wants berths for the elders.
  - Saleha turns on Day −3 and wins the monsoon seat on Day 0.
- **Ottai.** The holed-vane envoy who carries one letter.
- **The rust envoy** from Thondi. It sets down the price in round 3.
- **The Proprietor.** "The house does not act. The house is leased."
