# The Unsigned: sealed hidden history and ending (fourth pass: cool, not spooky)

This is the solution document. Everything the player reconstructs is a view onto what is written here. It is **locked** unless marked *working*, and it has one keeper. Any change needs a re-audit of the clue matrix in `05`. The rules digest below is the source for `03` to `06`.

**Tone rule (BRIEF.md).** Nothing here is spooky or morbid. There is no foreseen death, no corpse, no funeral, pyre, crypt or mourning rite, and no ordeal. The foreseen moment is **a sealing**: a signature that moves a fortune. The crime is a **time-napping** (a person is banked forward in a slow-time vault against their will). Crimes are played as thriller beats: a disappearance, a shooting on a stair, a rooftop chase, a vault extraction, a swap under guard.

**No horror or gross imagery (BRIEF.md).** There is no body horror, mutation, grafting, parasites, rot or gore.
- Proxies are grown in clear **glass cradles** at Marutham's clinics: clean, quiet and lit.
- The bracelet is locked on the wrist, never sewn in.
- The blooms are kilometre-high lilies of mineral silk.
- Carriers are luminous seed-heads that fold through space.
- Pollen is gold light in the air.

Alien life is shown for awe.

## Units and constants

- **Years** are counted in AS (*aandu* of the Seal). The present is **AS 212**.
- **Days** are counted from the wedding: **Day 0**. Minmini vanishes in the early hours of **Day −8**.
- **The Mella** (from *mella*, "slowly"): Paalai's berth-ring around a dark companion star in Ilavenil's system. **One berth day equals fifty outside days**, so one berth minute is fifty outside minutes.
- **Carried film (leaves):** a late leaf stays live for about **one year of its own time**. It is crisp for days, usable for a season, and after about half a year only dense metal and mineral read clearly.
- **The Kaanthal's ribs:** one per carrier season (9 to 16 outside months; about a year). The bloom is not cyclical.
- **Sublight:** Thinnai to Ilavenil takes about four outside years, about one ship year.
- **Currency:** the *kaasu*.

## Rules digest (ten rules, locked)

1. **Leaves, sheaths and cadence.**
   - X-ray film that has ridden a Vandu carrier is cut into an early leaf and a late leaf. The developed early leaf shows every x-ray exposure the late leaf receives while it is live.
   - A leaf is a **scan**: metal and mineral bright, bone as pale fog when old, no faces, no colour. **It shows where things are, never who or why.** ("It only says *there*.")
   - There is one self-consistent timeline.
   - Live leaves are kept in lead **sheaths** and unsheathed only inside a cassette.
   - The Proprietor scans private rooms at 04:00, thresholds every quarter hour and halls on the hour. It scans **the Sealing Hall in the Kaanthal's heart at 23:00**. Each scan goes on its own ordinary **record film**.
   - A **cassette lease** puts a lessee's leaf in the beam for named scans only.
2. **Identity by metal.** Ilavenil's records are scans, and scans show no faces. So at a sealing **the law identifies the sealer by the metal on record**: a firm's controlling anklets and seal, worn with no proxy bracelet, bind the firm. A merger sealing must also be **witnessed by an Irai assessor**, whose brass seal (with a hole) appears in the record.
3. **Authorship.**
   - A scan is the Proprietor's Charter act. Under the **Doctrine of the Unsigned** (AS 0), a closed loop is "the act of no one, for no one can be shown to have closed it".
   - Every lease has a **purpose** field. A lessee who signs it **before the hour** becomes the closure's author, liable to duty and law.
   - **The field seals at the hour.** No one has ever signed it.
   - Only a signer has **standing** to challenge the Doctrine in court.
4. **The vidai (licence of foreseen loss, AS 31).**
   - A claim filed on a house-closed leaf that shows a place at an hour with a piece of **property absent from it**. The claim is perfected when the loss is certified.
   - A perfected vidai makes the loss no one's: the property's removal cannot be prosecuted or reclaimed. The fee is one tenth of its value.
   - Proxies are property.
   - It **never covers a person with standing**: taking a free person is kidnapping.
   - It is **void if the holder caused the loss**.
5. **The Balance.**
   - It shows whether a thing's history is looped, how far back the loop reaches (depth) and how much depends on it (weight).
   - **It shows the shallowest loop first.** A deeper loop appears once the shallower one is entered in the ledger with an author. It is taught on the Irai's **double coin**, which has a one-day loop with a ten-year loop behind it.
   - Weighing a person needs consent or a warrant.
   - Readings of others are **published** on the public board. Self-readings are **sealed** until the assessor's next entry.
6. **Proxies.**
   - One per banked depositor, grown in a glass cradle in about ten months and imprinted up to the fork day.
   - A proxy is its original's agent **until the folding**, and its original's property always. Everything a proxy acquires belongs to the original.
   - A steel **bracelet** is locked on the left wrist and opens only with an Irai assessor's key. Opening it without a warrant is a crime.
7. **The Proprietor.**
   - It acts only by lease or Charter, only on Ilavenil, and never lies in a lease.
   - It discloses a record only to that record's lease-holder or to an assessor with custody.
   - Clockwork carries no signal, so the Proprietor **cannot track, lease or attribute** clockwork devices, or anyone moving through streets with no leases or signal relays (the "clockwork streets"). Its scans still image their metal.
   - It never names an author. Its **suspense account** holds 4,117 unsigned closures.
8. **The Proof** (the Five Hundred's trial).
   - A public **contest of exhibits** in the round hall: three rounds, in which each side lays exhibits on the great lightbox in turn. After each round, delegates drop silver beads (acquit) or bronze beads (convict) into a sealed **urn**.
   - The Proprietor counts the urn at the end under a **count lease**. The standard term is *"count the urn as it lies"*.
   - **The accused's priority:** she may file the count lease first.
   - **Sole author:** an accused may stand as sole author of the act charged. She is then convicted on all counts of that act, and her co-accused are discharged.
   - **Declining** the Proof convicts the decliner on her own counts. Co-accused face their own Proofs.
9. **Sentences ("sent ahead").** A term spent living in a Mella berth while the outside world runs on. The Irai holds **200 statutory berths** outside Paalai's pre-sold notes.
   - Accomplice: 10 years.
   - Breaking a depositor's berth: 20.
   - Opening a proxy bracelet without warrant: 20.
   - Kidnapping a person, or ordering a shooting: 40.
10. **The Crossing.**
    - The Vandu are rooted **blooms** (minds) and folding **carriers**. Human cargo rides in pollen masses.
    - They deliberate by **envoy rounds**, about two days apart: envoys carry only a bloom's pollen, coated to state a position. Routes, and so post capacity and price, are set by a majority of route-partners.
    - **Law:** nothing carried may be kept from its crossing. A bloom whose crossings were kept closes, and its partners may close in sympathy. **A bloom that is itself in breach has no vote.**
    - **Factions:** the **Old Crossing** (the oldest blooms, Thinnai's among them, all routed through the Kaanthal; they want the hub readmitted) and the **Far Blooms** (led by Thondi's; they gain route share if the hub is cut).
    - Envoys answer whoever **parleys**: a human who reads the coats and lays a reply on a roost floor out of returned crossings, choosing which bundles go, toward which partner, and in what order. The reply can win or lose a round and change its price (see `03`).
    - **Ottai**, an old Thinnai envoy with a holed vane, keeps coming back to the same people. Once it carries a letter.
    - Concentrated Kaanthal pollen is the resort's euphoric in low doses. Hoard pollen kept unaged in the Mella is a **knockout** in a sealed room.

1. **AS −61.** First contact with the Vandu.
2. **AS −52.** A consortium builds the Proprietor. **AS −20:** it deeds Ilavenil to the Proprietor itself.

9. **AS 208.** Minmini buys the AS 0 count lease, which carries title to the hall film, in Kayal's name.
