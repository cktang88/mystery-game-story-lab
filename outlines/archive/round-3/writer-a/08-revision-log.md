# The Unsigned: revision log

Response to `judging/review-writer-a.md` (7.3/10; Scope and genre 6). Fixes were made at the source (rules and chronology in `02`) and then carried through `03` to `07`. For each numbered revision: what changed, in which files, and why. Where I did not take the grader's exact direction, I say so and explain.

## Required revisions

### 1. Make the authorship law airtight; write the licensed-death statute

- **Changed.** Leasing and authoring are now separate.
  - An inspection is the Proprietor's Charter act. A cassette lessee rents only which film sits in the beam, so lessees are *traced* but are not *authors*.
  - Every lease form since before AS 0 has a **purpose of closure** field. Filling it in under your own name makes you the author, liable to duty and law. No one ever has.
  - The Proprietor may not attribute authorship itself.
  - A new **standing** rule: only a signer can bring the Doctrine to court. That is why Selvan's planned signature was a landmark worth killing him for, and why Ponni's is the first.
- **The vidai statute (AS 31).** It permits only foreseen loss of **property** in a house-closed leaf, registered with a fee of a tenth of the property's value. Proxies are property, so Minmini's killing was "lawful". It never covers a person with standing, so Paavai's and Selvan's deaths are murders: the ledger rejects any other entry, and Kuyil is charged on Day +2. The vidai falls with the Doctrine.
- **Files:** `02` (rules digest 4 to 6, chronology), `03` (law section), `05` (Ch 9, 13, 22, 25, 37, 39, 40), `06` (scenes 4, 6, 19, 21), `01` (H2 promoted).
- **Why:** it resolves the contradiction at the centre of the theme, and it turns the grader's H2 into the law that makes Ilavenil frightening for proxies but not a free-fire zone.

### 2. Repair the x-ray foresight rule and the dates

- **Shielding:** live leaves are kept in lead sheaths and unsheathed only in a cassette, and a sheath shows on safety film as a dense rectangle. The chamber's 22:55 unsheathing is now a tense beat.
- **Cadence:** private rooms at 04:00, thresholds every quarter hour, halls hourly, the chamber at 23:00. Cassette leases name the inspections they cover.
- **Balance:** shows only the shallowest loop; deeper loops appear once the shallower ones are entered. The body reads two days in Ch 3, and the twenty-four-year loop is hidden behind it. The parrot reads twenty-four years, which raises a question without giving the answer. Persons can be weighed only with consent or a warrant. Self-readings are sealed until the assessor's next entry, which also answers "who reads her Day −2 self-reading": no one until Day +1.
- **Selvan's leaf** covers Day −8 04:00 only.
- **Prologue** reframed as the 04:00 safety-film sweep, ending on a sleeper who may be dead.
- **Dates:**
  - The leaf is developed early in AS 188, so the proxy is grown from AS 188 to 189 and the loop depth is twenty-four years, made consistent in all files.
  - Ponni docks on Day −7.
  - Paavai's tip-off now comes from Selvan reading Kathir's slip on Ponni's desk.
  - A carrier season is defined (nine to sixteen months, about one year).
  - The five houses are now five *great* houses among five hundred seats, with Kurinji, Mullai and Marutham given roles.
- **Files:** `02`, `03`, `05`, `06`, `07`.
- **Why:** the self-check's "no scene violates a limit" was marked Pass in error. It has been re-audited item by item.

### 3. Make the scope visible, not reported

- **(a) Alien polity.**
  - The Vandu's blooms form **the Crossing**. It deliberates by envoy flights that humans had dismissed for two centuries as "empty flights", sets routes and so post prices, sanctions breaches as a body, and readmits worlds at a reduced route share.
  - **Thinnai's bloom closes in sympathy** on Day −5, so the Irai cannot post its coupon, misses it on Day −3, and the Mawsim council sits to call the bonds.
- **(b) Another polity on screen.** A Thumbi chapter aboard the Mawsim flagship *Kaaru*, a foreign court of captains voting with astrolabe tokens. Thinnai and Uppalam appear through handled objects (medicine vials, coupon orders, board minutes), and the epilogue letters carry x-ray portraits from three worlds.
- **(c) Playable intrigue.**
  - A new verb, **trade beads** (Kayal, from Act 2): delegates show their wants as objects, and Kayal pays with berths, route votes, old leases or favours.
  - Thamarai resigns on Day −5 and campaigns visibly with Mawsim and Kurinji backing.
  - Two votes the player shapes. On Day 0, keeping the bloc is cover; moving it raises the guard. On Day +10, Thamarai offers Minmini's personhood decree for Paalai's beads.
- **Files:** `02`, `03`, `04`, `05` (Ch 6, 14, 15, 21, 30, 32, 44, 45), `06` (scenes 7 to 9), `01` (aliens and politics re-graded).
- **Why it differs from the grader's wording:** I did not send a viewpoint to a second planet's surface. Sublight travel takes years in this setting, so a planet trip would break the setting's own rules. The flagship, as a sovereign foreign deck, and the Crossing's envoys carry the reach without that break.

### 4. Respect refusal at both committed choices

- **(a) Casting is split from signing.**
