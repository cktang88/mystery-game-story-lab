# Archived outlines

The first round of writers B and C converged with writer A on the same template: a cold northern-European sea coast (Norse island, North Sea tidal flats, North Frisian Hallig), a drowning as the inciting death, an alien tied to sound or the sea, and a roughly 19-year time cycle. The owner chose to keep writer A (graded highest, 7.5/10) and redo B and C under a banned list with forced divergent settings. These v1 outlines and their reviews (`judging/archive/`) are kept for reference; the judge does not score them.

## Round 2 (archived for scope)

Round 2 kept writer A (revised after review) and redid B and C under the banned list and random seeds. The seeds worked against convergence, but the owner then clarified the scope: big futuristic science fiction with galactic reach, spy and court intrigue, politics and economic systems (now in `BRIEF.md`, "Scope and genre"). None of the three met it: A is a Norse island, B v2 an underground megacity, C v2 a buffalo-drawn walking city. The owner chose a fresh round of three.

- `writer-a-v1/`: "The Long Word", graded 7.5, fully revised (see its `08-revision-log.md`).
- `writer-b-v2/`: Amole megacity, graded 7.0. Its revision was stopped partway: files 01 to 04 are revised, 05 to 07 are not, and there is no revision log.
- `writer-c-v2/`: "The Walking Market", graded 7.1, fully revised.

Their reviews are in `judging/archive/`. Recurring shape across rounds 1 and 2, now banned: a society whose founding secret is a debt, pledge or promise to a bound, captive or dying alien that it lives off, settled in person at the end.

## Round 3 (archived: too weird, not simple)

Three fresh writers under the big-sci-fi scope: A "The Unsigned", B "The Light Weight", C "The Ouro's Hand". The Fable judge picked A (7.9); after a merge pass and a tone pass it held at 8.0. The owner then rejected all three as weird and hard to understand: the complexity lived in invented rules and jargon, not in characters and plot. `BRIEF.md` now requires a premise as simple as "5 kingdoms fight for the crown" (Game of Thrones) or "a dude and an AI fight the alien Covenant to save humanity" (Halo). Files are in `round-3/`; reviews and the verdict in `judging/archive/round-3/`.
