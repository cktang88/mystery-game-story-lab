# Writer B: the sealed solution

**Canon tier: LOCKED.** This is the true history the player reconstructs. Every clue in the game is a view onto something written here. Any change requires a change request and a re-audit of the clue matrix in `05-outline.md`.

## The one-paragraph truth

In 1371 an alien brood-mother fell into the North Sea flats and drowned the town of Ammerwarf. Her body became a reef two kilometres long, packed with dormant eggs. When something touches an egg-chamber at low tide, an egg defends itself by copying the intruder perfectly, body and mind, and hiding in the mother's own past wearing that shape. The survivors learned to exploit the reflex: a person lies in a hollow and a copy of them climbs out of it years or centuries earlier, carrying the future in its head. The founders cut out the mother's voice so she could never call her disguised children home, drowned the one woman who tried to stop them, and called the reef God's Grace. Six and a half centuries later the mother is dying, the last debts of the trade fall due, and the drowned woman of 1371 turns out to be the copy of the Chief Reckoner, who has spent seven years fighting her own future self and has just drowned a man to do it.

## What Mem is (sealed, mostly never stated in game)

- A brood-mother of a species that disperses between stars as dormant seed-bodies and lands in the shallow seas of living worlds. Origin, number and destination of the species: **texture**, never answered.
- Her body: a segmented reef, 2.1 km from the buried tower of Ammerwarf church north-west under the Grauwarf flats. About 38,600 egg-chambers in 1371.
- Her perception spans her whole life at once (1371 to 20 March 2026). This is the story's one admitted cheat. It is never explained and never used to solve anything.
- **The defence reflex.** When a creature lies fully inside an egg-chamber at low water, she reads its pattern, shapes one of her eggs at an earlier moment of her life into a perfect copy of it, and the copy climbs out of that chamber in the past. The empty chamber left behind is a **hollow**, a mould of the copy. Its walls carry a fine mineral **waterline** at the height of the water at the moment of the sending. She chooses the earlier moment by matching tide height and phase exactly.
- **The brood's purpose.** Hidden children live as whatever they copied, unaware. When the mother is about to die, she sings an all-clear; every hidden child feels it and may *choose* to come home (walk into the sea, shed the borrowed form and leave with the hatched brood) or stay as they are. It is a choice, never a summons.
- **Her voice.** A resonant organ the size of a ewe's head, the **throat**, near the church. Without it she can hum but not sing. Her hum replays every sound that has ever happened on or over her body, in every year at once; each moment's sound is pitched by the tide height of its moment. Her future sounds reach back only faintly; the loudest thing she will ever make, the call, carries back into every year as a thin high tone. The Hearers call it **the Promise**.
- **The Sob.** A permanent low break in the hum: the sound of the throat being cut on 25 January 1371.
- At the start of the game three egg-chambers remain closed (**the three domes**). Nobody spends them. They hatch when she dies.

## The rules the history obeys (locked)

1. **No hollow, no crossing.** A crossing can only fill a hollow that already exists, in the sender's exact shape. Nobody can be sent anywhere a hollow does not already wait.
2. **Every hollow has its tide.** The waterline in the hollow plus the sender's apparent age fixes one present-day tide. KERF computes it; a good pilot can check it by hand.
3. **Nothing crosses but the pattern.** Copies arrive naked, with memory up to the moment of lying down.
4. **The past is fixed.** Every hollow has always been filled by the person in it. Nothing can be undone. What can change is what people understand.
5. **Copies have no navel.** They are perfect otherwise, including scars and dental work, and are fertile and fully human in body and mind.
6. **Window.** No crossing arrives before Mem's landing (January 1371) or after her death (20 March 2026). Therefore every hollow's tide falls on or before 20 March 2026. This is public knowledge: the **Last Tide**.

## True chronology

### 1371: the founding crime

- **22 Jan 1371, the Long Ebb.** The sea withdraws for nine hours as Mem descends. The people of Ammerwarf walk out on the bared seabed to gather stranded fish. The return wave drowns the town; about 3,800 die. Survivors cling to the Grauwarf mound.
- **22 to 24 Jan.** Stranded seals and gulls touch open egg-chambers; fourteen animal copies emerge on earlier days of the week (the "seals that walked into the houses" of folk memory). **Texture.**
- **24 Jan.** A naked woman of about sixty with no navel walks out of the mud from the chamber by the drowned church tower, the **first human hollow, Kerf Nul**. She is the copy of **Marrit**, sent from 20 March 2026. She speaks a Sielsk the survivors half understand, "as if from far up the coast". She has a pocked mark on her upper arm (a BCG vaccination scar) and three small pale scars on her belly (keyhole surgery, 2003).
- She warns the survivors' leaders, reeve **Tammo Folkerts**, priest **Enno** and merchant **Ubbo Kanter (the first)**: the reef is a mother; anyone who lies in her makes a frightened child flee into the past in their shape; one day she will sing and her children may go home if they choose. She begs them to leave her alone.
- **25 Jan.** Mem hums; the woman, who feels it (she is one of Mem's children), says the mother is calling. The founders hear "the witch is calling the sea back". They mistake *free to go home* for *taken home*. They dig to the throat and cut it out by torchlight. The scream is the Sob.
- That night the woman steals the throat from the cart, carries it into the flooded nave, presses it into her own hollow and seals it with blue church-floor clay. She is caught at dawn and drowned at the new sluice on the Grauwarf dike, ever after called **the Witch's Gate**.
- **Kanter tests it.** On 26 January, the day of the drowning, a man with Kanter's face walks out of the mud and is locked in the church as a demon. On 30 January Kanter, sick with dread, lies down in the chamber the demon came from; it fits him exactly. The founders understand: copies come from later and know later things. They see a kingdom.
- **The Ebbe-Brief (Ebb Charter), Feb 1371.** Public text: the reef is the Grace given by God in recompense for the Drowning; its Echoes are God's gifts; the throat of the Grace was "cast into the deep"; the mud-witch who tried to bring back the flood was drowned. **Secret Annex**, read only by each new Diekgreve: "The Grace has a throat. If it sings, the Echoes go home. Let it never sing."
- A second hand, the priest Enno's private chronicle, describes the witch ("a devil's thumbprint on her arm, three pale marks on her belly, and our tongue spoken as if from far up the coast") and records the same night differently: the witch "went back into the grave she came out of and took the throat into the dark with her". The two accounts disagree. Enno's chronicle survives in the Grauwarf church library, unread for centuries.

### 1371 to 1949: the Grace (texture, glimpsed)

- The Sielacht forms around the reef. Hollows are notched on wooden tally boards (the first kerfs). Faces are lifted from hollows in plaster; people who match are **Skuldigen**, owed, and are **schooled** from childhood to know what their copies will need to know.
- The doctrine of **the Unravelling** grows from the Brief's line "every notch must be paid or the sea takes back the years": an unpaid hollow would undo history. Nobody can test it, so it holds.
- The Rib is fenced against seals and birds ("the Grace must not be spent on beasts"). Kerf Nul is sealed under a slab as the **Witch's Grave**, forbidden to touch.
- The Hearers appear around 1500: Slikborn who feel the hum and call the reef Mem. Persecuted, then tolerated.
- **1612, the Younger Sibling Law**: a copy is the legal younger sibling of its original.
- The Sielacht is never conquered, survives plague years forewarned, and is neutral in every war. Its luck is its own future, arriving in the mud.

### 1949 to 2025: the trade and the Reckoning Decade

- **1949, the Tidal Accords.** The Sielacht brokers crossings for foreign states: a foreign citizen who will one day lie in a hollow arrives as a copy in the Sielacht's past and is delivered to their country's government with their future knowledge. Nations hold lists of their own owed citizens from birth. Because the trade peaked in 1950 to 2000 and most of those copies were sent by people born after 1960, the debts fall due in the 2020s: **the Reckoning Decade**. Owed who flee are hunted by their own governments under the Unravelling doctrine.
- **1966.** The copy of **Tjalling Douwes** (sent 20 Mar 2026 at 52) arrives. Known as **Master Douwes**, he designs the Great Sluice (opened 1970). Dies 1999. Tjalling, born 1974, grows up in the shadow of a national hero who is himself.
- **1974.** The copy of **Inês Valadares** (sent 20 Mar 2026 at 34) arrives. Before the Rekkenkeamer hands her to Lisbon she persuades it to let her make the first hydrophone recordings of the hum. She returns to the islands in 1981 and records until 1990: **the 1974 to 1990 tapes**. She stays. In 2026 she is 86, nearly blind, known as **Dona Inês**.
- **1979.** Okke Hylkes born. **1988**: Femke Rinzes born. **1992**: Inês Valadares born (Lisbon). **1997**: Jildou Okkes born to Okke (18) and Sjoukje.
- **1987.** KERF commissioned by the Rekkenkeamer (Reckoning Chamber).
- **1994.** The copy of **Femke** (sent 2019 at 31) arrives: **Marrit**. She rises through the Rekkenkeamer; Femke, owed from birth, is schooled to become what Marrit already is. Marrit remembers, from Femke's side, watching herself across the street as a child.
- **1998. KERF v4** concludes from 627 years of ledger that no hollow has ever gone unpaid, including those whose owed fled, and that enforcement is therefore unnecessary. Rolled back by order of the then Diekgreve. Archived by law.
- **11 Mar 2001.** **Gerlof** arrives: the copy of Okke, sent 6 Mar 2026 at 47. He covers his hollow with a fish-box lid and silt so the state never learns Okke is owed, steals clothes from the Grauwarf church vestry (a police report records the theft and "a bearded tramp"), and takes his grandfather's name. He lives on Lütjewarf as an eel-fisher, bearded, avoiding Sielhaven.
- **14 Oct 2004.** Sjoukje, a pilot, is lost in fog on the flats. Gerlof knows the date from Okke's memory and goes out to save her. He cannot: his boat grounds on a bar he had forgotten. He reaches her in the Deep Gully as the tide rises; she is alive; he holds her hand and talks to her until she dies; she is not afraid. He records the last minutes on a minidisc recorder he used for eel-sounding. The rescue party photographs an unknown man in an eel boat. That night he reveals himself to Okke (25). Okke keeps the secret for 22 years.
- **2005 to 2014.** Gerlof teaches Jildou (8 to 17) the flats: listen to the gullies; always check the eel traps from the post with the red rag; seven traps, and the seventh is the last, only at the lowest tides; "Out here there's no enemy. There's only the tide."
- **2003.** Marrit has a keyhole appendectomy (three small scars).
- **2011. KERF v6**, connected to a new hydrophone grid, finds the Promise is a structured signal, not noise. Marrit, newly senior, has the Rib's audio banned from KERF. Rolled back. Gerlof meets **Hiske**, a Hearer, and begins listening.
- **2015.** Marrit becomes Chief Reckoner.
- **2019.** Femke sends to 1994; Marrit presides, watching her original become her. Same year, **KERF v7**, analysing a muon scan of the reef, flags Kerf Nul: modern body (BCG scar, keyhole scars), no navel, a dense foreign object inside. Marrit reads the body record and recognises her own scars. She orders the rollback. From that day she knows she will be the witch. Believing the Annex and the pageant, she resolves that her future self will fail, and that whatever lies in the grave stays buried.
- **2024 to 2025.** Gerlof finds Enno's chronicle and its disagreement with the Brief, and hears in the 1974 tapes that the Sob dates to the founding. He concludes the throat is in the Witch's Grave.

### March 2026: the present

- **2 Mar, evening.** Gerlof shaves off his beard so that the face Jildou finds will be her father's. He knows the date of his death from Okke's memory and knows Jildou finds him. He does not know who kills him.
- **2 to 3 Mar, 01:50 low water.** Gerlof lifts the slab of the Witch's Grave, digs out the blue clay and finds the throat. KERF's reef seismometers flag a disturbance at Kerf Nul; the alert goes to the Chief Reckoner on call: Marrit.
- **02:40.** Gerlof hides the throat in the seventh eel trap of Jildou's old line, under water except at the lowest tides, and starts back.
- **03:40, the Deep Gully.** Marrit intercepts him. He will not give her the throat. She recognises Okke's face. He says, "Out here there's no enemy. There's only the tide." She takes his lamp and his pole, leaves him with his boot fast in the soft gully mud as the flood comes in, and tells herself the flats did it. He drowns about 04:30. On the way back she checks his hut and searches it; she does not know about the traps.
- **3 Mar, 07:10, the Spring Reckoning.** Jildou pilots a party to the Rib and finds the body.
- **6 Mar, 05:12.** Jildou walks Okke to his uncovered hollow. He sends. (Gerlof arrives 11 Mar 2001.)
- **12 Mar, night.** Jildou, Femke and Inês cast Kerf Nul illegally. The face is Marrit's. Marrit arrives and says she knows. The KERF log of the casting reaches the Diekgreve, **Ubbo Kanter**, who now knows the witch has come and the founding will repeat. He orders compulsory crossings for every remaining owed at the Last Tide and the arrest of Skuldfrij members.
- **13 to 14 Mar, night.** Tjalling's Skuldfrij cell plants charges on the Witch's Gate to flood the Rib basin and make the Equinox crossings impossible. Okke, who checks the gate himself at every spring tide, finds them and is killed when they fire. The basin channel silts.
- **15 Mar.** Femke and Jildou recover KERF v4's conclusion. Femke is arrested.
- **16 to 17 Mar.** Jildou proves Marrit drowned Gerlof and confronts her in the Gallery of the Owed. Marrit confesses, orders Femke's release and lifts KERF's ban on the Rib's audio. Ubbo takes Marrit into custody so that she can be sent. On the 17th no new hollow appears in the reef: Ubbo's plan to send a copy of himself back from the Equinox to the 17th (the "Last Grace") will never happen.
- **18 Mar.** KERF and Inês rebuild 1371 from the hum. Marrit, in custody, hears the founding night and the witch's own voice: *free to go home, not taken*.
- **19 Mar.** Tjalling surrenders and offers to open the Great Sluice, which his copy designed, to drain the silted basin.
- **20 Mar, the Equinox, the Last Tide.** Lowest water 06:58.
  - 05:40: Tjalling opens the Great Sluice; the basin drains.
  - 06:20: Jildou walks the seventh trap and recovers the throat.
  - 06:30 to 06:58: Jildou pilots the queue of the owed (foreign delegations, Inês, Tjalling, Marrit) across the bared Rib. Marrit asks Jildou to walk her. Jildou does, and holds her hand at Kerf Nul.
  - 06:58: Marrit sends. Her copy arrives 24 Jan 1371. Inês sends to 1974; Tjalling sends to 1966.
  - 07:05: Jildou presses the throat into the wound by the church, the **Mouth**. Mem sings the call and dies.
  - KERF registers the last hollow as paid. Under Rekkenkeamer law a closed ledger is public record. KERF publishes the complete ledger to the world, including the v4 proof that nothing could ever unravel.
  - Every living Slikborn, about 11,000 worldwide, feels the call. Around 4,000 walk into the nearest sea and are gone, among them Dona Inês and Hiske. Marrit, Slikborn herself, hears it and stays to stand trial.
  - Ubbo lies on one of the three domes, still believing the Grace may take him. It opens beneath him; three luminous hatchlings swim past him out to sea. Jildou walks him back before the flood.
- **Epilogue, May 2026.** No more crossings, ever. The world has no foreknowledge for the first time in 655 years. Marrit and Tjalling await trial. KERF, its books closed, asks Jildou whether she wants it to compute a forecast of her life.

## Final emotional image

Jildou standing in the bared reef at sunrise, holding the hand of the woman who drowned her father's other self, while the whole sky of the flats hums; then letting go, and walking back toward shore as the tide comes in, while behind her a few hundred people walk the other way.
