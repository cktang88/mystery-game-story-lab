**Closest neighbours and the differences.** A famous German series also has a small northern town and time travel: here there is no portal, no fixed-interval jump, no travel at all (only copies), no generational family knot, and the unit is a reef and a nation, not a town. A famous space film has a father who is the unseen presence at his daughter's crisis: here it is a copy, at the mother's death, disclosed in Act II, and the daughter is not the one rescued. A famous horror game has copies who are left behind: here the story is told from both sides, and the copy is explicitly a separate person from the first scene.

## Fixes made during drafting (summary)

1. Okke's death given a cause chain from the midpoint (was "and then").
2. Inês's presence made causal (she always knew she was owed).
3. A four-day Ubbo copy loop cut and replaced by an observable absence (no hollow on the 17th).
4. A late "fresh hollow" with Jildou's own face cut: it doubled the anchor and muddied clarity.
5. Marrit kept out of viewpoint until the player has seen the murder.
6. The founding night reordered so the Kanter test follows the throat-cutting; the founders cut the throat out of fear, before they understood crossings.
7. Okke placed at the body in Ch1 so his calm is a fair clue.
8. Enno's chronicle given the witch's scars and speech so the "someone from now" conclusion has a third clue outside KERF.
9. Jildou's habit line planted in Ch1 so the gully recording in Ch8 lands.
10. Gerlof's silence toward Okke explained by the fixed-past rule itself.

## Open risks (for the grader)

- **Ages and copies.** Five people exist in two versions at different ages. Mitigations: distinct looks (beard, dyed hair, dark glasses), distinct eras by colour, the Tide Board, and names that differ (Gerlof, Marrit, Dona Inês). Needs a cold-reader test on the Ch2 and Ch9 boards.
- **Required reading.** Two short texts are required. If testers stall, voice both in Ch11.
- **The 1371 chapter's lack of a fail state.** Intended; must be signposted as the record (the player carries it) so it reads as tragedy, not as a broken game.
- **Step 13 work** (sequence sheet, reels, silent tests, reachability check) is not done; it is outside an outline's scope.
