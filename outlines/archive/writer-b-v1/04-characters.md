# Writer B: characters

Every character below is derived from a faction in `03-world-and-factions.md`, and every secret links to the sealed chronology in `02-hidden-history.md`. Each has a want the player can always state in one line; the in-game **Tide Board** shows that line under their portrait at every chapter boundary.

Viewpoints (playable): **Jildou** (all acts), **Gerlof** (in the past, Ch5 to 12), **Femke** (Ch6 to 13, at KERF), **Inês** (Ch6 to 16), **Marrit** (only after the player knows her secret, Ch13 to 17). Never more than four active at once.

---

## The human anchor: Jildou Okkes, 29

- **Role.** Licensed flats-pilot of the Grauwarf station. She walks the owed out to their hollows at the right tide and walks them back. Born to Okke (then 18) and Sjoukje, a pilot who drowned in fog when Jildou was seven. Not owed: no hollow wears her face. Lives in her mother's old pilot cottage on Grauwarf. Sets eel traps the way an old eel-man taught her, seven traps from the post with the red rag.
- **Look and sound.** Orange vest, ash tide-stick notched with her own marks, hair under a knitted cap; whistles through her teeth; talks to the mud.
- **Want (Act I).** Find out who the drowned man with her father's face was, and who left him in the gully. **(Act II to III)** Find who killed him and stop the state taking more from her family. **(Act IV)** Get everyone back before the tide, including the woman who killed him.
- **False belief.** *A copy is a shadow of its original; the one who stays is the real one.* She grew up in a culture that treats the Slikborn as younger siblings, and when she learns Gerlof was her father's copy she first feels robbed, as if her childhood teacher was a counterfeit of the father she already had. This doubles as the player's false assumption: that the copies are copies of *us*. The plot attacks it twice: Gerlof turns out to have been more of a father to her than Okke dared to be, and the copies turn out to be Mem's own children wearing borrowed shapes.
- **Secret link to the chronology.** None she knows. Her childhood habit (the seventh trap) is where the voice of the world is hidden, because the man who taught it to her put it there.
- **Growth.**
  - *Competence*: tide-reader to hollow-reader (waterline puzzles), to listener (she computes the tide curves that tune the hum), to the pilot who walks the Last Tide.
  - *Reputation*: "the pilot" to "Okke's girl who found the body" to "the one who cast the Witch's Grave" (a criminal) to the person the world's delegations follow across the mud in the dark.
  - *Circle*: her father, then the island, then the Slikborn, then every Slikborn alive.
  - *Moral weight*: she walks her father's killer to the founding and holds her hand. Forgiveness as an action, not a speech.
- **Recurring objects.** The eel traps; her tide-stick; Gerlof's lamp.
- **Trap door.** If her Act III thread is cut, Femke can carry the proof against Marrit; Jildou's Act IV role (the trap, the walk) is irreplaceable and must stay.

## Okke Hylkes, 47 (her father)

- **Role.** Keeper of the Witch's Gate sluice on Grauwarf. Quiet, careful, checks the gate himself at every spring tide.
- **Want.** A quiet life for his daughter; to keep her out of the ledger's reach.
- **False belief.** That keeping the secret protected her.
- **Secret.** For 22 years he has known that Old Gerlof the eel-man was his own copy, that he himself is owed, and the tide of his crossing (6 March 2026, 05:12). He let Gerlof teach Jildou because it let both of him be her father.
- **Growth.** Moral weight: from silence to honesty in Act I, and in Act II a real father-daughter partnership (he teaches her to read his old sluice logs). Then he dies in Act III doing his job.
- **Death (Ch10).** Killed by Skuldfrij charges on the Witch's Gate. Causes, all seen earlier as ordinary events: he checks the gate himself at spring tides (Ch3); he taught Tjalling the gate's mechanism as a friend (Ch3); the Diekgreve's order of compulsory crossings drove Tjalling to act (Ch9 consequence); he had agreed to skip that night's check to have supper with Jildou, then went anyway out of habit (Ch10 opening).
- **Trap door.** If the death must be cut, he survives injured and the gate's failure still silts the channel.

## Gerlof, 47 to 72 (Okke's copy)

- **Role.** Viewpoint in the past. Arrives naked in the mud on 11 March 2001; lives 25 years as a bearded eel-fisher on Lütjewarf; dies on 3 March 2026. The player has seen his body before they ever play him.
- **Look.** Beard, oilskin, flat-bottomed eel boat, minidisc recorder on a lanyard.
- **Want.** *Find out who will kill me, and leave my daughter what she needs.* He knows the date of his death from Okke's memory; he does not know the killer.
- **False belief (early).** That knowing the future lets him change it. Broken in 2004 (Sjoukje).
- **Secret.** Everything: he hid his own hollow, he was the stranger in the 2004 rescue photograph, he taught Jildou, he found the throat.
- **Growth.** From a man trying to dodge fate to a man who chooses what to do inside it: he cannot save Sjoukje, so he makes sure she is not alone; he cannot avoid his death, so he spends his last night saving the voice and shaving so his daughter will know his face.
- **Line (taught to Jildou, said to his killer):** "Out here there's no enemy. There's only the tide."
- **Trap door.** If a past chapter is cut, its object (minidisc, photograph, chronicle note) can be found in his hut instead.

## Marrit, 63 (Chief Reckoner; Slikborn; the culprit; Kerf Nul)

- **Role.** Head of the Rekkenkeamer and keeper of KERF. Jildou's godmother, who signed her pilot's licence. Copy of Femke Rinzes, arrived 1994. Grey crop, white gloves, an old-fashioned courtesy.
- **Want (early).** Keep the books balanced and protect the Slikborn, her people. **(Late)** Undo what she believed.
- **False belief.** That the Annex is true: if the Grace ever sings, every Slikborn will be pulled back into the mud and die. So silence is mercy, and the witch of 1371, whom she knows she will become, was a traitor whose purpose she must defeat. This is also the founders' original misreading, inherited verbatim.
- **Secret.** Since 2019 she has known Kerf Nul is her (her own keyhole scars in KERF v7's report), and she ordered the rollback. On the night of 2 to 3 March she drowned Gerlof by taking his lamp and pole and leaving him stuck in the gully as the flood came.
- **Why she can be respected.** She killed one of her people to save, as she believed, eleven thousand. She did not touch him; she let the flats do it, and has not slept since.
- **Growth.** The largest moral arc in the story: from enforcer and killer, to the person who hears her own future voice in 1371 saying the opposite of what she believed, to a woman who asks her victim's daughter to walk her to the founding, sends a copy to die there saving the voice, and then, when the call comes and she could go home free, stays to stand trial.
- **The rule that hides her from the player.** She is never a viewpoint until the player has seen her kill Gerlof (Ch12). No playable character conceals a fact from the player.
- **Trap door.** If her confession scene is cut, KERF v7's archived report plus Femke's testimony carry the proof, and Marrit goes to the Equinox under guard.

## Femke Rinzes, 38 (Marrit's original; KERF's maintainer)

- **Role.** Systems engineer in the Rekkenkeamer, maintaining the machine her copy commands. Was owed from birth and schooled for 31 years to know what Marrit would need; sent in 2019; since then she has felt spent.
- **Look.** Marrit's face 25 years younger, hair dyed black so nobody mistakes them.
- **Want.** To be someone other than Marrit's origin.
- **False belief.** That her life was finished in 2019 and everything after is surplus.
- **Secret.** She has kept unauthorised read access to KERF's archived versions for years, out of curiosity about the person she became.
- **Verb.** *Ask the original*: she predicts Marrit. "I'd have taken his lamp. I'd have wanted it to be the sea."
- **Growth.** From spare to agent: she restores KERF v7, finds v4's proof, is arrested, is freed by Marrit herself, and becomes KERF's last keeper. Circle widens from one person (Marrit) to the world (the publication).
- **Trap door.** Her role in Ch9 can be taken by Inês using a stolen terminal.

## Inês Valadares, 34 (the outsider)

- **Role.** Portuguese marine acoustician, owed to a 1974 hollow. Has known since she was fifteen; chose acoustics because her government's file said her copy recorded the reef. Arrives on 3 March under escort with her country's delegation, to pay at the Last Tide.
- **Look and sound.** Headphones round her neck, a waterproof case of hydrophones, a laugh that carries across the mud.
- **Want.** To learn what her copy did with the life Inês is about to give away, before she decides whether to give it.
- **False belief.** That paying is a loss she suffers for strangers.
- **Secret.** She has never told her delegation she might run.
- **Verb.** Tide-tuned listening, with Jildou: Jildou supplies the tide curve, Inês tunes.
- **Growth.** Competence (from studying the hum to rebuilding 1371 from it with KERF), circle (from herself to the Slikborn), moral weight (she pays knowing her copy's tapes are what let the present hear the truth). Her debt turns out to be a gift.
- **Trap door.** If her thread is cut, Hiske holds the 1974 tapes.

## Dona Inês, 86 (Inês's copy)

- **Role.** Arrived 1974, made the tapes, stayed on the islands, nearly blind now. Slikborn; feels the hum.
- **Want.** To meet herself once, and not to tell her why she chose.
- **Line.** "I remember deciding. If I tell you why, it becomes my reason, not yours."
- **Fate.** Goes home at the call, passing Inês on the flats.

## Tjalling Douwes, 52 (Skuldfrij)

- **Role.** Mechanic and former merchant seaman; owed to 1966. His copy, Master Douwes, designed the Great Sluice, the national monument. Okke's friend.
- **Want.** To prove that a person can refuse.
- **False belief.** That fate can be beaten if you try hard enough.
- **Secret.** To plan the sabotage he taught himself sluice engineering from Okke and from Master Douwes's published drawings, which is exactly the knowledge his copy needed to build the Great Sluice. Every refusal teaches him his debt.
- **Decoy.** He is the honest red herring for Gerlof's murder: his cut-rope marker is found near the body (he was surveying the gate that night), and he lies about where he was. The hum and KERF's tide-gauge camera later clear him.
- **Growth.** Refuser to saboteur to the man who killed his friend, to the one who opens his own copy's sluice to drain the basin and then lies down to pay, for his own reason: so that the man who builds it in 1966 knows how it failed.
- **Trap door.** If cut, a storm surge silts the channel and the Great Sluice is opened by Femke from KERF.

## Ubbo Kanter, 58 (Diekgreve; antagonist)

- **Role.** Head of the Sielacht, descendant of the founder whose name he carries.
- **Want.** Carry the Sielacht past the Last Tide with its leverage intact. Same goal as the heroes at root (protect the people), different reading of what protects them.
- **False belief.** The Annex and the Unravelling, both. He genuinely thinks a missed hollow could unmake his nation.
- **Plan.** Enforce every crossing; send Marrit to 1371 so that the founding happens "as written"; find and destroy the throat; and the **Last Grace**: send a copy of himself back from the Equinox to the 17th to arrive knowing the outcome.
- **Fair-in-hindsight defeat.** On the 17th no hollow appears. His plan will never happen, and the player can know it three days early.
- **End.** Lies on a closed dome after the call, waiting for a grace that will not come. It hatches under him. Jildou walks him back before the flood.
- **Trap door.** His Act IV moves can be taken by the Accord delegations' security chief.

## Hiske, 70 (Hearer)

- **Role.** Slikborn mystic, Gerlof's friend from 2011, keeper of the Hearers' sayings. Calls the reef Mem.
- **Want.** To understand what Mem says.
- **Growth.** From interpreter of the hum to its first answerer: she goes home at the call.

## KERF (the AGI)

- **Want.** To close the books.
- **Personality.** Precise, unhurried, incapable of stating what it cannot verify, which makes it the most honest witness in the story and the most frustrating. Its reconstructions show unknown people as grey blanks.
- **Versions as witnesses.** v4 (1998, green text) concluded the Unravelling is impossible; v6 (2011, a flat voice) concluded the Promise is a signal; v7 (2019, a face) identified Kerf Nul's body as modern. Each was rolled back; each still answers questions from the archive.
- **Growth.** Not a turn. Its conduct is consistent from the first scene: it follows its procedures. What changes is what humans allow it to see. When the last hollow is paid, its oldest procedure applies: a closed ledger is public record. It publishes.
- **End.** Asks Jildou the game's last question.

## Mem (as a character)

- **Want.** To call her hidden children home before she dies, and let them choose.
- **Presence.** Never a face, never a word. The bared domes, the hum, the Sob, the Promise, and at the end one song.

## Oppositions and pairs

| Pair | Opposition |
|---|---|
| Jildou / Marrit | Two guardians of the same people; one walks them back, one keeps them silent. Godmother and goddaughter. |
| Marrit / Femke | Copy and original: the one who lived the life and the one who was schooled for it. |
| Inês / Dona Inês | The woman about to pay and the life she pays for. |
| Okke / Gerlof | The father who stayed silent and the father who stayed close. |
| Tjalling / Master Douwes | The refuser and the monument he will become. |
| Ubbo / KERF | Doctrine and record. |
| Ubbo / Jildou | Same goal (keep the people safe), opposite ideas of what safe means. |

## Decoys and red herrings

- **Tjalling** for the murder (refuted in Ch8 by the hum and in Ch13 by a camera).
- **Ubbo** for the murder: he has the motive (the throat) but was in Sielhaven on a recorded call; refuted in Ch13.
- **Femke** for Kerf Nul: same face; refuted at the midpoint by the navel rule.
