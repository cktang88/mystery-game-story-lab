# Writer C: outline, *The Ouro's Hand* (revision 2)

## One sentence

When money is disarmed weapons, an interpreter learns the peace needs her body.

## One paragraph (master plot)

In the Shiet system a fifty-year ceasefire between the human Nomes and the voiceless, grafting Bnne is forty days from renewal. Every holok in 42 worlds is the stored computation of a disarmed weapon, and the steward mind that keeps them will be wiped if the weapons are rearmed. When her mentor is torn from the living treaty by an invisible killer in the king's own Mantle, and the censor's Daybook says he died at home, interpreter Tamoun Besa starts paying for the truth one audited second at a time. Seven nine-second motes from the future arrive the same night.

- **First disaster:** her audits book themselves to the dead signing queen's account, and the spymaster and the Treasurer both work out, from her face and gait, that she is the queen's woken copy. By the realm's Law of the Body that makes her the rightful Ouro, and also the forger of the peace.
- **Second disaster:** her proof of the forgery sets off a hunt. Her friend smuggles one of her eleven sleeping copies out of a burning crypt, a banker dies at her door having been warned by her own future, and Tamoun flees through her bedroom wall into the alien half of the ring. There a rival alien court cages her, and the Treasurer makes her claim public to turn the whole court against her.
- **Third disaster:** to make the treaty take on the night of the dead, she must forge an alien text to escape, refuse two crowns, and graft her own arm into the union. The Treasurer's second, sent to tear her out, is asked instead to cut, and does.

The peace becomes permanent and owed by no one's body again. The censor airs the scar. Before the court of the nomes, Tamoun is asked whether she is the queen, and her answer breaks the oldest law in 42 worlds.

## Thread paragraphs

- **Thread A, the Tree (Tamoun).** A murdered mentor and a lying newspaper lead to a living slip in a lunch box, then to a forgery under lacquer, then to a message in her own teacup. She is the signature and, by law, the forger. She flees through her wall, is caged by aliens, forges her way out, comes out of the treaty itself on renewal night, pays with her arm, and at the Hearing chooses what she is.
- **Thread B, the Daybook (Pamin).** A spymaster who hides a king's crime the king did not commit. He watches a time-travel plume move the markets, identifies the interpreter as the rightful Ouro by her walk, keeps the secret, catches a leak, defies his king, buys the truth from a frightened machine, cuts the seven clips into the past, and airs the scar.
- **Thread C, the Sickles and the Hulk (Phib).** A debt-bonded informer whose report killed his mentor. He is sent across six jump-days to burn eleven sleeping women and smuggles one out instead. He learns his handler was never who he thought, turns double agent, loses the sleeper to the governor who holds his sister, carries that governor's offer to his friend, and steals back the kibot.

## Structure at a glance

Five acts, 25 chapters and a coda. Each act opens on a small, personal hook that carries lore. A persistent **40-day board** appears at each chapter boundary, with days across the top, the seven clip slots, three thread lanes, and **term tags on handled objects** (handling the kibot tags "kibot"; touching the Mantle log tags "Law of the Body"). The board works as a glossary built from objects.

## ACT 1: THE LIE IS FREE (D0 to D10)

**Act question:** who killed Apa Pjol, and why does the Daybook say he died at home?

### Ch 1. Tea Through the Wall (D0, Tamoun)

- **Cell 31, morning.** Tea in Talou's cracked porcelain cup. A vine comes through a hairline crack in the lacquered wall and grafts "rain" to her wrist (**tutorial: graft-reading and graft-writing**). The vine leans toward the cup, where a fine green line sits in the crack.
- **The welcome at the Wall.** The king, unmantled, rehearses pressing his palm to the lacquered union. Through the palm-sized reading port Tamoun reads Paopi's graft: *"Ours is ready. Is yours?"* On the Bnne face, a swelling bud. **The obligation is two-way, and the player sees the Bnne half of it from the first hour.**
- **Paopi forgets a name.** Paopi prunes an old greeting slip to make room and then cannot recall Pjol's name until it touches the slip again (**plant: cutting a callused graft takes its memory with it**).
- **The bloom.** The union's "Epip bloom" sheds lacquer flakes; the wardens shrug.
- **The court.** The treaty is recited from sherds: *"…renewed at the Wall by the Ouro's hand."* Kyra pays for the tea with counting beads and a cane. Maro, her younger copy in gloves, signs the bill for her and asks Tamoun what the Bnne word for a second would be. Apol says the Bnne will strike at the lapse. Horsi of Pemdje, loud, toasts the end of debt.
- **Tamoun teaches Phib.** At the reading port: "Never pull an interpreter off the wood. Cut. Clean. Here." She adds: "The first thing a Bnne grafts to a stranger is *near*."
- **Pjol at tea:** "Rice keeps things alive." "I'll be at the Wall tonight."

### Ch 2. The Kibot (D0 night, Phib)

- Phib carries Pjol's lacquered kibot, opens it in a scrape and finds a living slip in rice.
- He drops a report in a Treasury lacquer box for "the Strategos's runner" (**tradecraft tutorial**) and is paid in Treasury-sealed holoks.
- He delivers the kibot at 22:10. **The player knows Phib is an informer. Tamoun does not.**

### Ch 3. Died at Home (D1, Tamoun, then Pamin)

- **Tamoun at dawn.** She finds Pjol dead below the reading port, his graft **torn**, not cut. By noon the Daybook says "at home".
- **Pamin at 02:00 (playable).** He scrubs the raw hall: Pjol at the port; a person-shaped **gap** walking in; Pjol jerked backward by an invisible grip; the fall. Pamin believes the Mantle means the king.
- **Committed choice: the caption.** "At home" is canon. "Killed" stands if chosen: the murder becomes public on D1, Kyra destroys Maro's stained gloves, and the king's trust in Pamin drops. That has consequences in Ch 14.
- **Promotion.** Tamoun is made acting Chief Interpreter and gets an audit line.

### Ch 4. The Truth Costs (D2, Tamoun)

- **First audit.** Place, time and radius; the cost counter ticks. She watches her mentor torn away by nothing.
- The gap leaves by the **Treasury corridor**. At 23:41:07 seven sparks flare.
- The charge is booked to "the Signer's Share", which she assumes is an office account.

### Ch 5. The Shadow Spend (D2 to D3, Pamin)

- The Djeme Houses tell clients that forty-day motes have arrived. Such a send costs a third of the reserve. The Houses' own pawned cores hold 2 percent, so only the reserve could pay, so both signers' roots exist. Futures slide.
- Through a core's secret power draw, Pamin's network watches the Houses send a one-hour test mote, and a clerk finds it an hour earlier (**time travel taught by experiment**).
- The Signer's Share has moved for the first time in 29 years.

### Ch 6. Rice, Not Water (D5 to D6, Tamoun)

- She reads the slip: *"each giver's root sends a new shoot… then the two trees are one and need no keeper."* It contradicts the treaty, and it explains Paopi's bud.
- Senetom (Archnotary, wax and reed pens) offers an **assay lens** for "what the union really says". Choice: tell or keep. Canon is keep; telling brings Senetom a day early in Ch 13.
- **The first mote hunt.** **C7**: an arm hanging from the trunk, fading into void below the elbow. **C1**: a hand cutting lacquer by the port.

### Ch 7. The Queen's Walk (D10, Pamin; the act-ending reveal, delivered by play)

- **Pamin does the attribution himself:**
  - He buys the W.Y. 0 signing dust: Ouro Tsenoute at 52, walking to the union.
  - He lays Tamoun's D2 walk (from her audit-hall entry) beside it and age-matches the face.
  - He sets the gait cadence and finds the third-step habit in both.
  - On the board he pins the W.Y. 15 "vat failure" and the W.Y. 15 foundling date together himself.
- **The game confirms the set:** "Same root."
- **The player learns, before Tamoun, that she is the queen's woken sleeper:** by law the Ouro, and by law the forger.
- Intercut: Kyra reaches the same answer by Treasury oversight and signs an order: Phib to the Relic Crypt. "Relic law wants two Treasury witnesses. Let it be him."

## ACT 2: THE OURO'S HAND (D10 to D26)

**Act question:** what does the treaty really say, and who is the shoot? After the midpoint: who is hunting the queen's copies, and where can Tamoun go?

### Ch 8. Six Days to Shmin (D10 to D16, Phib)

- **Hook:** a sherd from his mother says his sister has been moved to the Pemdje hulk's salvage gang.
- He buys a jump route: routes arriving after the renewal cost triple, a war-risk premium. He passes the Vault-Ring.
- **At Shmin:** 42 banners; a debt court where seconds sign for their originals and the bailiffs accept it. The galaxy's money, shown.

### Ch 9. The Window (D12, Tamoun)

- Ethen raises a bloom, a 2 m void the wardens think seasonal. Tamoun cuts a palm-sized window beside the port and matches the slip's cut face to the scar (a forensic ring-matching puzzle). **The forgery is proven, and the forger is Talou.**
- Reading the forged slip, she sees the trap: to a Bnne it says the same thing ("the giver's hand renews"), and to humans it says fifty years. Thout never objected because the cut came before the callus.
- She re-lacquers the window before the bloom closes.
- Later she finds **C4**: the vine cut and withdrawing from her crack. She knocks; the vine answers. Not yet.

### Ch 10. The Gap That Walks Wrong (D14 to D17, Tamoun and Pamin)

- **Gait attribution of an invisible person.** The gap's height line sits against the banner rail. The king's public rehearsal walk has a pause on the third step; the gap's does not. The gap fits Kyra's height but not her cane. **Not the king.** A body of Kyra, walking young.
- Pamin sees the charges, realises he protected the wrong man, catches **Tsia** leaking and keeps her running as his channel. He finds **C2**.

### Ch 11. Eleven Faces (D19, the MIDPOINT; Phib and Tamoun intercut)

- **Phib in the crypt:** twelve vats, one dry since W.Y. 15. Eleven sleeping women, all with Tamoun's face.
- **Committed choice: burn, refuse, or refuse and smuggle.** Canon is to smuggle: he unplugs one vat into a cold-cargo pod labelled "bond archives" and walks out. Behind him the warden burns ten and reports eleven.
- **Tamoun, the same hour.** The vine strains for the cup and tears its own bark. The green line in the crack has grown the scar's callus shape. Fingertip, touch-view: *her / shoot / choose*. Talou's recorded voice: "Choose it."
- **Two reveals in one beat:** she learns what the player has known since Ch 7, and the player learns that the copies are being killed and one survives.

### Ch 12. Archive Fire (D20 to D22)

- **Pamin** captions the crypt "Archive fire, no one hurt" and keeps the raw.
- **Tamoun's committed choice: whom to tell** (Pamin, the king, Senetom or no one). Canon is no one human: she grafts it to Ethen, and Ethen begins to widen the crack.
  - Telling Pamin brings his warning on D22 instead of D26, and she is away from home on D24.
  - Telling the king brings her arrest on D23, and she crosses that night.
  - Telling Senetom moves Senetom's visit to D23, with Djeme guards; Senetom lives and a guard dies.
- She finds **C6**: her own face saying "Cut there."

### Ch 13. The Warning (D23 to D24, Pamin watching through Tsia)

- Senetom watches her own mote: herself at the freight dock at 17:50, with Maro behind her. She goes anyway, with guards. Maro sees them and melts away. **The moment happens; she lives through it.**
- At 18:00 she sends the mote back, closing her loop.
- At 19:30 she cuts through a scrape toward cell 31. The dust goes dark for thirty seconds, then she staggers out and falls.
- At 19:34 Tamoun arrives and kneels. Senetom presses a sherd into her hand: "your share".
- The Treasury buys and publishes nine seconds of Tamoun with red hands.
- **Rule proven again:** foresight shows a moment, not a fate. Truth can be bought in the part you want.

### Ch 14. Held for Her Protection (D25 to D26)

- Phib lands the pod on the Pemdje hulk with his sister's gang (**off-ring location; bond labour on screen**).
- Pamin tells the king. The king orders Tamoun "held".
- **Pamin's betrayal of his master:** he walks to a scrape and warns her by hand. If the Ch 3 truth caption was chosen, the king has frozen him out, and the warning comes only as the guards arrive.
- **The crossing.** Guards at the door; Tamoun peels the lacquer and Ethen's opening takes her. Dust-sight fails at the bare wood, and **she goes through her bedroom wall into the Bnne half.**
- Kyra's men take the kibot. Phib finds **C3**.

## ACT 3: THE CLAIM (D26 to D39)

**Act question:** can she reach the union, and on whose terms? Opposition: the Koiak court, Kyra, Horsi and the king.

### Ch 15. Groves Without Dust (D27, Tamoun)

- **Genre shift.** Silence, darkness, warm sap; the HUD's dust overlay dies. Navigation and conversation are touch.
- **The Bnne court, shown.** Paopi's union groves nurse the Bnne shoot. Elder **Koiak's** trade-groves stack hull-timber like money and graft: *"A graft that fails can be grafted again in fifty years. A market lost is lost."* They hold Wall Bonds and a prepaid contract (they do not say with whom).
- **The consent rule**, explained to her by Paopi's refusal to explain.
- Reading Ethen's rings, she finds it was **placed** (W.Y. 37): her friend was arranged. The vine's twelve years say it was also real.
- **Hathor**, attentive and gentle, becomes her attendant.

### Ch 16. The False Flag (D28, Phib)

- He goes to Apol for protection and recites his reports. Apol: "I've never heard your name." The Treasury boxes and Treasury holoks click into place. **Kyra ran him.**
- He turns: Pamin recruits him as a double agent. He reveals the Bnne graft-messages he read aloud for Kyra: **Hathor is Koiak's spy**, and Koiak is Kyra's partner.
- He rushes to cross and warn Tamoun, but it is too late (Ch 18).

### Ch 17. The Claim (D31, Pamin at court)

- **Kyra's move, on screen.** At the full court she publishes a bought face-and-gait match of the interpreter against the signing queen: *"The fugitive is the old queen's body."* Then she names the second edge: by the Law of the Body, Tamoun **is** Tsenoute, who ordered the forgery, so she is the forger.
- **The court fractures on screen.**
  - The king's party: hold her.
  - **Horsi** stands: "Then she is Ouro, and an Ouro can repudiate her own bonds." Three governors stand with him.
  - Tsia (acting Archnotary) pales as the bond book shakes.
  - Apol watches.
- **Pamin's verb:** the court mood gauge is now six coloured bands, one per faction, and his captions push them.

### Ch 18. The Cage (D29 to D34, Tamoun)

- **D29.** Hathor moves her "for safety" to a Koiak grove.
- **D32.** The wood grows shut around her, grafted *"guest until the lapse"*. A cage that is also a courtesy.
- **Opposition and a decision.** Ethen can't break another grove's graft, but it reaches her through the roots, slip by slip. She reads the cage's grammar and starts planning.
- **D34. Kyra crosses.** Kyra and Maro face Tamoun through the living bars: the antagonist and the anchor in one frame.
  - **Kyra's offer:** stay here until midnight and walk free tomorrow, unhunted. "Go to that tree and you go as the woman who forged it."
  - **Tamoun asks Maro her name.** Maro has none. Tamoun: "In my trade, you name a thing by the first thing it does."
  - Kyra's hand tightens on her cane.

### Ch 19. Horsi's Price (D33 to D35, Phib, then Tamoun)

- **D33, the hulk (Phib).** His sister's gang was informed on, and Horsi's overseers have the pod. Horsi: "Two bodies of the queen. One who'll sign, and one who'll sleep until I need her." He offers Phib's sister's release if Phib carries an offer across.
- **D35 (Phib crosses as interpreter).** Horsi's offer to Tamoun: protection, a crown, and the repudiation of the Wall Bonds.
- **Tamoun's decision: refuse.** The bonds are the savings of the Wall Quarter: her tea-sellers, her neighbours.
- **Phib's cost:** his sister stays on the hulk. Tamoun tells him, "Then we owe her." **He chooses Tamoun.**
- **Her own forgery.** That night, alone, she regrafts the cage's message from "guest until the lapse" to "guest until the fruit", committing an interpreter's crime to escape a Bnne cage. The grove accepts it, because to the Bnne any graft is speech.

### Ch 20. The Vault (D35 to D36, Pamin)

- **Off-ring: the Vault-Ring.** Cold racks of stripped weapon-cores humming as banks.
- **The Oikonomos's stake, shown.** Its price list shifts as Pamin phrases questions. Any question that raises the risk of rearming is ruinous; one that lowers it is cheap. Pamin finds the cheap question (a puzzle in phrasing): *"What has the Treasury prepaid that settles after the lapse?"* **Price: one holok.**
- **The answer:** 2.1 million holoks to the Koiak groves for wood-routes. Kyra's hedge, and proof of intent.
- The balance-click, a sound that means relief.

### Ch 21. Framed in His Own Mantle (D37 to D39)

- **D37.** Pamin shows the king the gait audit, the crypt raw and the hedge.
- **D38.** Phib copies the Mantle log ("to the Treasurer's person", D0, 21:20 to 23:50). They show Apol on his platform. **Apol decides to hold the sickles at the lapse.** The turn happens two days before the climax, not in its last minutes.
- **D39.** The king orders Kyra's arrest. She is already gone: sanctuary with Koiak, Maro beside her. Kyra to Maro: "Tomorrow you'll go into the hall."

## ACT 4: NAIROUZ (D40)

**Act question:** will the union take, and whose hand renews it?

### Ch 22. The Set Places (D40, 19:00 to 22:52; three threads intercut)

- Nairouz. The Wall Quarter sets places for the dead along the Wall. Horsi arrives at the hall with a sealed cold pod.
- **22:15 to 22:21 (Phib):** steals the kibot from the annex. **C3**, recognised.
- **22:30 (Pamin):** the market board falls under "VAULTS RECLAIMED AT LAPSE". **C5.** Horsi messages Apol: "Rearm only the seven." Apol, on his platform, refuses (a short Pamin-and-Apol channel scene).
- **22:40:** the vine withdraws from cell 31. **C4.**
- **22:45 (Tamoun):** the union begins to fruit. "Until the fruit": the cage opens. She walks the dark groves to the trunk.

### Ch 23. The Union (22:52 to 23:35, Tamoun)

- **22:52.** She steps out of the Bnne face into 42 banners, a public claimant walking out of the treaty. Phib gives her the kibot. Horsi's surgeons stand by the pod.
- **22:58, C1.** She cuts away a hand-span of lacquer. The dust void blooms around the knife.
- **23:04, C2.** The union buds. The king kneels. In the Daybook, Pamin cuts the live feed "for a fault".
- **23:06: her committed choice: graft or refuse.**
  - **Graft (canon).** She regrafts the true slip (the final graft-writing puzzle, one-handed, on a timer) and sinks her forearm in. Ethen's vine meets it from the Bnne side.
  - **Refuse, with a sleeper.** Horsi's surgeons wake Nesi and join her arm. Tamoun stands beside her.
  - **Refuse, with no sleeper.** Horsi's men force Tamoun's arm in.
- **23:11.** Maro comes through the Bnne face and grips the shoulder to **tear**. The touch-view flashes red at the edges (the Ch 1 cue).
- **23:12, C6.** Tamoun looks at Maro and lays her hand on her own elbow (or on Nesi's): "Cut there."
- **23:13.** **Maro cuts.** Clean. It is her first act. The guards seize her alive.
- **23:15, C7.** The forearm hangs from the trunk, fading into void.
- **23:30.** The union takes. On the Bnne side Ethen goes still, then turns from her politely, like a stranger.
- **23:35.** Koiak surrenders Kyra to Apol's guards.

### Ch 24. The Cut (23:38 to midnight, Pamin)

- **The send, with a decider and dissent.**
  - The Oikonomos announces that both roots are present and the arrived plume is due.
  - **Tsia** dissents for the savers. **Horsi** dissents for the debt.
  - **Tamoun decides:** "Someone already paid for those forty days. It was us." Blood on the plate. Paopi asks Ethen, who does not know why, and Ethen seals.
- **The final cutting puzzle (Pamin).** He finds the seven exact clips (moment, angle, nine-second window). Every wrong frame gets the balance-click: "That send does not arrive." The player may try to cut a warning to Pjol on D0, and it is refused.
- **The send.** The reserve board drops a third.
- **The broadcast (committed choice, 23:51).**
  - **Air the true cut (canon):** the kneel, the bared scar, the slip, Maro's cut, Tamoun alive.
  - **Splice the rehearsal (the lacquer):** in four days the Bnne court's own grafted record reaches the nomes, and the lie collapses.
  - **Nothing:** the Treasury's bought clips are all the realm has.
- **Midnight.** No vault is reclaimed. The two trees are one.

## ACT 5: THE HEARING OF THE BODY (D60)

### Ch 25. The Hearing (21 Thout W.Y. 50)

- **Small hook:** Nesi, woken, learning to hold a cup. She cannot yet say her name; she has none.
- **The nome court at Kellia.** Five governors on screen, Horsi loudest; the king; Tsia with the bond book; Paopi as observer; Maro (Shotem-to-be) in custody; Kyra in custody.
- **The question:** is Tamoun Besa Ouro Tsenoute? The court's interpreter is Phib.
- **The arguments, on screen, each a faction's stake.**
  - **Horsi:** "She is; crown her," because an Ouro can repudiate her bonds.
  - **The king's advocates:** "She is, so she is the forger. Try her."
  - **Kyra's advocate:** "She is *not*." If copies are separate persons, Maro alone killed and Kyra merely instructed, and every bond Maro signed is void. **The true answer serves the villain**, and the player hears it argued by her before choosing.
  - **Tsia:** silent on the law; the book shakes either way.
- **Tamoun's testimony (final committed choice).**
  - **"I am not her" (canon).** The Law of the Body breaks. Copies are persons:
    - she is neither Ouro nor forger, and the king reigns by descent;
    - Maro is herself: her killings and her cut are hers, and she takes the name **Shotem**;
    - Kyra is convicted as instigator on the Mantle log, the hedge and the burn order;
    - the ten burned sleepers are recorded as murdered persons;
    - every debt signed by a second is void, which takes a fifth off the crown's book (Maro signed the rollovers);
    - across 42 worlds, seconds gain names.
  - **"I am her":** crowned and indicted at once. She refuses repudiation and reigns under a charge no court can try. Seconds stay their originals.
  - **Silence:** the court applies the law, and she is crowned against her will.
- **Ending beat:** in canon, Horsi's puppet plan dies in one sentence, because Nesi is now only a person.

### Coda: The Set Places (1 Thout W.Y. 51)

- **Tamoun Besa**, under her own name, one-armed, teaches at the school. Nesi lives with her.
- On Nairouz they set places at the Wall: for Pjol, Talou, Senetom, the ten sleepers, and **Ouro Tsenoute**, the woman Tamoun was in law and declined to be.
- Nesi drinks from the cracked cup. The green line in its crack has kept growing. Tamoun reads it for her: *choose*.
- The crack in the Wall has callused shut. **Knock or not.** If she knocks, after a long silence, a shoot comes through and grafts **"near"**, the stranger-greeting from Ch 1. Ethen is beginning again.
