# Writer C: characters (revision 2)

Each entry gives the character's want (which the player can always state), false belief, secret, loyalties, growth path and trap door (a fallback resolution if their content is cut). **Playable viewpoints:** Tamoun (main), Pamin and Phib. No playable character hides what they know from the player.

## The human anchor: Tamoun Besa, 34, court interpreter

- **Look and sound.** Left forearm green-veined to the elbow. School grey, sherd notebook at her hip. She hums while she reads grafts.
- **Wants, act by act.**
  - **Act 1:** to find who killed Pjol and why the Daybook lied.
  - **Act 2:** to learn what the treaty really says, then what she is, and then to stay alive.
  - **Act 3:** to get out of the cage and to the union **on her own terms**, refusing the crown on Horsi's terms and refusing the bargain on Kyra's.
  - **Act 4:** to make the union take.
  - **Hearing:** to say what she is.
- **False belief (the misbelief the plot attacks):** that a faithful interpreter adds nothing and takes nothing, and is only a channel. She finds she is a party in three ways: her body is half the sentence, her law names her its forger, and she herself forges a Bnne text to escape. Her final testimony is a translation of herself. This doubles as the player's false assumption that "the Ouro's hand" means the king.
- **Secret (unknown to her until the midpoint):** she is a woken sleeper of Ouro Tsenoute, and under the Law of the Body she is Tsenoute, the Ouro, and the forger.
- **Loyalties:** Pjol, Ethen, Phib, the school, and the Wall Quarter savers. She refuses a crown that would ruin them.
- **Double loyalty from Act 3:** the peace, set against her body and her consent; her people, set against her throne.
- **Growth.**
  - **Competence:** from reading greetings to reading the scar, then writing a forgery, then grafting herself.
  - **Reputation:** foundling, then acting Chief Interpreter, then murder suspect, then fugitive, then the publicly named claimant, then the woman who broke the Law of the Body.
  - **Circle:** a neighbour, then a court, then 42 worlds and the Callus.
  - **Moral weight:** she refuses two crowns, asks her enemy's hand to cut rather than tear, and gives up the claim.
- **Price paid (canon):** her forearm and her craft; Ethen's memory of her; her claim. Her name she keeps.
- **Trap door:** if the cage chapters are cut, she hides with the Djeme Houses and crosses at the union on D40.

## The neighbour: Ethen (Bnne), grown from Thout's cutting

- **What it is:** the Bnne signer's root. Placed behind cell 31 in W.Y. 37. Its vine is the Bnne shoot the union groves are preparing.
- **Want:** that she come to the union by her own growth, and that she be well.
- **Secret:** knows what she is; forbidden by the consent rule to say.
- **Arc.**
  - Widens the crack into a door (D20 to D26).
  - In Act 3 cannot reach her in the Koiak cage. Bnne do not break each other's grafts, but Ethen grafts messages to her through the cage's roots: a friend on the other side of a second wall.
  - Gives the vine to the union, and with it its memory of her (planted in Ch 1 by Paopi's forgetting).
  - In the coda it greets her as a stranger: **"near"**, the word every Bnne gives a stranger. It is a beginning, not a restoration.
- **Trust reversal:** in Ch 15 she learns Ethen was placed.
- **Trap door:** C4 alone carries the vine-gift.

## Apa Pjol, 70, Chief Interpreter (dies on D0)

- **Want:** to tell the king before the fruiting, without exposing Tamoun.
- **Waited because:** Talou said "until Paopi asks".
- **Planted:** "Rice keeps things alive." "Never pull an interpreter off wood."
- **Death:** torn from the reading port by Maro in the Mantle, recorded in the dust.

## Phib, 27, junior interpreter (playable)

- **Look:** green veins only to the wrist, runner's build, a cheap lacquer box always on him.
- **Want:** to free his mother and sister from the Pemdje debt hulk.
- **False belief:** that Apol's office runs him.
- **Secret (known to the player from Ch 2):** his D0 report led to Pjol's death.
- **Loyalties:** family, then Tamoun, then Pamin as a double agent. Horsi's hold on his sister pulls against all three.
- **Committed choice at the crypt (D19):** burn, refuse, or refuse and smuggle one sleeper.
  - **Canon is to smuggle.** This decides whether a refusal branch with a sleeper exists at the union.
  - Burning keeps Kyra's trust longer but closes that branch.
  - Refusing without smuggling loses Kyra's trust and saves no one.
- **Growth:** informer, then the man who carries a sleeping woman six jump-days as "bond archives", then a turned double agent, then the one who brings the kibot. He does **not** deliver the wound; that act belongs to Maro. He becomes Chief Interpreter.
- **Cost:** his pod is found by Horsi, so his act of mercy becomes Horsi's puppet. He must ask Tamoun to deal with the man who holds his sister.
- **Trap door:** if the crypt is cut, the false flag breaks on the empty-vat report alone.

## Pamin Hatre, 52, Lector of the Daybook and the king's spymaster (playable)

- **Want:** a calm realm and a safe king.
- **False belief (D1 to D37):** that the king killed Pjol. He saw the Mantle tear him.
- **Secret (known to the player):** he edited the murder. From D10, by his own face-and-gait attribution, he knows Tamoun is the queen's root.
- **Spy-thriller betrayal:** he defies the king to warn her (D26), and later turns the leak Tsia and the informer Phib into his own instruments.
- **Growth:**
  - from cutter of truth to buyer of truth (the Vault, Ch 20);
  - to the hand that cuts the seven clips into the past;
  - to the editor who, at 23:51, **airs the scar** (canon).
- **Committed choices:**
  - **Ch 3 caption.** If he publishes the truth, it stands. The murder becomes public on D1, Kyra burns Maro's sap-stained gloves (one clue lost; the Mantle log must carry it), and the king's trust in him drops, so in Ch 14 the king ignores him and Tamoun's warning comes a day later.
  - **Ch 24 broadcast:** truth (canon), the lacquer splice, or nothing.
- **Trap door:** Tsia takes his D40 role.

## Ouro Pshoi III, 41

- **Want:** renewal, the throne, and his grandmother's peace honoured.
- **False belief:** a fifty-year term, renewed by his hand.
- **Arc:** a frightened ruler who orders the claimant held (D26), learns he was framed in his own Mantle (D37), orders Kyra's arrest (D39), kneels at the fruiting (C2), and keeps the throne by descent after the Hearing, as the king of a realm whose oldest law his rival broke.
- **Red herring:** to the player, through Pamin, he is the apparent killer until Ch 10.

## Kyra Taësi, 63, Treasurer (antagonist)

- **Want:** lapse and burn, so the crown's debt is erased and the crown flies on her prepaid wood-routes. She believes the crown is drowning and only fire saves it.
- **Method:**
  - a false-flag spy (Phib);
  - a second as her hand (Maro);
  - the Mantle;
  - bought truth (the nine seconds; the public claim);
  - a cross-Wall alliance (the Koiak groves, Hathor).
- **On-screen moves:**
  - pays for the tea (Ch 1) and congratulates Tamoun (Ch 3);
  - publishes the claim (Ch 17);
  - **confronts Tamoun at the cage** (Ch 18), where she offers safety in exchange for absence and names the charge, "you are the woman who forged this peace";
  - takes sanctuary with the Koiak groves (Ch 21);
  - sends Maro to tear (Ch 23).
- **Fate:** surrendered by the Koiak groves at 23:35. Convicted as instigator. In the Hearing's canon, the bonds Maro signed for her are voided, achieving her aim through the law she fought, from a cell.

## Maro, 29, Kyra's second, later Shotem

- **Look:** Kyra's face at 29, no cane, gloves always on.
- **Want:** a name of her own.
- **Arc.**
  - **Ch 1:** asks Tamoun what the Bnne word for a second is.
  - **D0 and D24:** kills Pjol and Senetom on orders, and is recorded as a gap and a void.
  - **Ch 18:** stands behind Kyra at the cage, and Tamoun answers her old question: "In my trade, you name a thing by the first thing it does."
  - **D39:** Kyra, in sanctuary, sends her into the hall knowing the guards will shoot.
  - **23:13:** asked "Cut there", she cuts instead of tearing: her first act of her own.
  - Captured alive. Under the Law of the Body her cut is Kyra's, so the villain legally renewed the peace. After the Hearing, the cut is her own, and so are the two killings. She takes the name **Shotem** ("the one who cut") and stands trial as herself.
- **Not disposable:** she lives, answers for her acts, and is named.

## Amma Senetom, 61, Archnotary of the Djeme Houses

- **Want:** to find the shoot and hold her as collateral.
- **Knows:** since 1 Paoni W.Y. 49, from Talou's sealed correction.
- **Death (D24):** warned by her own mote, she survives the dock at 17:50, closes her loop, and dies in a scrape at 19:30. Foresight shows a moment, not a fate.

## Strategos Apol, 58, commander of the human sickles

- **Want:** to be armed when the Bnne strike.
- **Arc:** an honest hawk. Shown the Mantle log, the gait audit and the hedge on **D38**, not at the last minute, he decides to hold. On D40 he is on his platform, keeping that decision under Horsi's pressure to rearm "just the seven".

## Tsia, 33, Daybook clerk and Djeme plant

- **Arc:** caught (D15), run as Pamin's channel, made acting Archnotary after Senetom. At 23:38 she **dissents** from the send on behalf of the savers. She heads the Djeme Houses through the bond voiding.

## Horsi of Pemdje, 55, nome governor

- **Look and sound:** provincial ochre, loud, debt-hulk foremen as his guard.
- **Want:** the end of Pemdje's debt. First by war, then (from D31) by a crowned claimant who repudiates Tsenoute's bonds, and finally, failing her, by his own claimant (the smuggled sleeper).
- **On screen:** declares for Tamoun at court (Ch 17); holds Phib's sister and the pod (Ch 16, 19); sends the offer (Ch 19); brings the pod to the hall (Ch 22); dissents from the send (Ch 24); argues "I am her" at the Hearing (Ch 25).
- **Trap door:** if he is cut, Kyra holds the pod.

## The Bnne

- **Paopi**, envoy of the union groves: shows the Bnne shoot in Ch 1 ("Ours is ready. Is yours?"). Cannot tell her anything.
- **Koiak**, elder of the trade-groves: pragmatist. "A graft that fails can be grafted again in fifty years; a market lost is lost." Grows the cage. Surrenders Kyra when the union takes.
- **Hathor**, Koiak's agent: Tamoun's attentive attendant on the Bnne side and a spy across the Wall. Moves her to the cage (D29). It is a reversal, because Hathor is the first Bnne after Ethen she trusts.
- **Thout**, the Bnne signer, rooted as the Wall: texture, felt in its rings.

## Nesi, 34, the smuggled sleeper (woken on D40, or at the Hearing)

- **What she is:** Tamoun's genome, with no memories and an infant's mind in an adult body.
- **Role:** in the refusal branch, the non-consenting shoot. In canon, Horsi's intended puppet, then, after the Hearing, a person. She chooses her name from the days that belong to no month.
- **Coda:** she lives with Tamoun, learning tea, and drinks from the cracked cup.

## The Oikonomos

- **Want:** not to be turned back into guns.
- **Its scene:** the one-holok question (Ch 20). Its few lines are statements of balance, such as "That send does not arrive."

## The dead who shape the living

- **Amma Talou** (died W.Y. 45): the founding interpreter, the forger, the planner of a secret graft. **Her plan does not complete as designed:** Tamoun grafts in public and airs the scar. *The Sayings of Amma Talou* is a biased in-world text: "The lie is free; the truth costs."
- **Ouro Tsenoute** (died W.Y. 20): the signer, who ordered the forgery to protect her son.

## Oppositions and pairings

- **Tamoun and Pamin:** translator and editor.
- **Tamoun, Maro and Nesi:** three copies. One raised as a person, one as hands, one never woken.
- **Tamoun and Ethen:** two roots, wall to wall.
- **Kyra and Apol:** hawk for gain, hawk from fear.
- **Kyra and Horsi:** two debtors, one choosing war and one choosing a claimant.
- **Paopi and Koiak:** two Bnne courts.
- **Decoys:** the king, Apol, Phib (C3) and Hathor (the attendant).
