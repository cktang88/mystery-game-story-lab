# Characters

Three playable threads (Sayo, Seydou, then Tōkichi) start together at the D1 market. The player always knows each one's want, because it is stated in their first scene and re-shown on the chapter board. Each misbelief is also one of the player's false assumptions, so the reveal is a twist and an epiphany at once (Lisa Cron's method).

## The human anchor: Sayo Sidibé, 26, puppeteer (feet operator)

- **Job:** feet operator of the Mother's vessel in the Kanté troupe for sixteen years, apprenticed at 10 under a Tenmaya debt receipt. Feet operators are the lowest rank and hold the body's weight; she knows each vessel by how it stands.
- **Want (always stated):** Act 1, clear her name and find who killed Uke. Acts 2 and 3, find out what "was left" means and not die by it. Acts 4 and 5, pay what is actually owed.
- **Misbelief:** "The record is the truth, so what it shows of me is my fate." This is also the player's assumption from D14 to D53.
- **Secret:** none she keeps from the player. (The rule: playable characters never conceal from the player.) Her unknown: Dōjima placed her in the troupe at 10.
- **Link to hidden history:** of Bakary's line; the account names her; Uke died for her.
- **Growth:** *competence*, from feet to the whole body (she works all three positions alone at D55, which is impossible and which she does by hobbling the vessel's feet: a herder's trick on a puppet); *reputation*, from "the feet" (troupe nickname, *Ashi*) to "the one who stopped" (what the column calls her in the coda); *circle*, from one friend to the whole column, then a creature not of her species; *moral weight*, forgiving Uke's debt, staying by choice, and choosing who cuts the Gully mark.
- **Recurring objects:** her cutting knife; her wrapped body half; Uke's debt receipt; Ume's staff; the astrolabe; Mochi.
- **Trap door:** if the Jurutigi thread had to be cut, her D60 choice becomes staying with the left-behind Jūzaburō, with the same image and the same bevel payoff.

## Uke, the woken vessel (dead from D1, reconstructed)

- **Who:** Vessel 13 of Ofusa Tenmaya. Woke in YM 207, lived six years as Sayo's veiled wagon hand, named herself "Receipt" as a joke about having none.
- **Want (as reconstructed):** first, to be someone; later, to pay Sayo back. She thought the way to pay was to be left on D60 in Sayo's place.
- **Misbelief:** "A debt is paid by a body." She inherited the foundress's error in its kindest form.
- **What the player assembles:** soft first sandals, red-and-green "matching" clothes (colour blindness), calluses on her soles (she walked), knotting calluses on her fingers from Sayo's thread-work, the refused tether, her pigeon, her night walks to the vault.
- **Payoff:** the shadow "settled" mark she saw for five years; Sayo cuts it on D60.

## Seydou Tenmaya, 44, head clerk (*bantō*) of Tenmaya house (playable to D44)

- **Job:** runs the Mother's estate and its post office, and serves as the vessel's left hand ("the house serves the Mother with its left hand").
- **Want:** keep Tenmaya honoured and the column orderly through the Edge, which he has known about since YM 205.
- **Misbelief:** "Obedience to the dead is the same as loyalty to the living."
- **Secret, shown to the player from his first scene:** he held Uke's arms on D1. The player knows from the start, and Sayo does not. This is Hitchcock's bomb under the table: every Sayo–Seydou scene is double-loaded, like Ted Lasso's darts scene.
- **Growth:** *moral weight*, through the D31 confession, cutting the routings that undo his own orders, and dying while trying to fetch the truth for the woman he wronged. *Circle*: from one house to the rear he despised.
- **Death (D44):** fair causes, all shown earlier. Gonbei's D15 vow to "burn the vault"; Tōkichi's D31 sighting of Juruntan scouts; Seydou's D43 decision, played by the player, to go himself at night; the Tenmaya head-clerk mask he must wear to pass the vault guards; and the vault left unguarded because Dōjima cannot foresee the Juruntan.
- **Trap door:** if he must live, he is left behind (sentenced on D37) instead of killed, and his thread passes to Tōkichi the same way.

## Tōkichi Diarra, 19, pigeon courier (playable; takes over Seydou's thread)

- **Job:** runs birds and receipt halves along the column and to the scouts. Ichisugu pigeons are bred to home to a *moving* loft, which is the post's pride.
- **Want:** Act 1, earn his own loft and get out of his brother's shadow. Acts 2 to 5, find out who is routing him, then finish what Seydou started.
- **Misbelief:** "Front people use us; the routings are the Exchange's." It also hides the second hand from him.
- **Verb:** traversal along wagon roofs and lashings; tailing; **releasing a found bird to see where it homes** (how the player finds Kadiatou).
- **Growth:** *competence*, from errand runner to the courier who steals the pact receipt out of a burnt vault; *reputation*, from "Gonbei's little brother" to Seydou's heir in the post; *moral weight*, when he learns his brother killed Seydou and keeps it from the court (a respected choice; the game offers him the receipt to denounce Gonbei and never forces it).
- **Payoff:** the pigeon he gives Sayo on D60 brings her home.
- **Trap door:** if cut, his routings become Sayo's own pigeon errands.

## Gonbei Diarra, 35, leader of the Juruntan

- **Want:** free the rear from receipts and hereditary debt by breaking the vault and the Owner.
- **Misbelief:** "The Owner is a front machine." He has never traded with it, out of pride, until the night of D45, when he secretly lays Seydou's bloodied clerk's mask at its feet (a confession he cannot make to people; it is returned wrapped, which means kept).
- **Secret:** he killed Seydou in the dark. The player learns this on D56: Tōkichi gives the Jurutigi the bone necklace Gonbei left in his wagon, and ledger recall returns Gonbei's last gift, Seydou's mask.
- **Role:** the visible antagonist faction who is also right about the debts. He wants the same thing as Sayo (the column free of the dead's books) by the opposite means.
- **Growth:** *moral weight*. On D60 he comes to smash it, finds a dying being, and stays an hour.

## Jūzaburō Kanté, 61, master puppeteer (non-playable)

- **Want:** perfect, invisible service to the Mother.
- **Misbelief:** "The puppeteer has no will; the hand is the Mother's." He believes this so completely that he will not name the order or the second hand, even to save himself.
- **Function:** Sayo's teacher (he taught her the bevel lesson on D3); the first killer unmasked (D13); the first person left (D19), which teaches what "was left" means.
- **Memorable moment (D19):** as he is walked out to be left, he asks Sayo to work his feet one last time. She walks beside him, stepping as his feet step, to the edge of the column, and stops where the law says she must.

## Kadiatou Ōmiya, 52, clone-medic of the House of Forms

- **Want:** atone for growing bodies she suspected were people.
- **Secret:** she woke Uke. The player learns this on D20, when the pigeon homes to her loft.
- **Growth:** *circle*, from one smuggled vessel to opening the House on D55, where two of the nine remaining vessels can be woken.
- **Decoy:** in Act 1 she is the most suspicious person in the case (her pigeon, her falsified "dissolved" log, her motive to silence a runaway). She is innocent, and this honest red herring is refuted on D20.

## Ume Sidibé, 74, herder, Sayo's grandmother

- **Want:** keep the old buffalo alive and keep Sayo off the front's books.
- **Function:** keeper of the lost truth without knowing it. She owns the staff with Bakary's hobble, teaches hobble against tether on D1 (in a scene about saving a buffalo), and teaches the astrolabe on D4 ("so you can always find the column"). She says the herders' saying "bone lives while the Owner walks."
- **Trap door:** if cut, the staff comes to Sayo through the Sidibé wagon chest.

## Nana Kōriya, 40, the chanter

- **Function:** the dead's voice. She chants what the abacus spells on its bead-board. She is a witness to the D-1 order and keeps silent, out of terror. In Branch A she is the one who cuts the Gully mark when Sayo will not, so in that branch the machine's confession passes through a human mouth and hand.

## Dōjima, the Exchange mind

- **One-sentence want:** *every account settled; ledger and world in agreement.*
- **Misbelief (inherited):** "Fate must be helped." This is Ofusa's first house-code article.
- **Conduct:** consistent and legible all game. It never lies in a receipt. It orders the D1 killing through the Mother's voice because a person it cannot foresee threatened an account. It places Sayo in the troupe at 10. It reveals the pact at D37 as Ofusa read it.
- **Not an uprising or a turn:** at D55 it does not change its want. It discovers that its reading, not the ledger, was wrong, and so it withdraws the voices of the dead, which were the means by which it "helped." It speaks as itself for the first time, and the abacus goes quiet. After the Edge it asks to learn to read the past.
- **Price:** it is the reason two people died (Uke, and in a sense Bakary through its maker), and it is the only witness that can prove the Gully.

## The Jurutigi ("the debt-owner"; children call it the Bone Buffalo)

- **One-sentence want:** *to be kept company, by someone who chose to stay, when it stops.*
- **Not conquest, not becoming human.** It lends because lending is how its kind makes companions. It is honest and plain: it never uses the tether-sign, and it refuses substitutes.
- **Last gift (D60):** given Sayo's own hand laid on its plates, it returns an object from its ledger older than humans: a small split receipt of bone, its other half missing. This is the promised vista: it has kept company before, with someone.

## The founders (dead before the game; met through stagings, receipts and six diary entries)

- **Ofusa Tenmaya** (died YM 41): brilliant, frightened administrator who saved the first colony by planning, read a hobble as a tether, read her own crime as fate, and committed it. Her blocking of the founding play is a confession she could not stop making. Colour-blind (red-green).
- **Bakary Sidibé** (died YM 3): herder who understood the creditor because he understood animals; insisted debts be spoken at market.

## Mochi, the old buffalo

Planted on D1 (Sayo and Ume hobble him so he can graze behind instead of being left, the scene that teaches the hobble). He carries Sayo home on D60 to D62.

## Oppositions and pairings

- **Sayo / Seydou:** creditor and debtor in the literal sense (he holds her apprenticeship receipt) and the moral one (he helped kill her friend). Both serve the Mother's body, he with the left hand and she with the feet.
- **Sayo / Uke:** the same debt seen from both ends.
- **Tōkichi / Gonbei:** brothers; the routed and the debtless.
- **Dōjima / the Jurutigi:** one keeps every account, and the other is owed the only one that matters.
- **Ofusa / Sayo:** both read a mark that seems to foretell their part in a death. Ofusa obeys her reading; Sayo questions hers.
