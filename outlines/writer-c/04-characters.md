# Writer C: characters

Each entry gives the character's want (which the player can always state), false belief, secret, loyalties, growth path and trap door (a fallback resolution if their content is cut). **Playable viewpoints:** Tamoun (main), Pamin and Phib. No playable character hides what they know from the player.

## The human anchor: Tamoun Besa, 34, court interpreter

- **Look and sound.** Left forearm green-veined to the elbow from her interpreter's graft. Plain school grey, sherd-notebook at her hip. She hums when she reads grafts, an old habit Talou disliked.
- **Want (Act 1):** to learn who killed Pjol and why the Daybook lied. **Act 2:** to learn what the treaty really says, and then what she is. **Act 3:** to make the union take and keep the people she loves alive.
- **False belief (the misbelief the plot attacks):** that a faithful interpreter adds nothing and takes nothing, so she is only a channel and never a party. The story proves she is the party: her body is the missing half of the sentence. This doubles as the player's false assumption that "the Ouro's hand" means the king.
- **Secret (unknown to her):** she is the woken stock of Ouro Tsenoute, and the rightful Ouro.
- **Loyalties:** Pjol, Ethen, Phib, the school. **Double loyalty from the midpoint:** realm and peace against her own life and name.
- **Growth.**
  - **Competence:** from a junior who reads greetings to the only person who can read the union's scar.
  - **Reputation:** the green-armed foundling, then acting Chief Interpreter, then murder suspect, then a fugitive among the Bnne, then (officially) the heroine who died at the union.
  - **Circle:** one neighbour, then the court, then two civilisations.
  - **Moral weight:** she chooses to give up her arm, her name and her neighbour's memory of her.
- **Price paid:** her graft-arm (her craft), her name (Pamin's edit) and Ethen's memory of her.
- **Trap door:** if the Bnne-side act is cut, she hides with the Koinonia instead and crosses at the union on Day 40.

## The neighbour: Ethen (Bnne), grown from a cutting of Thout

- **What it is:** the Bnne signer's body, as Tamoun is the human signer's. Placed behind cell 31 in W.Y. 37.
- **Want:** that she come to the union by her own growth, and that she be well. Its first word to her, in W.Y. 37, was **"near"**.
- **Secret:** knows what she is; is forbidden by the consent rule to say it.
- **Arc:** the friend who widens a crack into a door (D20 to D26), shows her the fruiting without telling her (D27 to D39), and gives the vine, which holds their twelve years, to the union. It forgets her. In the coda it answers a stranger's knock with the same first word.
- **Trust reversal:** the player learns on D27 that Ethen was *placed*. Its care was arranged. The vine shows it was also real.
- **Trap door:** if Bnne scenes are cut, Ethen's vine-gift happens off screen, and C4 alone carries it.

## Apa Pjol, 70, Chief Interpreter (dies on Day 0)

- **Want:** to tell the king the truth before the fruiting, without exposing Tamoun.
- **Secret:** holds Talou's pruned slip and knows what Tamoun is.
- **Planted traits that pay off:** always eats from a lacquered bento box ("rice keeps things alive", D0); teaches that one never pulls an interpreter from a graft.
- **Death:** torn from the union by Maro.

## Phib, 27, junior interpreter (playable)

- **Look:** green veins only to the wrist (newer graft), runner's build, a cheap lacquer box always on him.
- **Want:** to free his mother and sister from Wall Bond debt-bondage on Pemdje.
- **False belief:** that the Strategos runs him, and that the war party is honest about its reasons.
- **Secret (known to the player from his first scene):** he reports on Pjol and Tamoun. On D0 he read the bento and reported it, and that report led to Pjol's death.
- **Loyalties:** his family, then Tamoun, then Pamin as a double agent.
- **Growth:** from informer to the man who refuses to burn eleven sleepers, turns double agent and cuts his friend free. The rule Tamoun teaches him in the opening ("never pull, always cut") is the rule he uses at the climax. He ends as Chief Interpreter.
- **Committed choice:** in the crypt (D19), burn or refuse. **Refusing is canonical and respected:** the warden burns them anyway, and Phib loses Kyra's trust earlier. If he burns them, he keeps her trust until D28, and the player carries that.
- **Trap door:** if the crypt is cut, Phib learns the false flag from the empty-vat report alone.

## Pamin Hatre, 52, Lector of the Daybook and the king's spymaster (playable)

- **Look and sound:** half-moon lenses, ink on his edit finger. The snip of the edit key. Sleeps in his chair.
- **Want:** to keep the realm calm, and the king safe.
- **False belief:** that he knows the truth he hides (he believes the king killed Pjol, D1 to D37).
- **Secret (known to the player):** he edited Pjol's death, and from D10 knows Tamoun is a signer's body.
- **Loyalties:** the king, then Tamoun. His spy-thriller betrayal of his master is smuggling her out against the king's order (D26).
- **Growth:** the cutter of truth learns he cut the wrong truth, then becomes the hand that sends the truth back in time and also the hand that hides her for ever. He ends the story where he began, editing, but now he knows what each cut costs.
- **Committed choice:** the broadcast at 23:51 on D40. Editing is canonical (E1). Doing nothing or broadcasting the raw feed gives E3. Inaction is respected and has an ending.
- **Trap door:** if his thread is cut, his D40 role passes to Tsia.

## Ouro Pshoi III, 41

- **Want:** renewal, the throne, and his grandmother's peace honoured.
- **False belief:** the treaty is a fifty-year term he renews with his hand.
- **Arc:** frightened ruler (orders Tamoun "held", D26), then shown he was framed (D37), then kneels to her at the fruiting (C2), then reigns on her silence. Under E3 he loses the throne.
- **Red herring:** to the player, through Pamin's honest belief, he looks like Pjol's killer (the veiled gap) until D14 to D17.

## Kyra Taësi, 63, Treasurer and Keeper of the Mantle and Relic Stocks (antagonist)

- **Want:** the term to lapse and the holok to burn, so the crown's debt is erased. She believes this is the only way to save a crown drowning in bonds.
- **Method:** a false-flag spy (Phib), a second as her hand (Maro), the Mantle as cover, and buying selective truth.
- **Met early:** she pays for Tamoun's tea at the welcome (D0) and congratulates her on her promotion (D1). Kind, precise, an ally in appearance.
- **Fate:** arrested on D40. Convicted because under the Law of the Body Maro's acts are hers.

## Maro, 29, Kyra's second

- **Look:** Kyra's face at 29, without the cane. Gloves always on (sap stains Treasury gloves; see the clue matrix).
- **Want:** a name of her own. She once asks Tamoun, an interpreter who names Bnne, what the word for a second is (D1). Tamoun has none to give.
- **Arc:** the hand that kills Pjol and Senetom, shot at the union. In the coda Tamoun sets her an unnamed place.

## Amma Senetom, 61, Steward-Mother of the Koinonia at Kellia

- **Want:** to find the shoot and hold her as collateral for the peace and the bond book.
- **Secret:** heard Talou's last confession (W.Y. 45). Runs Tsia and illegal sends.
- **Death (D24):** warned by her own mote, she avoids the dock and dies at Tamoun's door. Her loop teaches the player that foresight shows a moment, not a fate.

## Strategos Apollo, 58, commander of the human sickles

- **Want:** to be armed when the Bnne strike.
- **False belief:** that the term lapses and the Bnne will strike.
- **Arc:** the visible hawk the player suspects, who turns out to be honest and holds the sickles when shown the evidence (D40, 22:45). Never a traitor.

## Tsia, 33, Daybook clerk and Koinonia plant

- **Arc:** a double agent the player can catch in Pamin's thread (D15). She survives, and in the coda heads the Koinonia: the leak becomes the bank.

## Paopi (Bnne envoy) and the Oikonomos

- **Paopi:** grafts the increasingly insistent question "Is the shoot ready?" at the union. It is the Bnne court's face. It cannot tell Tamoun anything.
- **The Oikonomos:** never a character in the human sense. Its few spoken lines are statements of balance ("That send does not arrive.").

## The dead who shape the living

- **Amma Talou** (died W.Y. 45): the founding interpreter, the forger and the planner. Her sayings are collected by the school as *The Sayings of Amma Talou*, a biased in-world text: "The lie is free; the truth costs." "A mended cup that hides its crack breaks there again."
- **Ouro Tsenoute** (died W.Y. 20): the signer, who ordered the forgery to protect her son's line.

## Oppositions and pairings

- **Tamoun and Pamin:** the translator and the editor. One must add nothing; the other must take things out.
- **Tamoun and Maro:** two clones. One was raised to be someone; one was raised to be someone else's hands.
- **Tamoun and Ethen:** two signer-shoots, neighbours, each placed, each choosing.
- **Kyra and Apollo:** two hawks. One for gain; one from fear.
- **Decoys:** the king (for the murder), Apollo (for the hawk plot), Phib (C3, C7) and Ethen (C4, "a watcher").
