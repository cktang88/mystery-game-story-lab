# Writer C: sealed hidden history (solution document)

This is the locked canon: what actually happened, in causal order. Every clue in the game is a view onto it. Its keeper is the lead writer, and any change requires re-auditing the clue matrix in `05-outline.md`.

## 0. Calendar and dating rule

- Years are counted **W.Y.** (Wall Year) from the signing of the Treaty of the Wall at dusk on **1 Thout, W.Y. 0**, the feast of Nairouz.
- The realm uses a Coptic-derived calendar of twelve 30-day months (Thout, Paopi, Hathor, Koiak, Tobi, Meshir, Paremhat, Parmouti, Pashons, Paoni, Epip, Mesori) followed by the "little month" Nesi. Nesi has 5 days, or 6 in a long year (years where W.Y. mod 4 = 3). **W.Y. 49 is not a long year, so its Nesi has 5 days.**
- The story runs for 40 days, from **26 Epip W.Y. 49 (Day 0)** to **1 Thout W.Y. 50 (Day 40)**. Check: Epip 27 to 30 is 4 days, Mesori is 30, Nesi is 5 and Thout 1 is 1, for a total of 40.
- Day numbers: 1 Mesori = D5, so *n* Mesori = D(4+*n*); 30 Mesori = D34; *n* Nesi = D(34+*n*); 1 Thout = D40.
- The coda is on **1 Thout W.Y. 51**, one year later.

## 1. The rules the history obeys (summarised; full rules in `03-world-and-factions.md`)

1. **Bnne graft-speech.** The Bnne speak by grafting living slips onto each other or onto "speaking wood". An utterance keeps growing, and its meaning matures. A graft only takes on living, compatible tissue. A **clean cut** with a blade is safe for both parts. A **tear** kills the tissue on both sides, and if a human interpreter's arm is grafted in at the time, the tear causes graft shock, which stops the heart within minutes.
2. **Bnne bodies.** To a Bnne, a body is all the tissue grown from one root. A cutting of a body *is* that body. A sexual offspring is a stranger, a "seedling".
3. **Dust.** Surveillance dust fills the human side of Kellia and records everything to the Oikonomos. **Bare Bnne tissue eats dust within about 2 metres**, so unlacquered living wood is blind. The human face of the Wall is lacquered for exactly that reason: lacquer makes the Wall visible to the dust.
4. **Backward motes.** Spending stored computation can push dust motes back in time. A mote carries at most nine seconds of record from a sphere about 4 metres across, with sound. The cost rises with the square of the depth. **The timeline is fixed:** whatever arrived was sent. The Oikonomos will only spend the reserve on a send that arrives, which means it will transmit only the exact records that are already in the past.
5. **Who may spend the reserve.** Under the treaty charter, only "the signers' bodies" acting together may spend the reserve or send backward. For the human side that means anyone carrying the signing queen's genome. For the Bnne side it means tissue of the Bnne signer's root.
6. **The Law of the Body (human law).** A body and all its copies are one legal person with one name. A woken copy (a "second") may sign for its original, and its acts are its original's acts. The crown passes to "the Ouro's body".

## 2. The founding crime (W.Y. -1 to 0)

- The Shiet war (W.Y. -19 to 0) is a stalemate between the human Nomes and the Bnne Callus. Both sides' orbital "sickles" are fed with time-sent dust intelligence, so every strike is foreseen and every battle is a slaughter without a result.
- The Bnne offer a **union**: a treaty in their own form, a living tree grafted from both signers. The Bnne signer, an elder whom the humans name **Thout**, roots itself in the ring at the line of contact. Its growth becomes **the Wall**, a living partition the whole length of Kellia. The **graft union** is at the base of the trunk in the treaty hall.
- The human signer, **Ouro Tsenoute** (aged 52), gives a cutting of her forearm tissue, which is grafted into the trunk.
- The interpreter is **Amma Talou** (aged 30). She reads the clause the Bnne grafted beside the union. The true text reads, in her literal rendering:

  > *"When the union first fruits, the giver's root sends a new shoot to join it. Then the two stocks are one and need no keeper."*

- Talou explains to the queen that "the giver's root" means Tsenoute's genome, that any clone of her is that root, and that by the Law of the Body any such clone is the Ouro. The fruiting of a union of this species comes at about fifty human years. Tsenoute sees two dangers. Her son's line will be outranked by her own clones at the fruiting, and a treaty that depends on a royal clone gives every faction a reason to kill or capture one. She orders the clause rewritten.
- **The crime.** On the night before the signing, Talou **prunes the true clause** with a clean cut, grafts in a slip she has grown herself from a purchased Bnne cutting, and has the human face of the union lacquered "for preservation". The human treaty text, inscribed on porcelain sherds in the realm's archive, reads:

  > *"This peace holds fifty years from Nairouz, and is renewed at the Wall by the Ouro's hand."*

- Talou cannot bring herself to kill a living sentence, so she keeps the pruned slip alive in a grafting jar.
- The Bnne never read human script. They believe the humans understood the union, and by their custom of consent ("the stock may not graft the shoot toward itself") they will never tell a shoot what it is.
- The substituted slip is sterile. It will never fruit, so a shoot grafted at it cannot join. Only the scar where the true clause was cut can take a shoot.

## 5. The plume and the "Shadow Spend"

- The Koinonia monitor the hall's dust through their plant in the Daybook, **Tsia**. On D1 they detect seven motes whose isotopic clocks run 40 days ahead.
- Their physicists calculate the depth (40 days) and the mass. The cost of such a send is about **one third of the entire reserve**.
- Since only the signers' bodies may spend the reserve, a signer's body must exist and must spend a third of it on renewal night. The rumour of the **Shadow Spend** reaches the markets on D2. Holok futures fall by a sixth in four days.
- Kyra reads it the other way: the renewal will happen unless the shoot is stopped. A third of the reserve spent also falls short of the total wipe she needs. Her plan becomes **to let the body live if necessary but make the graft fail**, by tearing the shoot from the union. A failed union means the term lapses, the sickles rearm, the money burns and the debt is erased.

## 9. Endings

**Canonical ending (E1, "The Ouro's Hand").** Before she lost consciousness, Tamoun said "Cut there", and Pamin takes it as her instruction to him too. He replaces the live hall with the **D0 rehearsal** of the king pressing his hand to the union, keeps Maro's attack, and reports: *"Chief Interpreter Tamoun Besa died defending the union from the Treasurer's second. The Ouro's hand renewed the peace."* Under the Law of the Body, Maro's guilt is Kyra's. Kyra is convicted on the Mantle log and the gait audit and sentenced to life aboard a stripped sickle hulk. Pshoi III reigns knowing the throne is held on Tamoun's silence. Phib becomes Chief Interpreter. Tsia becomes Steward-Mother of the Koinonia.

**Economic aftermath.** Spending a third of the reserve cuts the holok by about a quarter, which eases the crown's debt (Kyra's aim, half achieved by her enemies). The permanent peace lets both sides strip their fourteen remaining sickles within the year. Fourteen new vaults rebuild the reserve, and the holok recovers past its old value by the coda. The Wall Bonds are rolled into **Union Bonds**.

**Alternative (E3, "The Open Crack").** If the player, as Pamin, does nothing or broadcasts the raw feed, the realm sees its king kneel to a woman with one arm. By the Law of the Body she is Ouro. The treaty still holds, but three nome governors declare for Pshoi and the realm enters a succession crisis. Its coda is set in the palace at Shmin, far from the wall.

**Coda (1 Thout W.Y. 51).** Tamoun, one-armed, lives in cell 31 under the name **Nesi** (the little month, which belongs to no month), a tenant on Pamin's paperwork. The crack has callused shut. Ethen still lives behind the wall, out of a habit it cannot explain. On Nairouz she sets places at the Wall for Pjol, Talou, Senetom, the eleven sleepers, Maro (unnamed, a second's place), and "Tamoun Besa". She mends Talou's cup with lacquer, leaving the crack visible. She may knock. If she knocks, after a long silence the callus splits and a green shoot grows through and grafts its first word onto her wrist: **"near"**, the first word Ethen ever said to her, in W.Y. 37.

## 11. Knowledge consistency checks

- **Why the Bnne never told her:** the consent rule ("the stock may not graft the shoot toward itself"). It is taught to the player on D27, and it binds only the Bnne. Talou, a human, could tell her, and did so through the cup.
- **Why Pjol did not tell Tamoun:** Talou's instruction that the knowledge would make her a target before the fruiting. His note ("Read it when I'm not there to stop you") shows his hesitation.
- **Why the dust never saw the vine:** the crack is bare wood, so it is a blind spot.
- **Why Pamin cannot censor the market:** the Oikonomos's charge records and the Koinonia's physics are outside the Daybook.
- **Why Kyra cannot simply kill Tamoun early:** before D19 she has no proof. After D19 the Shadow Spend tells her the body will be alive at 23:41 on D40, and she believes the Oikonomos never books an impossible send, so she shifts to making the graft fail. Her D24 operation targets Tamoun's arrest, not her death. Maro kills Senetom because Senetom recognised her at the scrape.
- **Ages on D0:** Tamoun 34, Pjol 70, Pamin 52, Kyra 63, Maro 29, Pshoi III 41, Phib 27, Apollo 58, Senetom 61, Tsia 33. Talou would be 79 and Tsenoute 101.
- **Travel:** Kellia to Shmin is 6 days by ship (D10 to D16; D19 to D25). News packets take 1 day (the Shmin fire reaches Kellia on D20).
