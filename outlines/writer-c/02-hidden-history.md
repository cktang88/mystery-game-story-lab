# Writer C: sealed hidden history (solution document), revision 2

This is the locked canon: what actually happened, in causal order. Every clue in the game is a view onto it. Its keeper is the lead writer, and any change requires re-auditing the clue matrix in `05-outline.md`. The changes from the first draft are logged in `08-revision-log.md`.

## 0. Calendar and dating

- Years are counted **W.Y.** (Wall Year) from the signing of the Treaty of the Wall at dusk on **1 Thout, W.Y. 0**, the feast of Nairouz.
- The realm uses a Coptic-derived calendar of twelve 30-day months (Thout, Paopi, Hathor, Koiak, Tobi, Meshir, Paremhat, Parmouti, Pashons, Paoni, Epip, Mesori) followed by the little month Nesi. Nesi has 5 days, or 6 in a long year (W.Y. mod 4 = 3). **W.Y. 49 is not a long year, so its Nesi has 5 days.**
- The main story runs for 40 days, from **26 Epip W.Y. 49 (D0)** to **1 Thout W.Y. 50 (D40)**. Check: Epip 27 to 30 is 4 days, Mesori is 30, Nesi is 5 and Thout 1 is 1, for a total of 40.
- Day numbers: *n* Mesori = D(4+*n*); 30 Mesori = D34; *n* Nesi = D(34+*n*); 1 Thout = D40.
- After the climax: the **Hearing of the Body** on 21 Thout W.Y. 50 (D60), and the **coda** on 1 Thout W.Y. 51.

## 1. The rules (stated here once; 03 repeats them with costs)

**R1. Graft-speech.** The Bnne speak by grafting living slips onto each other or onto speaking wood. An utterance keeps growing, so its meaning matures.
- A **clean cut** with a blade harms neither part.
- A **tear** kills tissue on both sides. If a human interpreter's arm is grafted in at the time, the tear causes graft shock, which stops the heart within minutes.

**R2. Callus, feeling and memory.**
- A newly grafted slip is not yet part of the one who receives it. It **calluses** in about three days.
- Before the callus forms, a slip can be cut away unfelt.
- After the callus forms, the slip carries feeling and memory both ways. A Bnne that cuts away a callused graft **loses the memories that graft carried**, and they go wherever the graft is regrafted.
- The game first shows this in Ch 1: Paopi cuts away an old greeting slip and then cannot recall Pjol's name until it touches the slip again.

**R3. Bnne identity.**
- To a Bnne, a body is everything grown from one **root** (one genome). A cutting of a root *is* that root. A sexual offspring is a stranger.
- **The consent rule:** "the tree may not graft the shoot toward itself." A Bnne may never tell a shoot that it is one, or push it to the union.

**R4. Dust.**
- Surveillance dust records by mesh: each mote rebuilds the space shared with its neighbours, about 4 m across, with sound.
- Bare living Bnne tissue **eats motes**, leaving a void in the record. The void's radius scales with the exposed area:
  - a hairline crack: a few centimetres;
  - a palm-sized patch: about 30 cm;
  - a hand-span: about 40 cm;
  - a residents' "scrape": about 1 m;
  - the whole Bnne face of the union, or a Bnne "bloom": about 2 m.
- The human face of the Wall and of the union is lacquered so that dust survives there. **Lacquer is refined Wall sap.** The only bare place on the union's human face is the palm-sized **reading port**.
- The Bnne half of Kellia has no dust at all.

**R5. The Mantle.** The royal Mantle repels dust, so its wearer is recorded as a moving void, a "gap". By custom the Ouro wears it **only in private and at night**. Public acts are done unmantled ("the Ouro is seen when he acts for the realm"). The Mantle is kept in the Treasury wardrobe.

**R6. Backward motes.**
- Spending stored computation pushes dust motes back in time. Each carries at most nine seconds of record.
- The cost rises with the square of the depth: one hour costs about 0.0006 percent of the reserve; one day about 0.35 percent; forty days about **33 percent**.
- **The timeline is fixed: what arrived was sent.** The Oikonomos will spend reserve only on a send that will arrive, so it transmits only records that match what has already arrived.

**R7. The reserve and who may spend it.**
- The reserve is the computation stored in the 40 vaults of the Vault-Ring.
- Under the charter (Talou's translation), only **"the signers' roots" acting together** may spend reserve computation or send backward. The human signer's root is anyone carrying Ouro Tsenoute's genome. The Bnne signer's root is any tissue grown from Thout.
- The only other backward-capable computation in Shiet is in **three sickle-cores pawned to the Djeme Houses**. Together they hold about 2 percent of the reserve's capacity, enough for sends of a few days at most.
- **Therefore a forty-day plume could only have been paid for from the reserve, so both signers' roots must exist and must spend it.** This is the inference the market makes on D2.

**R8. The Law of the Body (human law).**
- A body and all its copies are one legal person with one name. A woken copy (a "second") may sign for its original, and its acts and crimes are its original's.
- The crown passes to "the Ouro's body".
- Sleeping clones ("sleepers"; the legal term is rootstock) of a dead Ouro are relics, and waking one is treason.

**R9. Sleepers and interpreters.**
- An interpreter's forearm carries Bnne tissue adapted over years, so it can join a union and live.
- An unadapted human limb **can join a union** (the root is the root), but it brings on a rejection fever. A limb left joined for an hour costs the owner the limb; a limb left joined longer kills.

## 2. The founding crime (W.Y. -1 to 0)

- **The war.** The Shiet war (W.Y. -19 to 0) is a stalemate. Both sides' sickles are steered by short backward sends, so every strike is foreseen and every battle is a slaughter without a result.
- **The union.** The Bnne offer a union: a treaty in their own form, a living tree grafted from both signers' roots. Their signer, an elder humans name **Thout**, takes root at the line of contact; over the following months its growth becomes **the Wall**. The human signer, **Ouro Tsenoute** (aged 52), gives a cutting of forearm tissue, which is grafted into the trunk.
- **The two-way obligation.** The Bnne graft their clause beside the union three days before the signing. In Talou's literal rendering it reads:

  > *"When the union first fruits, each giver's root sends a new shoot to join it. Then the two trees are one and need no keeper."*

  Both sides owe a shoot. The Bnne will send growth of Thout; the humans must send growth of Tsenoute. Fruiting comes at about fifty human years.
- **Why the queen wants it changed.** Talou tells Tsenoute that by the Law of the Body any clone of hers is the Ouro, so at the fruiting her own copy would outrank her son. A treaty that needs a royal copy also gives every faction a reason to make, steal or kill one. Tsenoute orders a wording that will keep the throne her son's.
- **The forgery (an interpreter's trap, not a crude substitution).** Two nights before the signing, the clause has not yet callused, so it can be cut away unfelt (R2). Talou **prunes it with a clean cut** and grafts in a slip she has grown herself from a purchased cutting of a different Bnne root.
  - To a Bnne, her slip reads: *"When the union first fruits, the giver's hand renews it."* In graft-speech a "hand" is a branch, which is a shoot, so Thout reads it at the signing as the same obligation and does not object. The two-way wording and "need no keeper" are gone, but the Bnne lose nothing they care about.
  - To humans, Talou renders the same slip as: *"This peace holds fifty years from Nairouz, and is renewed at the Wall by the Ouro's hand."* That is inscribed on the porcelain treaty sherds.
  - Because her slip is foreign tissue, it can never fully callus. A shoot joined at it would take only as a renewable, keeper-bound union: one that needs a new shoot of the Ouro's root at **every** fruiting, for ever. Only the cut scar of the true clause, beside the reading port, can take a shoot that makes the trees one.
  - The human face, scar included, is lacquered "for preservation".
- **The kept slip.** Talou cannot bring herself to kill a living sentence. She keeps the pruned true clause alive in a grafting jar.

## 5. The plume and the Shadow Spend

- **D1.** The Djeme Houses watch the hall dust through their plant in the Daybook, **Tsia**. They find seven motes with forward isotopic clocks.
- **The calculation.** Forty days times seven motes costs about 33 percent of the reserve. The pawned cores hold only 2 percent, so the send must come from the reserve, which means **both signers' roots exist and will spend a third of the reserve on renewal night** (R7).
- **D2.** The rumour, called **the Shadow Spend**, reaches the markets. By D6 holok futures have fallen by a sixth, because markets price the spend at roughly even odds against an outright lapse.
- **Jump fares.** The Oikonomos stops pre-computing routes that arrive after 1 Thout (a vault might be wiped first), so the fares for such routes **triple**, a war-risk premium. Routes that arrive before the renewal are unchanged.
- **Kyra's reading.** The renewal will happen unless the shoot is stopped. She moves from killing the slip-bearer to **making the graft fail**: a torn shoot, or no shoot at all.
