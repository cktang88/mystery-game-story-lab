# Writer C: key scene sketches

Each sketch shows what the player **does** and **sees**, with almost no reading. A line marked "Says:" is voiced. "Planted / pays off" lists what the scene seeds or cashes.

---

## 1. Opening: Tea Through the Wall (Ch 1, D0, morning)

**Sees.** A narrow cell, warm lamplight, a lacquered back wall gleaming black. Tamoun's left arm is green-veined to the elbow. A porcelain cup with a crack; she pours, and steam rises. A hairline crack in the black wall. A pale vine noses out of it, slow as a clock hand.

**Does.** The vine touches her wrist and leaves a slip. The player turns her forearm (the graft) toward it, and the screen dims to a touch-view: growth lines, a bud and a tilt. A small grammar wheel offers shapes, and the player matches the shape to "rain". She answers by snipping a slip from a pot on the sill and pressing it to the vine at an angle (the player chooses the angle: friendly or formal). The vine curls away satisfied. Before it withdraws, it leans toward the cup. The player can zoom in: a green hair-line in the porcelain crack. Nothing happens. (**Planted:** the cup.)

**Then.** A boom of docking clamps through the floor: the court fleet arrives. Through the cell window, a long white hull with 42 banners. Tamoun knocks twice on the wall, as she does every day. The vine twitches once in answer. (**Planted:** the knock.)

**Hall.** Tamoun stands at the lacquered union, a waist-high swelling of black-lacquered trunk with a band of bare grey bark at the root. The Bnne envoy Paopi, tall and barky and utterly silent, presses a slip to the bare wood. The player reads it: a greeting, plus a second shape the grammar wheel can't place ("Is the shoot…?"). Tamoun renders the greeting aloud. A court reader recites from a porcelain sherd. **Says:** "…and is renewed at the Wall by the Ouro's hand." The king lays his palm on the lacquer for the rehearsal; the gesture is slow and tall. (**Planted:** the rehearsal footage, the king's height and stride.) Kyra pays for the tea with a click of counting beads, her cane tapping. Behind her stands Kyra again, thirty-four years younger, gloved. **Maro says:** "What do the Bnne call a second?" Tamoun has no answer.

**Phib's lesson.** Tamoun takes Phib's hand to the bare root-collar. **Says:** "Never pull an interpreter off the wood. Cut. Clean. Here." The player performs the clean cut on a practice slip; a ragged pull on a second slip makes the slip blacken and die. (**Planted:** cut, not pull.)

## 2. Pamin's First Cut (Ch 3, D1, 02:00, playable as Pamin)

**Sees.** A dim room of a hundred tiny dust-screens and a man in half-moon lenses. One screen: the treaty hall at 23:00, a frozen 3D cloud of light.

**Does.** The player scrubs. Pjol shuffles in and kneels at the root-collar, and the dust around him goes dark (a bare-wood blind bubble). At 23:16 a **hole** in the dust walks in, a person-shaped absence. Pamin's lenses catch it. **Says (to himself):** "The Mantle. Majesty, what were you doing?" At 23:41 seven sparks flare in the cloud and drift. The player opens the edit tray: three captions as icons (a bed, a tree, a blank), and a mood gauge for the realm.

**The choice.** Choosing "bed" ("died at home") settles the gauge. Choosing "tree" spikes it, and an hour later a chamberlain arrives and the caption is changed back anyway, with the king's trust dial down one notch.

**Planted / pays off.** The gap (paid off in scene 4). The edit verb (paid off in scene 9). The player now suspects the king, honestly, through Pamin.

## 3. The Truth Costs a Holok a Second (Ch 4, D2)

**Does.** At her new slate, Tamoun sets three dials: place (hall), time (D0, 23:00 to 23:45), radius. A counter in the corner starts ticking holoks as the scene loads. The player walks the frozen hall: the body arriving, the bubble, the hole entering from the left. The player can stand where the hole is. It leaves by the **Treasury corridor**: the player follows its exit with a breadcrumb of displaced dust. At 23:41:07 seven lights bloom in her face like sparks off a grinder. The audit ends; the counter stops. Booked to "account B".

**Feeling.** Truth is a meter running. Every second you look costs money, so the player learns to choose seconds.

## 4. The Gap That Walks Wrong (Ch 10, D14 to D17)

**Does.** Tamoun audits two scenes side by side: the D0 rehearsal (the king climbing the dais steps) and the hole at 23:16 climbing the same steps. The player lays a **height line** at the top of each, against the banner rail behind. The hole is lower. The player taps footfalls in the displaced dust to set a stride: the king's is long and deliberate; the hole's is short and quick, with no pause on the third step, where the king always pauses. The player then pulls old dust of court figures and drags each silhouette over the hole. Only one fits the height: Kyra. But on the overlay, Kyra's cane-and-hip rhythm does not match the hole's light, even step. The player can drag one more silhouette from the welcome scene: the gloved woman behind Kyra. It fits.

**What the game confirms:** "Not the king." A second line stays grey ("Kyra's body", with two candidates) until the Mantle log (Ch 18).

## 5. MIDPOINT: Eleven Faces / The Cup (Ch 11, D19, intercut)

**Phib, at Shmin.** A cold vault under the palace. Twelve tall glass vats in a ring, one dry, its plaque reading "W.Y. 15, failure". Eleven sleepers float, aged 34, hair moving slowly, eyes closed. The player walks Phib around the ring. The camera lets him stop at each and look. The same face eleven times, and it is Tamoun's: the player knows it, Phib knows it, and his breath fogs the glass. The warden hands over a lacquered order under the Treasurer's seal and a lit taper. **Choice:** the player can hold the taper to the vent, or set it down and walk out. If he walks out, the player hears the vents roar behind him and sees the light change on the corridor wall. No cutaway.

**Cut to Tamoun, Kellia, the same hour.** She is making tea. The vine is straining so hard at the crack that its bark splits. She follows its reach to the cup. In the crack, the green line has grown a tiny knot that is the **same callus shape** the player saw in the union's scar (Ch 9). The player lays her fingertip on it, and touch-view opens inside a crack in porcelain. The grammar wheel turns slowly, and three shapes resolve: *her / shoot / choose*. A short voice line follows in Talou's old recorded voice (her sayings recordings, heard earlier in the school), which the game plays as the shapes lock: "Choose it."

**Then.** Tamoun sets the cup down very carefully. She knocks twice on the wall. The vine answers once. Fade on the cup.

**Planted / pays off.** Pays off the cup (Ch 1), the scar shape (Ch 9) and the crypt's dry vat (Ch 7). It sets up the hunt (Act 2b) and Ethen's knowledge (Ch 15).

## 6. The Warning (Ch 13, D23 to D24, watched as Pamin)

**Does.** Through Tsia's leaked feed, Pamin watches Senetom watch her own mote: nine seconds of herself at the freight dock, and over her shoulder a gloved young woman. Senetom changes route. The player, as Pamin, can follow her in dust. She passes the dock safely. At 18:00 the board in the corner dips: she has closed her loop. Then she turns into the Wall Quarter corridor, and the dust **goes dark** at a scrape outside cell 31. Twelve seconds of nothing. Light returns: Senetom is down, and Tamoun is running in from the far end. A Treasury notice pops into Pamin's tray: "Audit purchased and published: 9 s." On every screen in the room, Tamoun kneels over a body with red hands.

**Feeling.** Foresight did not lie; it was about a different moment. Truth is something you can buy in parts.

## 7. Through the Wall (Ch 14, D26)

**Sees.** Fists on the door. Tamoun's cell. The crack in the back wall, now a handspan wide and oozing sap, the lacquer around it buckled.

**Does.** The player pulls the lacquer away in sheets (it peels like bark). The wood beneath opens like lips. The dust overlay on the HUD crackles and **switches off** as she pushes her shoulder into bare wood. The guards burst in. Their dust-sight shows an empty room with a black hole in the wall. Tamoun crawls through wet heartwood in darkness (audio: her breath, the creak, the sap-tick) into warm, dim green. A shape, tall and slow, touches her face. Ethen. Not a word. Just a slip on her wrist: "near".

---

## 8. THE CLIMAX: The Union (Ch 20, D40, 22:52 to 23:30)

**Sees.** The hall from the Bnne side, dark and green, the trunk's bare face. Then light: Tamoun steps out of the union into the human hall, before 42 banners and a thousand faces, and the dust renders her into existence mid-step. The crowd turns.

**Does, in order. Each beat is a clip the player has already watched, now lived.**
- Phib reaches her, gasping, and pushes the bento into her hands (**C3** was his run).
- She draws the pruning knife and cuts the lacquer from the scar (**C1**). The player feels the lacquer flake under the knife; the HUD's dust thins and fails at the edges.
- The union swells and splits into buds. The king steps down and **kneels** (**C2**): the crowd cries out, exactly as in the clip, but now the player sees why.
- The final graft-writing puzzle, done with one hand under a timer: set the true slip at the scar so the rings meet. The grammar wheel snaps: *join*. Then she sinks her green arm into the wood to the elbow, then the shoulder. In touch-view the player feels the Bnne side push back: a vine meeting her (Ethen).
- A gloved hand closes on her shoulder: Maro. The arm begins to **pull**, the touch-view screams red, the screen edges blacken (the "tear" cue from Ch 1).
- Tamoun turns her head to Phib. **Says:** "Cut there." (**C6**.)
- The player, as Phib for three seconds, makes the clean cut, the one taught in Ch 1. A gunshot behind. Silence.
- The arm hangs from the trunk, its hand sunk in the wood, blood running down the lacquer (**C7**). The camera holds at the clip's exact angle.
- Tamoun sitting against the dais, alive, her shoulder already greening over. The buds open. The union takes. On the Bnne side Ethen goes still, then turns away like a stranger.

## 9. The Cut (Ch 21, D40, 23:41)

**Does.** In the annex, Tamoun presses her bleeding stump to the vault plate. Ethen, puzzled and polite, sets its graft-seal beside it because a hand asked. The player, now Pamin, faces the night's dust and the seven empty clip slots from the board. He scrubs and finds each moment, sets angle and window, and locks it. A wrong frame gets the soft balance-click: "That send does not arrive." Last, the player may try to cut a frame from D0 showing Pjol, to send *him* a warning. Rejected. The seven lock. The reserve board drops by a third.

Then the broadcast tray: a ten-minute delay bar draining. The live hall shows the king on his knees before a one-armed woman. Two buttons: the rehearsal clip (the king's hand on the union) and nothing. In Pamin's earpiece, a replay of "Cut there."

---

## 10. CODA: The Set Places (1 Thout W.Y. 51)

**Sees.** The Wall Quarter on the day of the dead, lamps along the black wall, bowls and cups set before it, families murmuring. Cell 31. A one-armed woman (the player knows her; the world calls her Nesi).

**Does.** The player sets the places one by one, each an object on the low table: a rice ball for Pjol in his lacquered bento; a sherd with a saying for Talou; wool for Senetom; eleven pebbles; one bowl with no name (the second's); and, last, a small card the player sees her write slowly: "Tamoun Besa". She mends the cup one-handed with lacquer, deliberately leaving the crack showing as a gold-black seam. She sets it at the wall where the crack used to be. It is callused over now, a smooth scar in the lacquer.

**The choice.** Knock, or don't.
- **Knock.** Two knocks. Silence, held long enough that the player thinks nothing will come. Then a tick, and the callus splits. A pale shoot noses out and finds her wrist. The slip, in touch-view: **"near"**. The first word, from the first day.
- **Don't.** The lamps burn down. The cup sits by the scar. Fade.
