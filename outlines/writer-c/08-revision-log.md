The review is `judging/review-writer-c.md` (7.1/10). Fixes were made at the source: `02-hidden-history.md` first, then `03` to `06`, then `01` and `07`. "Files" lists every file changed for each item.

## Required revisions

**1. Cutting protocol and receipt audit.** *Files:* 02, 03, 05, 06, 07.
- **What changed.** A locked protocol table in `02` says which events are cut on which receipt. Life receipts carry births, debts, marriages, children, verdicts and sentences (never deaths). Court receipts carry *only corrections to the court record*. Debt receipts carry payments and settlement. Post receipts carry routings. The old stayer's receipt carried the "stayed" mark. The D7 court receipt now shows only the D31 correction.
- **Sentences.** Jūzaburō's "was left" is cut on his life receipt, publicly, at the leaving. I took the grader's suggested beat: Dōjima read that mark for sixty-one years and sent him to kill anyway ("fate must be helped"), and the audit exposes this.
- **Plate supply (problem g).** One plate yields about 300 receipt pairs, about 18,000 a year, rationed; since YM 190 the plates thin, so births get the shortest slivers.
- **Deaths (problem n).** Deaths are not cut. The body half is burned with the body, which ends the pair, and funerals wait until no shadows remain. So Dōjima never foresaw Seydou's death, and the receipts never "foretold" anyone dying on D60; that point is now a deduction on D32 (R10b).
- **New rule.** A pair lives only while both halves exist.
- **Audit.** Consistency check 1 in `02` re-audits every receipt in the plot: court, life, post, pact, debt, newborns', day-count.
- **Why this direction.** The grader's suggestion was the cleanest fix, and it adds a strong beat.

**2. Ledger recall of refused gifts.** *Files:* 02, 03, 05, 06, 01.
- **What changed.** Market custom: refused gifts are shamefully left at the creature's feet, and its plates take them in. Ledger recall returns the last thing a person *left* with it. Uke left her refused tether on D-5. The custom is taught on D25 (a merchant walks off without his refused gift).
- **Why.** This option makes recall a rule rather than an exception, and it gives the market a small, visible custom.

**3. Branch B rebuilt.** *Files:* 02, 04, 05, 07.
- **What changed.** If Sayo refuses to stay, the hard-liners' halt holds for four hours and the scour reaches the horns: **Ume dies**. Sayo is tethered, and her "was left" is cut by the hard-liners' clerk (passive, literally true). **Gonbei** chooses to stay, as the grader suggested, and cuts the hobble-mark (active and true). The creature turns Sayo's tether into a hobble and gives Gonbei the old receipt.
- **Branch B2.** If Tōkichi denounced Gonbei on D55, Gonbei is in custody, and **Kaeshi** stays instead.
- **Locked rule.** The "same cutter" line is replaced: three marks can change cutter (the Gully mark, Sayo's own mark and the hobble-mark); every other foreseen mark keeps its cutter. The receipts record only true acts in every branch.
- **Why.** Refusal now changes who pays and what it costs, and the D55 choice feeds the D60 branch.
