## 7. Known risks I could not resolve on paper

1. **The graft and the send are not refusable.** This is deliberate (the fixed timeline), but it is a strain against "refusal is respected". Mitigation: the broadcast and the knock carry the agency, and the game is honest from Ch 5 that what arrived was sent.
2. **Thout rooted in the Wall** might read near the banned bound-alien shape. It is a voluntary Bnne life stage, and nothing is owed to it.
3. **Graft-grammar depth.** The touch-view wheel must have a large enough answer space to avoid guessing. It needs a prototype.
4. **Three playable viewpoints** cost production. Trap doors in 04 allow cutting Phib to non-playable.
