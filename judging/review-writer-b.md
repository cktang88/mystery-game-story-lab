# Review: round 3, writer B ("The Light Weight")

Grader: independent. I read all seven files in `outlines/writer-b/` and judged them against `BRIEF.md` (with particular weight on "Scope and genre"), `outlines/ROUND-3.md` and `research/REPORT.md`. I did not open the other writers' folders, the archive or the rest of `judging/`.

## Verdict in brief

This is a well-made, tightly plotted political murder mystery with an excellent human anchor, the best use of seeds I would expect to see, and one genuinely fresh time-travel rule: a message that is acted on can never be verified. Its problems are of three kinds.

1. **Scope.** It is a one-elevator story with a region, not a galaxy, behind it.
2. **Logic.** Several load-bearing mechanisms break under audit: the AI that has held the answer to the founding crime for 63 years for anyone who asks, an economy whose arithmetic does not work, and a climax whose decisive object turns out to be redundant.
3. **A disguised near-breach.** The banned "founding debt to an alien, settled in person at the end" shape is present.

None of these is fatal. All of them have to be fixed before this could win.

## Top strengths

1. **Seeds that generate rather than decorate.** All thirteen visibly shape the plot. The sash is the murder weapon, the ledger, the gift and the farn conduit. The scale weight is the climax. The tea taster is the main verb. Static is both forensics and alien speech. The guard-and-guarded relationship is the emotional spine. The naming ritual is the game's licensing, punishment and ending. This is the model for how seeds should work.
2. **The human anchor and the guard.** Miwnay is guarded by the man who unknowingly killed the witness she is blamed for. His habits are Farnxund's habits. The confession is planted through door-touching, beeswax and the nickname "Seven". The door he opens for Farnxund at the end is a memorable, earned moment carried by objects rather than speech.
3. **The orphan rule.** "Act on the future and you lose the proof; wait for the proof and you lose the chance." This gives a tribunal of prevented crimes, an unconfirmable ledger entry that the economy rests on, and a crime whose hiding place is a law of physics. The Day 4 plate ("THE TASTER PROVED IT LATE") is a superb unmade-future hook with a sender, a cost and a clock.
4. **Fair, multi-channel clueing.** The clue matrix, the honest red herring (the Dust glyph and the Dust bricks), batched case-board confirmation, and each twist reclassifying earlier scenes (the guard, the sister) follow the research report closely.
5. **Respectful, specific use of the Sogdian culture seed.** Farn as fortune turned into income, the sartapao as caravan-master turned into paymaster, ossuaries and the silent tower, the Afrasiab-style murals, theophoric -vandak and -dhat naming, chakars, and tea-brick money. It is used as the texture of a future culture, not as costume, and the Turkic Ordu are the wronged party rather than the stereotyped raider. (A small note under revision 14.)

## Options in 01-options.md: strawmen and where I would pick differently

**Rounds with genuine rivals:** Round 2 (founding crime), Round 3 (central question), Round 8 (intrigue structure) and Round 11 (key scenes).

**Rounds whose rivals are strawmen or variations:**

- **Round 4 (hidden history).** H3 (a mistranslation) is set up to lose. H2 (a genuine lie sent back by the beneficiaries) is dismissed too quickly.
- **Round 5 (ending).** E3 is the reset button, a strawman.
- **Round 6, time.** T3 is "not time travel" by the writer's own words.
- **Round 6, AGI.** A2 and A3 get one line each.
- **Round 6, cloning.** C2 is the stock disposable clone.
- **Round 6, aliens.** D2 is rejected because it *is* the banned round-2 shape, so it was never a real option.
- **Round 7 (economy).** P2 cheapens the time rule by construction.
- **Round 10 (premise).** P-a, P-b and P-c are three wordings of the same premise. That is exactly the "variations of one idea" the instructions forbid.

**Where I would pick differently:**

- **Round 1: graft S3 onto S1, and promote S2.**
  - S3 (several elevator cities under one paymaster standard) is the scope answer the brief asks for. It was rejected because "nine cities blur", but two or three cities on screen would not blur.
  - S2's idea of humans as a minority client people at an alien court is the freshest court-intrigue idea in the file, and the Counterweight should become that court.
  - Keep Marakand as the hub, but make Chach (already a "texture world") a second elevator city whose income also rests on the verdict, and make the Counterweight a full Draxt court with factions.
- **Round 7: take P3's core into P1.** A UBI that is secretly a loan against licensed clones' future labour ties cloning, the economy and the "pay for what you did not do" question together better than P1's flat dividend. It also fixes the arithmetic problem (revision 2). Keep P1's legibility of the spark at dawn and the Bench board.
- **Round 4: give H2 a second life as a late twist, not as the founding.** For example, the Ordu war party really had a burning write ready in YT 41, so the forgery was a lie about a crime that was really being planned. That would give Act 4 the reclassifying reveal it currently lacks (see revision 9) and would put real pressure on Q3.
- **Round 10:** write three premises that differ in mechanic, not wording. One could be about the orphan rule itself, one about the paymaster AI, and one about pattern-guilt.

## Required revisions

1. **Close the hole that the AI has held the founding answer for 63 years, for anyone who asks, and make the climax's weight necessary.**
   - *The hole.* Under the Charter of Silence the Sartapao "answers any named person's question about the ledger truthfully" (03). So on any day since YT 41, any named Ordu sympathiser, Ustrushana delegate, curious clerk, or Pukar himself could have asked who ordered a city-wide grounding on Unburned night, and been told "Duster Nanai-farn". The Ordu have called the verdict a frame for 63 years and never asked. Nanai-farn, who wrote the Charter, repeats the same logged method on night −13.
   - *Why it breaks the climax.* On Day 31 Miwnay gets that answer. Together with the missing Old Column ring and Makhdhat, that is human-checkable proof, and the same kind of evidence collapsed the Second Weighing on Day 30. The cause chain's claim that "only a human-checkable object will settle it, therefore the weight" is therefore false: the weight is redundant.
   - *Direction.* Gate the ledger.
     - Option (a): the Charter answers only the party a ledger line concerns, or only with the Ikhshid's seal.
     - Option (b): the YT 41 grounding was booked under Tarkhun's seal or the Council's, so the log names the wrong person, and the YT 41 log becomes a misleading clue that the weight corrects.
     - Option (c): every question costs the asker's farn, or is itself logged and visible to the Dust, so asking is dangerous spycraft.
   - Then rewrite night −13 so that an 85-year-old spymaster covers her tracks (a dead proxy's name, a staged fault), and so that Pukar's eyewitness account is what makes the question askable at all.

2. **Make the economy's arithmetic hold.**
   - *The shadow quota cannot explain the fall in farn.* About 40,000 extra licences against about 4.8 million named persons is less than 1% dilution, which cannot be "the main reason" farn fell 30%. Either the quota is around 1.5 to 2 million (an industrial-scale clone bubble, which is more interesting) or the fall has another cause.
   - *The repayment drives farn below zero.* Repaying 63 years of tribute "from tolls over 31 years" costs about twice the annual tribute (about 76% of today's pool) out of tolls that are only 55% of it. That is not "nearly half". Set a repayment term and rate that produce the stated drop, or make the drop larger and stage the consequences.
   - *The "freed" clones are children.* The shadow quota began in YT 96 and clones are decanted as infants, so the 40,000 clones "freed of their vat debts" are all under eight. Releasing their debts means abandoning children. Either have clones decanted as adults (and say so in 03) or change the knock-on effect.
   - *Minor:* reading fees at 7% of a city's income from about 1.3 plates a decade; "a handful a decade" (01, Dune check) against 14 genuine arrivals in 104 years; and the shadow quota dated from YT 94 when Shayn took the Bench in YT 96.

3. **Deliver galactic scope on screen, not in a texture list.**
   - Currently Kesh, Chach, Khwarazm and the Dry are named and never visited, and the other "galactic courts" never appear.
   - *Direction:*
     - Stage the Counterweight as an alien court with at least two Draxt factions (for example, those who want to punish Marakand and those who want to keep the relay) with their own intrigue around the 26 falsified years.
     - Put at least two more polities' envoys at the Scale (clients who buy sends and whose own policies rest on Marakand's relays).
     - Make the verdict's reversal hit other worlds' economies (Chach's copied floor law and farn system).
     - Give Aytash's thread a full act off the elevator: the fleet's own court, where the Yabghu's war party plots against Tardu and Aytash, succession fights inside the Ordu, and Ustrushana's politics.
   - The test: at least a third of play time off the elevator, and three or more polities whose decisions move the plot on screen.

4. **Move the ending away from the founding-debt-to-alien shape.**
   - *The rhyme.* The city lives off an alien-spun cable. Its founding obligation is a pledge to the aliens (the Compact: relay faithfully). That pledge was broken. And it is "settled in person at the end" by the anchor drinking the aliens' cup.
   - No alien is captive, so the letter of the ban is met. But in shape this is the round-2 average with the "bound alien" swapped for a free one, and the self-check's "Pass" understates it.
   - It also misdirects the moral weight: the wronged party is the Ordu, yet the anchor's personal price is paid to the Draxt.
   - *Direction:* make the price owed to, or chosen in relation to, the human victims, for example something Miwnay gives up to the Ordu or to the Root. Or make the Draxt demand a political act (Marakand ceding the reading monopoly, or the Scale's dissolution) rather than a bodily sacrifice. If the cup is kept, re-derive it: the Draxt identify a person by charge signature, so the rewrite changes her signature. Show a Draxt re-spinning, with its consent rule, in Act 1 or 2 (for example, a Draxt at the Counterweight re-spun before the player, seen through Takhsich). Drop the taste-receptor loss, which is engineered for pathos rather than derived from the rule.

5. **Give the time pillar active verbs and a player-owned dilemma.**
   - Every time verb is forensic: reading streaks and rings (which are really static-seed verbs), reading flourishes, comparing Hushes. The player never decides anything about the future.
   - That weakens a pillar the brief says must be "front and centre", and it wastes the orphan rule, the best idea in the file.
   - *Direction:* the Draxt sell sends to any client, so give the player one.
     - For example, Aytash in the fleet (or Miwnay, through Wakhsh) can buy a send and must compose the sixty glyphs, knowing it will unmake days for everyone (including her own progress and perhaps Dewasht's death) and will make its own claim unverifiable.
     - Or the player receives a plate mid-game and must choose to act (losing the proof) or wait (losing the chance).
   - Also fix the contradiction in "Unmaking is what liars do": the Draxt sell sends and consult their own futures routinely, and every send unmakes days for everyone without consent, which collides with their own law that "a write without consent is the worst crime".

6. **Seal the forged-plate rules.**
   - Rule 6 says every genuine message carries the Sartapao's ledger seal of its sending day. The file never says whether the two forgeries carry a forged seal, how a Duster forges one, or why its absence is not an instant tell.
   - Also: why has the 29%-light sacred weight (71 drachms against 100) gone unnoticed for 63 years by clerks and tasters who handle it at every session? Make the difference fine (a few drachms, perceptible only to a master taster's hand) or explain why no one handles it.
   - The empty slot shaped like the Scale's weight in Nanai-farn's personal weight set is a contrived breadcrumb unless she meant it to be found. Say so (see revision 7).

7. **Make Nanai-farn's motives consistent.**
   - 02 (Day 27) says she means "to keep the founding secret". 04 says "she has left the truth in her ossuary for her pattern-kin to carry". She asks Miwnay specifically to carry her bones, knowing the lid holds the YT 78 plate and the weight-slot clue.
   - Decide: she keeps the secret while she lives and releases it at her death. Make that her explicit, quotable choice (it pays off her question about the sixty glyphs).
   - Also explain why she presses on with the Second Weighing after the Day 4 plate tells her it ends in war and exposure (Varkhuman withdraws on hearing it; the spymaster should have a stronger reason not to).
   - And explain how Farnxund learns, on Day 28, that Nanai-farn will confess.

8. **Fix the procedure of the Day 30 and Day 38 hearings, and stage why the guilty judge presides.**
   - On Day 30 Miwnay holds physical evidence (the thinning write and the wrapper with the household device) that needs no named witness, so her silence needs an on-screen reason. For example: Farnxund controls the Scale's evidence chain; or Shayn's pact protects her; or Miwnay tries and is overruled in a scene.
   - On Day 38 the file does not say who presides, or how a Review of a 63-year-old verdict also tries the Chief Weigher for three murders.
   - Give Aytash and Takhsich active roles at the Review. At present Aytash "arrives" and Takhsich's testimony appears only in the thread grid.

9. **Give Act 4 a reveal that reclassifies, not one that confirms.**
   - The founding forgery is guessable from Day 8, effectively confirmed by Day 22, and the YT 78 text adds no meaning.
   - *Direction:* use the iceberg piece already in hand, the 290 unmade days of YT 78 to 79, in which someone exposed the forgery.
     - If the unmade exposer was Farnxund (or Miwnay's younger self, or Makhdhat), the Draxt plate reclassifies a character.
     - Alternatively, use the H2 graft above: the Ordu really were preparing a burning, so Q3 bites at the climax.
   - Keep "proof and price" as the drama, but give the final act one thing the player did not supply themselves.

10. **Reduce the vocabulary and cast load.**
    - Cut or merge terms: Descent and Hush as one named event; drop "orphan" as a term; fold "rider" into "write"; drop YT from player-facing text.
    - Rename Varzakk (it collides with Varkhuman).
    - Consider cutting Qutlugh and the Ustrushana delegate as named speakers.
    - Add the on-screen relationship board at the opening that the self-check marks "Untested".
    - Plant the Draxt consent law in Act 2 (revision 4) so it is not first stated on Day 36.

11. **Respect refusal at Shayn's bargain and at the other scripted commitments.**
    - "Miwnay promises" (Day 33) is scripted, yet it supplies the cost of weighing. Make it a player choice.
    - If she refuses, the guard needs another route to a name, for example a Cup charity licence or Draxt sponsorship at a cost, and the endings should branch on it.
    - Nanai-farn's Day 3 offer to join the Dust is also scripted as refused. Accepting it could be a short, respected dead-end branch, or the scene can be reframed so there is no choice.

12. **Fix continuity slips.**
    - Aytash arrived as hostage in YT 87, when her grandfather, not her father, was Yabghu, so under "each Yabghu's heir" the hostage should have been Tardu. Fix the rule (the heir's heir) or the dates.
    - After the guard breaks his oath on Day 22, his lord's sash device should stop opening doors, yet Miwnay stays unnamed until Day 28 and depends on him for doors. Say how they get through.
    - The Old Column has never been replaced and its rings are visible to any Duster, so explain why no one (the Ordu included) ever counted them against the Register.

13. **Redo the weak options rounds.**
    - Rounds 4, 6 (AGI, cloning, aliens, time) and 10 need three genuinely different contenders each, not one plus strawmen.
    - Note in 07 that the self-check's "Three graded options per important element" item is Partial for this reason, not only because the grading was the writer's own.

14. **Evolve the real historical names forward.**
    - Tarkhun (a real Ikhshid of Samarkand), Ishbara and Tardu (real Turkic qaghans), the Afshin of Ustrushana, and Marakand and Sughd for Samarkand and Sogdia map too directly onto real people and places.
    - Keep the titles, but derive personal names by the in-world logic (sound change across a century of space travel, theophoric patterns), so the setting reads as a future culture descended from Sogdiana rather than a reskinned eighth century.
    - The anchor's name taken from the Ancient Letters is a fine homage. Log it as such.

15. **Spread the Act 4 payload to avoid a season-8 crush.**
    - Days 35 to 38 introduce the Draxt law, convoke the Review, name the guard, read two plates, weigh, open the stamp and serve the cup. The Kesh thread, the Ordu stand-down and the clone release then land in the epilogue.
    - Move the Draxt law to Act 2.
    - Move the Kesh negotiation into Aytash's Act 4 thread as on-screen play.
    - Let the market crash play out as at least one scene the player lives through (Root or Height), not only a coda image.

## Notes on specific questions asked of this review

  - The round-1/2 list fails in disguise on the founding-debt-to-alien shape (revision 4).

- **The guilty judge presides on Day 30:** acceptable only if staged on screen (revision 8).
- **Clarity:** under-counted by the writer (revision 10).
- **Agency:**
  - Weighing or not, drinking or not, the guard's name, the answer to Nanai-farn and Aytash's three ways to open the parley are all respected.
  - Shayn's bargain is not (revision 11).
