# Review: Writer A, "The Unsigned" (merged pass)

Grader: independent. I did not write this story. I read every file in `outlines/writer-a/`, `BRIEF.md`, `outlines/ROUND-3.md`, the principles, checklist and trope table in `research/REPORT.md`, and `judging/verdict.md`. ## Verdict in one paragraph

The merge improved the story. Thinnai, Ottai, the Mawsim council and Sevvanthi are real additions. The killer now faces Ponni, Thamarai is confronted in play, and Act 5 is four days instead of twelve. The premise is still the best in the round. But the merge introduced new faults in the sealed history, and the self-check marks them Pass. The worst is that the new Thinnai thread's main stake, the coupon, is blocked by Ilavenil's own closure from Day −8, so Thinnai's closure changes nothing about it. The ordeal sentences disagree between files. The ledger entry that "Mine" clears cannot exist under the new purpose-field rule. And the second frame-break hangs on a coincidence. The alien verb is a sorting step whose result never changes. Score: **8.0**, up from 7.9. It is not ready to lock.

## Sealed history audit

I rechecked every date, age and sum in `02`, then each rule's application in `03` to `06`.

**These hold:**
- Kuzhi arithmetic. Kayal's 168 vault days give about 23 outside years (AS 189 to 212). Thirty vault minutes give 25 outside hours, from Day −2 06:00 to Day −1 07:00. 188-1's 24 outside years are about 175 vault days, inside the one-year life of the leaf.
- Ages. Ponni is 44 outside and 41 biological; Minmini 43; Sevvanthi 46; Kayal 20.
- Envoy rounds come about two days apart (Day −5, −3, −1).
- The money sums: 1.5% of 60 million is 0.9 million; 60 million at 750 is 80,000 berth-years; the back-duty is 48 million and 80% of the debt; equity left is 7 million; the vidai fee is 4,000.
- The double coin and the shallowest-loop rule work for the body (two days) and the parrot (24 years, nothing shallower) in Ch 3.
- Choice 2 is consistent with rule 2: the field seals at the hour.

**Contradictions and gaps (new or surviving):**

1. **The coupon chain.** The Kaanthal closes at 07:30 on Day −8. All post through Ilavenil stops: the old Seal-holder dies for want of it, and "the post stops" (`03`, step 1). On Day −7 (Ch 6), Aruvi still expects the coupon to reach Ilavenil in four days. The coupon was undeliverable from Day −8, whatever Thinnai did. Thinnai's sympathy closure is therefore not a cause of the default, and Aruvi's stated want cannot be met by anything she does.
2. **Sentences.** `07` says Ponni's forty years are "20 plus 10 and 10 absorbed for Kayal and Kathir". `02` and `05` charge Ponni with both the body (20) and the ring (20), and rule 7 says the sole-author rule *discharges* the others, not that she absorbs their terms. The "standard after a bargain" branch gives 20, 10, 10: it drops Ponni's ring charge and sentences Kayal and Kathir as accomplices, although they were charged as principals. The "refuse" branch frees Kayal and Kathir ("Ponni alone") with no rule that does so. Nothing says whether cutting a ring is still a crime once the decree makes proxies persons, though the decree comes the day before the ordeal.
3. **"Mine" and the count of 4,116.** Rule 2 says the purpose field seals at the hour. The chamber closure was signed, so it never entered suspense. The Day −8 closure was Selvan's, and no one can sign it after the fact. So which entry does Ponni's "Mine" remove? As written, it breaks the rule the merge added to fix gap 3.
4. **Ponni's hand (Ch 25, scene 11).** "The shallow loops are already entered, so the needle settles at twenty-four years." Nothing puts Ponni's hand in any shallower loop: thresholds use ordinary safety film. Either delete the clause or name the loop.
5. **The Mawsim purser at the pyre.** Thamarai says "Let it run" to a Mawsim purser at the dawn pyre on Day −7, but the fleet arrives that afternoon (`02`; Ch 5). The clue matrix also cites the phrase in Ch 17, where it does not appear.
6. **"Blind to clockwork".** The rule sits beside safety films that clearly image toy carriers (the prologue and the gate film), and Ch 12b has "clockwork streets the Proprietor cannot see". One line should say what the blindness covers, presumably agency and attribution, not imaging.
7. **Kathir's candidacy.** On Day +2 the Seal vote is "Thamarai against Kathir", and in branch B he wins. Nedumaran's bid is planted, but Kathir's is not. He is 31, co-accused in a pending ordeal, and the son of an arrested murderer. One planted beat is needed (for example, Neithal's seat passing to him on Day 0, with a bloc that backs him to hold its plates).
8. **Who is in the 23:00 frame.** The AS 188 leaf shows one lying frame and a disc. Ponni kneels at the edge holding the seal, so at least her hand bones should appear, and the text never takes Kayal and Kathir out of the chamber before 23:00. Two lines fix it.
9. **The urn.** Kayal carries Arumbu's urn out "because it seems wrong to leave it". That is a coincidence supplying the key exhibit of the second frame-break, which fails the checklist rule "no coincidence supplies an answer". Also, nothing says how Arumbu kept the urn through her century-long sentence.

## The specific checks requested

**Scope: played or described?** Mostly played now, for two worlds and one foreign deck. The Mawsim council is a genuine court with a succession, and Saleha's monsoon seat is a plot outcome. Thinnai is played, but its plot function is broken by audit item 1, and it has no politics of its own. The alien counterpart exists, but the faction that profits never shows a face. The galaxy beyond two worlds is still texture. That is acceptable for an iceberg, but it is not yet "galactic reach on screen".

**Clarity.** Better on branches, endings and terms. The board shows the current question at every break. The risks are in Act 2: twelve consecutive teaching chapters (Ch 8 to 19) across four threads, plus some thirty rule clauses compressed into "ten rules". The ordeal alone has the urn, the standard term, priority, the stricter term and the sole-author rule. Until a cold player has been observed, the claim that the player can always state the current question is unproven.

**Act 5.** It is no longer a season-8 rush in shape. Its six chapters each have a played verb, and refusal is respected at choices 2, 3 and 4. But Day +1 carries "Mine", the interrogation, the funeral, the voided marriage, the charge, the AS 0 re-performance and a frame-break. Day +2 carries a court ruling, a private confrontation, a duel, an election and a decree. Spread these out, or cut one. The ordeal's emotional logic also needs one line: why would the urn convict all three after a bargain?

**Parley.** It is better than "read strata", and it is not dialogue in disguise: the reply is made of objects placed by route. But as specified it is a sorting step with a fixed result. No layout changes the round, the price (42% to 28%) or the timing, and nothing can fail. As it stands, the alien pillar's verb is the only one whose input never matters.

**Originality.**
- **Dune.** Unchanged. The marriage between rival houses plus a foresight economy is honestly logged as Partial.
- **Minority Report.** The stronger echo is not the licence. It is the constellation of an investigator who finds herself in the foreseen image, plus the head of her own agency quietly exploiting the foresight system to achieve a killing. `07` logs only the vidai. The funeral reading differs, and signing a closure is new, but the two beats should be logged and pulled further apart. For example, Thamarai's "let it run" could be visibly a fiscal act, not an investigative one.
- **Banned shapes.** Clear: the founding crime is human, and the Crossing is a free polity that sanctions and profits.
- **Grafts.** All five take technique, not content, from B and C. The nearest is the fleet captains' council, which shares a silhouette with B's Ordu council (a fleet court, a rival senior captain, a seat at stake). A's version is a creditor court with a lifeboat motive, so it passes, but it should be logged with B as the nearest work.

**The thali scene.** It is specific: Kayal's own anklets, the parrot, Kathir as her lover. The intent is tender. Three problems before review:
- **Sumangali.** It is a status for a woman who dies while her husband lives. Calling Minmini one after she died unmarried on Day −8 is a doctrinal claim that Tamil readers may find wrong, not just unusual.
- **"One thali, one wife".** It turns the marriage rite into a legal loophole that dissolves Kathir's marriage to Kayal that same morning. That instrumentalizes the most sacred object in the scene, and it does so to Kayal, whose consent is never shown.
- **Posthumous marriage.** Practices of this kind exist in some South Asian communities, but not as a general Tamil custom. The writer should not assume one.

Keep the anklets and the parrot. Have Tamil readers review the thali before it locks, and end the merger by another mechanism so the rite is not a plot lever.

## Top strengths

1. **The premise still generates its own scenes.** Fit, cast and sign appear nowhere else, and "it only says *there*" is planted in the prologue, a letter and an alley before it pays off.
2. **The first frame-break** (blob over blob, hole over hole), followed by the player entering *Laid out by: Ponni*, reclassifies everything the player handled, and it is fully fair.
3. **The Mawsim council.** It is a second court with a real faction fight and a quantified stake (two-thirds of the notes at 750). Its sessions mesh with the Crossing and the coupon instead of running in parallel.
4. **The anchor's arc is complete.** "Nobody's" is refused at the pyre. She steps between the pistol and the killer ("Let me assess him"), then faces him across a table. She buys every proxy's personhood with her silence and her friends' freedom with her own term. "Mine" is stamped.
5. **Sevvanthi and Ottai** give the vidai and the Crossing living faces cheaply. Sevvanthi's night on the roost, and Ottai not shaking off the letter, are quotable, planted moments.
6. **The branch budget and object glossary** are a model of load discipline for the other writers.

## Required revisions (most important first)

1. **Repair the coupon chain so that Thinnai matters causally.** Ilavenil has been shut since Day −8, so state where the coupon is paid and why Thinnai's closure blocks it. Two options:
   - The coupon is payable to the Mawsim's factor on a third bloom-world, routed through Thinnai, so Thinnai's closure is the blockage.
   - Make Aruvi's want something only Thinnai's reopening unlocks.

   Then fix `03` step 2, `04` (Aruvi's want), `05` (Ch 6 and 15), and the coupon note in `07`'s rules audit.
2. **Reconcile the ordeal law in one place.** Decide the charges (body, ring) and whether the sole-author rule *discharges* or *absorbs* the others' terms. Then state:
   - Ponni's total in each branch;
   - Kayal's and Kathir's terms in the standard branch;
   - why refusal frees them, if it does;
   - whether the ring charge survives the personhood decree;
   - why the urn convicts after a bargain.

   Make `02`, `03`, `05` and `07` match.
3. **Fix the second frame-break and the urn coincidence.** Give Kayal a reason to take the urn: the AS 0 lease in Minmini's archive naming Arumbu's room, a Balance reading of the jar, or Valli's confession. Explain how Arumbu kept it through her sentence. Make the player deduce the rigging from the Irai's "without condition" term before the film confirms it, so the reveal reclassifies rather than repeats. Move one beat off Day +1 and one off Day +2.
4. **Make parley a verb whose input matters.** Specify:
   - the answer space;
   - what a wrong layout does (a worse price, a later reopening, Thinnai left closed for the coupon);
   - at least one variable outcome tied to the economy or the branch budget.

   Give the Far Blooms one on-screen moment, such as a rust-coated envoy landing beside Ottai in round 3, so the faction that profits is seen.
5. **Repair the ledger mechanics the merge broke:**
   - Name the entry that "Mine" removes from suspense, consistent with the field sealing at the hour, or drop the count change.
   - Delete or justify the "shallow loops" on Ponni's hand.
   - Define what "blind to clockwork" covers.
   - Fix who is in the 23:00 frame.
6. **Run the originality pass on the real Minority Report echo.** Log the investigator-in-the-vision and agency-head-enabler constellation, and differentiate it on screen. Log B's fleet council as the nearest work for G1. Keep Dune at Partial unless the marriage can be made less dynastic.
7. **Have Tamil readers review the thali scene, and end the merger by another mechanism.** Resolve the posthumous *sumangali* claim, and show Kayal's consent if Kathir's act affects her marriage.
8. **Correct the arithmetic table.** Fix the berth-capacity line, place the 900 slow rate on the timeline (or cut it), and say "notes", not "all berth-years". Add one line on where convicts' berths come from when 30 years of capacity is pre-sold.
9. **Close the remaining plants:**
   - Thamarai's second payoff on Day −14. She knows of Valli's AS 207 chamber lease through the Irai's reading of the letters, so she can expect a closure Ponni might hold, or else make it explicitly a long shot.
   - Plant Kathir's candidacy.
   - Move the purser out of the dawn pyre.
   - Fix the Ch 17 citation in the clue matrix.
10. **Prove clarity before adding anything.** Run a cold read or playtest of Acts 1 and 2, with a per-chapter "current question" list. Consider merging re-performance into lease and tail into fit, so that Act 2 is not twelve consecutive teaching chapters.
11. **Give Thinnai one played decision by a Thinnai authority.** For example, the treasury hearing could become a small intrigue scene in which Aruvi trades or withholds the retention orders. Thinnai would then be a court, not only a roost.
12. **Correct `07` and `08`.** Remark the rules audit, P3 and P4 honestly, and mark the superseded second-pass entries in `08` as replaced, so that the next grader can trust the self-check.
