# Review: Writer A, "The Unsigned" (tone and consistency pass)

Grader: independent. I did not write this story. I read every file in `outlines/writer-a/` (`01` to `08`), all of `BRIEF.md` (especially "Tone: cool, not spooky"), `outlines/ROUND-3.md`, the principles, checklist and trope table in `research/REPORT.md`, and the previous review, `judging/review-writer-a-merged.md`. ## Verdict in one paragraph

The tone pass worked. The death, pyre, funeral, ordeal, sewn ring and thali-on-the-dead are gone from the story files. What replaced them is cool rather than merely scrubbed: a dock under a kilometre-high bloom, a rooftop chase, a gantry chase, a starship council, an alien envoy waiting for an answer, and a vault heist in which every minute costs fifty. The time-napping is the best replacement crime any writer could have found here. It is a crime only this world allows, it builds to a heist, and it lets the pen pals meet. Most of the previous review's ledger, arithmetic, coupon and thali faults are fixed. But the reframe opened a hole in the premise itself. Under rule 2 the law identifies the sealer by metal alone, so Minmini sealing the deed in Kayal's anklets is, in law, the same act as Kayal sealing it herself. Casting Minmini is therefore legally redundant, and Ponni takes twenty extra years (the bracelet count) for it. The breach clause, which is the climax's main legal stake, is never resolved. Several smaller rule violations are also new, and `07` marks them Pass. Score: **8.0**, level with the previous pass. It is not ready to lock.

## Tone audit

**Clean.** A search of `02` to `06` finds no death, corpse, funeral, pyre, crypt, grave, ghost, ordeal, gore or body horror. The images are deliberately clean: glass cradles, a locked steel bracelet, mineral-silk lilies, luminous seed-head carriers and gold pollen light. The sentence ("sent ahead" to a bright garden berth with letters arriving) is warm, not a living death. The wedding is a celebration.

**Actively cool, not just scrubbed.** Yes. The chapters now carry spy-thriller verbs and set pieces:
- a slow ship docking under a shut kilometre-high bloom (Ch 1);
- a rooftop chase over kolam light (Ch 14);
- a gantry chase among carriers folding in (Ch 26);
- a deck court on a district-sized starship (Ch 8, 23, 30);
- an envoy that lands and waits for an answer (Ch 16, 28);
- a heist against two clocks (Ch 32);
- a swap under guard (Ch 33).

The weakest link is Ch 33. The "swap under guard" is announced, but Minmini is simply "inside" the Sealing Hall. Play how she gets past Neithal's runner: that is the scene the brief wants.

**Residues to clear** (all small):
1. **"Urn"** is used for the bead jar throughout. A ballot urn is correct usage, but in a story that has just removed a "conscience urn" and a pyre, the funerary echo is avoidable. Consider a *kalasam* or a "count jar".
2. **"The bought omen"** (Ch 8, 23; red herrings). The brief bans death omens, not markets in forecasts, but "omen" is the occult word. Call it the readers' *forecast* or *reading*.
3. **Arumbu's room.** It has been sealed since AS 140, with a founder's effects "retained", and the heist lifts an object from it. Unless it is stated that Arumbu is alive and banked inside, or that the room is a registry store, it reads as a tomb being robbed. If she is alive and banked, that is also a large unused beat.
4. **"A child's shoe from a ship that never arrived"** (scene 8) is a mourning image. Use an object that expresses the want, not a loss.
5. **"A dead roost"** (Ch 16), "the elders will die aboard" (`03`), and "dead-man's box". The last is genuine spycraft and can stay. Change the first two.
6. **`01-options.md`** keeps the superseded death rounds as a process record, under a note. That is fine. But its required seed table (top of file) still lists "the shadow ordeal, with its conscience urn", "the weapon" and "the Kuzhi". Update it to match `07`.

## Did the reframe keep the premise's power and the stakes?

**Mostly yes on stakes, not yet on the premise.**

*Stakes and danger.* There is still a crime worth solving. Minmini is time-napped for forty years, and if the wedding passes she loses everyone she knows. The toymaker is taken. Selvan is shot on a stair. A pistol is raised. A firm's 120,000 berth-years and a sovereign debt hang on one night. The thriller register suits time-napping better than it suited a corpse. What is lost is the gut-punch. `07` admits the "grief engine" is gone. The danger to Ponni herself is low until Act 5: no one hunts her, and the runner never turns on her. One beat in which Neithal or the Commission moves against Ponni directly (a warrant on her, the runner at her window) would restore the pressure of the second disaster.

*The premise.* The death version worked because who lay in the foreseen image mattered: someone died in it. In the sealing version, rule 2 says the record "identifies the sealer by the metal on record", so Minmini in Kayal's anklets *is* Kayal in law. That has two consequences:
- `04` rejects "Kayal herself, sealing the deed" because "the breach clause would trigger". But Minmini sealing the deed produces exactly the same legal record. Either both trigger the clause or neither does.
- The story never resolves the breach clause. Nedumaran arrives at 23:40 to "seize the anklets under the breach clause", Ponni arrests him for other crimes, and the clause's effect on the berth-notes is never ruled on. That is the climax's main legal stake.

The casting must change something in law, or the central move of the premise is decoration. One way to fix it: make the marriage contract bind *the bride's person*, as the original in person, while the deed binds *the firm* by metal. Kayal at the table sealing anything else is the bride refusing, and the breach clause triggers. A stand-in sealing in the firm's metal is the firm acting, and the bride, who was never at the table, has not refused. Nedumaran cannot prove otherwise, because the scan shows no faces. That turns "it only says *there*" into the winning legal argument, and it makes Choice 1 a real choice.

How Ilavenil's sealings were witnessed between AS 208 and Day −7, while Ponni was in transit, also needs one line.
7. **Ending D is not forced.** If Kayal seals the merger, "Minmini stays banked for forty years". But by Day −2 everyone knows the berth, Selvan has agreed to testify that the holder caused the loss (which voids the vidai), and the toymaker in the next berth is a kidnapped person. Why could no lawful opening follow after the wedding? Rule 9 and `07` hint that berths are inviolable. 8. **Transit to the Mella.** The ring circles a "dark companion star" deep enough to slow time fiftyfold, yet the runner gets Minmini from her bed into a berth between 02:30 and 04:00, and the heist team crosses each way in about an hour. ## Seeds reinterpreted

- **Trial by ordeal → the Proof.** It is visible but weakened. A three-round contest of exhibits with bead voting is a jury trial, and nothing in it is an *ordeal*: a test the body or the person must pass. The sole-author stand, where the accused takes the whole weight to free the others, is the ordeal's remaining spirit. Make it explicit, for example by having the accused who stands as sole author re-perform her act in the hall under the scan. That keeps the seed's sense of a public test without any superstition.
- **X-ray film → scans, leaves and lightboxes.** Strong. Every major reveal is a leaf on a lightbox.
- **Pen pals who never met.** Strong. Twenty-two years and 39 of 40 chapters without meeting, then one meeting in a vault, then letters again in the epilogue. The meeting (Ch 32) and the roost evening (Ch 35) are the emotional peak of the pass.

## Top strengths

1. **The time-napping.** A crime only this world allows: a victim banked forward in a vault, four hours older while nine days pass. It turns the climax into a heist against two clocks and lets the pen pals meet. It is the right answer to the tone brief, not a compromise.
2. **The first frame-break is intact and fair.** Blob over blob, bar over clasp, hole over hole, the deed inside the bird. "It was never the merger" reclassifies everything the player handled.
3. **The tone is cool on screen.** The tone pass is backed by real set pieces: the dock, the two chases, the deck court, the envoys and the vault. Proxies, blooms and pollen are clean and full of awe.
4. **Thinnai now works.** The coupon chain is causal, the Treasury Bench is a small court with a played decision, and Ottai carrying Aruvi's letter is quotable.
