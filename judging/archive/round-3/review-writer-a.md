# Review: round 3, writer A ("The Unsigned")

Grader: independent. I read all seven files in `outlines/writer-a/` against `BRIEF.md`, `outlines/ROUND-3.md` and `research/REPORT.md` (principles, checklist, trope table). I did not open writer B or C, or the archives.

## Verdict in brief

This is a strong, specific draft. Its core image would carry a game on its own: the future arrives as a faceless x-ray, so you can survive your own foreseen death by growing someone to fit it, and a corpse can fill a "death" because a shadow doesn't say dead. The anchor is excellent, the seeds work hard, the Tamil-derived naming is the most specific in any round so far, and the planted objects (the parrot's solder blob, the seal with a hole, the cup held left hand under) pay off honestly.

It has three serious weaknesses. **(1) The central law contradicts the AGI's own rule.** Leasing already makes you the author of what the building does, so Ponni's signature cannot be unprecedented as written. **(2) The x-ray foresight rule is broken in several places**, because routine inspections would expose live late leaves and the Balance gives wrong depths. **(3) The scope is galactic on the ledger board but not on screen.** Every playable scene is in one star system over seventeen days, the aliens read as flora rather than a civilisation, and court intrigue is something the player watches rather than plays. Agency at the two big committed choices is also weaker than the self-check claims.

## Top strengths

1. **The premise generates the plot.** "A shadow doesn't say dead" is taught in a comic alley scene in Act 2 and then decides the climax. This is exactly the Pacific Rim test: the idea itself makes the dilemma.
2. **The anchor and her verb are one thing.** An assessor who refuses to stamp "Nobody's" in scene 2 and stamps "Mine" at the end. The question (who owes for an authorless act?) is mechanical in every pillar.
3. **Object payoffs.** The solder blob, hole over hole, the left-hand-under cup hold, the dead-man's box and Valli's pistol are all handled by the player before they matter. Cutting out the ring in silence and the parrot walking to a stop in a dead palm will be remembered.
4. **The political economy is causal, not ornamental.** The hoard's release closes the bloom, which halts the post, which crashes the Shadow Market, which triggers the Mawsim ultimatum. The halted post kills the Seal-holder's medicine supply, so the vote moves to the wedding morning. This is the best part of the scope.
5. **Cultural specificity.** Sangam *thinai* moods mapped onto business (Paalai keeps people apart, Neithal is anxious waiting), kolam as both lease-line and cipher, the parrot- and bee-messenger genres, *Ainnurruvar*, and caste titles deliberately avoided. It is respectful and textured. The one exception is the thali rite (revision 12).
6. **The Proprietor.** A bookkeeper AGI with the want "an author for every entry". It avoids every banned AI shape and still matters.
7. **The pen-pal turn.** "I have stopped writing to you for them. This one is mine", followed by a flat, not-looped reading, is the best quiet reveal in the draft.

## Options review (01-options.md)

- **Strong, genuine rounds:** central question (Q1 to Q3 are really different), setting (S1 to S3), hidden history (H1 against H2; H3 is weaker), and ending (E1 to E3, where E2 and E3 are real alternatives).
- **Foils or strawmen:** AGI A2 ("literal genie", rejected by trope label) and A3 (one line). Cloning C2 and C3 (one line each). Aliens V2 (rejected because it is banned, so it was never in contention) and V3 ("duplicates the vault"). Politics P2 and P3 (one line each). Founding crime F3. **Aliens and politics need real second and third options**, because those two areas are where the scope is weakest.
- **Where I would pick differently:**
  - **Aliens: none of V1 to V3 as written.** Keep the Vandu's biology from V1, but add a civilisational layer: the blooms as a polity that sets routes, and so prices, deliberately, with a reason of its own. V1 is the right base, but it gives the brief's "advanced civilisation" a pollinating plant.
  - **Ending: E1, split in two.** Separate *casting* the shadow from *signing* for it (revision 4). Signing is the thematic choice and should be refusable on its own.
  - **Premise sentence:** option 2 ("When the future arrives as an x-ray, you survive your death by growing someone to fit it") is the most striking. It gives away the midpoint, though, so use it internally as the pitch line and keep the merged sentence for player-facing copy. The merged pick runs to about twenty words; trim it.
  - **Hidden history:** H1 is right. H2's serial "licensed deaths" deserves more than texture. It is the law that makes Ilavenil frightening, and it needs rules (revision 1).

## Banned lists

- **Round 1 and 2 averages: no breach.** One echo should be watched. A society lives off an alien's service, keeps a hidden wrong done to it (the hoard), and the fix is an in-person return by the heir inside the vault. The founding secret is human, so this passes, but the reopening beat should not read as "making it right with the creature". Revision 3 (giving the Vandu agency) solves this too.
- **Kurinji:** the real *neelakurinji* is famous for flowering every twelve years. The story's rib count is irregular, so there is no breach, but the bloom must never be described as cyclical.
- **Space-opera averages: Partial, not Pass, on "Dune lookalike"** (revision 10). No AI-uprising, AI-god or good-all-along shape appears, there is no precursor gates, and there is no Jedi analogue.

## Rule and date audit (problems found)

1. **Authorship law versus leasing.** `03` says that by leasing "you become the author of what the building did". Every cassette exposure therefore already has a named lessee (Uppalam Salt, Neithal's Shadow Market leases), and the Proprietor could move thousands of entries out of suspense. Ponni's signature is then not the first. This contradiction sits at the centre of the theme.
2. **Licensed deaths make murder legal.** Under the Doctrine, anyone with a cassette lease can apparently kill on Ilavenil if they first see a leaf of the outcome. That raises questions the files never answer. Why is Selvan's confession a landmark? Why does no one fear living there? Why is Kuyil never charged for Paavai and Selvan, even after the Doctrine is voided "ten years back"?
3. **Late leaves exposed by routine inspections.** The Proprietor x-rays every room routinely. Kayal's room inspection shows the envelope in her chest, so the late leaf inside was in the beam. Ponni also handles the bare late leaf from Day −4 to Day 0. Under the rule "every exposure the late leaf will receive", the AS 189 early leaf would show the Slow House bedroom and lightbox rooms superimposed on the chamber. Shielding needs a rule (for example, vault envelopes are lead-lined and leaves are only ever unsheathed inside a cassette).
4. **Selvan's licence leaf.** It was developed on Day −10 and shows "the next 04:00 inspection", which is Day −9, not Day −8. If the bedroom is inspected nightly, his leaf would show two exposures: Day −9 with no toy and Day −8 with the toy. The inspection cadence needs to be stated.
5. **Balance depths.** The body reads "about a day deep", but the licence leaf jumped about two days (Day −8 back to Day −10). More seriously, Minmini's body exists *because of* the AS 189 leaf, so it should read twenty-three years deep, as should Kayal and Valli. Chapter 3 reads twenty-three years on the parrot but not on the body. Either define how the Balance reports several loops (for example, only the shallowest, or the heaviest), or the chapter 3 reading gives away the plot or contradicts the rule.
6. **The prologue sweep** shows Minmini winding the toy at 22:20 under x-ray. The stated inspection is at 04:00, and any 22:20 exposure would also land on Selvan's late leaf. The fix could be the hook itself: reframe the prologue as Selvan's early leaf, or as the Proprietor's non-x-ray safety film, and say which.
7. **Dates.**
   - Minmini is "grown AS 188 to 189", but Valli only develops the leaf in AS 189, so the growth starts before the reason for it exists.
   - `02`, Day −14, says Ponni arrives "on Day −8"; she docks on Day −7.
   - Paavai is killed on Day −5 "tipped off that Ponni is coming", but Ponni is already there.
   - Valli's leaf shows a rib count "twenty-three *seasons* ahead" and she reads it as twenty-three *years*. Define a season.
   - The houses are named after "the five Sangam landscapes", but the assembly has hundreds of kudi. Clarify that these are five great houses (and say where Mullai and Marutham are).
8. **Proxy property.** At the folding the proxy hands back all leases and property and has "no estate". Yet Ponni is "executor" of Minmini's estate, which holds an old-lease archive including the Ordeal Lease, bought while Minmini was Kayal's legal agent and so legally Kayal's. Add a rule that lets a proxy hold leases in its own self-chosen name, or have the archive pass to Kayal, who gives it to Ponni.
9. **Nedumaran's knowledge.** The tracker says he learns on Day −2, via Kuyil, that the shadow is a funeral. He then fails to guard the bier, lets Kuyil escort the couple into a chamber where the body waits, does not contest the chamber cassette, and at 23:40 comes to "take the bride" as if he expected her dead. Either he does not learn it, or his actions must change.
10. **The steeped garlands** appear only on the refusal path. If Neithal planned them, they exist on the main path too and threaten Kayal in the chamber. Plant them, and defuse them on the main path.
11. **Valli hides the late leaf in Kayal's chest** with no stated reason, and on the refusal path Neithal somehow knows where it is.
12. **Selvan's courier log** sits in an office controlled by the agency of the person it incriminates, for eight days after a murder in the same office, and nobody secures it. This is tolerable only if Thamarai does not know the box exists. State that she doesn't.

## Required revisions (numbered, in priority order)

1. **Make the authorship law airtight.** Distinguish *leasing an exposure* from *authoring an act*. One direction: under the Doctrine, a cassette lessee rents only the inspection, which the Charter would perform anyway, so it is "the house's act". A loop becomes *signed* only when someone files a **purpose of closure** under their own name, an option that has existed in the Proprietor's lease forms since AS 0 and that no one has ever used, because doing so creates liability. Ponni's "to close a leaf" is then the first. Then write the **licensed-death statute**: what it permits (only the death of persons without standing, such as proxies, or only within a lease-holder's own premises), what it costs (a fee to the Proprietor, an exile clause), and why Paavai's and Selvan's deaths are still crimes under it. This turns H2 into a real engine and explains why Ilavenil is frightening.
2. **Repair the x-ray foresight rule across all files.** Add a shielding rule for live leaves (lead-lined vault envelopes and cassettes; an unsheathed live leaf is a hazard everyone avoids, which would make a good tense scene when Ponni must handle it). State the inspection cadence per room type. Define how the Balance reports multiple loops and fix the chapter 3 readings (body: two days *and* a fainter twenty-three-year trace, if that is the rule; either way the player must not get the answer in chapter 3). Fix Selvan's leaf day, the prologue's exposure source, the AS 188 growth date, Day −8, "seasons", and the Paavai tip-off. Re-run the self-check's "no scene violates a limit" item honestly; it is currently marked Pass in error.
3. **Make the scope visible, not reported.**
   - (a) Give the **Vandu a civilisational layer**: the blooms collectively set routes, and so prices, as a deliberate polity with a want beyond pollination (for example, they treat human cargo as tribute and judge a world by how it handles kept things). Let one off-Ilavenil bloom act in the plot, such as a second bloom-world closing in sympathy, which forces the Mawsim's hand.
   - (b) Put **another world on screen** through the setting's own means: carried-film shadows and post-borne objects from Uppalam and Thinnai that the player handles, the epilogue letters carrying x-ray plates of events on three worlds, or one playable Thumbi chapter on the Mawsim flagship as a foreign polity with its own court.
   - (c) Make **court intrigue playable**: give Kayal a bead-whipping or vote-trading verb across Acts 2 to 4. Run Thamarai's Seal campaign visibly from Act 2, with the Mawsim as her backers. The succession should be a contest the player can affect, not a count they watch.
4. **Respect refusal at both committed choices.**
   - (a) **Split casting from signing.** Let the player cast the shadow (save Kayal with Minmini's body) *without* signing: Valli, or an anonymous shell, holds the cassette lease. Then Kayal lives, the Doctrine stands, Thamarai does not rise, and Ponni stays free but complicit. That is a real, respected outcome with its own epilogue. The current refusal path, in which Kayal dies, is the rejected E2 in disguise: a fail state that teaches "you should have obeyed".
   - (b) **Make the ordeal a real branch.** With the standard term the coin should be genuinely open. One self-consistent way to do this is to have the leaf's result follow from what the player has already committed to, such as whether they release the Ordeal Lease in Act 5. An acquittal should lead to a different, live political ending, with Ponni free and holding the Ordeal Lease against Seal-holder Thamarai. That is a spy endgame, not a coda.
   - (c) **Motivate the strict term.** Self-conviction currently reads as martyrdom because the standard term already prevents rigging. Give it a concrete purpose: taking sole authorship clears Kayal and Kathir, who are accomplices to the body theft and to defying the court order, and makes the precedent unappealable.
5. **Stage the six rule systems and de-rush Act 5.**
   - Act 1 should teach only **lease** and **weigh** (Ponni), plus the proxy *fact* (the ring at the pyre), which needs no verb.
   - Move Thumbi's petal test and **strata** to the opening of Act 2, where they pay off within the hour.
   - Merge **fork-find** and **pass** into one verb (study a habit, then perform it).
   - Merge **develop, fit and stage** into one "fit" family, taught in a single lightbox scene.
   - Split chapter 10: the alley "doesn't say dead" scene and the market ordeal should be separate beats on separate days.
   - Keep the ledger board, but put no more than one new rule in any chapter.
   - For Act 5, move the **Thamarai reveal** to the end of Act 4 (for example, have the courier box open on Day 0 *during* the wedding, as a Kathir or Thumbi beat). Then Act 5 is a full act about the founding crime and the ordeal, with its own investigation, rather than three reveals in one chapter after the climax.
6. **Cap the verb list.** Publish a verb budget (about eight core verbs) and say which pillar owns each. Twelve verbs across four viewpoints in a game meant to be easy to follow will fail the cold-playtester test.
7. **Fix proxy property law** (audit item 8) so that the archive and the Ordeal Lease can legally be Ponni's. Ideally make it a scene: Kayal *gives* Ponni the archive, which deepens their bond before the climax.
8. **Fix Nedumaran's knowledge and the chamber security** (audit items 9 to 11). Plant the garlands on the main path and have the player defuse them, perhaps as a Kathir stealth beat. Give Valli a reason to hide the leaf in the chest: for example, the chest is the one object the vault's own staff never inspect.
9. **Ground the dilation-bank economics.** Money parked in the vault does not compound any faster than money outside; only the *depositor* gains. Make Paalai's notes claims on **vault berth-time**. Berths are scarce (one dark star), and every rich person wants them to skip a bad decade. That is why they are the reserve asset and why "the slow rate" is the base rate. Then say what happens to Paalai, the berths and the notes after the hoard and Valli's sentence. At present the confederation's reserve bank has no aftermath.
10. **Distance it from Dune.** The current set has feuding great houses joined by a marriage alliance, foresight tied to an alien-mediated resource, a guild that monopolises faster-than-light carriage (the Thoothar against the Spacing Guild), and a house named for the desert opposite a house of the sea. Change at least two of these:
    - Make the roost a **municipal cooperative or a Vandu-chartered body** rather than a monopoly guild with a venal master.
    - Present Paalai and Neithal visibly as **firms** (shareholders, a board, a balance sheet the player reads in the Shadow Market) rather than dynasties.
    - Keep Paalai's *thinai* meaning (separation), but never show desert imagery.
    - Log the Spacing Guild parallel in `07`.
11. **Keep the player unsure of Kathir's leak.** Chapter 5's flashback tells the player in Act 1 that Nedumaran "will handle it", which answers "which house?" before the investigation starts. Cut it before Nedumaran is shown, so the listener is a silhouette that could be Valli, Nedumaran or Thamarai. Reveal the listener in chapter 22. This also strengthens the Valli red herring.
12. **Replace the invented thali rite.** "Giving your thali to another woman to dissolve a marriage" inverts deeply felt meanings (removing the thali signals widowhood) and will read as careless. Use something real and moving instead. A married woman who dies before her husband is honoured as a *sumangali*, dressed with her thali. Let **Kathir tie a thali on Minmini's body**, marrying the woman he loved and honouring her as a wife in death. This fits the shadow, gives Kathir his redemption act, and ends the alliance by a legal route (the Five Hundred's law voids a marriage the groom has made to another). Kayal's anklets then remain her own gift. Have it reviewed by Tamil readers either way.
13. **Plant the climax rites earlier.** Neither the chamber custom, Kuyil's escort role, nor whichever thali act you choose is shown before Day 0. Show one wedding-night chamber procession (another couple's) in Act 2.
14. **Raise the weak option rounds.** Write genuine second and third options for aliens, politics, cloning and AGI, with the same depth as the setting and ending rounds, and re-grade. The aliens round is the one most likely to change the story.

## Smaller notes

- Ilavenil as a post hub for forty-one worlds is asserted, not explained. One line of reason would do (for example, the Kurinji is the oldest bloom and carriers route through it).
- A Seal-holder shot by a house head draws sixty years. Say whether the slow sentence is uniform or scaled, because it decides how Valli's and Ponni's terms compare.
- Ponni's self-reading on Day −2 (looped, twenty-three years) is published on the board by her own rule. Who reads it, and what do they do?
- Kuyil, Kaatravan and Saleha are static. That is acceptable, but give Saleha one turn, since she is the Mawsim's face and the Mawsim are the best scope asset you have.
