# The five pitches side by side

All five explorations are in this folder. This page is the coordinator's summary to help the owner pick.

| | 1 Throne | 2 Armada | 3 Prince | 4 Two futures | 5 Clone army |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **One line** | Five houses fight for the throne after the emperor is murdered. | A soldier and her AI fight an alien armada that always knows the next move. | An alien prince is murdered on a human station; a cop and the prince's alien bodyguard have ten days to stop a war. | Two teams of time travellers each say the other is lying about the war to come. | A clone army holds the line, and one clone murders another on the long-dead general's order. |
| **Who you play** | Ward, the palace AI | Ines, a soldier regrown with a week missing, and her AI Bosun | Sam, a human cop, with Patience, the alien bodyguard | Dez, a spy, with her AI partner Sabi | Joana, a clone military police captain, with the general's old AI and a human lawyer |
| **Sci-fi rules** | A Gate run backwards sends one person into the past; clone doubles can't inherit | Messages go back exactly one week; the dead regrow with their last week lost | Aliens send letters up to ten days back, from a small supply; humans grow body doubles of leaders | Only information travels back; changes make it go stale | A Gate is a one-way door thirty years into the future |
| **Invented terms** | 5 | 5 | 1 ("Aun") | 5 | 5 |
| **Biggest twist** | The killer is the young heir you back, forty years older | The traitor is your own Fleet, which made a deal with an alien prince | The dead prince was a double, and the real one is hiding | The team that is always right is a fake built by your own AI | The order was real, and every clone remembers giving it |
| **Iceberg underneath** | A hidden founder who was also a time traveller; an alien civilisation behind a dark Gate | A 12,000-year-old alien civil war fought with foresight; humans built their colonies in the aliens' harbours | Doubles invented to beat assassins who knew the future; a dynasty founded on a betrayed peace table | A faked alien attack that started the war | The general was copied a week before learning the truth about the aliens |
| **Nearest lookalikes** | Dishonored; Game of Thrones | Halo | Star Trek VI | Terminator; Dark | Star Wars' Order 66; Ender's Game |

## Coordinator's read

- **Simplest and clearest:** 3 Prince. It is a buddy-cop murder with a ticking clock, has only one made-up word, and its twists are easy to follow (a double, a hidden prince, a mother who has lived it before).
- **Closest to the Game of Thrones feel:** 1 Throne. Five houses, a crown, a war, an AI detective. Its time-travel twist is familiar but placed in Act 3, not the finale.
- **Closest to the Halo feel:** 2 Armada. A soldier, her AI, an alien fleet, a deep alien history. Its best idea is a soldier investigating the week she can't remember.
- **Most intricate twist chain, so the hardest to keep simple:** 4 and 5.
