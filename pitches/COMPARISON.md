# The five pitches side by side

Each pitch has its own folder with a full write-up. Every write-up opens with the story on one page. Read those five pages first; they take ten minutes in total.

| | Throne | Armada | Prince | Two futures | Clone army |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| In one line | Five houses fight for the throne after the emperor is murdered. | A soldier and her AI fight an alien fleet that always knows the next move. | An alien prince dies on a human station, and a cop and his alien bodyguard have ten days to stop a war. | Two teams from two futures each say the other is lying about the coming war. | One of a million clones of a dead general kills another, on the general's own order. |
| You play | Ward, the palace AI | Ines, a soldier missing a week of memory, with her AI | Sam, a cop, with Patience, the prince's bodyguard | Dez, a harbour officer, with the station AI | Joana, a clone detective |
| The time rule | One person can go back through a Gate, one way | Messages come back exactly one week | The aliens can send short letters up to ten days back | Only signals come back, so travellers get new clone bodies | The Gate sends ships thirty years forward |
| Made-up words | 5 | 5 | 1 | 5 | 5 |
| The big turn | The killer is the heir you back, forty years older | Your own Fleet feeds the aliens the future | The dead prince was a copy, and the real one is hiding | The team that is always right is a fake | The order was real, and every clone remembers giving it |
| Feels most like | Game of Thrones | Halo | A buddy-cop film | A spy thriller | A military courtroom drama |

## My read

Prince is the easiest to follow. It has one made-up word, a ticking clock and a clean twist on day five.

Throne is the closest to Game of Thrones, and Armada is the closest to Halo. Armada has the best hook: a soldier hunts through the week she can't remember.

Two futures and Clone army have the twistiest endings. That makes them the hardest to keep simple.
